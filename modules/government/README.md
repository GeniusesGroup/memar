# Government
A [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government) is the system or group of people governing an organized community, generally a state.   
In the case of its broad associative definition, government normally consists of [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature), [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_(government)), and [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary).

## State
A [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_(polity)) is a political entity that regulates society and the population within a territory. Government is considered to form the fundamental apparatus of contemporary states.
