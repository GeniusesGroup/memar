## Model

- `(product)-[:customable_by]->(rule)`

## Policies (rules)

- Customized products are unique and built-to-order.
- Typically these products have a large number of variants, such as
- furniture with different materials and colors,
- and jewelry with different settings and stones.
- Or they may be personalized, like t-shirts with printed logos and picture frames with a name engraved.
