## Findings
### 1. What the domain is for

**Statement (agent, Tentative).** The account domain answers one question for the rest of economics: *how much of one thing is held at this place, and how did it come to be so?* It owns the **memory of realized quantity change**: for one node with one count unit, the ordered, append-only history of effects that actually happened, with the resulting running total stored at each step so that any past total is a single read. It deliberately decides nothing about who may instruct a movement (authority), who holds legal title ([ownership](../../rights/ownership/ownership.md)), what may leave right now ([restriction](../../../modules-plugable/economics/restriction/restriction.md)), what was asked for ([transaction](../transaction/transaction.md)), what the unit is ([count_unit](../../identity/thing/edges/count_unit/count_unit.md)), or what the quantity means to a business (cash, credit, budget, points, stock).

**What the documents already say (source).**
- An account is the node that holds the state of a quantity that must be kept, increased, decreased, transferred or settled; it is a `thing` whose unit comes from the general `count_unit_is` edge; its kernel is exactly one edge, `bookkeeping` ([account.md](account.md#model)).
- The balance is not stored on the account; availability is evaluated from rules ([account.md](account.md#policies-rules)); holding is not title; a negative balance is neither allowed nor forbidden by the kernel; everything else is pluggable.
- A bookkeeping record is the realized effect of a transaction on one account, never edited nor deleted; reversal is a new record from a new transaction ([bookkeeping.md → Policies](edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md#policies-rules)).
- Unit homogeneity is on the account; cross-unit operations are two same-unit transactions linked by `matching_system` ([account.handoff.md](account.handoff.md#decisions)).

**The finding that moves the domain (agent, Tentative; owner questions 1 to 3).** The documents state what the account *is not* with great care, and almost nothing about what it *is*, so three things a ledger owner must say are left in no document:

- **Where quantity comes from and goes to.** A transaction "conserves quantity" and may not credit before it debits ([transaction.handoff.md](../transaction/transaction.handoff.md#decisions), item 3), yet a transaction's source is optional at registration ([transaction.md](../transaction/transaction.md#model)), the credit module records that "money that exists as debt" is not modeled, and the settlement module rejects "money creation from nothing" as a primitive in favour of two transactions in two units ([settlement](../../../modules-plugable/economics/settlement/settlement.md#rationale-and-alternatives)) without saying what funds the first leg. Issuance, destruction, spoilage and transformation (scenarios S4, S8) have no stated other side.
- **When an account exists and when it stops.** No document says what makes a thing an account, who may make it one, whether it may be closed, or what a closed account does. The domain has no status, no creator, no remover. The edge to the account's holder is stated three ways (finding "Contradictions", item 3).
- **What the account promises its readers.** The balance of an account that has no record yet is undefined; the order in which effects are recorded is not tied to the time they take effect; an account with two co-holders has no stated shape.

**The completable classification sentence** ([Modeling → Classification Emerges From Rules and Relations](../../../docs/modeling.md#classification-emerges-from-rules-and-relations-not-from-intrinsic-labels)) for the proposal: *a thing may be classified as an account because it has exactly one count unit and has been opened as a ledger by a declarer at a time (an acquired fact), and therefore a transaction may name it as source or destination, each realized effect on it is recorded once in order with its resulting total, the total at any past record may be read, and rules may attach to it.* If a label cannot complete that sentence, it is not an account relation (finding 5).

**What the domain is not for (agent, with sources).**
- It is not the bank, the wallet, the chart of accounts, the cash register, the stock room or the budget. Those are views and capabilities over it; aggregates are consequences of modeling, not primitives ([Domain Decomposition over Aggregate-Root Modeling](../../../docs/modeling.md#domain-decomposition-over-aggregate-root-modeling)).
- It is not a kind taxonomy. Real, Personal, cash, credit, deposit are words on an account, not kinds of it ([account-labels](../../../modules-plugable/economics/account-labels/account-labels.md#abstract)).
- It is not money. The quantity may be money, kilograms, shares, points or capacity ([account.md](account.md)).

#### Scenarios

Ten cases from several countries and industries. For each: what happens, what the obvious model says, what breaks, and what it asks of the domain. The first eight are load-bearing for the proposal; all ten are used in the model comparison. Names and figures are illustrative.

**S1. A rotating savings circle (susu in West Africa, tanda in Mexico, hui in China, chit funds in India, dowreh-style circles in Iran).** Twelve neighbours each pay 100 units a month; each month one member takes the whole pot of 1,200; the order is agreed or drawn. The organizer holds the pot and is trusted, not licensed.
- *Obvious model:* each member has an account and the organizer moves money between them.
- *What breaks:* (a) the pot belongs to no one member, and the person who holds it is neither its owner nor its beneficiary; (b) a member's future 11 contributions are obligations, not balance, and must not appear as quantity; (c) default by a member is a social event with a rule, not an account event.
- *Asks of the domain:* the account must not know who its holders or members are; obligations are deferred, conditional transactions; the group holder is `ownership`'s and `group`'s concern. The kernel survives unchanged, which is itself a finding: the demands fall on authority, ownership and transaction.

**S2. A mobile-money agent network (the Kenyan M-Pesa style) and an informal broker network (hawala).** A customer hands cash to an agent; the agent sends electronic value to the customer's phone. The agent holds two stocks, cash in the drawer and electronic float; the operator holds a trust account at a bank whose balance must equal the sum of all customers' and agents' electronic value. In a broker network, two brokers owe each other the difference of many transfers and settle it days later, sometimes in goods.
- *Obvious model:* every holder has one account in the currency.
- *What breaks:* (a) the drawer cash and the electronic float are the same currency yet separate stocks that must be told apart; (b) "the trust account equals the sum of the others" is an invariant across many accounts that no single account owns; (c) brokers' mutual balances are negative by design and settle in another unit.
- *Asks of the domain:* a holder may have several accounts of one unit; a cross-account invariant needs an owner (the settlement tier already exists for tiered bookkeeping, [settlement](../../../modules-plugable/economics/settlement/settlement.md)); cross-unit settlement is two transactions, as already decided.

**S3. Mutual credit and time banks (Sardinia's Sardex, local exchange trading systems, Japan's care-credit "fureai kippu", community time banks).** Members open at zero. Buying makes a member's balance negative and the seller's positive; the sum over all members is always zero; there is no issuer and no deposit. A limit on how far negative a member may go is set by reputation and grows.
- *Obvious model:* an account is funded first and spending is refused beyond the balance.
- *What breaks:* "opening balance" has no meaning; negative is the normal state; the credit limit changes over time and is a rule with parameters, not the single flag the credit module holds ([credit](../../../modules-plugable/economics/credit/credit.md#model)). The unit (an hour) is not even interchangeable by skill in some systems.
- *Asks of the domain:* an account may exist with no record and read as zero; the kernel's neutrality about negative balances (Decided) is exactly right; the zero-sum property is a property of the set of accounts of one unit, so conservation is a graph-level fact worth stating (owner question 2).

**S4. A bakery's stock room (Istanbul, Tehran, Lyon).** Flour is held in kilograms; loaves in pieces. A baking run consumes 50 kg of flour and yields 70 loaves. At closing, a count finds 3 kg of flour fewer than the ledger says. Unsold bread is discarded or given away. A bag of flour is opened and 25 kg moved to the day bin.
- *Obvious model:* stock accounts and transfers between them.
- *What breaks:* a transformation has inputs in one unit and outputs in another, so it is not one same-unit transaction ([account.handoff.md](account.handoff.md#decisions)); a loss or a found surplus has no second account; the stocktake is an observation that disagrees with the ledger, and the ledger must not be silently overwritten ([bookkeeping.md](edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md#policies-rules)). Transaction invariant 3 forbids crediting before debiting, yet a harvest, a production output or a donation is a credit with no debit.
- *Asks of the domain:* an answer to "what is the other side?" for creation, destruction and correction (counterpart accounts, owner question 2), and for a transformation, two same-unit transactions linked by the producing capability as an exchange is.

**S5. Loyalty points with expiry (airline miles, a Japanese store point card, a shopping-mall club).** One point is earned per 100 units spent; points earned in March expire two years later; the issuer may devalue unilaterally; points cannot be sold.
- *Obvious model:* one balance.
- *What breaks:* the balance at a time is not the sum of the effects, because each earning has its own expiry; consumption order matters (earliest expiry first); the usable balance changes without any transaction by the holder.
- *Asks of the domain:* an account's scalar balance must stay true (a snapshot of recorded effects) while expiry is a rule that creates its own transaction at the expiry time, like a restriction creates blocking transactions ([restriction → Policies](../../../modules-plugable/economics/restriction/restriction.md#policies-rules)); or each earning is a lot of its own (owner question 10). [club-coin](../../../modules-plugable/economics/club-coin/club-coin.md) is a bare edge today.

**S6. Serial-identified units (carbon credits, a car dealer's vehicles, a cheque book, a gallery).** A registry holds credits each with a serial and a vintage; retiring a credit consumes a specific serial; a car is one VIN; a cheque is one numbered instrument (its number a `uri` reference, Decided in [account.handoff.md](account.handoff.md#from-organization-ideas-backlog)).
- *Obvious model:* count unit "tonne of CO2", quantity 10.
- *What breaks:* "one countable quantity" assumes interchangeable units. A transfer must name which units move, and the same serial must not stand in two accounts.
- *Asks of the domain:* whether an account may hold identified units at all. The product research already gives the museum piece an account of one piece ([Product Research 001, S7](../product/product.research.001.md#scenarios)); the same shape fits a car. The fungible account then counts how many, and the identity lives in the thing (owner question 9).

**S7. The holder dies or disappears (inheritance and probate; a dissolved company; a minor under guardianship; unclaimed funds handed to the state after years of silence).** The balance remains. Access is frozen pending the estate; the heirs receive shares of one balance by a court's order; what is never claimed goes to the state.
- *Obvious model:* the account belongs to a user and follows the user.
- *What breaks:* the account outlives its holder; changes of title are not account events at all (holding is not title, Decided); the right to instruct changes, the balance does not; splitting a balance among heirs is several movements with one cause.
- *Asks of the domain:* an account lifecycle independent of the holder's; closing is an act with a declarer; the freeze is `restriction`, the distribution is transactions with a court order as their reference ([transaction.handoff.md](../transaction/transaction.handoff.md#decisions), item 10), the heirs are an [ownership](../../rights/ownership/ownership.md) matter.

**S8. A company issues shares; a central bank settles with commercial banks.** A new company creates 1,000,000 shares and gives 600,000 to the founders. Commercial banks hold reserve accounts at a central bank and their customers' accounts are mirrored there ([settlement](../../../modules-plugable/economics/settlement/settlement.md)).
- *Obvious model:* deposit shares into the founders' account.
- *What breaks:* the deposit has no withdrawal behind it. Under invariant 3 the shares would be "unbacked quantity", forbidden by the kernel; yet the securities module says holding shares is plain bookkeeping ([securities](../../../modules-plugable/economics/securities/securities.md)).
- *Asks of the domain:* the same as S4: a counterpart (here the issuer's "authorized but unissued" account) whose balance is the negative of what is outstanding, so that the sum over a unit stays zero. This is what double-entry practice does with equity and issuer accounts; the question is whether Memar adopts it (owner question 2).

**S9. A court freezes 1,000 across a debtor's accounts, one of them joint with a spouse (US bank-levy practice, Iranian judicial block).** The freeze targets a person; later deposits are blocked until the amount is reached; the joint holder is not the debtor.
- *Obvious model:* a blocked amount on each account.
- *What breaks:* nothing in the kernel, which is the point: this case was already settled by [restriction](../../../modules-plugable/economics/restriction/restriction.md) (a constraint targets a person, not each account; copying 1,000 to 100 accounts restrains 100,000). What remains is joint holding: the rule must find the debtor's accounts and the debtor's share of a joint one. A hotel's pre-authorization hold is the same mechanism with a different cause.
- *Asks of the domain:* nothing new for the kernel, but the "debtor's accounts" must be a traversal over ownership with fractions ([ownership](../../rights/ownership/ownership.md) asks whether ownership has fractions), so joint holding stays ownership's and the account never carries a holder list.

**S10. A ministry's annual budget and a company's cost centres.** Parliament appropriates 10,000 to a ministry by line; the ministry allocates parts to departments; spending is booked against the allocation; unspent amounts carry over or lapse; some lines may be overspent with approval.
- *Obvious model:* each budget line is an account with a planned balance.
- *What breaks:* the quantity is a ceiling and a plan, not a realized holding; the [budget](../../../modules-plugable/economics/budget/budget.md) document itself says it "is not real money accounting and JUST a map for future planning". Yet an appropriation drawn down by spending behaves like bookkeeping, and the budget module hangs six different edges on `account`.
- *Asks of the domain:* whether a budget line is an account (an allocation unit drawn down by movements) or a constraint that observes accounts, like a restriction (owner question 17). Explored.

### 2. Minimal kernel and what must be pluggable

**Tests applied (source).** [Modules → Placement](../../modules.md#placement): kernel only if removal forces a foundational type to be fabricated or distorted; pluggable if an optional capability attaches through an explicit relationship and owns edge plus processing together; an edge becomes its own module only with independent identity, lifecycle, authorization, rules, or processing; no bare edges (who may create or remove it, and what it triggers); the kernel never references the rule domain directly; analytics is never kernel; behaviour binds to a module's UUID, never a name.

#### The removal test run on the account itself (agent, Tentative)

Remove the account domain. What is forced?
- A transaction's `bookkeeping` edge must end somewhere. It can end at a `thing`, and a thing already carries its count unit ([count_unit](../../identity/thing/edges/count_unit/count_unit.md)). No new foundational type is fabricated.
- But "this thing has a history of realized effects, ordered, each with its resulting total, and no effect is ever edited" is then a fact with no home. Either every thing carries it (a distortion of `thing`), or it is a marker on some things with rules of its own. That marker, with those rules, is the account.

The account therefore earns its place not by holding a number (the balance is derivable from the records) but by three rules no other domain owns: **the order is gapless and per account** (a write-serialization point, [bookkeeping.md → Drawbacks](edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md#drawbacks)); **the unit is one and does not drift under the history**; and **the history is append-only**. The independent-lifecycle test ([Concept Existence vs. Model Existence](../../../docs/modeling.md#concept-existence-vs-model-existence)) is then met only if opening and closing have rules of their own, which the cases support (S3, S7) and the documents do not yet say. That is owner question 1, and it decides between models B and C below.

### 3. Rival models

Six rival models of the account, with the status of each, then two smaller choices that the larger one depends on.

#### The models

- **A. Status quo: account = thing + unit + one `bookkeeping` edge, with no lifecycle, no holder, no counterpart (Explored; the current state).** Minimal, and right for the common case. Trade-offs: the invariants the domain depends on (gapless order, unit fixed under history, append-only) are enforced by nobody in particular; issuance, correction and transformation conflict with invariant 3 (S4, S8); an account has no stated beginning or end (S7); the balance of an account with no record is undefined.
- **B. Ledger kernel: A plus opened/closed status events, an event target, a unit that is fixed once a record exists, and counterpart accounts as the stated answer to creation, destruction, correction and transformation (Tentative; recommended).** Keeps every Decided statement true. It adds no account edge: status is shared, the event target is a capability, counterparts are ordinary accounts. **Migration cost is low by construction:** an existing account is simply opened at its first record; nothing visible changes until the first close, the first issuance or the first correction.
- **C. Account as a classification on thing, no domain (Explored; the strongest rival).** Following the [Premature Classification Check](../../../docs/modeling.practice.md#premature-classification-check): "is X an account?" is answered by whether bookkeeping edges end at it (the batch is called an account, a pot is one, a piece in a museum is one). The `bookkeeping` edge would stand as a module of its own. Fewest foundations ([Foundational Concepts Should Remain Few](../../knowledge/modularity/modularity.md#foundational-concepts-should-remain-few)). It becomes the better model if the independent-lifecycle test fails (owner question 1): if opening, closing and the ordering rule are not rules of their own, the account is a role.
- **D. Journal model: a transaction is a set of entries and the account is a chart-of-accounts item (Explored; tied to the contested [transaction open question 1](../transaction/transaction.handoff.md#open-questions)).** The classical double-entry shape. Rejected for the transaction by Decided item 2 ("A→B 500 + A→C 500 and A→B 700 + A→C 300 produce the same change set"); noted here because the account side is *indifferent*: whether a transaction has one pair or many legs, each affected account gets one bookkeeping record. The only account-side change would be a leg identity if a compensating record must point at one leg ([bookkeeping.md → Unresolved questions](edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md#unresolved-questions)).
- **E. Position model: an account is a derived pair (holder, unit) (Explored; the cases refuse it).** Simple for a wallet: one balance per person per currency, with no node. It fails where one holder has several accounts of one unit (S2's drawer and float, savings and escrow), where an account has no single holder (S1's pot, S9's joint account), and where holders change (S7). The `main_account` rule ("each user in a currency must have just one account with this label", [main_account-give](../../../modules-plugable/economics/account_relationship/edges/main_account-give/main_account-give.md)) is a label on a node, not the node's identity.
- **F. Lot-centred: an account is the set of its identified lots (Explored).** The UTXO and tranche view: every earning, serial or batch is an entry with its own attributes. Conceptually strongest for S5 and S6 and expensive for the plain case. Model B is compatible: a lot may be an account of its own, with the pool a grouping that [account.md → Future possibilities](account.md#future-possibilities) already anticipates.

**Not re-argued (Decided or confirmed, source):** balance stored on the node is rejected; `AvailableBalance` as account state is rejected; the unit is on the account; holding is not title; a non-UUID identifier is a `uri` edge; a transaction is a request and a booking is its realized effect; one source and one destination per transaction (under test, but Decided as the kernel's choice); no rollback; reference by UUID, not by embedded aggregate ([bookkeeping.md](edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md#reference-by-uuid-not-aggregates-cross-domain-modeling-rule)).

"Fits" means no distortion; "strained" means it works only with a workaround; "fails" means it forces a wrong statement.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 savings circle | fits | fits | fits | fits | strained (pot has no holder) | fits |
| S2 agents and brokers | strained (cross-account invariant homeless) | strained, fits with settlement | strained | fits | fails (two stocks collapse) | n/a |
| S3 mutual credit | fits (credit flag strained) | fits | fits | fits | fits | strained |
| S4 bakery | fails (no other side) | fits (counterparts) | fails | strained | fails | fits |

#### Smaller choice 1: what is the other side of creation, destruction and correction (Tentative: counterpart accounts)

- **(i) A transaction may have no source.** Matches the optional source at registration; breaks invariant 3 and makes every total unverifiable (money can be conjured by a request with one account).
- **(ii) Counterpart accounts per unit (recommended).** Issuance, destruction, stocktake adjustment, spoilage, expiry and the production leg are ordinary transactions to or from an account whose negative balance is the mirror of what exists. Conservation then holds for every transaction and for the unit as a whole. Cost: negative balances become normal for counterparts (the kernel is already neutral), and GUIs must hide them from holders.
- **(iii) Per-unit global conservation is not a kernel claim.** Real systems leak (a stocktake, a currency reform). Leaves S4, S8 to the capabilities with no common pattern; repeats the `pack`, `production` and `exchange` problems in three places.

#### Smaller choice 2: is the order of records the order of effects (Tentative: no)

The ordinal is the order of *recording* in one account (a write-serialization point). A bank's value date, a back-dated invoice and an offline agent that syncs a day late all produce effects whose real time precedes their recording. Keep two times: the index orders records, the transaction carries the effective time, and a statement "as of a date" is discovered data over both. The alternative, ordering by effective time, would force rewriting snapshots when a late record arrives, which the append-only rule forbids.

### 4. What the domain must own, as requirements questions

Written as questions favouring no answer where unsettled. A citation means the document already answers or constrains the question; "open" means nothing in the tree decides it.

#### Identity
- What makes two accounts the same: the `ThingUUID` alone? A number (IBAN, card, ledger number) is a `uri` reference (Decided), but may it also carry routing information, and is it then identity? Open.
- Does an account survive a change of holder, of organization (a merger), of the system that keeps it (a migration)? Open.
- Is the count unit fixed once the first record exists? A currency reform multiplies every quantity by a factor; a unit change by supersession would need a conversion ([count_unit](../../identity/thing/edges/count_unit/count_unit.md) says only "almost all things must have just one"). Open (owner question 5).
- May one holder have several accounts of one unit? The `main_account` rule presumes yes. Cited, not stated by the kernel.
- Is a part of an account (a pocket, a virtual number, a lot) an account of its own? Open (owner question 10).
- May an account have no holder at all (a cash drawer, an issuer, a sink)? Open.
- May an account hold identified units, or only fungible quantity? Open (owner question 9).

#### Lifecycle
- What states does an account have: opened, closed; dormant? Which are one-way? Open.
- Does an account exist before its first record, and what does its balance read? The bookkeeping rule is "the Balance of the highest-index record"; with no record it is undefined. Open (owner question 4).
- May an account be closed with a non-zero balance? If not, where does the remainder go? Open.
- What does closing do to what depends on the account: pending transactions naming it, restriction rules targeting its holder, settlement references, `main_account` preferences, budget lines? Blocked while dependents exist, or an event they answer? Open (the product research asks the same of withdrawal, [Product Research 001, question 9](../product/product.research.001.md#open-questions)).
- Is deletion ever allowed? No record is deleted (Decided); for the account node itself, open.
- How does quantity first arrive (opening balance, migration from another system)? Open (owner question 2).
- What happens at the holder's death or dissolution, and after long silence (S7)? A rule on events, not a state of the account: open who owns it.
- May history be archived or truncated by a retention policy? The bookkeeping document warns the ledger is not self-sufficient if the transaction is archived ([bookkeeping.md → Drawbacks](edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md#drawbacks)). Open.

#### Authorization
- Who may open an account: the future holder, an organization on its behalf, an authority? Who may open a counterpart account? Open.
- Who may instruct a debit from an account: the holder, a signatory, a delegate, an agent acting under a role (a billing agent, a standing order)? Is that account state or an authority relation? Open (owner question 3).
- Who may read the balance and the history: the holder, a co-holder, an auditor, a tax authority, a court, the counterparty? Open.
- Who may close an account, and who may reopen one? Open.
- Who may attach each pluggable relation to an account (the credit permission, a transfer allow-list, a main-account choice)? Today no pluggable states it (finding 2).
- Across organizations: may a transaction name an account of another organization, and under whose authorization? Open (owner question 13).

#### Rules
- Which rules does the kernel itself require, and which are organization policy through `condition_is`? Candidate kernel rules (own proposal): one count unit; unit fixed once a record exists; per-account gapless increasing index; `Balance(n) = Balance(n-1) ± quantity of the transaction`; both ends of a movement in the same unit; a closed account accepts no record. Open.
- Where does "both accounts of one transaction share one unit" live: the transaction states it ([transaction.md](../transaction/transaction.md#policies-rules)), the account owns the unit ([account.handoff.md](account.handoff.md#decisions)). Open (owner question 15).
- Is conservation across a unit a kernel claim (S3, S8) or a property of a convention (counterparts)? Open (owner question 2).
- What is the smallest indivisible amount of a unit, and who owns it? A decimal quantity has a scale per unit, now held in code inside [money](../../../modules-plugable/economics/money/money.md#model) ([money.handoff.md](../../../modules-plugable/economics/money/money.handoff.md)); a transaction below the scale is invalid or rounded. Open.
- May the balance read differently from pending state? E6 default: Balance excludes pending. Open (owner question 7).
- Which time orders the ledger (finding 3, smaller choice 2)? Open (owner question 6).

#### Events
- Which facts must the account domain emit: recorded (account, index, resulting total, transaction, request), opened, closed? The kernel exposes an event target, not a new event model ([Modularity → Event as a Module Capability](../../knowledge/modularity/modularity.md#event-as-a-module-capability)).
- Who listens: restriction (re-evaluate a satisfied target), credit (overdraft), settlement (represent on the upper tier), fraud, notification, analytics, followers. Is each reaction the listener's own rule, never a cascade the account performs? Open.
- Ordering and exactly-once: is the event emitted once per record in index order, and is a failed listener allowed to change the record? Open (the answer should be no).
- Are events delivered across organizations through [follow](../../identity/thing/edges/follow/follow.md), and is a balance followable? Open.
- Is write serialization (`Lock`/`Unlock` in `storage.kh`) a contract or an engine detail? Wallet-style processing locks per account ([transaction.handoff.md](../transaction/transaction.handoff.md#decisions)); a cached last-index pointer is open ([account.md](account.md#unresolved-questions)). Open (owner question 8).

### 6. Missing edges and modules

Each is a proposal (Tentative or Explored as marked); none is created here. Each names what, who creates and removes, and what it triggers, because an edge without those does not exist ([Modules → Placement](../../modules.md#placement)).

**6.1 Account status — kernel loop-edge on the shared status vocabulary, not a module (Tentative).** Opened and closed, each an event with a time and a declarer, taken by the account under the account's own rule for when it may take each: closed only at a zero balance (default), a closed account takes no new record, reopening is a new status event, never an edit. Created by the opener (the organization, the holder or an authority); removed by no one (history). Triggers an event to listeners; a closing request while pending transactions name the account is answered by the pending transaction's rules, not by a cascade. Sits on the shared statuses and so waits on survey question R4.

**6.2 `counterpart` — a pluggable beside `settlement`, under `modules-plugable/economics/counterpart` (Tentative).** Issuer, sink and source accounts per unit and the standing rule that creation, destruction, stocktake adjustment, spoilage, expiry and the production leg are ordinary transactions to or from them. Created by the organization that owns the unit's issuance (a company for its shares, a bakery for its loss), never by a holder acting alone; removal ends future use only. Triggers: the sum over the unit's accounts stays constant, so a monitor can detect a leak; a reporting view hides counterpart balances from holders. Check against [settlement](../../../modules-plugable/economics/settlement/settlement.md) (its tiered upper accounts are counterparts) and [securities](../../../modules-plugable/economics/securities/securities.md) before it is a new module: it may be a role label with a rule family inside `settlement`.

**6.3 `lot` and `expiry` — one pluggable under `modules-plugable/economics`, to be checked against `batch` and `club-coin` (Tentative).** A lot is an account of its own with an earned date, an expiry or a serial; the pool of an owner's lots is a grouping (analytics for the total, [account.md → Future possibilities](account.md#future-possibilities)). Expiry, earliest-first consumption and issuer devaluation are rules that create transactions to a counterpart at the right time. Created by the issuer of the points or stock; removal ends future expiry only. Triggers: an expiry transaction at the time; an event for notification. Serial-identified units (S6) take the same shape with a lot of one.

**6.4 Instruction authority — probably `rights/delegate` and `management/authorization`, not a new module (Explored).** Who may cause a debit from, or read, an account, with a scope (debit, credit, view), a limit and a validity: the signatory of a company account, a guardian, a standing order's agent, an auditor. Created by the owner (title) or an authority under a law; revocable. A transaction request from the account is accepted only for a requester who holds the authority. Open: whether delegate's lifecycle covers it ([task research, question 6](../../management/task/task.research.001.md#open-questions) asks the same of assignment) or an account-side check is needed.

**6.5 Account holder through ownership, with fractions — an extension in `rights/ownership` (Explored).** `owner.md` already states `(user)-[:owner_of]->(account)`; the ownership document asks whether ownership is an edge or a node with fraction, start, end and transferability. Joint accounts (S9), heirs (S7) and the pot (S1) need the node reading. Not an account module: the account's contribution is to say it needs the answer and carries no holder list.

**6.6 `reconciliation` — a capability in `settlement` (Explored).** The cross-account invariant of S2: the sum of many accounts equals one mirrored account. Created by the organization that runs the tier; the comparison is discovered data and a mismatch is a reported fact, never a silent correction; a correction is a compensating transaction. Triggers an event on drift. Waits on the settlement edge-shape decision.

**6.7 Account event target semantics — a capability of the kernel account (Tentative).** The kernel exposes an event target for recorded effects and for status; it does not introduce an event model. Without it, every listener (restriction, credit, fraud) rediscovers new records by polling the ledger.

**6.8 Account role vocabulary — one registry in `account-labels` (Explored).** The words that pos (`CashAccount`, `DepositAccount`, `RefundAccount`), vat (`TaxDepositAccount`), cash and credit use for "what this account is for" become labels in one registry, with a loop-edge kept only where processing needs it (the cash flow). The bookkeeping role set (Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue, Expense) is registered or refused there ([account-labels open question](../../../modules-plugable/economics/account-labels/account-labels.md#open-questions)).

**6.9 Interest, demurrage and other time-driven effects — rules, no module (Tentative).** An account's total changes only at records; interest on a balance, a monthly fee or a demurrage charge are rules that create transactions on a clock. The kernel needs a way to say "a rule triggered by time", not a field. Check against the rule domain's time conditions before sketching a module.

### 7. Test scenarios

Given/when/then in prose, so another session can write tests once the owner decides. Each is marked with what it depends on: **kept** if it follows from settled text, **B** if it assumes model B, **Q n** if it assumes the default of open question n.

1. **Unit is on the account (kept).** Given account X in kilograms and account Y in grams. When a transaction names X as source and Y as destination. Then it is refused as a movement; the cross-unit intent is two same-unit transactions linked by an exchange.
2. **Balance is the latest record's snapshot (kept).** Given an account with records 1 to 5. When the current balance is read. Then it is the `Balance` of record 5 and no value is read from the account node.
3. **Append-only, corrections are new (kept).** Given a booked record. When it is found wrong. Then a compensating transaction books the reverse effect and links back; record 1 is unchanged.
4. **Gapless order per account (Q 8).** Given two transactions that affect the same account at the same moment. When both are booked. Then their records have consecutive indexes and each `Balance` follows the previous one, with no lost update.
5. **One transaction, two records, withdrawal first (kept).** Given a transaction from A to B. When only the source effect has been realized. Then A has one record, B has none, and B's balance has not changed.
6. **Pending does not change the Balance (Q 7).** Given a transaction in draft naming account A. When A's balance is read. Then the draft is not in it; the availability shown to the holder is evaluated from rules.
7. **Availability from a restriction, once (kept).** Given a freeze of 1,000 on a debtor with three accounts. When one deposit arrives. Then restriction creates its own blocking transaction until 1,000 is satisfied; the total blocked is 1,000, not 3,000.
8. **Negative balance is the rule's, not the kernel's (kept).** Given an account with balance 10 and a debit of 30. When no rule is attached. Then the kernel neither allows nor refuses on its own; with a prohibition rule it is refused; with a credit permission it is booked and the balance reads -20.
9. **Mutual credit is zero-sum (Q 2, Q 11).** Given three members opened with no record. When A pays B 50. Then A reads -50, B reads +50, the sum is zero, and no issuer or deposit is required.
10. **Issuance through a counterpart (B, Q 2).** Given an issuer counterpart account for "shares of X". When 1,000,000 shares are issued to the founders. Then the issuer reads -1,000,000, the founders read +1,000,000, the sum is zero, and the issuer's negative balance is not a debt.
11. **Stocktake adjustment (B, Q 2).** Given a ledger total of 100 kg and a count of 97 kg. When the count is recorded. Then a transaction of 3 kg to the loss counterpart with the stocktake as its reference is booked; the original records stay.
12. **Transformation is two transactions (Q 2).** Given 50 kg of flour consumed and 70 loaves produced. When the run completes. Then one transaction in kilograms (to a production counterpart) and one in pieces (from it) are linked by the production event; no transaction mixes units.
13. **An empty account reads zero (Q 4).** Given an account opened and never written. When its balance is read. Then it reads zero in its unit, and the first record's `Balance` is the quantity of the first effect.
14. **Close at zero (B, Q 4).** Given an account with balance 5. When its closing is requested. Then it is refused until the 5 is moved out by a transaction; given balance 0, the close is recorded as a status event with declarer and time and the account accepts no further record.
15. **Unit fixed under history (Q 5).** Given an account in "old units" with one record. When its unit is changed to "new units". Then the change is refused; a redenomination is a new account and a conversion pair with the old one closed.
16. **The holder dies (Q 3, Q 4).** Given an account whose single owner is deceased. When the estate is opened. Then no bookkeeping record changes by the death; a restriction freezes instruction; the heirs' shares are transactions with the court order as reference; the title change is in ownership only.
17. **Several accounts, one holder, one unit (kept in practice).** Given a holder with a savings and an escrow account in one currency. When a main account is chosen for incoming transfers. Then exactly one account carries the main label for that holder, unit and purpose, and the other two are untouched.
18. **Lots expire (Q 10).** Given a lot of 100 points earned in March with a two-year expiry and 40 spent from it. When the expiry date passes. Then a transaction of the remaining 60 to the issuer counterpart is booked on that date, the pool's total drops by 60, and no record was edited.
19. **A serial moves once (Q 9).** Given a carbon credit with serial X in account A. When it is transferred to B and again to C. Then the second transfer is refused for A, because X is no longer there; B's transfer to C succeeds.
20. **A joint account under a levy (Q 3).** Given an account with two owners by fractions and a levy on one of them. When a deposit arrives. Then the restriction rule evaluates the levied owner's share through ownership; the co-owner's share is not blocked.
21. **Cross-organization payment (Q 13).** Given organization A pays organization B. When the payment is booked. Then A's ledger records the movement from A's account to a counterpart that represents B, B's ledger records it from a counterpart to B's account, and a settlement reference links the two transactions; neither ledger reads the other's accounts.
22. **A cross-account invariant drifts (Q 18).** Given customers' electronic value summing to 9,990 and the trust account at 10,000. When the reconciliation runs. Then the mismatch of 10 is reported as a discovered fact, nothing is silently corrected, and a compensating transaction is the only repair.
23. **Back-dated effect (Q 6).** Given a transaction booked today with an effective date last week. When a statement "as of last Friday" is requested. Then the effect appears in that statement, while its record keeps today's index and today's `Balance` snapshot.
24. **An event per record (B).** Given a booked record. When it is committed. Then the account's event target emits one event with the account, the index, the resulting total, the transaction and the request; a failing listener does not alter the record.
25. **Erasure detaches identity, not effect (Q 14).** Given a holder who obtains erasure of personal data. When it is executed by the authority. Then the ownership link and the uri identifiers are removed, and every bookkeeping record and its totals remain.
26. **A budget line is a ceiling (Q 17).** Given a department allocation of 1,000 and spending of 1,100. When the last spending is booked. Then the movement on the real accounts is booked, and the budget module's rule reports the excess; the real account is not blocked by the budget unless the budget's rule says so.

### Contradictions found

Reported, not resolved, as asked. Each cites both sides.

1. **The meaning of `Balance`.** The Go comment in `dt-balance.kh` says Balance includes "any pending transactions", while [bookkeeping.md §8](edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md#8-balance-snapshot-is-a-record-fact-availability-is-not-account-state) makes it an immutable snapshot of this record's realized effect ([account.handoff.md, open 5](account.handoff.md#open-questions)). Already recorded; confirmed here and tied to owner question 7.
2. **Conservation versus creation.** [transaction.handoff.md, item 3](../transaction/transaction.handoff.md#decisions) forbids crediting before debiting and requires what leaves the source to equal what enters the destination, while [transaction.md](../transaction/transaction.md#model) makes the source optional at registration, [settlement](../../../modules-plugable/economics/settlement/settlement.md#rationale-and-alternatives) rejects "money creation from nothing" without naming what funds the first leg, and [securities](../../../modules-plugable/economics/securities/securities.md) says shares are plain bookkeeping. No document says where the first unit of a thing comes from.
3. **How an account relates to its holder, four statements.** [account.md](account.md#policies-rules) says title may attach to the account through `rights/ownership`; [owner.md](../../rights/ownership/edges/owner/owner.md#model) lists `(user)-[:owner_of]->(account)` beside `(ownership)-[:owner_is]->(user)`, though [ownership.handoff.md](../../rights/ownership/ownership.handoff.md) treats ownership as a node; [payroll](../../../modules-plugable/economics/payroll/payroll.md) and its [salary shortcut](../../../modules-plugable/economics/payroll/edges-shortcut/salary/salary.md) walk `(account)-[:own_by]->(organization)`, an edge no document defines; and [account-labels Personal](../../../modules-plugable/economics/account-labels/personal/personal.md#definition) defines a word by whose quantity it is.
4. **Two vocabularies for an account's role.** The label registry holds Real and Personal ([account-labels](../../../modules-plugable/economics/account-labels/account-labels.md#registered-words)), while `cash_account`, `credit_account`, the POS roles and `TaxDepositAccount` are separate loop-edge or label vocabularies in their own modules, and the registry's own open question lists Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue and Expense unregistered.
5. **A unit-less quantity.** [account.handoff.md, item 4](account.handoff.md#decisions) says the transaction stores only a quantity, because the unit is on the account, while [transaction.md](../transaction/transaction.md#model) makes both accounts optional at registration and the [justice pilot note](../transaction/transaction.handoff.md#open-questions) (item 6) registers a draft with only the amount asked. ### Reconciliation with the four sibling researches

The same task was done for [product](../product/product.research.001.md), [task](../../management/task/task.research.001.md), [content](../../knowledge/content/content.research.001.md) and [text](../../knowledge/text/text.research.001.md). [concept-synthesis.md](../../../temp/sessions/20261014T071207Z-28148-0/concept-synthesis.md) names this domain among the "next domains worth the same treatment". Read as claims; the only passages relied on are those cited.

**Agreements.**
1. **The same kernel shape.** Identity, one or two structural relations, events, a `condition_is` attachment, and pluggables that own relation and processing together. Here: account identity, the ledger edge, status, event target. The synthesis found the same in all four.
2. **State change is an event and a correction is a new fact.** Bookkeeping's "never edited or deleted, reversal is a new record" is the oldest instance of what content (immutable revisions, delete as a tombstone status), text (immutable values, erasure as an authority-gated event that keeps the fact) and task (a reopen is a new task that supersedes) now say. Together they could be stated once as a cross-domain rule: the fact of a change is never erased by the change.
3. **Lifecycle status as events on the shared status.** Product (admission), task (outcome vocabulary), content (deleted, hidden) and the account (opened, closed) all place status in a shared vocabulary and wait on the same owner ruling, survey R4.
4. **Explicit act with a declarer.** Product's admission and the account's opening are acquired facts with a time and a declarer. Task differs by design: a team is acquired by first membership ([task, question 2](../../management/task/task.research.001.md#open-questions)).
5. **Reference, not embed.** The account's cross-domain rule (UUID only, [bookkeeping.md](edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md#reference-by-uuid-not-aggregates-cross-domain-modeling-rule)) is what content (body by reference to text), text (relation, not copy) and task (intent as content) reached independently.
6. **Sender is not requester.** Content's default (the kernel keeps only the request chain, [content, question 5](../../knowledge/content/content.research.001.md#open-questions)) is what bookkeeping already does with `Field_RequestID`; an employee instructing from the company's account is the same case.
7. **The stock of a piece is an account.** [Product, question 11](../product/product.research.001.md#open-questions) defaults to an account of the piece, `rights/ownership` for title and `batch` for place. S6 supports it and adds the condition that the account then counts one and the identity stays in the thing.
8. **One home for the budget relation.** [Task, question 12](../../management/task/task.research.001.md#open-questions) defaults to budget holding the one budget relation; this research agrees, and adds an Explored alternative (owner question 17).
9. **Declare units explicitly.** Text asks what the unit of a length is ([text, question 8](../../knowledge/text/text.research.001.md#8-open-questions)); the account asks the same of the smallest indivisible amount of a count unit (finding 4, Rules).

**Contradictions and tensions across the five.**
1. **Never erased versus erased.** Bookkeeping says no record is ever deleted. Text and content defaults say erasure is an authority-gated event that removes readability and keeps the fact. A ledger entry that names a person is such personal data in some jurisdictions. The account's answer must be that the *identity link* is erased and the *effect* stays (owner question 14); the three domains should say it in one sentence.
2. **One identity or one per organization.** Product defaults to one product node per organization with a mapping ([product, question 4](../product/product.research.001.md#open-questions)); task creation is always inside the creator's organization ([task, question 16](../../management/task/task.research.001.md#open-questions)); the invoice handoff leaves one-versus-two invoices open ([invoice open questions](../invoice/invoice.handoff.md#open-questions)). The account's default (each organization's ledger is authoritative for its accounts, a cross-organization payment is a transaction in each, linked by settlement) agrees with all three, but contradicts the simplicity of a `bookkeeping` edge between one transaction and accounts of two organizations (owner question 13).
3. **A transformation has no common home.** Product proposes `pack` (open a parent unit, credit the child), production consumes and yields, the bakery loses and the account asks for counterparts. Three capabilities will each reinvent "the other side"; the account research proposes one pattern, which product and batch have not seen (owner question 2).
4. **Batch is an account, or not.** The product research counts stock as "an account plus batch" and [batch/accounting](../../../modules-plugable/economics/batch/edges/accounting/accounting.md) calls a batch an account, while [batch.md](../../../modules-plugable/economics/batch/batch.md) leaves open whether it is the identity of a realization or a grouping. The proposal here (a lot is an account of its own) depends on that answer.
5. **Statuses are everyone's blocker.** Four researches and this one wait on where statuses live (R4). Not a contradiction among them; a single ruling that moves five.

**On transaction and invoice (read, not edited).** Transaction is the account's other half and every finding above has a transaction side: the optional source (Contradictions, items 2 and 5), the `account-by` overlap, the duplicate same-unit rule (item 9), and its own open question 1 (one pair or many legs), to which model D shows the account is indifferent. The invoice touches the account only through the transaction that settles it and the currency that must match the paying account's unit; the invoice's open question about exchange between organizations is the same shape as the account's owner question 13. A transaction research and an invoice research should start from these findings, and the pair is best read together with this one.

## Open Questions
This is the research's eighth section. Only the owner can decide these. Ordered by what they change: those that change what the domain is first, then those that change how a part is done ([Ask rather than assume](../../../docs/cognition.md#discourse-norms-derived-from-this-model)). Each carries a recommended default and what it blocks. They are also entered in the [handoff](account.handoff.md#open-questions).

1. **Is the account a domain whose responsibility is the ordered, append-only ledger of one quantity of one unit (opening and closing, ordering and the unit having rules of their own), or a classification on `thing` with the `bookkeeping` edge as its own module?** Default: a domain. Blocks: the rewrite of the opening paragraph of [account.md](account.md), model B versus C, the shape of `AccountModel` beyond `ThingModel` (Khayyam rule 6), whether the batch and the account of one piece need a domain.
2. **What is the other side of creation, destruction, correction and transformation: may a transaction have no source, or is there a counterpart account per unit so that every transaction and the unit as a whole conserve quantity?** Default: counterpart accounts; the other side is always an account. Blocks: transaction's optional source, invariant 3, issuance of shares and money, opening balances, stocktake, spoilage, `pack` and production legs, S3, S4, S8.
3. **How does an account relate to its holder: no holder edge in the account domain, title through `rights/ownership`, instruction through delegation, reading through authorization?** Default: yes, no holder edge in the account domain; title on `ownership` with fractions if it becomes a node; one edge name in use. Blocks: the `own_by` / `owner_of` contradiction, `salary`, joint accounts, heirs, the `Personal` label, S1, S7, S9.
4. **Is opening an account an acquired fact with a declarer and time (and closing likewise), may a closed account be written to, and may an account be closed with a non-zero balance?** Default: both are status events; a closed account takes no record; closing is refused until the balance is zero; an account with no record reads zero. Blocks: the lifecycle, the status vocabulary, dormant and unclaimed accounts, S7, test scenarios 13 and 14.
5. **Is the count unit fixed once the first record exists?** Default: fixed; a change is a new account and a conversion pair, the old one closed. Blocks: the lifecycle, currency redenomination, unit conversion design, the product research's question 8 (the same question for products).
6. **Which time orders the ledger: recording order only, or an effective time as well?** Default: the index orders recording; the effective time is a field of the transaction; "as of a date" is discovered data over both. Blocks: statements, interest and back-dated entries, the bookkeeping data model, S2.
7. **Does the Balance ever include pending transactions, and what is the pending effect on availability?** Default: Balance excludes pending (catalogue E6); a pending effect on availability is a reservation by `restriction`. Blocks: [handoff open 2 and 5](account.handoff.md#open-questions), the comment in `dt-balance.kh`, authorization holds.
8. **Is `TransactionIndex` a domain contract (gapless, per account, increasing), with the cached last-index pointer and `Lock`/`Unlock` as engine details?** Default: contract for the ordinal, engine detail for the rest. Blocks: [handoff open 3](account.handoff.md#open-questions), `storage.kh` keys, statement APIs, test 4.
9. **May an account hold identified units (serials, vehicles, credits), or only fungible quantity?** Default: fungible only; an identified unit is a thing, in an account of one piece or a registry. Blocks: S6, product question 11, any carbon or asset registry.
10. **Is a lot (tranche, serial, batch) an account of its own with the pool a grouping, or a part inside one account?** Default: an account of its own; the pool is analytics and a grouping capability; expiry is a rule creating transactions. Blocks: club-coin, loyalty, inventory valuation by lot, `batch/accounting`, S5.
11. **Is the credit module's per-account flag enough, or is credit a limit with a value that rules change?** Default: a limit; the kernel stays neutral about negative. Blocks: [credit](../../../modules-plugable/economics/credit/credit.md), mutual credit (S3), overdraft rules, the negative-balance prohibition rule in restriction.
12. **Do the words that say what an account is for (cash, credit, deposit, refund, tax deposit, Real, Personal, and Asset/Liability/Equity/Revenue/Expense) live in one registered label vocabulary, with a loop-edge kept only where processing needs one?** Default: yes. Blocks: the account-labels open question, the POS and VAT `financial-account` edges, `cash_account` and `credit_account` folders.
13. **Across organizations, is each organization's ledger authoritative for its own accounts, with a cross-organization payment being a transaction in each linked by settlement?** Default: yes. Blocks: invoice exchange, the settlement edge shape, product question 4, task question 16, S2 and test 21.
14. **May a holder's identity be detached from an account's history, leaving the effects?** Default: yes, by an authority-gated event on the ownership and `uri` links; the ledger is never erased. Blocks: retention policy, the convergence with content question 7 and text question 15, test 25.
15. **Who refuses a movement between accounts of different units: the account (owner of the unit) at booking, or the transaction at registration?** Default: the account at booking; the transaction states intent only. Blocks: the duplicate rule in [transaction.md](../transaction/transaction.md#policies-rules), where the same-unit rule is tested.
16. **Does the concept keep the name "account", with "bookkeeping" for its edge?** Default: keep both; "Accounting" stays avoided. Blocks: [handoff open 1](account.handoff.md#open-questions), the `Field_AccountID` naming sweep.
17. **Is a budget line an account (an allocation unit drawn down by movements) or a constraint that observes accounts?** Default: a constraint of the budget module; allocation accounts only when an appropriation is booked as quantity. Blocks: [budget](../../../modules-plugable/economics/budget/budget.md)'s six edges, the one copy of the budget relation with task, S10.
18. **Where does a cross-account invariant live (a trust account equals the sum of customers' electronic value)?** Default: a reconciliation capability in settlement; a mismatch is a reported fact, never silently corrected. Blocks: S2, settlement's internal structure, regulated e-money and custody products.
