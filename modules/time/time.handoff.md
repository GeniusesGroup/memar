## Decisions
Recorded 2026-10-15 by the module pilot (session 20261014T124124Z-3000-0, records branch `dev-knowledge`, `.agents/tasks/20261014T124124Z-3000-0/`), each from the owning material it cites and without a second opinion yet; each graduates into `time.md` or a protocol declaration on the owner's word, and the Open Questions entry it answers stays until then.

- **The Instant contract carries no representation term** — Tentative. Width, range and precision are the realization's: A concept is an abstraction; a concrete instance is a capsule (`docs/khayyam/abstraction.md#a-concept-is-an-abstraction-a-concrete-instance-is-a-capsule`) makes the unit or width "the implementation's to choose", and [A contract states a duration, not a unit](time.md#a-contract-states-a-duration-not-a-unit) says the same of this protocol; a monotonicity guarantee is not an Instant term by [the per-term check](time.md#the-definitional-question-kept-open). So nothing of [The Instant representation](#the-instant-representation) remains a contract question. Consequence: `SecondElapsed` and `NanoInSecondElapsed` in `modules/time/protocol/time.kh` fix one representation (seconds plus nanoseconds within the second) and leave the contract; their replacement is the owner's, because the declaration is a definition other modules consume. Rejected: a contract-level width or range, which would make every realization exactly that resolution.
- **`NanoInSecond` is an instance of `Duration`** — Tentative. What it names is the span from the start of the current whole second to an instant, which is a span by Duration's own definition (elapsed time between two instants); its bound below one second is the capsule's invariant. Once the contract stops naming it (above), it is one realization's capsule and no contract's term. Rejected: an instance of `Instant`, since it identifies no point without the whole second it is counted from.
- **The timer contract reports the instant it fires at** — Tentative. `modules/time/timer/protocol/timer.kh` declares `When mt (self Timer) () (t Time)` today, and `Timing` (`modules/time/timer/protocol/timing.kh`, `AddTimer`) orders timers on a heap or a wheel, which needs each timer's firing instant; so `When` stays. No module outside `modules/time/` names `Timer`.
- **Causality and cross-component ordering are not this module's question** — Tentative. The per-term check keeps them out of the Instant contract; what remains of [Distributed ordering versus the Instant contract](#distributed-ordering-versus-the-instant-contract) is GP's and sRPC's. Moving the entry to their handoffs removes it here, so it waits for the owner.
- **A fuller concept document** — Deferred: it waits on session 12672, which settles where documents live, and on the definitional discussion's own record (Anticipated Work).
- **`maxWhen`, `verifyTimers` and `heapAry` belong to the timing heap's realization** — Tentative. `Timing` names a heap and a wheel as interchangeable mechanisms, so an arity is a property of one of them; `maxWhen` is a width bound, which is the realization's by the first decision above; `verifyTimers` is a debug switch. None enters a contract; they are written with the timing heap's realization under `modules/time/timer/`.

### Does the time contract become ordering, comparison and a clock?
- State: `time.md` gives an instant's contract as ordering and comparison and a duration's as arithmetic, but the declarations carry neither: `Time` (`modules/time/protocol/time.kh`) has `SecondElapsed` and `NanoInSecondElapsed`, which fix Go's seconds-plus-nanoseconds representation, and `Duration` (`modules/time/duration/protocol/duration.kh`) has only `Stringer_To` and a comment fixing an int64 nanosecond count, against [A contract states a duration, not a unit](time.md#a-contract-states-a-duration-not-a-unit). Nothing outside Go residue names `SecondElapsed`, `NanoInSecondElapsed` or `Time_Stringer`. A draft that the Khayyam front end accepts together with every consumer of the two files is kept in session 20261014T124124Z-3000-0 (records branch `dev-knowledge`, `.agents/tasks/20261014T124124Z-3000-0/protocol/`): `Time { Observer_Epoch }` with `Elapsed`, `Before`, `Same`, `Since`, `Later`; `Duration { Stringer_To }` with `Shorter`, `Plus`; a new `Clock { Observer_Epoch }` with `Now`, the source of instants a timer and a deadline read. A comparison across epochs is refused with an error.
- Options: (1) adopt the draft and keep the name `Time` (recommended; costs one edit of each of the two files and a new `clock.kh`, and no consumer changes); (2) adopt it and rename `Time` to `Instant`, the word `time.md` uses (costs an edit of every file that binds `Time`); (3) keep the current declarations until the representation design starts.
- Anchor: `modules/time/protocol/time.kh`, `modules/time/duration/protocol/duration.kh`; [The four separable concerns](time.md#the-four-separable-concerns).
- Blocks: writing any realization under `modules/time/` (see [The unix realization declares nothing](#the-unix-realization-declares-nothing-so-this-modules-protocol-has-no-implementer)). Meanwhile the Tentative decision under Decisions stands: no representation term in the contract.
- Asked: 2026-10-15, module pilot session 20261014T124124Z-3000-0 (Claude Opus 5.5).

### Is there a common concept over the duration units?
- State: settled: no such concept is admitted. A concept is an abstraction; a concrete instance is a capsule (`docs/khayyam/abstraction.md#a-concept-is-an-abstraction-a-concrete-instance-is-a-capsule`) states that what a contract names is the concept and that "the unit or width it is measured in is the implementation's to choose, finer-grained, and never a floor the contract must meet", and [A contract states a duration, not a unit](time.md#a-contract-states-a-duration-not-a-unit) says the same of this protocol: the contract keeps the magnitude asked for and gives up only the measure it is expressed in. A resolution concept would be exactly the floor both passages rule out, so the one-directional conversion objection is answered by refusing the question rather than by a narrower abstraction. Residue: `NanoInSecond` is already held as an instance of `Duration` under `modules/time/duration/` although what it names is a component of a decomposed instant rather than a span on its own, which the ruling that made it an instance did not argue.
- Blocks continuation: nothing. No contract in the sources states a resolution today, and none is to.
- Next: owner ruling on `NanoInSecond` alone — whether it remains an instance of `Duration`, becomes an instance of `Instant`, or is something else. Every contract names `Duration`. Answered Tentatively under Decisions: it remains an instance of `Duration`.

### What protects a time-limited value from a clock that moves?
- State: this document keeps monotonicity out of the contract on purpose, listing it among the richer semantics that belong to [networking](../net/net.md) and [process](../process/process.md) rather than being smuggled into the Instant contract, and the representation itself is open above. The owner's position, recovered 2026-09-30 from an audit of Omid Hekayati's Go-community Telegram-group messages (Gommunity), is that this deferral is safe for instants that record *when* something happened and unsafe for a value whose validity is a window: a one-time code, a signed grant, a session bound to a deadline. Measured against an origin outside the system's control — a unix-epoch instant corrected by time synchronization or set by a user — such a window can be extended or ended without any elapsed real time passing, which turns a validity period into a request. The owner's stated instrument is a monotonic source, whose origin is the system's own start.
- Candidate source: the owner's own standing question, recovered 2026-10-04 from a retired session note and unexamined since - a library helper named `relativetime` in [go-zero](https://github.com/zeromicro/go-zero) (`core/timex/relativetime.go`) raises the question in its own shape: is a relative-time computation ever an acceptable source for a deadline, or is a monotonic source the only acceptable one? The file has not been read, so what is registered here is the question and where to examine it, not a finding.
- Question for the owner: is this a rule of this protocol — a contract that bounds validity states which source its deadline is measured against — or a rule each protocol repeats where it issues something expiring? And can the requirement be stated as a constraint on the contract while [The Instant representation](#the-instant-representation) stays open, or does it wait for that answer?
- Queued 2026-10-15: session 20261015-T080417Z-23696 makes this question smaller. A live consumer exists: `Deadline` in `modules/process/operation/protocol/deadline.kh` takes a `Time` of any clock, and the Go residue of `modules/time/timer/timer-async.kh` holds a timer's `when` as a monotonic instant.
- Next: state it before the first expiring credential is designed; [SDK → The agreement artifact for delegation](../process/service/rules/sdk/sdk.handoff.md#the-agreement-artifact-for-delegation) and the revocation question beside it are the first cases that would need it, and a contract written without the answer has no way to say which clock it trusts.
