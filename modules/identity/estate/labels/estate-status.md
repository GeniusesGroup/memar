# Estate states

## Model

- Candidate (unconfirmed): estate states — Reserved, Occupied, Vacant, Closed.

## Policies (rules)

- Names recorded: Reserved, Occupied, Vacant, Closed. No transition rule is recorded.

## Placement

- These names are filed on the status module.
- [Status](../../../../modules-plugable/knowledge/label/status/status.md) keeps what a status is (kind+rule+event); attachment is [Label](../../../knowledge/label/label.md). A domain declares its Status-kind labels in `labels/`. Reserved, Occupied, Vacant, and Closed name estate occupancy, not a vocabulary every record shares.
