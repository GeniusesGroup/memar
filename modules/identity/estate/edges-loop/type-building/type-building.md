# Building

## Dictionary

building  ['bɪldɪŋ]

n.    
1. a structure that has a roof and walls and stands more or less permanently in one place
2. the act of constructing something
3. the commercial activity involved in repairing old structures or constructing new ones
4. the occupants of a building

## Model

- `(estate)-[:type_building]->(---)`

## Note (from the Estate session, 2026-08-05..09 — confirmed)

A building is an estate **in its own right**, not an attribute or extension of the land it stands on — land and building are two different estates. The spatial relation between them (and quantities like occupancy derived from it) is modeled in [`estate`](../estate/README.md), not as a field of either side.
