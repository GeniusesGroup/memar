# Case

## rules
- An arbitrator is a judge. One adjudication model serves an organization that calls itself arbitration, a state court, and an ordinary company such as a social network that resolves disputes among members. Who may sit, which labels exist, and which services a claim may invoke are pluggable. There is no second adjudicator type. Whether any person may be a judge stays open. Civil versus criminal is a label, not a different adjudication model. Execution is a compelled service whose input is not only a number.

## Kinds of case
This domain is the dispute node. A kind of dispute — theft, a conflicting law, an update of a law — is not part of that node. Those edges are optional and live in [claims](../../../modules-plugable/rights/claims/claims.md). An organization includes the ones it hears.

## Domain status and naming
The Case / Dispute Resolution domain is only partially modeled. Candidate umbrella name: `Case`. It is explicitly NOT directly connected to `Organization` - it connects to transactional domains like `Invoice` instead.

Key principle already established: avoid words that imply certainty/finality (e.g., "final ruling", "settled") unless the state they describe is modeled with an explicit state machine. Real-world legal outcomes labeled "final" are frequently not actually final - e.g., a third party's claim can overturn a supposedly final ruling years later, even under legal doctrines (such as Iranian law) that formally use the word "final" (qat'i). Modeling must use precise, revisable states rather than terminology suggesting permanence.

Related but distinct: the enforcement/coercion capability question (who can enforce an outcome and to what extent - see [case.handoff.md](case.handoff.md)) is separate from the dispute-resolution *process* modeled here.

- How does a Case reference the Rule(s) allegedly violated, given the Rule temporal-validity question is unresolved (Memar `docs/modeling.md` -> Separating Structure (Code) from Policy (Rule))?

- **Settlement for, and [participant](edges/participant/participant.md).** Particular accusations now live in [claims](../../../modules-plugable/rights/claims/claims.md). [Settlement for](edges/settlement_for/settlement_for.md) still names what a dispute settles. It is not recorded as the path that carries out a ruling. When the margin stops on accept, the filed service executes. A stop on reject executes nothing. That execution is a service invocation the system compels. The defendant is not the voluntary caller. The input is not only a number. A public apology is a compelled write on that person's timeline, addressed to the claimant, and that write is another compelled service, not a second procedure. A later break requires a reversal of that execution. That reversal is a note for the transaction domain. The transaction rules are not invented here.

## Petition and complaint (phase 0 draft)

A complaint (shekvaieh) is a claim-type [label](../../../modules-plugable/rights/claim-labels/complaint/complaint.md). It groups claim types, plus the text explanation a type already carries. It is not this node. A petition (dadkhast) is the path of [petition-next](edges/petition-next/petition-next.md) edges, not a node above this one. A petition of one case has no such edge. The draft chain may change after a registry inquiry, or it may not exist yet. Links between claims are pluggable. There is no generic edge meaning only that two claims are related.

One case invokes one service ([claim service](edges/service/service.md)). Any user type may be [plaintiff](edges/plaintiff/plaintiff.md), the one [defendant](edges/defendant/defendant.md), or an expert. Organization and group are user types, so the plaintiff, or that one defendant, may be a group or an organization. Solidary or unequal shares are chained claims, one defendant each. [Judge](edges/judge/judge.md) points at the user. A role may exist together with that edge for assignment. This domain does not hide that person behind an alias, and a competence claim is not how the person is revealed. A person hiding their own identity is a choice in the user domain. That choice is not modeled here. [Participant](edges/participant/participant.md) holds parts that may later be pluggable edges. It is not a kernel edge to write. Expert is not its own edge. An expert who votes is seated on the judge edge. Votes are [vote-accept](edges/vote-accept/vote-accept.md) and [vote-reject](edges/vote-reject/vote-reject.md). Accept means the request as filed, with no attachment. A judge who wants a change rejects. That reject may point at one `computer/request`, born by editing the draft request. Editing produces a new request and leaves the filed service in place. The vote's text explains. The registered request wins. The pointer is a proposal: the edge is not drawn, and who creates it, what validates it, and what triggers it are not stated. A plain reject may point at no request. Each opinion-giver writes an independent opinion and an independent vote. A vote on one case is never a vote on another case. There is no abstention. The seated persons' consensus on this case is the shortcut [ruling](edges-shortcut/ruling/ruling.md), derived from accept and reject of the filed service. Continuation stops on the vote margin, not on wealth. When that margin stops on accept, the filed service executes. The system compels that invocation. The defendant is not the voluntary caller. When it stops on reject, nothing executes. Human-language explanations are timeline texts on the case, not a field of the vote, and they are not the request. Until every required judge has voted, no party sees any judge's or expert's vote or explanatory text. That visibility is a note for the content and timeline domains. Those domains are not modeled here.

The claimant may register a later case whose filed service is exactly one request a reject on this case points at. The same judges each cast a fresh accept or reject of that one service. That later case does not grow a new attached request. Conditional accept is not used on it. If the claimant never files, nothing executes. The case does not store a monetary quantity. A number is one possible input of the compelled service. A text written on the defendant's timeline is another. The case does not store several positive votes at several amounts or at several services. Opened sources that grant part of the same claim are a recorded departure, not this rule.

There is no cap on creating cases. Until the party accepts the full fee, only the fixed system fee is charged. How long a party may continue is the margin rule, not wealth. A registry inquiry is its own claim, like any other. The claimant must prove the need. It does not hold other cases in draft. See [registry inquiry](../../../modules-plugable/rights/claims/edges/registry-inquiry/registry-inquiry.md).

The case stays unconnected to Organization as a domain edge. Kinds stay in claims. Kidnap is an edge from this case to the kidnapped user. Stolen stays an edge of that kind of claim. A pluggable module may attach a process when a kidnap claim arrives. This section does not redraw them. Civil versus criminal does not split this model. Execution of either label is the same compelled service.

After a ruling exists, a third party's attack is a new case, and the claim type is annulment of that ruling. During the proceeding, a third-party objection is a linked claim heard together. Each of those reasons is its own edge. They are pluggable. They are not one generic related edge. A vote on one case is never a vote on the other.

He says this UUID is at once a group, so it can be a timeline, and a task, so a judge can assign an expert. That is a premise. It is not the type identity of this node. What would falsify it is [case.research.001.md](case.research.001.md).

The arbitrator line above is the evening decision: one judge edge, and who may sit is pluggable, for an organization that calls itself arbitration, a state court, or a social network. Whether any person may be a judge stays open. The falsifier is a query one of those three must answer that this graph cannot express.
- What would falsify the no-generic-related-edge rule: one edge whose meaning is only that two claims are related, used both for a third-party objection and for annulment. Model v4 point 9. Each of those reasons stays its own edge, as the paragraph above states.
- What would falsify the rule that links between claims stay pluggable: a kernel ban on a repeated claim; or a kernel edge whose meaning is only that two claims are related. Model v4 point 13. Whether a repeated claim is allowed at all is an organization choice, and the surface of an earlier claim is evidence.
- What would falsify the label decision, if that test is run and fails: a second adjudication model, with different vote edges or a different ruling shortcut, required because the label is civil or criminal. Model v4 point 8. The test is not settled. The civil-versus-criminal subtlety was reopened on 2026-10-07 and is open on the [claim labels handoff](../../../modules-plugable/rights/claim-labels/claim-labels.handoff.md). What the line above says today is the standing label decision; this falsifier is the standing test of it, not a record that the split is closed.
