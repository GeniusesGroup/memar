# Executive guarantee
How the law takes effect in the world: the operational path that makes the text actually happen.

## Model
- `(law)-[:executive_guarantee_is]->(text)`

## Policies (rules)
- Present when a binding signature is opened.
- Punishment for disobedience is one form of the path. Voiding an act, blocking a transaction, reversing a marker, creating a meeting, or honoring a power may be the path.
- A constitutive or power-conferring text has an operational path as well.
- This field is the observable commitment, including the path when the machine cannot run. [Computer code](../computer_code/computer_code.md) is the evaluable score.
- Stays as signed once a binding signature is open.
