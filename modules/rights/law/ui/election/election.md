# Election
The widget that records two separate acts on a proposition: public critique, and a binding signature. The acts can run in the same period. A reaction in the critique is a reading signal. A signature is the vote.

Placement of this widget relative to the law folder is open in [law.handoff.md](../../law.handoff.md).

## Critique
Critique is the public discussion of a proposition. A person publishes a reason. Others react to that reason (like, dislike, and the same family of reactions). Each reader sees those reactions through the people they already treat as worth reading. When a person values A, and A liked a reason that sits beside a visible signature, that reason ranks higher for that reader.

The reaction ranks reading. The signature is a different act.

## Signature
A signature is a binding vote on one proposition, over the digest of that proposition's payload.

Appointing someone to vote in your place is [Delegate](../../../delegate/delegate.md): a person gives a delegate and receives one, and [representative](../../../delegate/edges/representative/representative.md) is the edge between them. The delegate's signature on each proposition is visible to the person who appointed them. On one proposition that person may sign themselves. The appointment continues for every other proposition. Giving the whole appointment back is the delegate record whose rule is empty.

## Considerations
- A signature given because of a private benefit, rather than because the delegate is worth trusting on that subject, weakens the election.
- Schulze method, when a comparison among several propositions is required: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schulze_method
