# Contract

Contract creates an agreed relation between parties, from which rights and obligations arise.

## Principle (from the rights domain-boundaries proposal - awaiting review)

**Contract != Right.** A contract is one *origin* of rights; law, inheritance, court decision, and registration are others. The machine-checkable form of a contract is [computer code](edges/computer_code/computer_code.md). The human-language sentence is not that edge. The word code alone does not say which of the two a writer means. When a contract yields to a law is not a field of this type. An organization that wants that rule adopts [contract within law](../../../modules-plugable/rights/laws/contract-within-law/contract-within-law.md). Whether a law takes the place of the contract itself is a different statute, [law supersedes contract](../../../modules-plugable/rights/laws/law-supersedes-contract/law-supersedes-contract.md). Candidate edges from the proposal (all pending the two-ends/edge-kind review): `party_to`, `creates` (Right/Obligation), `concerns`, `terminates`. A contract that takes the place of an earlier contract uses [supersedes](../../identity/thing/edges/supersedes/supersedes.md), the thing edge, not a contract-local copy.

## Confirmed position (estate session, Omid)

- Marriage is a contract like any other - its terms (e.g. dowry) are ordinary contractual obligations; **no new legal-relation entity per relationship kind**. Industry-style per-relationship types are shortcut views at most.
- Contracts can also be the source of Authority (see [delegate](../delegate/README.md) open questions).
