# Rights
Rights covers the normative layer of organizations: law, ownership, contract, case, and delegation of authority.

## Boundary notes
- **Accounting is outside Rights.** Accounting records and measures rights; it does not create them. "Who owns X?" (here) and "how is that ownership entered in the books?" (there) are different problems — `Rights → Accounting` is a gives-rise-to relation, not containment.
- **Estates are subjects of rights, not members of this category.** An estate can simultaneously carry identity, rights, accounting, product, and location perspectives — several lenses over one reality, not several estates.
- **Rights come first; ownership/legal relations arise from rights** (Omid, confirmed in the estate chat) — ownership is not the primitive that grants rights.
