# Status

Status is a label other labels take when they name where a record stands in its life: [`Label_Status.py`](Label_Status.py).

General Status-kind words live under [`modules/identity/thing/labels/`](../../../identity/thing/labels/) as `Label_<Word>`. Domain-specific Status-kind words stay in that domain's `labels/`. Catalog helpers: [`../services/status_words.py`](../services/status_words.py).

A Status-kind label may attach to **content** as to any other record — attachment is the Label edge, not a second mechanism.
