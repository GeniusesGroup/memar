# Cognition Rule — Critique Scope
A rule held for [Cognition](../../cognition.md), applying when critique is the dominant activity.

## The rule
- **Run critique as part of producing material, not as a separate phase after a finished result.** Examine claims against the cognitive standards while drafting, including when modeling.
- **Treat critique as its own activity only when its end is a verdict on material presented for evaluation.** A proposal, model, design, or architecture can make critique dominant; the operations and standards it applies remain owed by every act of thinking.
- **Keep critique in proportion to generative and structural work.** An exchange that only criticizes stops producing the material critique needs.

## Why it exists
Critique is a composition of Cognition's operations and standards, not a kind of thinking that owns them. Its distinct end warrants a focused rule without separating it from the work that produces and improves the material.
