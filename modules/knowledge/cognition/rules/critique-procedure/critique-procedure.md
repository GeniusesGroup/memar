# Cognition Rule — Critique Procedure
A rule held for [Cognition](../../cognition.md), for evaluating a proposal, model, or design.

## The rule
1. **Understand before critiquing.** Restate the proposal, its problem, and its relied-on definitions; distinguish reported criticism, an inferred root, and a confirmed generalization.
2. **Identify explicit and implicit assumptions.** State their evidence and what follows if they fail.
3. **Check consistency.** Definitions keep their meanings, relations do not contradict, and conclusions follow from their support.
4. **Evaluate explanatory power.** Compare the material with the phenomenon, alternatives, and any model it would replace.
5. **Assess implications.** Examine evolvability, maintainability, composability, and communicability.

## Why it exists
A critique reaches beyond surface terminology only by following the conceptual stack from definitions and assumptions through consequences and alternatives.
