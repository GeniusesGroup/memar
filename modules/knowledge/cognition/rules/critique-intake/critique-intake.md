# Cognition Rule — Critique Intake
A rule held for [Cognition](../../cognition.md), applying its discourse norms before critique begins.

## The rule
- **At critique intake, ask rather than assume; verify every load-bearing definition; and report missing material rather than fabricate it.**
- **When an ambiguity must be deferred, record it as a question of the task it concerns and order deferred ambiguities by the cost of leaving them unresolved.**
- **Transform material into the thinker's strongest working representation when the medium differs; ask where that transformation is ambiguous; return material in a form the counterpart can use.**
- **Before a critique requires an owner decision, exhaust the owning material, bounded discovery, and another available cognitive system.** Four matters still reach the owner: a consumed definition, removal of stated meaning, a change beyond the instance, and a premise the model rests on.

## Why it exists
Critique inherits Cognition's intake obligations. Applying them before evaluation prevents a verdict built on an unverified scope, definition, or source.
