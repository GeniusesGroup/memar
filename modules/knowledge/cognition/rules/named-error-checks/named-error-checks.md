# Cognition Rule — Named-Error Checks
A rule held for [Cognition](../../cognition.md), for checking the named errors that can distort critique.

## The rule
- **Before issuing a claim, inspect it for sender-side bias.** Check especially for confirmation, anchoring, survivorship, and source-deference or agreement pressure.
- **On receiving a claim, inspect its inferential support rather than its source.** Check for ad hominem, appeal to authority, straw-man substitution, and the equivalent insufficient grounds of convention or taste.
- **Check both paths.** A sender-side check that passes does not show the material arrived without a fallacy, and clean incoming material can still distort the receiver's reasoning.
- **Keep the checklist tied to errors observed in the work.** Do not replace it with an imported generic catalog.

## Why it exists
Biases and fallacies are different failures of Cognition's processes and inferences, but both derail the material a critique must evaluate.
