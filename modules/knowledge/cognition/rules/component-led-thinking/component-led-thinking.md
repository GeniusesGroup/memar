# Cognition Rule — Component-Led Thinking
A rule held for [Cognition](../../cognition.md), for choosing the thinking work a material requires.

## The rule
- **Begin from what the material lacks, not from the name of a kind of thinking.** Translate a named request into its operations, standards, and awareness disciplines; the name never permits omitting a component.
- **Choose the next operation from the lack found:** decompose an unexamined whole or unstated premises; compare alternatives; infer from unsupported conclusions; instantiate an untested model; and commit when the field is sufficiently closed.
- **Hold the input, each step, and the output to Cognition's standards, with clarity first.** Ask about material that cannot be restated rather than guessing at it.
- **When a judgment's force matters, name the operation and standard that support it.** When work drifts, return to awareness of goal or assumptions.

## Why it exists
The components of Cognition are shared across named kinds of thinking. Working from the missing component avoids treating a label as a substitute for analysis.
