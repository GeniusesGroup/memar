# Cognition Rule — Critique Quality
A rule held for [Cognition](../../cognition.md), for judging whether critique is worth issuing.

## The rule
- **A critique must offer a stronger explanation, identify a meaningful contradiction, show an assumption false or unstable, or present a clearly superior alternative.**
- **Terminology preference, industry convention, authority, taste, and implementation difficulty without architectural reason are insufficient grounds by themselves.**
- **Match the required quality to the inspectable strength of the challenged model.** Proportionality raises quality; it never suppresses a critique that meets it.
- **Keep admission rules distinct from rankings.** A hard requirement is met or unmet; a dimension orders alternatives and never creates partial credit for a hard requirement.

## Why it exists
Cognition requires criticism on inspectable merits while preserving the ability to challenge mature-looking material with a genuinely strong critique.
