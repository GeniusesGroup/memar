# Modeling Rule — Concept Challenge
A rule held for [Modeling](../../modeling.md), for deciding whether a proposed concept earns an abstraction.

## The rule
- **For every candidate, ask whether an equivalent already exists; whether it is a contextual view; whether it introduces a responsibility or only a name; whether it has an independent lifecycle or rules; and whether clarity is lost if it disappears.**
- **A candidate that cannot justify its existence does not become, or remain, an independent abstraction.**

## Why it exists
Modeling discovers independent responsibilities rather than preserving every name encountered in a requirement.
