# Modeling Rule — Initial Discovery
A rule held for [Modeling](../../modeling.md), for beginning from an unstructured requirement.

## The rule
- **Before proposing a node, identify needed information, how it is acquired or discovered, implied responsibilities, independently evolving concerns, existing equivalents, derived views, and relationships.**
- **Treat the requirement's wording as evidence to investigate rather than an authoritative boundary.**
- **Keep a proposed concept provisional until its responsibility, relationships, and behavioral consequences have been examined.**

## Why it exists
Initial questions give a modeler a starting point without mistaking the requirement's vocabulary for the domain's structure.
