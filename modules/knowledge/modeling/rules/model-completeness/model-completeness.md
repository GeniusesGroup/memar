# Modeling Rule — Model Completeness
A rule held for [Modeling](../../modeling.md), for testing whether a graph covers its requirement.

## The rule
- **Ask what the model answers, what it should answer but cannot, whether it has orphaned concepts, and whether it omits relationships the requirement implies.**

## Why it exists
Completeness is a claim about the model's ability to answer the domain questions it was made to serve, not its amount of detail.
