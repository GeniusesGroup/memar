# Modeling Rule — Requirement Context
A rule held for [Modeling](../../modeling.md), for reopening a requirement before modeling its proposed mechanism.

## The rule
- **Before investing a session, reopen the requirement through at least one of three questions:** what becomes better and for whom; what larger question this answers; or whose problem it is.
- **Treat a proposed mechanism as a candidate answer, not a statement of the problem.**
- **When the requester cannot answer, return both the requested answer and the reading of the underlying problem that answer assumes.**

## Why it exists
The last link in a prior chain of reasoning may solve the wrong problem; exposing that chain makes the model's premise inspectable.
