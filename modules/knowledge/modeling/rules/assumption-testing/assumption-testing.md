# Modeling Rule — Assumption Testing
A rule held for [Modeling](../../modeling.md), applying to stakeholder assertions and existing-model claims.

## The rule
- **Treat a claimed need or already-present node as an assumption to test, not a fact to record.**
- **Ask why it differs from the closest existing concept, which responsibility it alone owns, and whether it is a domain concept or a presentation distinction.**

## Why it exists
Recording assumptions as requirements reproduces an untested model instead of discovering the domain.
