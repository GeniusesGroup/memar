# Modeling Rule — Modeling Convergence
A rule held for [Modeling](../../modeling.md), for observing progress before graph stability is established.

## The rule
- **Treat fewer newly introduced concepts, increasing agreement on meanings, reduced duplicate terminology, fewer exceptional behaviors, and the ability to explain the domain with fewer fundamentals as evidence of convergence, not proof of maturity.**
- **A successful session may produce questions, clarified terms, rejected assumptions, refined relationships, or improved boundaries; it need not produce implementation artifacts.**

## Why it exists
Early indicators direct further modeling without mistaking a short run of convergence for a settled model.
