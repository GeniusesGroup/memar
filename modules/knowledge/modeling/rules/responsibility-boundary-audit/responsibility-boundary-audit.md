# Modeling Rule — Responsibility Boundary Audit
A rule held for [Modeling](../../modeling.md), for detecting mixed responsibilities.

## The rule
- **Treat a name joined by “and” or “or”, unrelated discussion topics, unrelated relationships, or a definition requiring unrelated clauses as evidence of a mixed responsibility.**
- **Decompose the mixed responsibility into separate concepts and an explicit relationship.**

## Why it exists
A node represents one coherent idea. Combining unrelated responsibilities hides the boundary a model exists to discover.
