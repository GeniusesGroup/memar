# Modeling Rule — Model Reading
A rule held for [Modeling](../../modeling.md), applying before a model is critiqued or extended.

## The rule
- **Identify every existing node, edge, and loop-edge; when there is no model, identify the candidate concepts implied by the requirement.**
- **Trace the model's story:** state the reality it claims to describe.
- **Record initial questions or confusions without resolving them before the neutral restatement exists.**
- **Restate what the model appears to say before judging it.**

## Why it exists
An evaluation can test only the model actually presented, not a reconstruction that silently resolves its unclear parts.
