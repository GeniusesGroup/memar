## Policies (rules)

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsletter
- A newsletter is a printed or electronic report or news concerning the activities of a business or an organization,
- that is sent to its members, customers, employees or other subscribers, or a communication containing individuals' personal opinions or interests.
