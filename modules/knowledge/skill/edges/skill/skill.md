# Skill proficiency

## Model

- Candidate (unconfirmed): proficiency vocabulary — "beginner, intermediate, proficient, expert" and the Dreyfus-style ladder "novice, advanced beginner, competence, proficiency, expertise".
- Candidate (unconfirmed): skill classes — cognitive (mental and intellectual abilities), behavioral (attitudes and social interactions), techniques (practices and specific tools), sector (expertise specific to a sector).

## Policies (rules)

- The source offers two proficiency vocabularies and four skill classes. It does not state who sets them or what they trigger.

## Placement

- Stays in the skill module: both lists are vocabulary for a skill, not a separate capability.
- Whether proficiency is an ordered scale, and whether the classes are labels or a taxonomy, is open below.

## Open questions
