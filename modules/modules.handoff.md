## Ambiguities Resolved
- **Structural definition of `edges-loop/` and `edges-shortcut/`** — answered (2026-10-08): `modules.md` → Edge buckets now states all three buckets — `edges/` two distinct nodes (an acquired relation); `edges-loop/` a classification on one node, the node related to itself, spelled as a single `Type_*` or `Status_*` marker on that node; `edges-shortcut/` a derived cache of a walk already in the graph, never the source of truth, its document naming the source edges it recomputes from, with a user-set fact (mute, pin, invite) explicitly *not* a shortcut. `modules.md` → Edge folder fixes the folder shape itself: `<name>/<name>.md` plus a one-line `README.md`, sections Model, Policies, Placement, Open questions.
- **Analytics namespace: one per category, or one global analytics category?** — answered (2026-10-08): one per category. `modules.md` → Placement states analytics is never kernel — domain analytics sit inside the pluggable that produces them, and cross-domain analytics sit under *the category's* analytics namespace, named by the knowledge produced. The tree agrees: `modules-plugable/economics/analytics/` and `modules-plugable/management/analytics/` both exist as category namespaces. No global analytics category; a query crossing browsing categories is unaddressed because none exists.

## Re-reading this repository's categories
Proposal (Tentative), written 2026-10-17 after the owner's rulings in Memar's [Classification handoff](https://github.com/GeniusesGroup/memar/blob/main/modules/knowledge/classification/classification.handoff.md#decisions): Memar is the authority for placement; neither repository's category list is final; categories are one level ([Category Depth](https://github.com/GeniusesGroup/memar/blob/main/modules/knowledge/classification/rules/category-depth/category-depth.md)); a category names a subject, holds more than a handful of modules, and a concept with no independent identity belongs to the category it is part of ([Category](https://github.com/GeniusesGroup/memar/blob/main/modules/knowledge/classification/primitive/category.md)). Nothing below is moved; each item says what the tests suggest and what the owner must rule first.

### `storage` under `computer`
- State: `modules/storage/` holds `graph` (the vertex and edge structure; `graph.md` repeats what `modules.md` → Model says), `key_value` (a database paradigm, with a list of other projects) and an empty `analytic/` folder, under a `storage.md` that says Memar provides the local storage engine and this repository the distributed layer above it. Three modules do not make a category, and "storage" names a mechanism, not a subject. Memar's `computer` already holds `memory` and the pluggable `filesystem`; storage is the same family. The owner reads `storage` as having no identity of its own and belonging under `computer` (Classification handoff, Decisions).
- Proposal: dissolve `storage` as a category. Candidate shapes, owner to choose: (a) `modules/computer/storage/` as one module whose facets are `graph` and `key_value` — only if they are parts of one concept, since [Category Depth](https://github.com/GeniusesGroup/memar/blob/main/modules/knowledge/classification/rules/category-depth/category-depth.md) calls a module whose parts could stand alone a category by another name; (b) `computer/graph` and `computer/key_value` as sibling modules.
- Per-module placement still to test: `graph` is probably kernel (the repository's model is a graph of vertices and edges; removing it distorts every domain), while `key_value` and the empty `analytic/` read as pluggable (analytics is never kernel, [Pluggable](https://github.com/GeniusesGroup/memar/blob/main/modules/knowledge/classification/primitive/pluggable.md)). `storage.md`'s sentence on Memar's storage engine suggests part of this belongs to Memar, not here.
- Ask the owner: shape (a) or (b)? Is `graph` kernel? Does the `analytic/` folder go (it is empty) given `modules-plugable/computer/analytic` and `analytics` already exist? Moving also means rewriting every link to `modules/storage/` (for example from [organization](identity/organization/organization.handoff.md)); do it as one scripted pass after the ruling.

### `management/organization` versus `identity/organization`
- State: `identity/organization` is the concept home (document, edges, `protocol/dt-uuid.kh`). `management/organization` has no document: it holds only `_import/organization/` Go service leftovers (register, get, change-domain, block, storage), and `management/management.md` says the organization module has no `module.md` of its own and keeps its idea items at category level. A module has exactly one placement ([Placement](https://github.com/GeniusesGroup/memar/blob/main/modules/knowledge/classification/rules/placement/placement.md)); here one subject has two folders, one of them an unconverted leftover.
- Proposal: `identity/organization` is the module; the `management/organization` services are services of it (or, where they are management of an organization rather than its identity, the first candidates for a pluggable under `modules-plugable/management/`), and the lifecycle idea items in `management.md` (finish date, no delete, reopening restricted) move to the organization's own handoff. By the Kernel test an organization is a bearer of identity next to user and person, which is why it stays in `identity`.
- Ask the owner: do the register/block/change-domain services belong in `identity/organization` or in a pluggable `management` capability? This is the existing owner-gated item "`modules/management/organization` versus `modules/identity/organization`" in Open Questions above; do not move before the ruling.
