# Research Directory

## Purpose
This directory contains exploratory research produced by human contributors and AI systems (Claude, ChatGPT, z.ai, Gemini, etc.) during the discovery phase of Memar sub-projects.

## Status of Content
Nothing in this directory carries architectural authority. Research documents represent the researcher's synthesis at a point in time — including AI-generated research — and have not undergone the critical review applied to documents in `/docs`.

Findings, statistics, taxonomies, and technology assessments found here should be treated as hypotheses requiring independent verification, not as established facts. Numeric claims (percentages, coverage estimates) are frequently qualitative judgments presented in numeric form and should not be cited as measured data without re-derivation.

## Relationship to /docs
Only concepts that have survived critical review and are formally adopted move into `/docs`. Research documents may be superseded, contradicted, or abandoned without formal deprecation process — they are working material, not versioned specification.

## Attribution
Each research file should identify its origin (which AI system or person) in its filename or header, so provenance remains traceable.
