## Open Questions
1. **Is the crisis posture disconnection (island) or redundancy (ring)?** The off-grid record's zero-percent dependence and its "island society" tag read as an absent connection; the mesh record on the same page, under the same passive-defense heading, survives every single point precisely by being nothing but connections. [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s own wording supports both ("no dependent consumption connections at all" / "closed cycles within the blocks and the village"). One question, two answers: rings (dispersed dependence — many loops, redundant carriers, village-scale circuits, argued from [Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm)'s design and [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s own village scale) vs. islands (abolished dependence — the building as sealed cell, argued from both flaw texts' indictment of cuttable lines, since a ring is still lines someone can cut). *(Why: the two postures cast different foundations — ring loops carry flood, fire and shrapnel with them; islands need nothing and survive nothing outside themselves. Blocking: yes for any verdict on this page's absolutes, and for the energy ledger's carrier posture the off-grid record cites as synergy.)*
2. **For how long?** Neither record states a horizon. "100-percent stability through nationwide disconnection" is meaningless across a duration, and storage, biodigester and closed-treatment prerequisites are sized by the missing number. Does the settlement define autonomy in hours, days or a season, per domain (power, water, waste, communications, food, care)? *(Why: the one variable that converts both absolutes into auditable engineering is absent; the answer also picks which synergy records the page really depends on. Blocking: yes for adopting either record's metrics as written; no for the communication function in the meantime.)*
3. **Do the absolutes compose honestly — mesh speaks because off-grid keeps it powered, or off-grid is metered because the mesh carries its telemetry?** [Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm)'s prerequisite (per-block low-power routers with solar backup) is a load [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s storage premise must hold; [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s "100-percent stability" is an operational claim whose balancing and emergency calling run on [Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm)'s network. Each record's guarantee is the other's assumption, which neither states. Is mutual dependence between two autonomy records an acceptable composition, or does it violate the page's own zero-percent? *(Why: if island is the answer to Q1, the mesh must become per-island and [Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm)'s "network" reading collapses; if ring, [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s zero-percent is wrong as written. Blocking: yes for both verdicts jointly.)*
