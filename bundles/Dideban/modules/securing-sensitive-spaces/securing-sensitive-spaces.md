# Securing Sensitive Spaces

## Abstract
How the perimeter of a society's sensitive spaces is drawn, watched and crossed is answered here three times, by three different instruments pointed at three different moments of a threat: an automated cybernetic security system — AI and machine vision, patrol robots, fixed launchers that stop a threat 20 meters away with an electric shock, never a firearm, and, on its recovered aerial wing, shock-carrying, laser-signaling, face-recognizing drones — while cutting physical police presence by 95 percent ([Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots)); a custody protocol for the portal itself, in which no single hand can open a sensitive door, every access must arrive as a task confirmed by a large share of the block's residents, repairs are never "normal," and equipment failure never authorizes access ([Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access)); and an invisible boundary — bistatic microwave links and machine vision replacing walls and fences entirely, detecting intrusion in under a second while the wildlife walks through and the 100-hectare sightline stays whole ([Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#invisible-enclosure-by-bistatic-microwave-fence-fused-with-machine-vision-bistatic-microwave-invisible-boundary)). Between detection, authorization and response the three compose into one perimeter — and collide on a single principle the ledger has not stated: [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access)'s whole doctrine is that no machine may ever open a protected door on its own, even a broken one, while [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots) gives machines automated physical authority over bodies in the open; whether the anti-single-hand rule stops at the portal or must also reach the projector is the owner question these answers leave between them.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Security answers are the easiest for a ledger to scatter, because each one lives in a different medium — a wall line, a door logic, a camera mast — and a reader who adopts the invisible fence and never finds the portal protocol discovers, at the block's data room, that the fence's careful absence does nothing about the one technician with a key; conversely, a reader who adopts the protocol alone has sealed the most sensitive rooms in a settlement still ringed by "concrete walls and barbed wire" — the very condition [Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#invisible-enclosure-by-bistatic-microwave-fence-fused-with-machine-vision-bistatic-microwave-invisible-boundary)'s flaw text calls psychological imprisonment. And a reader who buys all three instruments inherits an unresolved premise none of them debates: [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots)'s robots and [Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#invisible-enclosure-by-bistatic-microwave-fence-fused-with-machine-vision-bistatic-microwave-invisible-boundary)'s classifiers both demand a legible landscape — open, monitored, with humans and animals separable — which the settlement's ground-plane records treat as nature to be preserved, not as security terrain to be designed; keeping the three answers on one page is what makes their shared design obligation visible instead of hidden in the gap between them.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How is the perimeter of sensitive spaces drawn and monitored, and how does entry become permissible? Every answer here must position two powers and choose where each one lives. First, the power to see: is surveillance concentrated in classifiers and biometrics that resolve threat from bystander ([Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots)'s behavior detection, [Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#invisible-enclosure-by-bistatic-microwave-fence-fused-with-machine-vision-bistatic-microwave-invisible-boundary)'s human/animal distinction, with the machine-vision burden of both) or refused at the portal entirely ([Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access) never needs to identify anyone — it makes identification irrelevant by giving no single hand, seen or unseen, the ability to open)? Second, the power to act: the shock projector acts on bodies before any consent, under 3 seconds; the protocol acts only after a majority has consented, on no body at all; the fence acts on nobody — it announces. The three flaw texts all indict the same conventional scene — ugly fences, tiring inspection gates, bodyguards, "arbitrary contacts" that make citizens feel choked — and the disagreement they cannot hide is about which non-arbitrary replacement the society trusts: faster machines than policemen, or doors no machine opens.

### Approaches

#### Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots
**Mechanism:** Provide the safety of life and property for citizens and cohabiting animals without the intrusive, friction-creating presence of police and physical human patrols. The settlement's security system rests on artificial intelligence and machine vision, and activates only when a concrete danger to a resident's life or property — human or animal — is recognized. Its defensive response arm comprises autonomous mobile robots, fixed standoff electric-shock projectors with a 20-meter range installed beside the surveillance cameras, and an aerial wing: drones «با ابزارهای غیر کشنده مانند شوکرهای الکتریکی» — carrying non-lethal tools such as electric shockers — that prevent any vandalous act in the complex environment; the drone's preventive instrument can be laser light used for semantic communication with the person concerned, and the drones must have face-recognition capability. The system stops a threat immediately, without lethal harm or firearms, and guarantees individual liberty for citizens at the highest level.
**Metrics:** Operational range of the shock projector: 20 meters in a straight line; response time to an imminent threat: under 3 seconds; reduction of physical law-enforcement presence in the living sphere: 95 percent; aerial arm: shock-equipped, laser-signaling, face-recognizing drones, non-lethal by construction.
**Prerequisites:** a network of smart cameras detecting risky behaviors; edge processing (Edge Computing) without violation of the privacy of in-home imagery.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Traditional security built on ugly perimeter fencing, exhausting inspection gates, physical bodyguards and arbitrary human contact, which induce a sense of suffocation and dispossession of freedom.
**Tags:** smart security, civil liberty, electric-shock projector, robotics, reduction of human intervention
**Scale:** Micro — building, and the settlement's open spaces
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Deploying reactive automatic weaponry in the chaotic traditional streets, with their blind traffic, creates catastrophic misfire errors against pedestrians and public panic; the system is usable only in the smart environments of the new settlement, free of unmonitored interference.
**Cognitive anchor:** The flawless sense of safety in "the diplomatic protected townships of Geneva," where children play in the gardens late into the night in complete security, with no need of fence or alarm.
**Narrative hook:** "The night shift has no flashlight and no badge — when your child falls in danger, what arrives in three seconds is a non-lethal jolt from a lamppost, and no one ever has to ask a stranger's permission to walk home."

#### Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access
**Mechanism:** Physical entry permission to sensitive spaces (such as the block's data-space in [Block datacenter: distributed compute and data storage on one floor of every block's ten basements, in opposition to the centralized megadatacenter](../information-systems/data-storage-and-processing.md#block-datacenter-distributed-compute-and-data-storage-on-one-floor-of-every-blocks-ten-basements-in-opposition-to-the-centralized-megadatacenter)) is in no single hand: no individual controls the opening of the door, and so the threat of one person never results in the door being opened. Every access request must be registered as a task and must bring the confirmation of a large share of the residents of the same building (or any other defined threshold). Two explicit conflicts of interest with the conventional model govern the protocol: repairs are never assumed to be "business as usual"; and the failure of a server or piece of equipment never creates an automatic license for physical access — the lawful path for a failure is a replacement part or remote decommissioning, never opening the door.
**Metrics:** Confirmation threshold: a large share of the block's residents (the quorum is fixed in the audit phase); individual authority to open the portal: zero.
**Prerequisites:** the voting and civic-identity infrastructure ([Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](../governance/decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) and [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](../governance/membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity)); a task-registration system and an immutable audit trail.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Relying on locks and human guards in sensitive spaces: a key or code in one person's hand means the space is broken by threatening that one person; and normalizing "maintenance access" means intrusion walks in through the permitted door.
**Tags:** access sovereignty, anti-duress, collective confirmation, security courtyard, critical infrastructure
**Scale:** Micro — the physical portal (generalizable to any sensitive space of the settlement)
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The protocol is a governance stack — task registration, quorum voting, an immutable trail — not concrete or hardware: its logic can be adopted over any existing facility's door, and the existing settlement already has the doors this mechanism was written for. The force-fit risk is not the building but the membership: "a large share of the residents of the same building" presupposes blocks of roughly the 500-person scale with a live civic-identity and voting infrastructure behind every request; in the conventional settlement the "building" is anonymous and the quorum unreachable, so retrofit practice devolves the vote to whoever holds the management contract — reinstating, in a committee, exactly the single hand holding the key that the flaw text condemns.
**Cognitive anchor:** The old shrine doors whose keys were kept in several hands — the custodianship of a holy threshold opened only with the trustees present, no one's ring alone enough: the Iranian memory that the most sensitive space is protected by the division of authority, not by one man and his key.
**Narrative hook:** "No one has the key — not because it was hidden, but because the door belongs to everyone whose lives run behind it, and no single one of them, even a guard held at knife-point, can be enough to open it."
**Syn (synergies):** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](../governance/decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — direct digital democracy (the collective-confirmation mechanism); [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](../governance/membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) — the sovereign civic identity
**Links:** [Block datacenter: distributed compute and data storage on one floor of every block's ten basements, in opposition to the centralized megadatacenter](../information-systems/data-storage-and-processing.md#block-datacenter-distributed-compute-and-data-storage-on-one-floor-of-every-blocks-ten-basements-in-opposition-to-the-centralized-megadatacenter) [complement] — the executive mechanism guarding that data-space; [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots) [complement] — kin in the physical-cyber protection of infrastructure

#### Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)
**Mechanism:** Clear away railings, precast concrete walls and metal fences that obstruct the ecosystem, and replace them with an invisible linkage of transmitter-receiver pairs of bistatic microwave beams, joined to smart cameras for face recognition and behavioral monitoring. Any unauthorized passage or intrusion is watched without any physical obstacle: the free movement of harmless wildlife is preserved, and the visual immensity of the 100 hectares is kept whole.
**Metrics:** Reduction of perimeter concrete walls: 100 percent; free visual range: 360 degrees along the plain; time to detect an intrusion and raise the security alert: under 1 second.
**Prerequisites:** radio antennas with standardized frequency coverage; machine-vision algorithms able to separate animal from human.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Confining citizens among solid concrete walls and barbed wire, which blind the landscape and generate a feeling of psychological insecurity and isolation.
**Tags:** environmental security, microwave fence, machine vision, removal of walling, continuity of the vista
**Scale:** Mid — neighborhood and the site's perimeter
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The instrument is equipment, not architecture — transceivers, cameras, algorithms — so its hardware can ring an existing facility long before the settlement's ground plane exists: retiring one concrete wall per retrofit site is the transitional dividend. But the record's premises are the greenfield's, and the force-fit critique lives in the gap: the bistatic geometry assumes open sightlines to and across the line (the metric is "360 degrees along the plain"), while the existing fabric is a forest of walls, parked cars and blind corners that clutter the beams and give the classifier faces with no clean human/animal question to answer; a microwave fence installed there is not an invisible boundary but a surveillance grid over ordinary streets — receiving every passage, physical or electronic, the mechanism was designed to let pass unobstructed.
**Cognitive anchor:** The Iranian garden itself — pairi-daēza, the walled world that became the word for paradise: the memory of the garden whose enclosure is water, shade and gate rather than prison, protected precisely in order to be walked through and looked across.
**Narrative hook:** "The settlement's wall is a line you cannot see: a footstep that does not belong is known in under a second, and the fox crosses the same line unalarmed — the fence became light, and the landscape stayed whole."

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access) forbids machines the last word at the door, and [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots) gives them a weapon in the street — and the ledger has not said which doctrine is the settlement's.** [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access) is built on a single intuition: authority over a protected space may never be exercised by one hand, and — its sharpest clause — never automatically: a failed server does not open a door; the machine may not even be trusted as an excuse. [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots), by contrast, hands machines a unilateral, automatic, under-3-seconds physical authority over bodies, with a human absent by design (the 95 percent reduction). Each record separately looks moderate — the door cannot open unless people say so; the street stops harm before a policeman arrives — but together they assert the opposite answer to the same question: what may a machine do to a citizen's body, without a person having chosen it? One question, two answers. The first answer: the doctrines occupy different domains of sovereignty and compose cleanly — the portal is governed by consent because it holds property and records (the block's data-space is [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access)'s own example), the open ground is governed by response because it holds bodies in imminent danger, and [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots)'s non-lethal, never-firearms constraint is precisely what makes delegated automatic force tolerable at 20 meters. The second answer: the anti-single-hand principle is one principle, and [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots) must carry it too — a projector beside a camera is a key in no hand and a hand under no vote, its duress case unnamed (who may not point it, and does a coercive threat revoke anything at all?); an owner adopting this document's security set without ruling on that extension has, in [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access)'s vocabulary, normalized automatic access — to bodies. The evidence is internal: [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access)'s flaw text is about the *architecture of trust*, not about servers, and [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots)'s Fit verdict concedes the architecture fails outside a designed landscape ("catastrophic misfire errors against pedestrians"), which is the same admission the protocol never had to make — doors, unlike faces, do not misread.

**The three answers agree the wall is the enemy and disagree about seeing.** Every conventional-model flaw on this page indicts concrete and wire — "ugly fencing," "inspection gates," "walls that blind the landscape." [Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#invisible-enclosure-by-bistatic-microwave-fence-fused-with-machine-vision-bistatic-microwave-invisible-boundary) realizes the agreement literally: 100 percent removal of perimeter walls. But its mechanism replaces the wall with transceivers plus face recognition, and [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots) layers on behavior-classifying cameras with an edge-processing privacy line drawn only around the home ("without violation of the privacy of in-home imagery"). So the settlement, having abolished the barrier, has not abolished the border — it has moved the border into the classifier: the fox passes ([Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#invisible-enclosure-by-bistatic-microwave-fence-fused-with-machine-vision-bistatic-microwave-invisible-boundary)'s prerequisite is literally to "separate animal from human"), and so, until a threat is recognized, does every citizen — but each unremarked passage is now recorded against a face. [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access) alone of the three never looks: its security is structural (no hand has the key) rather than observational. The owner-relevant collision is therefore a privacy-of-the-perimeter question the records raise jointly and answer separately: is the invisible fence, fused with biometrics, the liberation its flaw text promises, or the suffocation of [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots)'s own "arbitrary contacts" with the arbitrariness replaced by scale? No record here states how long boundary imagery is kept, who may query it, or whether the portal protocol's quorum culture is supposed to infect it — and a verdict adopting all three without that boundary rule installs the wall it claims to have removed, only transparent.

**As a system the set is complete in a way none of the records claims — detection ([Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#invisible-enclosure-by-bistatic-microwave-fence-fused-with-machine-vision-bistatic-microwave-invisible-boundary)), response ([Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots)), authorization ([Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access)), each covering the other's silence — and complete only on land that has been built to be readable.** The chain composes cleanly on the ground-plane record's terrain: open sightlines make the microwave beams sound and the classifiers confident; the wall-free pilotis ([Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots)'s synergy names them) give the robots their field; and everything the boundary protects sits behind a portal that no single hand can open, including the data rooms whose centralized form the record's source note says the owner opposes — [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access) exists to make the distributed block's data-space as sovereign as a shrine door. The residue is the same one both force-fit critiques expose from opposite sides: the instruments are one ecosystem, and the existing fabric can host any one of them only as an isolated device — a camera grid without doors-to-vote, a vote-protocol without a legible perimeter, a weapon system on chaotic streets. Sequenced, this page reads: [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access) today (its Fit is the only adaptable verdict on the page), [Invisible enclosure by bistatic microwave fence fused with machine vision (Bistatic Microwave Invisible Boundary)](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#invisible-enclosure-by-bistatic-microwave-fence-fused-with-machine-vision-bistatic-microwave-invisible-boundary)'s wall retirement site by site, [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots) last, on ground designed to be watched.
