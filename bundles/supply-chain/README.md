## Product supply chain
A [supply chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supply_chain) consists of the group of people, vendors, materials, and activities involved in the creation, production, and distribution of a product. Supply means the activity of supplying or providing something.

## Value chain vs Supply chain
To better understand this, let's start with a simple example. Let's consider the production chain of a simple car.

Supply Chain: This includes all the activities from extracting raw materials (like iron, copper, plastic) to delivering the car to the final customer. This includes processes like purchasing raw materials, producing parts, assembling the car, transportation, and warehousing.
Value Chain: This includes all the activities that contribute to creating value for the customer. For example, car design, research and development, marketing, sales, and after-sales service are part of the value chain.
Relationship between Supply Chain and Value Chain:

Overlap: As you can see, many of the activities in the supply chain, such as production and distribution, are also part of the value chain simultaneously. These activities directly contribute to creating value for the customer.
Supply Chain as a Subset of Value Chain: We can say that the supply chain is a subset of the value chain. All activities in the supply chain contribute in some way to creating value for the customer. However, the value chain also includes other activities that are not directly related to product production, such as design and marketing.
Answer to your question:

Based on the above explanations, we can say that if we want to represent these two concepts as circles, the supply chain circle would be completely inside the value chain circle. In other words, everything that happens in the supply chain is also part of the value chain.
