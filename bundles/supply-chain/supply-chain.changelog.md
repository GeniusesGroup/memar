### Supply chain and value chain recorded as distinct open systems
- Time: 2026-10-08T08:43:02Z
- Type: rewrote
- Contributors:
  - [Omid Hekayati](../../CONTRIBUTORS.md#omid-hekayati) — decided

#### What changed
- The paragraph stating that the supply chain is a subset of the value chain, and the nested-circles answer built on it, were removed from the body.
- The body now states the relation the owner decided: the supply chain and the value chain are two distinct open systems that influence each other, and neither is a subset of the other. Each carries activities the other does not — the supply chain the physical flow of goods, the value chain also design, research and development, marketing, and after-sales service.
- A sentence was added stating why: a term borrowed from mathematics is not used to describe a relation outside mathematics.
- The definition and Traceability sections were kept.

#### Considered and not done
- **Supply chain as a subset of value chain, drawn as one circle inside the other** — not taken: it states a set-inclusion relation the two chains do not have, since each carries activities the other does not.

#### Deliberation
- The owner holds that a system is studied, and understood, only once it is described in the language of mathematics; a qualitative reading is admissible only where the science is silent and there is no time to answer by its own methods, and it must not be abused. (Omid Hekayati)
- The borrowing of a mathematical term for a non-mathematical relation was named as the error that reproduces itself across a domain the way *energy* is borrowed outside physics. (Omid Hekayati)
- The word *value* also reaching content production and marketing was given as the reason the chosen name does not mark the boundary of the process. (Omid Hekayati)
