# Which Plane Carries Which Flow

## Abstract
Every pedestrian, pipe, service hall and entrance of a society has to be assigned to some elevation, and every meter of movement put on a plane is a meter of ground its author claims back for soil. This document holds one question — which elevation band carries which flow, and what is left free at the ground? — and five recorded answers that are not interchangeable: a wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis at least six meters clear, walking over continuous soil across a 100-hectare substrate ([Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate)); a downward settlement of ten basement levels to minus 35 meters that buries every loud and polluting function ([Vertical zoning of 10 basement levels (Deep Subsurface Clustering) and 35-meter penetration](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#vertical-zoning-of-10-basement-levels-deep-subsurface-clustering-and-35-meter-penetration)); a safe pedestrian deck riding the roofs of the PRT guideways at plus 6 meters ([Safe pedestrian deck on the roofs of the PRT guideways (Elevated Guideway Pedestrian Deck)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#safe-pedestrian-deck-on-the-roofs-of-the-prt-guideways-elevated-guideway-pedestrian-deck)); and two separate records of concrete skybridges between supertall blocks, written as example rule-shapes (one in a 500-person cohort, one in a cost fraction), not as rigid owner statutes — the plane law under them is only that soil is not destroyed and origin-destination transport is concentrated aloft ([Aerial network of concrete structural bridges (Skybridges) between supertall blocks for absolute protection of the soil bed](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#aerial-network-of-concrete-structural-bridges-skybridges-between-supertall-blocks-for-absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed), [Suspended network of concrete structural bridges and soil-tread-free connections between blocks](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#suspended-network-of-concrete-structural-bridges-and-soil-tread-free-connections-between-blocks)). Four of the five promise the same liberated ground; they disagree about the direction of flight — down into the earth, across it beneath a floating building base, or up above it. The 6-meter figure is a proposed minimum of clear height under the aerial band, not a statute of the 20%-cap kind: more height is attractive if the structure can carry it, and raising the whole building has other costs; developers balance those numbers. The 9-meter PRT figure and the 6-to-9 meter bridge range are proposals of the same kind. Origin-destination travel between buildings runs aloft. Ground at 0 m is recreational and wildlife habitat — dry-laid stone, no mortar, severely limited — not the path from one building to another. The deck's recorded composition (routine on the ground, deck in crisis) does not govern. A registered address is a protocol that may name the building or a room — not an elevation. Reaching recreational ground is by elevator, the same shaft that already joins basement to the upper floors, not by an origin-destination path on soil.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Each of these five records was drafted as one elevation of the same section: the transparent floor, the ten basements, the deck on the guideway roof, the two bridge networks. Separated, each reads like an architectural detail, and a reader who meets only one of them files an objection to half a contract — calling the deck an extravagance, or the pilotis a waste of enclosed floor, or the basements an unbuildable dig. Read together they turn out to be rivals and complements of one decision, because they share their load-bearing elements: the basements are the sink that lets the ground floor keep its "no machinery above soil" promise; the pilotis volume is the air the deck arrives through; the two bridge records are one answer carrying two different price tags (a 500-resident population threshold versus a five-percent-of-skeleton cost cap); and every one of them spends the same 6-meter figure on a different thing — clear height under the building, height of a deck over the ground, or soffit of a bridge over the forest. The question cannot be settled parcel by parcel; it is settled once, for the whole section, or not at all.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
Which elevation band carries which flow, and what is left free at the ground level? Every approach below assigns the settlement's movements — walking, cycling, waste, utilities, service functions, building entrances — to one of three bands: the subsurface (minus 35 to 0), the ground plane (0 to 6 meters), or the aerial band (6 to 9 meters); and each promises the same prize, a ground plane returned to living soil, wind corridors and unobstructed nature. The disagreement is not about the prize. It is about the direction the flows are sent — down, across, or up — and about whether the freed ground is meant to be walked on (the pilotis answer), kept off by everyone except in emergency (the deck answer), or never touched again at all (the bridge answers). An adjudicator who picks one band owes the other bands an account of the same 6-meter datum, the same footsteps and the same soil; a settlement that leaves the question open carries five partial section drawings, each silently assuming an elevation the others did not agree to.

### Approaches

#### Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)
**Mechanism:** Obligate every building of the society to raise its ground floor to a ceiling height of at least 6 meters and to leave it without any perimeter, retaining or rigid dividing wall. The space beneath each building is given the exact same use as the 80 percent of open land around it — pedestrian path, green space, planted forest and open ground. The base of every building thereby floats and stays open, and the residents' walk across the entire 100-hectare area of the village passes through nature without meeting a single physical obstacle, fence or dead end.
**Metrics:** Minimum clear ceiling height of the ground floor: 6 meters; enclosure by load-bearing or dividing walls: zero percent; area of the continuous obstacle-free walking bed: 100 hectares (one million square meters).
**Prerequisites:** Structural calculation of the slabs and load-bearing columns over large spans, transferring the full load of the upper floors to the central cores so that sightlines and free air flow are preserved.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The conventional design of ground floors as dark parking, storage rooms and rigid perimeter walls around the plots, which cuts the resident's connection to nature and blocks the natural wind corridors completely.
**Tags:** urban morphology, ground floor, wind corridors, ecological continuity, spatial transparency
**Scale:** Inter-building connection plane / micro — building
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Enforcing open 6-meter pilotis on the traditional plots of the existing fabric, enclosed by vehicle streets, produces a dark, soot-laden space prone to social insecurity — like the space under an elevated highway. The space demands the absolute removal of the car and continuity with the ground-plane forest; otherwise, in the existing fabric, it is quickly partitioned into warehousing or unsafe parking.
**Cognitive anchor:** The spatial grandeur of the open porches of "Chehel Sotoun Palace in Isfahan" or the glass halls of "Singapore's Changi Airport" — places where the boundary between inside and outside dissolves and the mountain breeze flows through freely.
**Narrative hook:** "Chehel Sotoun already put its floor in the garden and its ceiling on columns — we only ask every building to keep that porch and never wall it in."
**Syn (synergies):** [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) — the ground-plane artificial forest; [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — preservation of the soil; [Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture) — free distance between buildings; facilitation of the nocturnal wind-draught and the heat flush of the settlement

#### Vertical zoning of 10 basement levels (Deep Subsurface Clustering) and 35-meter penetration
**Mechanism:** Negative vertical development down to a depth of 35 meters in ten underground levels: levels B1 through B4 house the industrial kitchen, slow-baking, smart warehousing and the central laundry; levels B5 through B7 hold the industrial workshops that make and repair parts and the emergency hydroponic farms; levels B8 through B10 carry the septic tanks, the anaerobic biogas digesters, the geothermal heat pumps and the generators. Every loud and polluting function is thereby moved to depth, and the ground floor stays free.
**Metrics:** Excavation depth: 30 to 35 meters; ten basement levels on a 2,000-square-meter section — 20,000 square meters of service floor area in total, built into the depth.
**Prerequisites:** Structural retaining walls resistant to soil landslide and to the hydrostatic pressure of groundwater.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The dispersal of service functions, warehouses and boiler rooms across the settlement, which occupy prime land and block the development of green space and pedestrian paths.
**Tags:** subterranean architecture, vertical zoning, facilities concentration, ground-floor release, compact settlement
**Scale:** Micro — building and foundation
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | A 35-meter dig in the dense traditional fabric, with a metro and decayed pipe networks already in place, is equivalent to subsidence of the whole district and the destruction of its buildings.
**Cognitive anchor:** The hidden subterranean city of "Montreal's underground complexes (PATH)" — depths fully equipped with services and utilities, lived in without ever seeing the weather.
**Narrative hook:** "Montreal built a second city two floors under the snow and loves it — ours goes ten floors deep, and hands the sun and the trees back to the one above."
**Syn (synergies):** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — the transparent, freed ground floor; [Slow bakery and gentle baking in the depths (Deep Slow-Baking) with soil inertia and recovered heat](../food/meals-and-shared-cooking.md#slow-bakery-and-gentle-baking-in-the-depths-deep-slow-baking-with-soil-inertia-and-recovered-heat) — subterranean slow-baking; [Hermetic gravity fall of shredded food waste to the anaerobic digester (Gravity Waste Slurry)](../water-and-matter/fate-of-residues-and-nutrients.md#hermetic-gravity-fall-of-shredded-food-waste-to-the-anaerobic-digester-gravity-waste-slurry) — gravity flow of wet residues

#### Safe pedestrian deck on the roofs of the PRT guideways (Elevated Guideway Pedestrian Deck)
**Mechanism:** Equip the upper surface of the suspended PRT routes, at a height of 6 meters, with a continuous, light pedestrian and bicycle deck carrying a standard guardrail. This suspended deck makes it possible to walk between all 100 blocks in critical conditions — flooding of the waterways, the mud left on the soil bed after heavy rain, or the possible intrusion of vandals or animals onto the site floor — without ever setting foot on the ground.
**Metrics:** Height above the soil datum: 6 meters; effective deck width: 2.5 to 3 meters; structural loading sized for dense pedestrian traffic; direct access to the first-floor lobby of every building.
**Prerequisites:** The light and rigid load-bearing structure of the Skytran lines; stairways and ramps for emergency connection down to the ground datum.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Treating elevated rail infrastructure as single-purpose, and the resulting total paralysis of pedestrian movement at ground level during climate or security crises.
**Tags:** aerial transport, suspended walkway, passive defense, spatial security, two-layer settlement
**Scale:** Inter-building connection plane (+6 meters)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The deck has no substrate without the guideway it rides: in the existing fabric there is no PRT roof to walk on, and building the deck as an independent structure on existing blocks means anchoring new pylons through streets the record assumes have been emptied of cars. Bolted to ordinary buildings, its "first-floor lobby" lands at the second or third floor of low-rise fabric, and every access stair consumes the very ground the plan set out to free.
**Cognitive anchor:** The memory of walking the covered main lane of "Isfahan's grand bazaar" — a continuous roofed route in which neither weather nor the streets beneath ever interrupt the walk from one end of the city to the other.
**Narrative hook:** "When the floodwater covers the soil, you still walk to your neighbor's door — six meters up, above the water and the mud."
**Syn (synergies):** [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — the PRT aerial network; [The network of waterways and in-settlement boat-navigation channels with secondary phytoremediation](../water-and-matter/water-bodies-as-common-infrastructure.md#the-network-of-waterways-and-in-settlement-boat-navigation-channels-with-secondary-phytoremediation) — navigation corridors; [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — the station-level first floor
**Source:** Future village design conversation | Yalda spatial documents

#### Aerial network of concrete structural bridges (Skybridges) between supertall blocks for absolute protection of the soil bed
**Mechanism:** Connect the large-scale residential buildings (at least 500 residents each) to one another through aerial concrete structural bridges at their middle and service floors, in order to delete road-making, asphalt passages and ground-level travel outright. The settlement's soil bed remains 100 percent untouched — no compaction from fill, no contamination from materials — and all pedestrian movement and utility lines run at height.
**Metrics:** Height of the bridge lines: 6 to 9 meters above the ground; share of hard passages on soil: zero percent; permeability of the soil across the span between buildings: 100 percent; minimum population of every connected block: 500 permanent residents.
**Prerequisites:** Scaling the buildings up to blocks of at least 500 residents, so the structural spans are economically justified; flexible seismic joints and thermal expansion joints in the bridges.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The fine-grained subdivision of plots and the pulling of hundreds of kilometers of asphalt streets and alleys over virgin soil — severe soil compaction, lost water-absorption capacity and destruction of the subterranean ecosystem.
**Tags:** structural connectivity, aerial bridges, soil protection, 500-person supertowers, suspended walkway
**Scale:** Between-buildings plane / mid — neighborhood
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Bridges need masses to marry: the fine-grained plots of the existing fabric offer neither a 500-resident block nor a service floor to anchor to, and forcing links between existing low-rise houses crosses every courtyard, light well and window — rebuilding, in concrete, the sealed street corridor this record exists to abolish.
**Cognitive anchor:** The memory of "Si-o-se-pol and the Khaju Bridge in Isfahan" — bridges built to be walked upon, destinations in their own right, where crossing is the pleasure and not merely the means; here the promenade spans a forest instead of a river.
**Narrative hook:** "Isfahan taught us that a bridge can be the destination — here you visit your neighbor over the treetops and never touch a road."
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — the 80% open ground; [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — the suspended PRT transport; [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — protection of the soil bed; [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) — the grain of the complexes
**Source:** Meeting conversation — the idea of structural connecting bridges and soil preservation

#### Suspended network of concrete structural bridges and soil-tread-free connections between blocks
**Mechanism:** Physically connect the 500-resident blocks to one another through prestressed concrete structural bridges at height, in such a way that no connection is made on the soil surface at all, and the living ground under foot remains protected forever from compaction, pipe-laying and vehicle traffic.
**Metrics:** Elimination of 100 percent of movement conflicts at ground level; construction cost of the connecting bridges: less than 5 percent of the total structural frame.
**Prerequisites:** Structural design of the bridges able to carry vibration and to route light utility lines in parallel.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The perpetual excavation of streets and the pounding of the ground with asphalt and vehicles for every simple pipe or connection between blocks.
**Tags:** suspended concrete bridges, soil protection, car-free connectivity, walkway independence, linking structures
**Scale:** Micro and mid — of the complexes
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The five-percent cost promise holds only when the bridges are designed into the frame of blocks that are already 500-person masses; anchored onto existing plots, each span needs its own piers in public space and its own retrofit cores, and the dig that reappears to seat them is precisely the street excavation this record exists to end.
**Cognitive anchor:** The hush of a walk on a canopy bridge above a forest floor you never compress — an image that for Iranians carries the older memory of Isfahan's river bridges, where walking above water was itself the civic pleasure; here the deck carries you above a continuous tilled and breathing ground instead.
**Narrative hook:** "Why cross a highway and its smoke to reach your neighbor? On the suspended bridges the whole settlement is in your hands and the earth beneath you keeps breathing."
**Syn (synergies):** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — the 6-meter open pilotis; legacy-ref (unresolved) — the two-layer settlement; legacy-ref (unresolved) — PRT stations

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The ground plane: recreation and wildlife at 0 m, origin-destination aloft.** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) and the two bridge records contradict each other on the footstep itself. The pilotis answer makes walking on the soil the point: the space under every building is declared "exactly like the surrounding 80 percent" — path, green, planted forest — and the metric is a continuous 1,000,000-square-meter obstacle-free walk. The bridge answers make walking on the soil the flaw: [Aerial network of concrete structural bridges (Skybridges) between supertall blocks for absolute protection of the soil bed](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#aerial-network-of-concrete-structural-bridges-skybridges-between-supertall-blocks-for-absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed) commands "zero percent hard passages on soil" and moves *all* pedestrian traffic to height, and [Suspended network of concrete structural bridges and soil-tread-free connections between blocks](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#suspended-network-of-concrete-structural-bridges-and-soil-tread-free-connections-between-blocks) eliminates "100 percent of movement conflicts at ground level." Both camps justify themselves from the same premise — absolute protection of the soil bed — yet one protects the soil by making the building float above it while people keep walking, and the other protects the soil by declaring the foot itself the compacting agent. The owner's composition uses both camps: ground at 0 m is recreational and wildlife habitat — dry-laid stone, no mortar, severely limited — and origin-destination travel between buildings runs aloft (bridges, deck, PRT). Sharing the soil with animals is a reason to keep utilitarian walking off it, not a reason to pave it. [Safe pedestrian deck on the roofs of the PRT guideways (Elevated Guideway Pedestrian Deck)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#safe-pedestrian-deck-on-the-roofs-of-the-prt-guideways-elevated-guideway-pedestrian-deck)'s recorded composition — ground for the routine, deck for the crisis — does not govern. The stewardship forest record's courtyard hook keeps play and wildlife on the soil; origin-destination travel between buildings does not. A registered address is a protocol that may name the building or a room, not an elevation. Reaching recreational ground is by elevator (the same shaft that joins basement to the upper floors), not by walking origin-destination on soil.

**The aerial band is a recommended range, not a hard statute.** Six meters is the proposed *minimum* of clear height under the walking and guideway band — room for wildlife and recreation at 0 m, and a volume that does not force the first occupied floor into a dark soffit. Figures above that minimum (the movement record's ~9 m PRT, the bridge records' 6-to-9 m) remain available where structure and overall building height can carry them; the taller the aerial band, the harder the frame, and the taller the whole building, the more other costs appear. Those trades are left to the developers of each work, not locked the way the 20% coverage cap is locked. The deck still rides whatever guideway height a project chooses, and a lobby still meets that height — that is construction, not a second statute. The movement page's 9-meter line is the same class of proposal; it does not outrank this minimum.

**The plane law is soil and height, not a bridge-permit formula.** [Aerial network of concrete structural bridges (Skybridges) between supertall blocks for absolute protection of the soil bed](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#aerial-network-of-concrete-structural-bridges-skybridges-between-supertall-blocks-for-absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed) and [Suspended network of concrete structural bridges and soil-tread-free connections between blocks](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#suspended-network-of-concrete-structural-bridges-and-soil-tread-free-connections-between-blocks) show how a rule *might* be written — one in a 500-resident cohort, one in a cost fraction of the frame. They are illustrations, not official owner statutes, and not rigid. The owner rule they stand under is only this: do not destroy the soil, and concentrate origin-destination transport in the air. A crossing may be needed wherever that rule requires one; the example numbers do not forbid it. The form of the aloft path (bridge, deck, PRT) is a developer's work inside the recommended aerial range. The records stay visible as examples. Neither the 500-resident bridge-gate nor the five-percent cost ceiling is plane law.

**As a section, the five answers compose even where they collide in principle — the basements are the sink that makes every other promise payable.** [Vertical zoning of 10 basement levels (Deep Subsurface Clustering) and 35-meter penetration](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#vertical-zoning-of-10-basement-levels-deep-subsurface-clustering-and-35-meter-penetration) is the only record that sends flows *down* instead of across or up, and it is what lets the pilotis floor claim "no machinery above soil" honestly: the septic tanks, anaerobic digesters, heat pumps, generators, central laundry, workshops and emergency hydroponics it buries are the functions that otherwise occupy the ground floor the other four records free. Its Fit critique (a 35-meter dig in traditional fabric equals district-wide subsidence) is the same greenfield-only verdict the pilotis record reaches from the other side (open pilotis in existing fabric become unsafe storage), so the records agree on what they silently share: this section can be built in full only on land that is empty before it is built. The ordinary-Tuesday residue is now ruled for origin-destination versus recreation; address is a protocol (building or room), and recreational ground is reached by elevator.
