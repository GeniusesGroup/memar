# Settlement Grain and Scaling

## Abstract
A settlement's dwelling units aggregate into numbers — a 500-person module, a 2,000-person complex, a 50-thousand cell, a million-person fabric — and the four records disagree about how hard those numbers bind. [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) legislates a grain (no site below 1 hectare, 5 to be efficient, a 2,000-person Dunbar module, mega-complexes to 50 thousand) while [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning) legislates placement (100-hectare cells of 50 thousand, locked 30 to 60 kilometers apart behind a sealed metropolitan boundary); [Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation) and [Flexible modular fractal system (from the 50-thousand village to the megapolis of the Shiraz plain)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#flexible-modular-fractal-system-from-the-50-thousand-village-to-the-megapolis-of-the-shiraz-plain) repeal legislation in favor of a grammar — one 500-person module tiling self-similarly from a single block to a million, or five million, without re-engineering any on-site system. The decision is whether grain is one law or three stacked ones (module, aggregation, placement), and the losers if it stays undecided are named: the smallholder whose plots never merge under a module law nobody sized, the plain consumed at its edges while the cell caps and the elastic megapolis claim cancel each other, and the service network told that 50 thousand is both a ceiling and a default.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The records quote the same figures in different senses — 50 thousand is [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module)'s complex capacity, [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning)'s legal cap and [Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation)'s optional cluster; 500 is, within one paragraph set of pages, a tower floor's population, a cognitive module and an aggregation unit — and the quotations look consistent only when the reader can put all four sentences side by side. If the module law is filed in one paper and the placement law in another, nobody ever notices that the same document family both forbids and permits the million-person fabric in the Qasr al-Dasht gardens. This page is where the number stack gets checked.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How many people should the fundamental unit of dwelling be, and by what logic do the units nest upward to the macro scale?

### Approaches

#### Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)
**Mechanism:** Prohibition of subdividing and partitioning urban land into fine-grained single-plot parcels. The minimum domain required to develop any stable building complex must be above 1 hectare (10 thousand square meters), and in the optimal, efficient state above 5 hectares (50 thousand square meters). The population scale of each complex is designed on the basis of an average capacity of 2000 people (the optimal ceiling of interaction psychology and social cognition in human societies), and up to 50 thousand people in concentrated mega-complexes (in the form of high-rise towers with a 500-person module on each floor), so that wastewater treatment, heat recovery, waste automation and public services find economic justification of scale.
**Metrics:** Minimum complex domain area: 50,000 square meters (5 hectares) against the traditional 10 to 300 square meters; base-module population: 2000 people; megastructure capacity: 50,000 people in 100 residential floors (500 people per floor); 25 service floors spanning 1,000,000 square meters (20 square meters of service per person).
**Prerequisites:** Reform of registration and property law so that shared lands and surrounding fine-grained plots can be merged and the superfluous traditional lanes between them removed.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Subdividing land into 200- or 500-square-meter plots, which imposes billions of tomans in utility-connection costs and isolates residents in decaying 4-story apartments.
**Tags:** physical grain, biological scale, land aggregation, economies of scale in installations, Dunbar psychology
**Scale:** Neighborhood middle and urban macro
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | Enforcing the 5-hectare plot on the traditional fabric confronts the resistance of thousands of smallholders and a legal deadlock. The model is first implemented on new large lands; then, once demand drains away and worn-out land cheapens, 5-hectare consolidation becomes economic and possible inside the traditional fabric.
**Cognitive anchor:** The functional self-sufficiency and ordered scale of "Tehran's Ekbatan Town" — a place where every living need of the residents was foreseen inside one coherent, high-quality module.
**Narrative hook:** Ekbatan already proved a single block can carry a whole life — we legislate the grain it stumbled to reach.
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — 20% coverage; [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly) — internal service provision; MBR water treatment; share economy and centralized building management
**Source:** Complex-Building-Proposal.docx | Yalda-Life-Complex-Proposal.docx | Yalda Town.docx | 980203-farakhan-shora.docx

#### Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)
**Mechanism:** A rigid physical ceiling and a legal lock on the boundaries of the country's swollen metropolises (such as Tehran, Shiraz and Isfahan), with a 100 percent halt to granting surplus density or annexing land to their rims. The population overflow is redistributed not as satellite bedroom towns but as a network of "self-sufficient biological cells" (100-hectare villages of 50 thousand people) at 30-to-60-kilometer intervals across the plains. The plains, gardens and agricultural domains between the cells stay untouched, and the right of continuous physical expansion is revoked.
**Metrics:** Metropolitan boundary lock: 100%; standard area of each biological cell: 100 hectares; population ceiling of each cell: 50,000 people; natural buffer distance between cells: 30 to 60 kilometers; complete halt of horizontal fringe expansion (Urban Sprawl).
**Prerequisites:** Reform of the national territorial-planning document; cancellation of municipalities' revenue from selling density; definition of a hard ecological reserve at the Supreme Council for Urban Planning.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The uncontrolled, continuous growth of the metropolises through density sales, the belts of infrastructure-less marginal settlement that form around them, the destruction of the surrounding farmland plains, and unsolvable traffic crises.
**Tags:** territorial planning, biological archipelago, 50-thousand-person cell, urban-reserve lock, elimination of marginalization
**Scale:** National macro and territorial planning
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | Only one face of this idea is greenfield — the cells, drawn on empty plains. Its other face is a legal freeze, and a freeze retrofits nothing: enforced before the cells exist, it makes the existing fabric carry the entire cost — prices jump inside the sealed boundary and the priced-out settle informally beyond it, turning the "hard ecological reserve" into a wall around the very belts of marginalization the plan exists to abolish.
**Cognitive anchor:** "The old road between the garden villages" as earlier generations knew it — each village self-sufficient with its own qanat and orchards, separated by living open plain, joined by road and kin rather than by one continuous wall of houses.
**Narrative hook:** Iran's old roads already drew the plan: a necklace of garden villages, not one spreading blob.
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — 20% coverage ceiling; [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) — complex grain; [Ultra-fast tubed maglev corridors between cells (Inter-Cellular Vacuum Maglev Grid)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#ultra-fast-tubed-maglev-corridors-between-cells-inter-cellular-vacuum-maglev-grid) — inter-cell rail corridors; [Gravity-fed aquifer recharge and subsidence control by injection wells and piezometry (Telemetry-Monitored ASR)](../water-and-matter/water-supply-and-security.md#gravity-fed-aquifer-recharge-and-subsidence-control-by-injection-wells-and-piezometry-telemetry-monitored-asr) — regional aquifer stabilization
**Source:** supplementary idea of the systems architect — macro-territorial planning

#### Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)
**Mechanism:** Rejection of the rigid reading of "a fixed 50-thousand village". The Sabz.City platform is a self-similar (fractal) system whose fundamental building unit is the "500-person cognitive module". These modules can be deployed as an independent 50-thousand cluster (100 blocks in 100 hectares), or networked in the geographic setting of Shiraz's Qasr al-Dasht gardens as 1000 to 2000 biological towers (hosting up to 1 million people) within living gardens — with no change in the nature of the in-place installations required (MBR, heat pumps, gravity waste chutes).
**Metrics:** Base unit of the network: the 500-person module; scale range: from 500 people to 1,000,000; a constant 20% coverage at all scales; no garden trees felled, with the structures' footprints placed in the gardens' empty or low-efficiency spaces.
**Prerequisites:** Digital adaptability of the governance-platform protocols to exponential user growth; factory modularization of structural components.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Rigid planning models that see the settlement either as a fixed small village or as a lawless, swollen metropolis, with neither the installation infrastructure nor the physical geometry able to expand and contract mathematically.
**Tags:** fractal scalability, urban modularity, Qasr al-Dasht gardens, 1-million living settlement, elastic infrastructure
**Scale:** From building micro to a macro of 1 million people
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The elastic module lands cleanly on open plain, and its stated purpose in existing terrain is the opposite of demolition: threading its towers through the gaps of Qasr al-Dasht's living gardens without felling a tree. Force-fitted the other way — reading fractal geometry as license to drop a 500-person block into every open pocket of the existing fabric — it reproduces precisely what [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) forbids: parcel-wise micro-development without land consolidation, self-similar smallness replicated a thousand times over.
**Cognitive anchor:** "The girih tilework of a Shiraz shrine" — the same star returns from the portal to the smallest grille at a hundred different sizes, and the pattern stays legible at every one of them; the scale changes, the grammar never does.
**Narrative hook:** Look at a tile panel: one star from gate to grille — we plan the settlement the same way, block to million.
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — 20% coverage ceiling; [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) — optimal land grain; [Clearing the concrete slab of services: ceiling-void routing and lightweight pumice/foam-concrete floor fill for parallel phase execution](../buildings/placement-and-routing-of-service-flows.md#clearing-the-concrete-slab-of-services-ceiling-void-routing-and-lightweight-pumicefoam-concrete-floor-fill-for-parallel-phase-execution) — Dunbar tier; [Balancing the thermal capacity of the settlement's heat sinks (Thermal Sinks Capacity Matching); pairing condensers with a thermophilic digester](../energy/directed-energy-transfer.md#balancing-the-thermal-capacity-of-the-settlements-heat-sinks-thermal-sinks-capacity-matching-pairing-condensers-with-a-thermophilic-digester) — metropolitan heat wells
**Source:** session dialogue — formulation of fractal scalability and the rescue of Shiraz metropolis

#### Flexible modular fractal system (from the 50-thousand village to the megapolis of the Shiraz plain)
**Mechanism:** The platform's structure is not limited to one village; it is defined on the "500-person block", with elastic aggregability from a small neighborhood up to covering 500 thousand to several million citizens in domains such as Shiraz's Qasr al-Dasht gardens or marginal lands, without any loss of quality.
**Metrics:** Fractal scaling factor: from 500 people to 5,000,000 without disturbing the biological ratios and per-capita standards.
**Prerequisites:** Foundational modular design of the residential blocks and of continuous installation networks.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The assumption that sustainable settlements must necessarily be small, isolated towns in the desert, with no role in rescuing the metropolises.
**Tags:** fractal scalability, 500-person module, Qasr al-Dasht gardens, megapolis, physical flexibility
**Scale:** National and regional macro scale
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | Elastic aggregation is meant to serve the open plain and the surviving garden pockets of the fabric alike; its force-fit failure is institutional rather than physical — the 500-block presumes a single platform able to merge shares and utility nets across an ever-growing aggregate, and in the existing fabric, where every block would be owned and governed separately, that same elasticity shatters into a thousand micro-parcels: [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module)'s land puzzle reproduced at module scale.
**Cognitive anchor:** The pleasing order of the checkerboard and the organic grain.
**Narrative hook:** A sustainable settlement is not just a small town! It is a fractal pattern that can rescue a metropolis as large as Shiraz from crisis.
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — 20% coverage ceiling; [Block datacenter: distributed compute and data storage on one floor of every block's ten basements, in opposition to the centralized megadatacenter](../information-systems/data-storage-and-processing.md#block-datacenter-distributed-compute-and-data-storage-on-one-floor-of-every-blocks-ten-basements-in-opposition-to-the-centralized-megadatacenter) — 100 mid-rise blocks; [Aerial network of concrete structural bridges (Skybridges) between supertall blocks for absolute protection of the soil bed](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#aerial-network-of-concrete-structural-bridges-skybridges-between-supertall-blocks-for-absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed) — bubble-drain engine

### How the approaches collide and compose
Taken together, the records answer one question with three instruments, and the case that grain is three decisions — not one misfiled four times — is strong: each can be reversed without touching the others. [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) fixes the module and its site law (how large a parcel must be before anything is built, and whose headcount makes a socially coherent unit — 2,000 by the record's social-cognition claim, which borrows the name of Dunbar's number though the classic figure for stable relationships is near 150, not 2,000; the 2,000 belongs to the record's own ladder of layers, not to the cited psychology, and needs a warrant of its own), 50 thousand as an engineered tower aggregate). [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning) fixes national placement (where cells may stand at all, behind a legal lock and 30 to 60 km of intact plain). [Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation) and [Flexible modular fractal system (from the 50-thousand village to the megapolis of the Shiraz plain)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#flexible-modular-fractal-system-from-the-50-thousand-village-to-the-megapolis-of-the-shiraz-plain) fix the grammar of aggregation (how 500-person modules compose upward without re-engineering a single MBR). A society could keep the module, break the caps and rewrite placement; or keep placement and let modules merge freely — the instruments genuinely unbundle.

The system nonetheless has one open contradiction that the verdict must settle: is 50 thousand a ceiling or a default? [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning) writes it as law — a population cap per cell and a 100% boundary lock that revokes the right of continuous expansion. [Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation) begins by negating "the rigid view of the fixed 50-thousand village" and ends by seating up to 1 million people in one continuous garden fabric at Shiraz's edge; [Flexible modular fractal system (from the 50-thousand village to the megapolis of the Shiraz plain)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#flexible-modular-fractal-system-from-the-50-thousand-village-to-the-megapolis-of-the-shiraz-plain) lifts the range to 5,000,000. Both papers claim to rescue the same metropolis with opposite instruments — freeze and redistribute versus grow-in-place — and their arithmetic looks reconcilable only while "aggregation" stays undefined: [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module)'s 50 thousand is one megastructure (100 floors × 500), [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning)'s is a 100-hectare cell, [Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation)'s is an optional cluster of 100 blocks; the same headcount names three different objects, and the million-person garden network, however modular, is a continuous fabric that [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning)'s plain-between-cells rule forbids by construction. The composition these records point toward, without any of them stating it, is a stack with one unit of account: legislate the 500-person module ([Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module)'s social-cognition ladder, [Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation)'s infrastructure constant), legislate the spacing of cells and the metropolitan freeze ([Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning)), and declare aggregation itself elastic ([Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation)/[Flexible modular fractal system (from the 50-thousand village to the megapolis of the Shiraz plain)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#flexible-modular-fractal-system-from-the-50-thousand-village-to-the-megapolis-of-the-shiraz-plain)). The stack, however, does not dissolve the contradiction — it relocates it. All four quotations stay true at once only under one specific reading of the placement law: that it binds *where* cells stand and never *how many people* may stand within a fabric — and the cellular record writes the opposite reading into its own mechanism, a 100-percent halt on granting surplus density. Under the flatter readings, [Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation) overturns [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning) or [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning) voids [Fractal scalability from a single 500-person block to a 1-million megapolis (Fractal Scalability & Elastic Aggregation)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#fractal-scalability-from-a-single-500-person-block-to-a-1-million-megapolis-fractal-scalability--elastic-aggregation)'s rescue plan.

So the decision that closes the stack's open seam is one question with two answers: does the placement law cap population per site, or does it secure separation between sites? Cap per site — the 50-thousand ceiling is law — and the elastic grammar shrinks to a networked total: the million and the five million survive only as sums distributed across capped cells joined by the inter-cell corridors, and the in-place rescue of Shiraz is struck, while the freeze keeps its full meaning. Secure separation instead — cells must always stand behind 30-to-60 kilometers of intact plain, but no ceiling binds a fabric that never merges into one — and the million-person garden network is lawful the moment it keeps plain between its clusters, while the metropolitan boundary lock and the surplus-density halt lose their object, since an interior that can aggregate to a million re-grows around any rim the lock protects. The evidence that argues the cap side is the cellular record's own failure list: the infrastructure-less marginal belts and traffic crises it indicts are rim phenomena, and a freeze exists precisely to stop rim growth. The evidence that argues the separation side is the constant every record in this family treats as invariant: at 20 percent coverage a garden fabric holds the same soil per head whether it counts 50 thousand or a million, so nothing in the ecology chooses between the readings — what remains is where the legal line sits, which makes this a placement decision, not a density one, and no headcount figure in any of the four records can settle it.
