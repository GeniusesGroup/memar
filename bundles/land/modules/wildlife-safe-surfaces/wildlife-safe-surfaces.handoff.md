[Bird-Safe Glass & Bio-Sonar: ultraviolet-reflective glazing for the ground floor](wildlife-safe-surfaces.md#bird-safe-glass--bio-sonar-ultraviolet-reflective-glazing-for-the-ground-floor) is a material property of the glass; [Dark-Sky Compliant & Bioluminescent Nightscape of the ground cover](wildlife-safe-surfaces.md#dark-sky-compliant--bioluminescent-nightscape-of-the-ground-cover) is an operational schedule of the lighting. A material spec and a behavior schedule do not adjudicate the same way (one is tested once at manufacture, the other continuously in use), yet the page treats them as one code. That framing may be doing rhetorical work the evidence does not support, and the reviewer flags it rather than resting on it. Second pass keeps the one-code frame but records its cost: the frame now carries two open seams (the strobe timing and the unregulated interior emission below), so a single spec sheet that hides either seam is one code in name only.

Evidence for one: the mechanism's own ambition is whole-facade mortality, not glazing cosmetics; evidence for two: no source record names interior light, and inventing the clause exceeds the answers as filed — the same restraint the stewardship page applied to its boundary. *(Why: it decides whether "one code" is a real code or two reviews wearing one header, and it is the only place the page's headline mortality number can be honestly audited.
