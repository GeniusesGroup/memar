# Ground Surface Treatment

## Abstract
What stands under foot across the society's open 80 percent is one allocation problem with four recorded answers: aerial concrete decks riding over untouched soil ([Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving)), grass-paver lattices that let a fire engine cross anyway ([Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)](ground-surface-treatment.md#green-permeable-paths-compatible-with-the-passage-of-heavy-civil-defense-vehicles-grass-pavers)), a bacterial crust that turns walking soil into breathable sandstone ([Biomineral stabilization of the soil bed with bacterial calcite against dust (Soil Bio-Consolidation via MICP)](ground-surface-treatment.md#biomineral-stabilization-of-the-soil-bed-with-bacterial-calcite-against-dust-soil-bio-consolidation-via-micp)), and an electrostatic mist at the piloti openings that cleans dust already in the air ([Electrostatic precipitation of dust and an invisible mist curtain at the 6-meter openings (Electrostatic Dust Scavenging)](ground-surface-treatment.md#electrostatic-precipitation-of-dust-and-an-invisible-mist-curtain-at-the-6-meter-openings-electrostatic-dust-scavenging)). All four obey one law — minimize sealed paving, never kill the ground — yet each answers a different sub-question: where loads and feet go, what happens when a heavy vehicle must cross, what the walking surface is made of, and what the walker breathes. If the allocation fails, someone pays by name: the aquifers and the roots if the surface seals, the lungs of 50 thousand people in the dry season if the dust stays loose, the ambulance if the soil cannot bear a 10-ton axle, the pedestrian if dry-stone paving is laid where tires will run.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Four mechanisms competing for the same square meters cannot be judged from four places. A surface allocated as a grass-paver wheel path is by that same act denied to the biomineralized dust crust, and neither is available to the untouched soil the aerial deck exists to protect; the permeability percentages the four records quote are claims on one physical total. This page holds them together so the ground's allocation can be checked once, against all four, instead of four times in isolation.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How does the surface under foot remain load-bearing, dust-repelling, and permeable/alive at the same time?

### Approaches

#### Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving
**Mechanism:** Preservation of 100 percent of the biological integrity and permeability of the plain's soil. No mortar roadworks, monolithic concrete pouring, asphalt paving, or hydraulic waterproofing is executed at ground level at all, and the soil remains untouched. The main pedestrian route runs at the upper level, on the very concrete lines built for the PRT aerial transport system. Where human passages at ground level are genuinely necessary, only non-mortar paving is used (dry-laid stone with open sandy joints), so that full rain infiltration and the soil's gas exchange are not disturbed.
**Metrics:** Permeable share of the open domain: 100%; area of rigid impermeable flooring over soil: zero square meters; complete preservation of the humus topsoil cover.
**Prerequisites:** Structural calculation of the PRT lines' prestressed beams such that the underside of the guideway functions as a wide, safe, slip-resistant pedestrian domain.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The destruction, compaction, and suffocation of the settlements' virgin soil with millions of tons of lean concrete, bituminous petroleum asphalt, and compacted mortar flooring that blocks aquifer recharge and causes urban floods to rage.
**Tags:** soil permeability, natural bed, concrete pedestrian bridges, dry-laid stone paving, natural drainage
**Scale:** Inter-building connective level / ground bed
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Mortar-free dry-laid stone paving on the streets of the existing fabric loosens, turns to loose cobble, and injures pedestrians within less than a month under car and truck passage. This floor is settleable and stable only in the 100 percent absence of the car tire on living soil.
**Cognitive anchor:** Walking "the wooden and stone boardwalks of the northern forest parks", or the courtyard of the historic caravanserais — soil that breathes under foot, swallows the rain, and shows no trace of hot asphalt tar.
**Narrative hook:** Walk a north-forest boardwalk in the rain: the ground drinks the water under your feet instead of sending it off your shoes.
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — preserving the 80% domain; [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — 6-meter open ground floor; [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — the PRT system; recharging the underground aquifers and uprooting land subsidence
**Source:** Yalda Town.docx

#### Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)
**Mechanism:** Prohibition of asphalt or monolithic concrete pouring on ground-floor surfaces. Pedestrian and bicycle paths are built with perforated grass pavers or geosynthetic networks that arm the soil bed. While preserving 100 percent vegetative growth and rainwater permeability, the structure provides enough mechanical resistance for heavy rescue, fire-fighting, and civil-defense vehicles to pass in unforeseen conditions.
**Metrics:** Load resilience up to the level of heavy rescue vehicles (axle above 10 tons); maintaining soil permeability above 80% of the paths' area.
**Prerequisites:** Layered subbase of crushed granular grading without cement mortar; geotextile reinforcement.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Impervious asphalt surfacing that, in the name of vehicle passage, suffocates the soil and manufactures urban floods whenever it rains.
**Tags:** permeable paving, perforated washed concrete, passive defense, soil protection, crisis management
**Scale:** Ground bed and inter-building level
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Conformable within the alleys of the existing fabric; its risk is the passage of unauthorized heavy vehicles over an unprincipled subbase, which wrecks the bed.
**Cognitive anchor:** The greenery of lanes in "modern European university campuses"; grass wheel-stopper cover with no black asphalt.
**Narrative hook:** A lawn that carries a fire engine — the campus-path trick, scaled to every route of the settlement.
**Syn (synergies):** [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — protection of the soil bed; [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — open and continuous ground floor; restraining surface runoff
**Source:** Yalda Town.docx | مستندات کالبدی یلدا (Yalda physical documentation)

#### Biomineral stabilization of the soil bed with bacterial calcite against dust (Soil Bio-Consolidation via MICP)
**Mechanism:** Preserving 100% natural soil without asphalt and concrete ([Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving)) in Shiraz's dry seasons carries the risk of dust rising under the footsteps of 50 thousand people. By spraying natural soil-dwelling bacteria (Sporosarcina pasteurii) together with a calcium-and-urea solution, calcite nanocrystals form between the soil grains and create a sandstone crust that is extremely hard yet keeps open micronic pores — high abrasion resistance that still soaks up rainwater like a sponge.
**Metrics:** Increasing the soil's shear strength up to 10 times; keeping the water permeability coefficient above 10^-4 m/s; 100 percent suppression of PM10 dust emission.
**Prerequisites:** Annual solution-spraying tankers; periodic control of the soil's biological activity by the landscape maintenance team.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Tar-spreading, heavy mechanical compaction, or concrete pouring, which block water from reaching the aquifers and kill the living soil.
**Tags:** soil permeability, biological stabilization, fine-dust prevention, calcite-precipitating bacteria, MICP
**Scale:** Ground bed and pedestrian paths
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The crust needs exposed, uncemented, sprayable in-situ soil whose grains can still be bridged by precipitated calcite — the existing fabric's streets offer none of that (asphalt over concrete over compacted fill), and ripping a retrofitted block back to raw soil to spray it fights curbs, utility trenches, and traffic alike; the annual tanker regime presumes an unpaved ground that the old street, by construction, is not.
**Cognitive anchor:** The swept, beaten-earth floor of "a grandmother's courtyard house in a desert town" — dustless under the children all summer, baked hard by years of care, yet the first autumn rain sinks straight into it.
**Narrative hook:** We spray a bacterium, and the March dust under 50 thousand footsteps turns into walkable sandstone that still drinks the rain.
**Syn (synergies):** [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — preserving full soil permeability; [Multi-signature custody protocol for the physical portals of sensitive spaces with duress revocation and no automatic repair access](../security-and-resilience/securing-sensitive-spaces.md#multi-signature-custody-protocol-for-the-physical-portals-of-sensitive-spaces-with-duress-revocation-and-no-automatic-repair-access) — asphalt-free paving compatible with the natural bed
**Source:** supplementary idea of the systems architect — sustainable bio-geotechnics

#### Electrostatic precipitation of dust and an invisible mist curtain at the 6-meter openings (Electrostatic Dust Scavenging)
**Mechanism:** The ground floor, 6 meters high, has no sealing rigid walls. In the seasons when desert fine dust attacks, micro-electrode wires around the columns create a local electrostatic field; the suspended particles become charged and are hunted and precipitated by a curtain of micrometer water drops (from the treatment plant's clarified effluent) with a diameter under 20 microns. The result: the air of the pedestrian ways stays clean without any glass enclosure.
**Metrics:** Precipitation of 85 to 92 percent of the suspended PM2.5 and PM10 particles; power draw under 0.5 watts per square meter of open front; gentle evaporation to temper the courtyard's temperature.
**Prerequisites:** Hardness-removed water from the MBR's output; a high-voltage supply with a safe, anti-electrocution microampere current.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Locking the citizens in sealed rooms behind expensive filters during dust events, cutting them off from presence in open nature.
**Tags:** fine dust, air filtration, electrostatics, microclimate, mist curtain, clean ground-floor air
**Scale:** Inter-building connective level (ground floor)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The scavenger's working volume is the unsealed 6-meter hall itself. The existing fabric offers no such open front — its dust is already locked behind closed windows and dealt with, badly, indoors — so hanging electrodes and mist nozzles on conventional facades feeds humidity and scale to rooms that were never open and cleans an airstream that never passes.
**Cognitive anchor:** The first breath on the porch of "a desert-town courtyard with its qanat-fed hōwz" in midsummer: air cooled and washed by underground water, with no wall between the breath and the sky.
**Narrative hook:** An invisible curtain of mist eats the desert dust before it reaches your face — no glass, and still clean air.
**Syn (synergies):** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — 6-meter open ground floor; [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](../water-and-matter/treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale) — clarified micro-MBR output; [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) — continuity of the ground-floor forest
**Source:** supplementary idea of the systems architect — aerosol physics and atmospheric purification

### How the approaches collide and compose
Minimizing sealed paving is the one law all four share, and their relation is clearest read as four sub-questions of it: [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) answers structure (where loads and feet go at all), [Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)](ground-surface-treatment.md#green-permeable-paths-compatible-with-the-passage-of-heavy-civil-defense-vehicles-grass-pavers) answers load (what happens when a heavy vehicle must nonetheless cross), [Biomineral stabilization of the soil bed with bacterial calcite against dust (Soil Bio-Consolidation via MICP)](ground-surface-treatment.md#biomineral-stabilization-of-the-soil-bed-with-bacterial-calcite-against-dust-soil-bio-consolidation-via-micp) answers dust at its source (what the walking surface is made of), [Electrostatic precipitation of dust and an invisible mist curtain at the 6-meter openings (Electrostatic Dust Scavenging)](ground-surface-treatment.md#electrostatic-precipitation-of-dust-and-an-invisible-mist-curtain-at-the-6-meter-openings-electrostatic-dust-scavenging) answers dust already airborne (what the walker breathes). Two of the pairings coexist almost for free. [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) and [Biomineral stabilization of the soil bed with bacterial calcite against dust (Soil Bio-Consolidation via MICP)](ground-surface-treatment.md#biomineral-stabilization-of-the-soil-bed-with-bacterial-calcite-against-dust-soil-bio-consolidation-via-micp) are one end pursued by two rival means: [Biomineral stabilization of the soil bed with bacterial calcite against dust (Soil Bio-Consolidation via MICP)](ground-surface-treatment.md#biomineral-stabilization-of-the-soil-bed-with-bacterial-calcite-against-dust-soil-bio-consolidation-via-micp) exists to fix [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving)'s own failure mode, since the untouched soil [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) promises is precisely the soil that becomes PM10 under the footsteps of 50 thousand people in a dry Shiraz spring — and the crust's open micronic pores are engineered so the sponge still breathes and the zero-mortar rule still holds. They are not, however, the same act: both texts prescribe the walking surface itself — [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) binds every necessary ground-level human passage to "only non-mortar paving (dry-laid stone with open sandy joints)", while [Biomineral stabilization of the soil bed with bacterial calcite against dust (Soil Bio-Consolidation via MICP)](ground-surface-treatment.md#biomineral-stabilization-of-the-soil-bed-with-bacterial-calcite-against-dust-soil-bio-consolidation-via-micp) claims "Ground bed and pedestrian paths" as walkable sandstone — and one plaza has one floor. [Electrostatic precipitation of dust and an invisible mist curtain at the 6-meter openings (Electrostatic Dust Scavenging)](ground-surface-treatment.md#electrostatic-precipitation-of-dust-and-an-invisible-mist-curtain-at-the-6-meter-openings-electrostatic-dust-scavenging) stakes no ground area at all, but it is not surface-free: it hangs on the piloti columns and buys its water from [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](../water-and-matter/treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale) rather than spending the soil, yet what it precipitates is not destroyed — the 85-to-92 percent of captured particulate falls back out of the air onto exactly the ground the other three answers are fighting over.

The real collisions sit on the same square meter and must be allocated, not harmonized. [Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)](ground-surface-treatment.md#green-permeable-paths-compatible-with-the-passage-of-heavy-civil-defense-vehicles-grass-pavers) contradicts [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving)'s premise at exactly one point that matters: [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) guarantees its 100 percent permeability by keeping every tire off the living soil, while [Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)](ground-surface-treatment.md#green-permeable-paths-compatible-with-the-passage-of-heavy-civil-defense-vehicles-grass-pavers) exists for the tire of a 10-ton axle on that surface, and its "above 80%" number openly admits some sealing. Read as simultaneous universal claims they cannot both be true of one path; read as a hierarchy they compose — [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) as the standing rule for ordinary walking surfaces (with the main flow moved to the deck), [Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)](ground-surface-treatment.md#green-permeable-paths-compatible-with-the-passage-of-heavy-civil-defense-vehicles-grass-pavers) as reserved insurance for rare emergency crossings. [Biomineral stabilization of the soil bed with bacterial calcite against dust (Soil Bio-Consolidation via MICP)](ground-surface-treatment.md#biomineral-stabilization-of-the-soil-bed-with-bacterial-calcite-against-dust-soil-bio-consolidation-via-micp) against [Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)](ground-surface-treatment.md#green-permeable-paths-compatible-with-the-passage-of-heavy-civil-defense-vehicles-grass-pavers) is the sharpest pair to separate: the calcite crust is meant to be walked bare, while the paver grid is meant to be grown through — cementing the open cells of a grass-paver path kills the 100 percent vegetation [Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)](ground-surface-treatment.md#green-permeable-paths-compatible-with-the-passage-of-heavy-civil-defense-vehicles-grass-pavers) claims — so they must be assigned disjoint areas, wheel and emergency tracks to the lattice, pedestrian dust sources to the crust. Finally, the four permeability and suppression figures (100%, above 80%, zero rigid flooring, 100 percent PM10) are one accounting over the union of these allocations, scored once for the society's ground; kept as four separate ledgers they will silently double-count the same soil or leave its worst stretch claimed by none.

The collision that needs a ruling rather than a reconciliation is the default surface itself, and it is one question with two answers: is the ground-level pedestrian plane quarried stone on sandy joints or bio-cemented native soil — does [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving)'s "only non-mortar paving" clause bind all necessary pedestrian ground, or does it yield wherever [Biomineral stabilization of the soil bed with bacterial calcite against dust (Soil Bio-Consolidation via MICP)](ground-surface-treatment.md#biomineral-stabilization-of-the-soil-bed-with-bacterial-calcite-against-dust-soil-bio-consolidation-via-micp) is maintained? Stone argues from budget physics: a laid block performs unconditionally, once, while a crust performs only as long as its spraying regime survives, and an annual tanker contract is precisely the maintenance line a lean year cuts first — the day it is missed, the "100 percent suppression of PM10" reverts, under foot, to the dust the crust exists to prevent. The crust argues from the document's own law: every imported block is quarrying, haulage, and a bedding course between feet and living soil, while the crust cements the very ground the deck answer promised to leave untouched — and beneath the [Electrostatic precipitation of dust and an invisible mist curtain at the 6-meter openings (Electrostatic Dust Scavenging)](ground-surface-treatment.md#electrostatic-precipitation-of-dust-and-an-invisible-mist-curtain-at-the-6-meter-openings-electrostatic-dust-scavenging), where the precipitated dust falls, a maintained crust receives the deposition as mineral feedstock while a grass lattice silts up and a stone plaza merely re-accumulates what the mist dropped. The evidence that argues each side is a maintenance-reliability figure (what fraction of missed spray seasons breaks the PM10 claim) and paired dust-loading measurements on crusted versus jointed-stone surfaces at equal footfall, with the curtain's deposition rate counted in the same survey.
