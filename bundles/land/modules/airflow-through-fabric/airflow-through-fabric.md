# Airflow Through the Built Fabric

## Abstract
Whether the breeze admitted by the open 20/80 plan actually arrives at the pedestrian is set by two different kinds of law recorded here against one another: a site-layout computation that decides how far apart the towers stand and which corridors the prevailing wind is steered through ([Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture)), and a local device that breaks the gale those corridors can accelerate beneath the 6-meter piloti ([Taming the Venturi effect with Coanda wind-breaks in the 6-meter piloti space (Coanda Bio-Baffles & Vortex Shedding)](airflow-through-fabric.md#taming-the-venturi-effect-with-coanda-wind-breaks-in-the-6-meter-piloti-space-coanda-bio-baffles--vortex-shedding)). Each answer silently fixes the boundary condition the other must live with — spacing is the far field, the piloti is where that field lands. If the spacing math goes wrong, residents of the society lose daylight and breathe trapped air in stagnant, shadowed slots; if the baffling goes wrong, the same openness that cools the plain turns every tower base into an 18-meter-per-second wind barrier and the promised ground floor into a place nobody crosses.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Master plans compute tower gaps; column and landscape design shape the piloti perimeter later, elsewhere. If the two answers live in separate papers, the CFD run that sizes the spacing is never handed to whoever tames the flow under it — each optimizes its own slice of one wind field, and the errors multiply exactly where both are spent: at ground level, in the body of the person walking between them. Keeping spacing law and baffle device on one page forces one airstream, one model, two verdicts.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How should the spacing and form of buildings steer the flow of wind and cross-draught?

### Approaches

#### Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture
**Mechanism:** The foresighted buildings of the society must under no circumstances be built wall-to-wall or attached to one another. In the aerial view, dense single towers at 20 percent coverage stand at a definite, computed open distance from one another within the 80 percent domain of nature. This computed spacing is tuned to the angles of solar radiation in the different seasons (preventing permanent cold shadow-casting in winter or trapped radiation in summer) and to steering the flow lines of the region's prevailing winds, so that the settlement always breathes climatically.
**Metrics:** Linear distance between buildings: proportioned to building height for the receipt of 100% natural daylight; mountain-breeze corridors kept open.
**Prerequisites:** Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations and solar models before fixing the position of the tower spots.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Building narrow trench-like streets lined with continuous rows of wall-to-wall attached apartments that blind out natural light and create traps of stagnant, suffocating air.
**Tags:** building arrangement, aerial morphology, light corridor, local wind, removal of nuisance shadow-casting
**Scale:** Inter-building level / plain-wide macro
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | In the traditional fabric with wall-to-wall parceling, forcing the computed spacing imprisons wasted and unsafe leftover spaces between the plots and forfeits the owners' accrued rights. Respecting the wind corridor requires integrated master-plan design and island-style construction.
**Cognitive anchor:** The cool of the evening breeze in "the summer villa-gardens of Shandiz or Qasr al-Dasht", where no building stands in the way of the winter sun's radiation or the passage of the cool summer breeze.
**Narrative hook:** Stand in a Shandiz garden lane on a July evening — the breeze travels the lane's whole length because no wall was ever built across its path.
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — 20% coverage; [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — 6-meter open ground floor; [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — permeable land; breaking Shiraz's heat island and night-ventilating the settlement

#### Taming the Venturi effect with Coanda wind-breaks in the 6-meter piloti space (Coanda Bio-Baffles & Vortex Shedding)
**Mechanism:** Siting 100 buildings on 6-meter stilts in the open domain of the plain can, when high-speed winds blow, produce a Venturi phenomenon and severe air vortices beneath the buildings that disturb pedestrian comfort. To break this flow without solid walls, the piloti columns are fitted with aerodynamically shaped surfaces (based on the Coanda effect), and flexible deep-rooted hedges are planted around them, converting the turbulent wind lines into gentle, harmless micro-vortices with a speed under 3 meters per second at pedestrian level.
**Metrics:** Reducing incoming storm-wind speed from 18 meters per second to under 3 meters per second at floor level; 100% elimination of the wind-shear phenomenon at lobby entrances; preserving 360-degree horizontal views.
**Prerequisites:** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) (6-meter open piloti), CFD modeling of wind flow around the buildings.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Building solid structures with sharp corners that hurl the wind directly down to street level, creating deadly, impassable wind gusts at the tower bases.
**Tags:** urban aerodynamics, Venturi effect, piloti wind tunnel, Coanda flow, pedestrian comfort, open ground floor
**Scale:** Inter-building connective level (ground floor)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The device needs an open ground plane before there is a piloti flow to condition: the wind-shear problem does exist in the existing fabric — at the plinths of its towers — but there the open 6-meter piloti the device mounts on is absent, so a retrofit could reach only the shaped-column collar, never the irrigated hedge ring whose soil no street offers; in the wall-to-wall low-rise lanes, buildings meet the sky through narrow slots, and whatever wind descends is spent on brick long before a lobby.
**Cognitive anchor:** The hush at "the leeward foot of a poplar windbreak at a village garden's edge" — where the same plain wind that flattens the treetops arrives beside the trunk as a soft, harmless swirl.
**Narrative hook:** A storm wind dies politely at the foot of a garden poplar — we teach a hundred piloti columns the same manners.
**Syn (synergies):** [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) — continuous ground-floor biome; [Electrostatic precipitation of dust and an invisible mist curtain at the 6-meter openings (Electrostatic Dust Scavenging)](ground-surface-treatment.md#electrostatic-precipitation-of-dust-and-an-invisible-mist-curtain-at-the-6-meter-openings-electrostatic-dust-scavenging) — mist curtain for dust filtration; [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering) — ecological landscape filter
**Source:** supplementary idea of the systems architect — CFD and microclimate comfort

### How the approaches collide and compose
[Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture) is a law of site layout — it decides the far field: how much wind is admitted, where, and at what solar cost. [Taming the Venturi effect with Coanda wind-breaks in the 6-meter piloti space (Coanda Bio-Baffles & Vortex Shedding)](airflow-through-fabric.md#taming-the-venturi-effect-with-coanda-wind-breaks-in-the-6-meter-piloti-space-coanda-bio-baffles--vortex-shedding) is a local device — it decides the last few meters before the wind touches a body. In principle they are complementary rather than double-counting, and not only because their scales differ: [Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture)'s own success manufactures [Taming the Venturi effect with Coanda wind-breaks in the 6-meter piloti space (Coanda Bio-Baffles & Vortex Shedding)](airflow-through-fabric.md#taming-the-venturi-effect-with-coanda-wind-breaks-in-the-6-meter-piloti-space-coanda-bio-baffles--vortex-shedding)'s problem. An 80 percent open plain, corridors deliberately kept clear, and towers standing as isolated islands give the prevailing wind almost nothing to brake against, so the flow the spacing law frees in the sky must pass under the buildings — and a 6-meter piloti is exactly a constriction, with the Venturi effect as its signature. The more faithfully the layout law is obeyed, the more baffle work there is at ground level.

The double-counting risk is real, but it sits in the seam and in the shared ground. First, both answers claim the same outcome: [Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture) promises open breeze corridors, [Taming the Venturi effect with Coanda wind-breaks in the 6-meter piloti space (Coanda Bio-Baffles & Vortex Shedding)](airflow-through-fabric.md#taming-the-venturi-effect-with-coanda-wind-breaks-in-the-6-meter-piloti-space-coanda-bio-baffles--vortex-shedding) promises under-3-meters-per-second pedestrian air; if the society's wind-comfort metric is scored against each document separately, the layout can pass its solar and corridor criteria while the ground floor fails comfort with each paper pointing at the other. One CFD chain must carry both claims, with [Taming the Venturi effect with Coanda wind-breaks in the 6-meter piloti space (Coanda Bio-Baffles & Vortex Shedding)](airflow-through-fabric.md#taming-the-venturi-effect-with-coanda-wind-breaks-in-the-6-meter-piloti-space-coanda-bio-baffles--vortex-shedding)'s comfort envelope acting as a constraint inside [Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture)'s siting loop, not as an audit after it. Second, both spend the same ground: [Taming the Venturi effect with Coanda wind-breaks in the 6-meter piloti space (Coanda Bio-Baffles & Vortex Shedding)](airflow-through-fabric.md#taming-the-venturi-effect-with-coanda-wind-breaks-in-the-6-meter-piloti-space-coanda-bio-baffles--vortex-shedding)'s hedge rings occupy part of the 80 percent domain [Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture) reserves for nature, and [Taming the Venturi effect with Coanda wind-breaks in the 6-meter piloti space (Coanda Bio-Baffles & Vortex Shedding)](airflow-through-fabric.md#taming-the-venturi-effect-with-coanda-wind-breaks-in-the-6-meter-piloti-space-coanda-bio-baffles--vortex-shedding)'s own 360-degree-view criterion caps how dense the rings may be — damp more wind, lose more transparency — an internal tension that pushes the true answer toward the shaped column rather than massed planting. Composed correctly, the pair is one gradient: spacing as the coarse regulator, piloti geometry as the fine trim, tuned on the same wind field before any tower spot is fixed. Mis-coordinated, they fail with a single common victim — the ground-floor pedestrian to whom both answers promise openness.

Against the direction of that first collision runs a second, sharper one that the seam framing hides: the baffle, when it succeeds, throttles the spacing law's own payoff. Keeping the corridors clear is not only a comfort device — the siting record's own synergy line carries breaking the heat island and night-ventilating the settlement among the results of open ground — and the baffle asks that same airstream to arrive beneath each tower at under 3 meters per second. A standing body at comfort-level wind and a settlement being flushed of stored heat want opposite volume fluxes through the same cross-section, and a passive device cannot tell the 18-meter-per-second storm from the modest night breeze whose passage is the point of an open plain: fixed shaped columns and standing hedge rings damp both alike. The owner decision that closes this seam is one question with two answers: is the under-3-meters-per-second envelope an every-hour requirement at the pedestrian plane — in which case the blockage must be verified to pass the night purge above itself, and a hedge ring is the wrong instrument from the start — or a storm-event requirement only — in which case near-transparent deflecting columns may suffice, massed planting is legitimate, and ordinary nights are left alone? The seasonal wind-hours record argues the first side: if most summer nights already sit inside the comfort band, a standing blockage silently taxes the flush against a hazard that rarely arrives. The site's gust distribution argues the second: if damaging speeds concentrate in short events while purge flow needs every calm night, the device must be nearly invisible to ordinary wind and active only against the gale — a demand that, again, pushes the answer toward column geometry rather than massed planting, and turns hedge transmissivity from a tuning knob into a pass/fail gate.
