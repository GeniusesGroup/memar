# Dokkan
Dokkan is Persian word that means shop or store.

The [retail format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retail_format) (also known as the retail formula) influences the consumer's store choice and addresses the consumer's expectations. At its most basic level, a retail format is a simple marketplace, that is; a location where goods and services are exchanged.
