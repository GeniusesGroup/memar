# Rewarding Cooperation and Thrift

## Abstract
The society that prices every service also prices out the volunteer who waters the trees, so the question this document holds is: with what non-monetary credit shall cooperation and thrift be registered and rewarded? Two answers are recorded, one minting time and one minting restraint. [Citizen time-banking and tokenization of cooperative micro-tasks (Decentralized Time-Banking)](reward-cooperation-thrift.md#citizen-time-banking-and-tokenization-of-cooperative-micro-tasks-decentralized-time-banking) issues "Karmayeh" time-credits at the parity of 1 hour of volunteer work = 1 unit of credit — inflation-free by construction, spent at minute-level precision, and redeemable against specialized services, artistic events and hospitality — cutting the settlement's shared-service costs 30 percent. [Gamified citizen carbon and water credits with urban-token rewards](reward-cooperation-thrift.md#gamified-citizen-carbon-and-water-credits-with-urban-token-rewards) issues "clean-carbon" tokens against metered *restraint* — reduced water and energy consumption, careful waste separation, clean PRT travel — redeemable as free sports and cultural services or meal discounts at the central hub, targeting a 30-percent fall in average household consumption. The two records differ in the physics of their issuance: one counts hours worked, the other counts kilowatt-hours not used, and the same token word ("Karmayeh") in this document names a *time* credit while [Joule-based work-backed monetary standard on net exergy](../economy/monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#joule-based-work-backed-monetary-standard-on-net-exergy-exergy-backed-energy-currency) in the monetary folder mints the same name in *joules* — a collision no single record can see. Both spend through the wallet an idea of [Payment Rails and Service Charging](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md), never a premise of this one.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Kept apart, each record looks like a self-contained incentive scheme; together they raise the requirement's real question — what happens when two non-monetary credits share one wallet. The time bank's promise is parity ("1 hour = 1 unit," "without inflation"); the gamified thrift record's promise is reward "to the degree" of behavior change, denominated in the same units of the same wallet. If an hour of park-weeding and a saved kilowatt-hour both settle as one token, the parity is broken — restraint mints without labor; if they are two tokens, the wallet holds a mini exchange-rate regime that neither record prices. And behind both stands the monetary standard record (a linked idea, never assumed here) declaring that money without an energy equivalent cannot be created — a sentence that condemns the time credit unless time itself is priced in joules somewhere its records do not show. The disagreement is the requirement; the motivation of this document is to hold it in one view.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
With what non-monetary credit shall cooperation and thrift be registered and rewarded? Money prices what is sold, not what is given; and it under-prices what is saved. The society's volunteer work — the maintenance, care, teaching and thrift the services documents assume as context — needs a ledger that is neither payroll nor charity, and the question is what that ledger's unit should be: an hour, a saved kilowatt-hour, or a behavior verified and recorded. The question itself favors none of the answers.

### Approaches

#### Citizen time-banking and tokenization of cooperative micro-tasks (Decentralized Time-Banking)
**Mechanism:** With reduced official working hours, citizens hold free time. For maintaining green space, caring for animals, farming in the fields, or teaching children in the Kiddo, the society implements a "time-bank system" on the platform instead of hiring bureaucratic employees. Every hour of volunteer activity creates in the wallet one "Karmayeh" token (Time-Credit) — inflation-free — directly spendable on specialized services, artistic events or the hospitality services of the complexes.
**Metrics:** Registration of transactions at minute-level precision; base parity rate: 1 hour of work = 1 unit of credit; a 30-percent reduction of the settlement's shared-service costs.
**Prerequisites:** A distributed-ledger software platform; a task completion registration and confirmation system by digital attestation (QR/Beacon).
**Conventional-model flaw:** The commercialization of all human dealings, the conversion of society into passive consumers, and the need for a municipal budget to water one little tree.
**Tags:** participation economy, time bank, Karmayeh token, social ecology, productive leisure
**Scale:** Macro — society, and the Sabz.City platform
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Of the two answers this is the one that can start in the existing settlement, because hours exist wherever people do; the prerequisites are a ledger and a confirmation protocol, not new construction. Its force-fit risk is a money problem, not a substrate problem: where an hour is minted against unverified volunteers and spent on specialized services held short, the bank becomes an employer of last resort for low-skill tasks and a queue-jumper for high-skill ones — the parity rate 1 hour = 1 unit is exactly the price control that produces both.
**Cognitive anchor:** The harvest hands of the Caspian rice villages — neighbors who worked one family's paddy this week and the next family's the next, the ledger kept in hours returned and never in coins counted.
**Narrative hook:** "An hour on someone else's trees buys an hour of a dentist's hands — the bank keeps no favors, only minutes."
**Syn (synergies):** [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) — the wallet and the platform's financial interface; [The ecology of child growth and the Kiddo emotional-intelligence kindergarten at the complex's base level](../living-and-culture/education-path.md#the-ecology-of-child-growth-and-the-kiddo-emotional-intelligence-kindergarten-at-the-complexs-base-level) — the Kiddo project; strengthening of social capital and neighborhood belonging
**Source:** Supplementary idea of the system architect (the micro-economy of social cooperation)

#### Gamified citizen carbon and water credits with urban-token rewards
**Mechanism:** Connect the smart water and energy meters of every suite to a citizen gamification application; in exchange for cutting water use, separating waste carefully and using clean transport (PRT), consumers receive "clean-carbon" tokens, convertible into free sports and cultural services or discounts on meals at the central hub.
**Metrics:** Reduction of average household water and energy consumption by up to 30 percent through continuous smart incentives.
**Prerequisites:** Internet-of-Things (IoT) meter infrastructure; the Sabz.City wallet software platform.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Punitive paper utility bills that create no positive feedback and no real motivation for the consumer to change behavior.
**Tags:** gamification, carbon reward, water saving, urban token, sustainable behavior
**Scale:** Micro — suite, and macro — village
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Smart meters retrofit; the gamification app rides the wallet. Force-fit onto a settlement whose meters are unread or whose shared services (the redemption catalogue: sports halls, cultural venues, hub meals) do not yet exist, the token has nothing to buy, behavior does not change, and the record's flaw text — punitive paper bills — reappears with a friendlier letterhead: points instead of penalties, theatre instead of thrift. The record's 30-percent consumption target is therefore a joint promise with the buildings and venues that must exist before it can be met — their ideas, linked in the syn lines, never assumed.
**Cognitive anchor:** Reward-based interactive mobile games.
**Narrative hook:** "Saving water and electricity is no longer a tedious duty; in the green settlement your sustainable behavior turns into digital coin and free services."
**Syn (synergies):** [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) — the digital wallet; [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) (the urban value token); [Green permeable paths compatible with the passage of heavy civil-defense vehicles (Grass Pavers)](../form-and-land/ground-surface-treatment.md#green-permeable-paths-compatible-with-the-passage-of-heavy-civil-defense-vehicles-grass-pavers) — the free sports village

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[Citizen time-banking and tokenization of cooperative micro-tasks (Decentralized Time-Banking)](reward-cooperation-thrift.md#citizen-time-banking-and-tokenization-of-cooperative-micro-tasks-decentralized-time-banking) mints from labor, [Gamified citizen carbon and water credits with urban-token rewards](reward-cooperation-thrift.md#gamified-citizen-carbon-and-water-credits-with-urban-token-rewards) mints from restraint — and the parity clause of the first is broken by the second in the same wallet.** The time bank's inflation proof is definitional: an hour costs an hour because credit issue is pegged to verified minutes of work (the record's own base rate, 1 hour = 1 unit). The gamified record issues the same platform's tokens against *negatives* — water and energy not consumed — which no hour of labor produced and no attestation (QR/Beacon) can witness; the witness is the meter, and meters are calibrated, not attested. If the two credits are one unit, restraint inflates the currency of labor; if they are two units, the redemption catalogue (both records spend on free sports, cultural events, hospitality, hub meals) becomes a fixed-exchange-rate border between hours and kilowatt-hours that neither record prices. Adjudication consequence: the "30-percent shared-service cost reduction" of the first and the "up to 30-percent consumption fall" of the second cannot both be verified from one ledger until the parity between hour-token and saved-kilowatt-hour token is declared or the tokens are declared inconvertible.

**Both tokens collide with the monetary standard the folder itself proposes.** [Joule-based work-backed monetary standard on net exergy (Exergy-Backed Energy Currency)](../economy/monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#joule-based-work-backed-monetary-standard-on-net-exergy-exergy-backed-energy-currency) forbids money creation without produced or stored physical energy, and its synergy line names this document's time bank as a partner — the two credits of this document are the first candidates for that prohibition: an hour is not a kilowatt-hour, and a saved kilowatt-hour is not a delivered one, so under the strict standard both records' tokens would fall outside money — vouchers, favors, not currency. The folder's own records use the word "Karmayeh" twice in two senses: [Joule-based work-backed monetary standard on net exergy](../economy/monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#joule-based-work-backed-monetary-standard-on-net-exergy-exergy-backed-energy-currency) mints کارمایه in joules; [Citizen time-banking and tokenization of cooperative micro-tasks (Decentralized Time-Banking)](reward-cooperation-thrift.md#citizen-time-banking-and-tokenization-of-cooperative-micro-tasks-decentralized-time-banking) mints توکن کارمایه in hours — and unless the terminology is ruled once, a reader cannot tell which "Karmayeh" a settlement's ledger records.

**The sharpest unresolved collision closes on one decision with two answers: are cooperation and thrift rewardable with a single non-monetary token, or does each require its own?** The first answer — one token — reads both records as issuing instruments of the same social account: the wallet (a payment-rails idea, linked) nets hours and saved joules into one balance, the 30-percent claims of both records then measure one price level, and the parity 1 hour = 1 unit becomes the exchange rate the monetary standard must eventually back or reject. The second answer — two tokens — keeps the time bank's inflation-proof peg intact by quarantining it, prices the thrift token as a metering rebate spendable only on services the meters make cheaper (water, energy, waste haulage), and concedes that the two 30-percent targets belong to different ledgers that may not be summed. The evidence that would settle direction is which redemption basket each record actually requires: the time bank redeems into specialized, scarce services (dentistry, hospitality) that rationing suggests a peg will over-issue; the thrift record redeems into free venue and meal services whose cost is the settlement's own — and only the owner ruling on "one token or two" determines whether the monetary standard's prohibition is obeyed or quietly bypassed.
