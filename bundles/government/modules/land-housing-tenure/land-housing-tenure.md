# Land and Housing Tenure

## Abstract
The six-dang deed is the answer Iran already gave to a question this document holds open: in what form are land and housing held, and whose shoulders carry the cost of keeping them standing for a century? Seven answers are recorded, and they are one machine only if their parts are admitted as parts. [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) replaces direct apartment ownership with shares in a legal person that owns the whole complex forever, so the owner of the building is also eternally the payer of its repairs — lifting useful structural life from the traditional 20-to-30 years to above 100 and cutting operational costs 50 percent. [Repeal of the standalone plot title deed and conversion of land into common ecological shares (REITs-based Land Dematerialization)](land-housing-tenure.md#repeal-of-the-standalone-plot-title-deed-and-conversion-of-land-into-common-ecological-shares-reits-based-land-dematerialization) applies the same machinery to the ground under the building in new development zones, where micro-plot subdivision and raw-land speculation are put at zero percent. [Decentralized development and maintenance investment fund (Green REIT & Asset Trust)](land-housing-tenure.md#decentralized-development-and-maintenance-investment-fund-green-reit--asset-trust) is the treasury behind the shares, paying upkeep out of surplus energy, industry, services and recycling revenue so that a building "never becomes klangi again." [Bifurcated lease contract: separating the price of space from automated furnishing depreciation (Bifurcated Rent & Automated Amortization Fee)](land-housing-tenure.md#bifurcated-lease-contract-separating-the-price-of-space-from-automated-furnishing-depreciation-bifurcated-rent--automated-amortization-fee) rewrites the tenant's side into two meters — space rent plus about 1 percent monthly furnishing amortization, with 100-percent removal of the mortgage-deposit — and [Shelter security and targeted social responsibility endowment (Shelter Security & Impact Endowment)](land-housing-tenure.md#shelter-security-and-targeted-social-responsibility-endowment-shelter-security--impact-endowment) puts a social floor under all of it: a fixed share of platform transactions forbids eviction into homelessness. Two recovered answers complete the stack from the other side: an inalienable public land trust that keeps the municipality's undivided title to garden lands unsellable under any circumstance and spends every revenue on more land ([Inalienable public land trust for garden lands: the municipality keeps undivided title forever, sells it in no circumstance, and dedicates every revenue to more land (Public Garden-Land Trust)](land-housing-tenure.md#inalienable-public-land-trust-for-garden-lands-the-municipality-keeps-undivided-title-forever-sells-it-in-no-circumstance-and-dedicates-every-revenue-to-more-land-public-garden-land-trust)), and the reversion of a deceased person's whole financial estate to public ownership, the concept of inheritance declared nonexistent ([Reversion of the deceased's financial assets to public ownership — the concept of inheritance does not exist (Public Reversion at Death)](land-housing-tenure.md#reversion-of-the-deceaseds-financial-assets-to-public-ownership--the-concept-of-inheritance-does-not-exist-public-reversion-at-death)). The recorded disagreement is not whether deeds are wasteful but how many decisions the share machine is: three property reforms wearing one mechanism, with the collision section below showing where their ledgers double-count and where two records carry fields copied from other requirements.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Four of the five records in this document name [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) as a prerequisite, a synergy or the structure they themselves complete: the fund says it is "the financial and legal structure behind the housing shares"; the land record extends the share to the ground; the lease record needs the corporate owner before it can meter depreciation; the endowment needs the same platform's wallet. Kept scattered, a reader meets shareholding five times and takes it for a settled premise; together, its five appearances are a single unresolved decision — the unit of property the society owns — argued at three levels (building, plot, portfolio) with no level's verdict recorded. The other collision the separation hides is one of quality, not doctrine: two of the records ([Shelter security and targeted social responsibility endowment (Shelter Security & Impact Endowment)](land-housing-tenure.md#shelter-security-and-targeted-social-responsibility-endowment-shelter-security--impact-endowment), and on closer reading its fit and anchor fields) carry analytical text that describes a different requirement — natural light in renovated units — evidence that fields migrated without their host ideas. The record store is frozen and the audit trail ends here, so the dispute and the defect both have to be visible in one place, and neither is visible in any single record.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
In what form are land and housing held, and whose shoulders carry the cost of keeping them standing for a century? The conventional answer is the six-dang deed: one person owns one delimited unit, sells when they like, and pays for repairs only when neighbors force the question — and the building's second fifty years is the answer's bill. Each approach below proposes a different holder and a different payer for the same structure and soil: a corporate legal person that never sells, a common ecological fund that cannot be fenced, a trust that finances upkeep from operating revenue, a tenant's metered amortization contract, or a transaction-funded social endowment. The two recovered answers add two further holders: a public trustee of garden ground that is forbidden to sell, and the commons itself, which takes back a dead person's value. The question itself favors none of them.

### Approaches

#### Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival
**Mechanism:** The six-dang deed of the apartment and individual physical ownership are the main source of the degradation of materials, of thermal waste and of the early destruction of buildings, because the builder builds housing to sell it fast and owes nothing to the energy bill or to the wear of 20 years! This pattern removes direct ownership and replaces it with "indirect shareholding in the legal company that owns the whole complex." Because the company is in perpetuity the owner, the operator and the answerer for residential quality and for repair and maintenance costs, it installs from day one the highest standards of insulation, ultra-low-consumption heat pumps and recovery systems — in order to minimize its own running costs.
**Metrics:** Useful structural life raised from the traditional 20 to 30 years to above 100 years; a 50-percent cut in operating-phase costs; the end of property dispute files between neighbors.
**Prerequisites:** Reform of commercial and registration law; formation of real-estate investment trusts (REITs); transparency of share ledgers on a digital platform.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The conventional build-and-sell system and apartment subdivision, in which the builder installs the lowest-quality envelope and the greediest heating systems and sees their profit in leaving the project fast.
**Tags:** indirect ownership, shareholding economy, conflict-of-interest control, lifecycle management, sustainable building
**Scale:** Macro — institutional, and micro — ownership
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The shareholding (REIT) system, in the traditional decayed fabric where the six-dang deed is rooted in culture, faces intense psychological resistance from small owners; traditional owners read the scheme as the confiscation of their property.
**Cognitive anchor:** Confident shareholding in "the great economic joint-stock companies"; release from the worry of a building's wear and the preservation of capital value in the form of undivided shares in durable buildings.
**Narrative hook:** "Own your home the way you own shares in a respected company — and, unlike a deed, the building never needs your money to stay young."
**Syn (synergies):** [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](../form-and-land/settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) — large-scale grain above 5 hectares; [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](../energy/energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) — building sustainability; [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](../form-and-land/service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly) — upkeep of the common sports and service areas

#### Shelter security and targeted social responsibility endowment (Shelter Security & Impact Endowment)

"scholarships for the talented" for gifted children; 3. "support for NGOs" through free space; 4. "full transparency of the financial ledgers" for all members.
**Metrics:** A fixed percentage of platform transactions assigned to the shelter fund; the homelessness rate reduced to zero percent; 100-percent publication of balance sheets on the platform.
**Prerequisites:** [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) (the wallet and the financial-transparency platform); [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) (shareholding ownership of the complexes).

#### Repeal of the standalone plot title deed and conversion of land into common ecological shares (REITs-based Land Dematerialization)
**Mechanism:** The gradual repeal of the traditional concept of "a six-dang deed to an enclosed plot of land" across all new territorial development zones. The land is defined as a common, indivisible ecological substrate. Owners and investors, instead of buying square meters of ground and fencing them, become shareholders in the legal person that owns the whole domain (in the form of real-estate investment trusts on the platform). The owner's profit arises exclusively from the operational efficiency of the village's services, not from impounding raw land and raising its price.
**Metrics:** Share of micro residential-plot subdivision: zero percent; share of speculative transactions in raw land within the village: zero percent; a 100-fold increase in the liquidity of assets through the tokenized REITs share market.
**Prerequisites:** Reform of registration law; enactment of a common-property real-estate market law; the digital settlement substrate of the society's platform.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The conversion of land into a speculative commodity and the accumulation of stagnant capital; development locked inside micro-plot boundaries; windfall profits born of land-use change.
**Tags:** property rights, repeal of plot subdivision, REITs funds, curbing speculation, land economics
**Scale:** Macro — national, and the legal system of property
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The mechanism itself states the limit — repeal applies "across all new territorial development zones," where there is no deed to repeal. Force-fit onto the existing fabric, it is not a reform but the largest title dispute in the country's history: every six-dang holder would be converted into a shareholder against the exact cultural root the shareholding record's own Fit names, and the zero-percent micro-plot metric would read as expropriation statistics rather than policy. Workable in the existing fabric only as the voluntary exchange offered to whoever has already lost the will to hold a deed — the path the reverse-renewal phases of other documents describe, never assumed here.
**Cognitive anchor:** The village commons the grandparents knew — the shared pasture and the water measured by the sun's turn ("pāy-āb"), ground that belonged to everyone in use and to no one on paper, and nobody thought the world had ended.
**Narrative hook:** "Before the deeds, the village shared its pasture and timed its water by the sun; this gives the land back to the commons — and hands every share a price that travels."
**Syn (synergies):** [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) — shareholding ownership of the buildings; [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — the 20% coverage cap without boundary conflict-of-interest; [Redefining the orchards' spatial justice (replacing the ban with the formula of 20% built in height and 80% living orchard)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#redefining-the-orchards-spatial-justice-replacing-the-ban-with-the-formula-of-20-built-in-height-and-80-living-orchard) — resolving the Qasr al-Dasht orchard conflict

#### Bifurcated lease contract: separating the price of space from automated furnishing depreciation (Bifurcated Rent & Automated Amortization Fee)
**Mechanism:** The complete removal of the heavy deposit (the "goodwill money") and of the conventional rental system. The lease of every suite is split into two separate parts: 1. a fixed price for the right to use the physical space of the structure; 2. a monthly rate for the depreciation of equipment and interior furnishings, based on a ten-year useful-life algorithm (about 1 percent per month). Whenever a resident requests furnishing renewal or a move to another tower, depreciation is read from function-monitoring sensors and part replacement is executed systematically.
**Metrics:** Monthly depreciation rate of interior equipment: about 1 percent of current value (on a 10-year life horizon); the cost of moving home reduced below the cost of conventional freight; 100-percent removal of goodwill money.
**Prerequisites:** The Sabz.City smart-contract accounting platform; [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) (shareholding ownership of the buildings).
**Conventional-model flaw:** Traditional landlords holding heavy deposits hostage, the fraying of apartment furnishings, and the attritional fights between tenant and landlord over broken taps and appliances.
**Tags:** housing economics, professional tenancy, depreciation accounting, shared furnishing, the housing market
**Scale:** Micro — building, and the financial model of the contract
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | A contract is the cheapest institution in this document: a bifurcated lease can be written on any rental unit of the existing fabric without moving a wall, and removing goodwill money relieves precisely the deposit-hostage flaw traditional landlords create. But the record's own mechanism reads depreciation "from function-monitoring sensors" and its prerequisites name the smart-contract accounting platform: force-fit onto a conventional lease with neither, "about 1 percent per month" becomes another negotiated deduction, and the fights over broken fittings this text indicts simply migrate to the depreciation column — the flaw survives with a better spreadsheet.
**Cognitive anchor:** The guarantee sticker on the first family refrigerator — the pride of an Iranian purchase in the 1340s and 1350s solar years that arrived with a named someone answerable for its aging.
**Narrative hook:** "The lease is two honest numbers: the price of the space, and the price of the sofa growing old — and when the ten years are up, the replacement arrives before the complaint ends."
**Syn (synergies):** [Fluid society and cognitive matching across moves among 100 blocks (Cognitive Matching)](../governance/resident-matching-and-mobility.md#fluid-society-and-cognitive-matching-across-moves-among-100-blocks-cognitive-matching) (fluid residential moves without hauling furniture); [Market design without information asymmetry and the removal of property brokerage (Market Design without Information Asymmetry)](permitted-intermediation.md#market-design-without-information-asymmetry-and-the-removal-of-property-brokerage-market-design-without-information-asymmetry) — removal of real-estate brokerage
**Source:** Yalda justification plan (pp. 17 and 25)

#### Decentralized development and maintenance investment fund (Green REIT & Asset Trust)
**Mechanism:** The financial and legal structure behind the housing shares: this fund gathers all revenues earned from surplus energy, industrial agriculture, commercial services and recycling, pays shareholders' dividends automatically and finances the maintenance costs of the buildings.
**Metrics:** A guarantee that structural depreciation and wear reach zero over time; the payment of stable cash dividends to shareholders.
**Prerequisites:** A distributed-ledger financial platform; automated intelligent auditing.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Small owners who have no money to repaint or to fix the elevator, and buildings that become ruins after 10 years.
**Tags:** REIT property funds, collective investment, sustainable upkeep, non-decay of capital, finance
**Scale:** Macro — economic and project finance
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The trust is the one answer in this document that can be retrofitted as pure governance — a maintenance fund with an audit trail can take over a decaying co-owned tower in the existing fabric long before any share reform arrives. Its force-fit risk is a revenue mirage: every income line the mechanism counts (surplus energy, industrial agriculture, commercial services, recycling) is a property of a working productive complex; installed where those flows do not exist, the fund is a subsidy with a ledger, and its "zero depreciation" guarantee quietly becomes the same municipal promise that today's repair funds already break.
**Cognitive anchor:** The financial security of reputable global pension funds.
**Narrative hook:** "Your house will never become a demolition case again! Under the shareholding structure, the value of the building rises every year alongside the technology of the neighborhood."
**Syn (synergies):** [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) — indirect shareholding ownership; [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) (the urban-value token); [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](../governance/law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) (infrastructure anchors of law)

#### Inalienable public land trust for garden lands: the municipality keeps undivided title forever, sells it in no circumstance, and dedicates every revenue to more land (Public Garden-Land Trust)
**Mechanism:** Preserve the abandoned and non-abandoned garden lands by economics, not by decree: the municipality keeps title to the garden ground, and the complexes built inside those gardens are held as undivided co-ownership between the public holder and the institutions formed to build them; the municipality is forbidden to sell these holdings in any circumstance; and the entire revenue earned from the complexes' benefits is dedicated to buying more land for more complexes in the same style. The complexes' remaining capital is never supplied by the municipality or by any other legal person: to make construction and upkeep costs transparent and balanced, it is raised from natural persons on a fully transparent, stock-market-like structure with popular oversight — which guarantees the public stake without spending the settlement's public budget.
**Metrics:** Sale of the public undivided title: zero, in no circumstance; complex revenue dedicated to further land purchase: 100 percent; useful green space delivered by the trust's building style: 4 hectares of living garden within a 5-hectare plot; preservation spending from the public budget: zero (the land pays for its own expansion).
**Prerequisites:** Complex-owning legal persons — the machinery of [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival); a share market with transparent ledgers and popular oversight; the coverage lock of [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule), which keeps each complex's 80 percent living garden.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Preservation-by-prohibition: as long as the economic aspect of holding these lands is unsolved, no rule can keep them — «هزاران قانون و مصوبه نتوانسته تاکنون این فضاها را حفظ کند، مطمئن باشید در آینده هم نخواهد توانست» (thousands of laws and ordinances have failed to preserve these spaces and, the author writes, certainly never will), because the ban without revenue rewards the owner for killing the trees.
**Tags:** land trust, inalienable public title, garden preservation, undivided co-ownership, self-financing conservation
**Scale:** Macro — the municipality and its garden belt
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | Where the municipality already holds garden land the inalienability clause is a condition on a deed it possesses, not a construction program — the trust can attach to the existing fabric; where it holds none, the machine only starts after the first complex earns, so the first parcels must still be acquired by ordinary purchase. Its force-fit mirror is [Repeal of the standalone plot title deed and conversion of land into common ecological shares (REITs-based Land Dematerialization)](land-housing-tenure.md#repeal-of-the-standalone-plot-title-deed-and-conversion-of-land-into-common-ecological-shares-reits-based-land-dematerialization): undivided public title and private share title co-holding one asset demand the same registration reform, and the trust inherits that debt.
**Cognitive anchor:** The waqf garden of the grandparents — ground whose deed could not be sold, only whose fruit could be spent.
**Narrative hook:** "The garden cannot be sold because the only hand allowed to hold it is forbidden to sell — and every coin it earns buys the next garden."
**Syn (synergies):** [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) — the complex-owning legal person the trust partners with; [Repeal of the standalone plot title deed and conversion of land into common ecological shares (REITs-based Land Dematerialization)](land-housing-tenure.md#repeal-of-the-standalone-plot-title-deed-and-conversion-of-land-into-common-ecological-shares-reits-based-land-dematerialization) — the indivisible-ground doctrine; [Redefining the orchards' spatial justice (replacing the ban with the formula of 20% built in height and 80% living orchard)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#redefining-the-orchards-spatial-justice-replacing-the-ban-with-the-formula-of-20-built-in-height-and-80-living-orchard) — the same diagnosis of the garden owners' economics, from the owner's side; [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — the 80 percent the trust keeps alive

#### Reversion of the deceased's financial assets to public ownership — the concept of inheritance does not exist (Public Reversion at Death)
**Mechanism:** After death, all of the deceased's financial assets pass into public ownership — «مفهوم ارث و وارث موجود در دیگر جوامع در این جامعه موجودیت ندارد»: the concept of inheritance and heirs found in other societies has no existence in this one. The entire freedom of transmission is moved into life: the person must, while alive, be generous enough or register a smart attorney or contract that settles the assets against events in the system. Because natural persons hold only shares in legal persons — never the land, the house or the car themselves — there is no physical estate to probate; what reverts is the value.
**Metrics:** Share of a deceased person's financial assets reverting to public ownership: 100 percent; automatic entitlement of surviving heirs: none; lifetime event-driven smart-contract dispositions: the only private transfer channel.
**Prerequisites:** [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) (indirect shareholding, so an estate holds instruments rather than things); the society's software, which registers attorneys and smart contracts and executes them on system events; a public register of the reverted value able to receive it — the trust structure of [Decentralized development and maintenance investment fund (Green REIT & Asset Trust)](land-housing-tenure.md#decentralized-development-and-maintenance-investment-fund-green-reit--asset-trust).
**Conventional-model flaw:** Inheritance as an automatic, lifetime-blind transfer that re-concentrates capital every generation and lets the second generation live off a closed ledger — and, as its compromise, the estate tax, which keeps the transfer whole and merely rents a slice of it to the state.
**Tags:** inheritance, public reversion, capital concentration, smart contracts, lifetime disposition
**Scale:** Macro — the inheritance system of property law
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The clause presumes the single settlement currency and the shareholding register the two constitutions build together; force-fit onto the existing fabric it collides head-on with family law and the six-dang deed culture this document's first record already indicts — every death becomes an estate fight rather than a reversion.
**Cognitive anchor:** Settling one's accounts before a long journey — the old traveller's will read aloud while the traveller still travels, so nothing has to be decided after.
**Narrative hook:** "Give it away, bind it by a contract that outlives you, or let it return to the commons — three doors, and inheritance is none of them."
**Syn (synergies):** [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) — shares, not things, are what a person holds; [Decentralized development and maintenance investment fund (Green REIT & Asset Trust)](land-housing-tenure.md#decentralized-development-and-maintenance-investment-fund-green-reit--asset-trust) — the treasury the reverted value enters; [Shelter security and targeted social responsibility endowment (Shelter Security & Impact Endowment)](land-housing-tenure.md#shelter-security-and-targeted-social-responsibility-endowment-shelter-security--impact-endowment) — the social floor an earmarked slice of reverted value could fund; [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) — the ledger that sees the death-event
**Source:** National_Constitution.odt

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival)'s company and [Repeal of the standalone plot title deed and conversion of land into common ecological shares (REITs-based Land Dematerialization)](land-housing-tenure.md#repeal-of-the-standalone-plot-title-deed-and-conversion-of-land-into-common-ecological-shares-reits-based-land-dematerialization)'s commons are one mechanism pointed at two objects — and the objects touch.** The building record abolishes direct ownership of the apartment; the land record abolishes the plot deed. Nothing in either says who holds the plot under a share-owned building: if the building company also owns the ground, the land record adds nothing and its zero-percent subdivision metric is simply the building record's consequence; if the land stays common while the building becomes shares, two legal persons hold one asset — the tower in the commons — and every figure in both records (the 100-year structural life, the 100-fold liquidity) is collateral in a dispute the tenure stack has to name. The asymmetry is sharper than a drafting gap: the land record's scope is new development zones only, while the building record carries a [Dual — transitional] Fit and reaches the existing fabric. A share-tower may therefore stand on common land where no plot deed exists to repeal, and on private land where the repeal has not arrived — the same mechanism, three land regimes.

**The tenant's two meters and the shareholder's zero-depreciation promise bill the same wear twice.** [Bifurcated lease contract: separating the price of space from automated furnishing depreciation (Bifurcated Rent & Automated Amortization Fee)](land-housing-tenure.md#bifurcated-lease-contract-separating-the-price-of-space-from-automated-furnishing-depreciation-bifurcated-rent--automated-amortization-fee) charges residents about 1 percent per month of a furnishing's current value — ten years of life, sensor-metered — while [Decentralized development and maintenance investment fund (Green REIT & Asset Trust)](land-housing-tenure.md#decentralized-development-and-maintenance-investment-fund-green-reit--asset-trust) guarantees "the zeroing of depreciation and structural wear over time" and pays it from energy, agriculture, service and recycling revenue. The lease record's own prerequisite is [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival)'s corporate owner; where that owner's shares are held through the fund, the tenant's depreciation fee flows back to shareholders who have also been promised zero wear at no cost. Either the depreciation fee is the fund's revenue stream for interiors and the zero-depreciation guarantee is quietly scope-limited to structure — or the two promises overlap on the same sofa, and one ledger is counting it as an expense while the other counts the identical object as income. The lease's "removal of goodwill money, 100 percent" and the endowment's "fixed percentage of platform transactions" make the same collision concrete: both live on one wallet, and neither record states whether the shelter slice is taken before or after the amortization slice.

**A record cannot be adjudicated on fields copied from another requirement.** [Shelter security and targeted social responsibility endowment (Shelter Security & Impact Endowment)](land-housing-tenure.md#shelter-security-and-targeted-social-responsibility-endowment-shelter-security--impact-endowment)'s Fit text ends "its risk is the lack of sufficient natural light in traditional buildings," and its cognitive anchor is a Japanese wellness hotel's dusk light regulating sleep — properties of a daylight or circadian record (the same wording pattern recurs in [Agritourism and eco-discovery on the controlled farm (Greenhouse Agritourism)](../living-and-culture/recreation-and-culture-venues.md#agritourism-and-eco-discovery-on-the-controlled-farm-greenhouse-agritourism) carried fields and in the buildings daylight records), not of a shelter endowment. The endowment's mechanism, metrics and prerequisites are coherent; the two analytical fields describe a different idea, and a verdict that weighed "install the fund in renovated units; its risk is daylight" would be weighing an accident of migration. Nothing is removed here — the fields stand as recorded — but the adjudication of this approach is suspended on the owner's ruling about their provenance.

**The sharpest unresolved decision with two answers: is the share machine one reform or three?** The first answer reads the stack as one decision with three applications — a single property-law regime (legal person holds; people hold shares; platform settles) extended from buildings to land to treasury — and then the prerequisites collapse into one legislative program (commercial law, registration law, common-property market law, distributed ledgers), the double-billing above becomes one fund's internal accounting rule, and [Shelter security and targeted social responsibility endowment (Shelter Security & Impact Endowment)](land-housing-tenure.md#shelter-security-and-targeted-social-responsibility-endowment-shelter-security--impact-endowment) is that regime's social clause. The second answer keeps three distinct verdicts — buildings, plots and portfolio each get their own reform, their own scope (the land only in new zones, the building even in the old fabric) and their own billable wear — and then nothing in the document may share a premise across the three lines until each is adopted; the fund cannot cite share ownership as its product, the lease cannot assume a corporate owner. The evidence that tips direction is the legal text of the prerequisites themselves: if a single registration reform can create the company, the commons and the trust in one instrument, the first answer is cheaper by two parliaments; if the law must distinguish the two REITs (tenant-occupied suites versus common ground), the second is already written in the statute. Until the owner rules, the document holds one machine and three bills, and the difference is not arithmetic — it is how much of the existing fabric is asked to surrender a deed at once.
