# Permitted Intermediation

## Abstract
Every recorded critique of the middleman in this folder ends in the same place: the platform that replaces them. This document holds one question — how much intermediation between production and consumption is permitted, and how much transparency does the market need? — and two records that answer it in two different markets with one unowned instrument. [Removing speculative intermediation in the commodity value chain and connecting production directly to consumption (the Fab.City model)](permitted-intermediation.md#removing-speculative-intermediation-in-the-commodity-value-chain-and-connecting-production-directly-to-consumption-the-fabcity-model) deletes four to six layers of produce brokerage, promises a 30-to-50-percent fall in final food prices and an 80-percent cut in fresh-waste, and installs in their place a platform that "manages the chain of production, storage and consumption as a transparent value network." [Market design without information asymmetry and the removal of property brokerage (Market Design without Information Asymmetry)](permitted-intermediation.md#market-design-without-information-asymmetry-and-the-removal-of-property-brokerage-market-design-without-information-asymmetry) deletes real-estate commission to zero percent and installs a 100-percent transparent technical history for every dwelling, with "software sovereignty" as neutral referee rather than operator. The two records share no mechanism but share everything else: the same wallet prerequisite, the same shareholding prerequisite, and the same suppressed question — whether the entity that removes all intermediaries has thereby become the final one, and what it charges for the privilege. The rails, the joule standard and the deflation engine are linked ideas of other documents, never premises of these answers.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Kept apart, each record is a subtraction with a clean arithmetic — four to six layers gone from the tomato, the commission gone from the lease — and a reader who adopts one believes the society has answered "how much intermediation is permitted" with "none." Read together, the subtractions are performed by the same actor, and the actor's revenue is recorded in a third document: the settlement fees of [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters). Is the 30-to-50-percent produce-price fall measured before or after the platform's take? Is the "neutral referee" of the housing record the same entity that "manages" the goods chain? Neither question is visible in either record alone, and the answers route to a decision neither document holds — the status of the platform itself — which is why the two records must be adjudicated in one place.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How much intermediation between production and consumption is permitted, and how much transparency does the market need? An intermediary is not one thing: the produce broker, the land broker and the settlement rail each stand between a buyer and something — a tomato, a title, a payment — and each earns differently: from distance, from ignorance, from passage. The question below forces the society to name which of those earnings is legitimate service and which is rent, and to decide whether a market that outlaws the first two can tolerate a platform that lives on the third. The question itself favors none of the answers.

### Approaches

#### Removing speculative intermediation in the commodity value chain and connecting production directly to consumption (the Fab.City model)
**Mechanism:** Remove all redundant speculation and negative intermediation in the distribution of provisions and products, resting on the principles of the international Fab.City consortium (locally produce what is locally consumed). The Sabz.City platform, eliminating the traditional brokers, connects the products of the 500-hectare Atna farm and the orchards directly to the restaurants and consumption stations of the complexes, and manages the chain of production, storage and consumption as one transparent value network.
**Metrics:** Removal of 4 to 6 layers of redundant intermediation; a 30 to 50 percent reduction in the final price of agricultural products for households; an 80 percent reduction in fresh produce waste.
**Prerequisites:** An order-driven logistics management platform; a clean electric distribution fleet; peer-to-peer digital marketplaces.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The corrupt structure of the traditional wholesale produce grounds and the brokerage network, which plunder the farmer's added value and impose a heavy inflationary load on families.

**Scale:** Macro — the plain and the region
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | In the traditional fruit-and-vegetable markets, because of the resistance of the brokerage mafia, it will meet obstruction and requires governing support.
**Cognitive anchor:** The freshness of "direct farmers' markets with the growers, in Europe (Farmers Markets)" — fruit straight from the field, without a middleman.
**Narrative hook:** "The tomato changes hands once now — vine to first-floor plate; the four brokers who made it expensive each kept a cut, and we can show you their invoices."

#### Market design without information asymmetry and the removal of property brokerage (Market Design without Information Asymmetry)
**Mechanism:** The complete removal of parasitic professions that live on the buyer's ignorance and the concealment of information — above all the real-estate agencies and the land brokers. Software sovereignty, as a disinterested referee, makes fully transparent all information on residential capacity, equipment depreciation, the biological quality of air and weather, and transactions; movement between units happens directly and free of charge through the platform's algorithms, on the basis of real supply and demand.
**Metrics:** The share of brokerage and commission in property transactions: zero percent; 100-percent transparency of the technical and cost history of every residential unit; reduction of the cost of access to shelter.
**Prerequisites:** [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) (the undivided shareholding of the assets); [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) (the wallet and real-time settlement); [Fluid society and cognitive matching across moves among 100 blocks (Cognitive Matching)](../governance/resident-matching-and-mobility.md#fluid-society-and-cognitive-matching-across-moves-among-100-blocks-cognitive-matching).
**Conventional-model flaw:** The traditional system of land and housing agencies, which feeds on market opacity and is the cause of runaway inflation, property hoarding and the shelter crisis.
**Tags:** market design, removal of intermediaries, full data transparency, conflict of interest, pure economy
**Scale:** Macro — economic, and the Sabz.City platform
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The mechanism needs no rebuilt city to start — a published technical history of existing units is a registry act, installable on the existing fabric one building at a time — but it cannot finish there. Force-fit onto the conventional settlement with the deeds, the shareholding and the depreciation ledgers absent, zeroing the commission abolishes the one party who today knows anything about the wall behind the plaster: the buyer's ignorance the record indicts survives, only now it has no intermediary and no file — precisely the opacity the flaw text accuses the agency system of producing. The 100-percent transparency metric is therefore a prerequisite wearing a mask: without the land-housing share reforms adopted first, this record's "free of charge" matching is a brochure, not a market.
**Cognitive anchor:** The old bazaar where every shopkeeper knew the going price — the "vāj" of the goods was common knowledge from the tea-house to the caravanserai, and haggling disputed courtesy, never facts.
**Narrative hook:** "The bazaar once priced everything without a broker — we are only giving every wall its file back; the commission was what kept it closed."

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[Removing speculative intermediation in the commodity value chain and connecting production directly to consumption (the Fab.City model)](permitted-intermediation.md#removing-speculative-intermediation-in-the-commodity-value-chain-and-connecting-production-directly-to-consumption-the-fabcity-model) deletes the broker's margin and installs a manager; [Market design without information asymmetry and the removal of property brokerage (Market Design without Information Asymmetry)](permitted-intermediation.md#market-design-without-information-asymmetry-and-the-removal-of-property-brokerage-market-design-without-information-asymmetry) deletes the commission and installs a referee — the same entity, in two incompatible jobs.** The produce record does not merely connect Atna's greenhouses to the first-floor restaurants; it says the platform "manages the chain of production, storage and consumption," an operator's sentence with an operator's exposure: the 80-percent waste reduction depends on the chain's physical success, and whoever manages a chain prices its failures somewhere. The housing record's sentence is the opposite: "disinterested referee," fully transparent, "directly and free of charge." If one entity is both, the conflict-of-interest flaw the housing record aims at the agencies returns verbatim at the platform: a referee that also manages produce, settles invoices (per the rails record's fee lines) and hosts the dispute resolution its own synergy names has interests in every market it referees — hoarding, in the housing record's own vocabulary, becomes a policy option. The records compose only if the platform is a regulated utility with published margins; they collide if it is a merchant — and no record in this document says which it is.

**The two subtractions are justified by two different physics — and the numbers cannot be merged.** The produce record's arithmetic is biological: brokers are removed because the good rots between them — four to six layers of handling against 80-percent fresh waste, thirty to fifty percent of the household's price — a logistics claim about distance. The housing record's arithmetic is epistemic: agencies are removed because the buyer cannot see — zero-percent commission against 100-percent technical history, an information claim about transparency. A society could rationally adopt either and reject the other: direct-produce networks fail without an electric fleet and order-driven logistics (the produce record's own prerequisites), while a zero-commission housing market fails without the shareholding ledgers that make the "technical and cost history" exist (the housing record's land-housing prerequisite, an idea of another document, linked, never assumed). Adjudication consequence: the requirement "how much intermediation is permitted" splits into "who may carry a perishable" and "who may know a price," and the collision section refuses to let the folder answer one and call both answered.

**The sharpest collision closes on one owner decision with two answers: is the platform a market participant or public infrastructure?** The first answer — participant — keeps the produce record's "manages" literally: the platform buys, stores and sells at the invoice fee the rails record already prices, and then the housing record's neutrality claim must be struck or walled off (the referee must be a separate organ — the candidate is the private municipal regulator the fee record names as synergy), and the produce chain becomes a single firm whose monopoly the flaw texts of both records describe perfectly. The second answer — infrastructure — holds the housing record's neutrality literally: the platform owns nothing, "manages" means "coordinates," its revenue is the cost-metered settlement fee and nothing of the margin, and the Atna-to-restaurant logistics line moves into the food folder's records as a producer-owned operation. The evidence that would tip direction is in the produce record's own metrics: the 30-to-50-percent price fall is either before or after the platform's cut, and only a declared revenue rule for the chain's operator makes the number auditable at all; until the owner rules, the two records of this document agree entirely that the broker is gone, and nothing else.
