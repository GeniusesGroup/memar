## Open Questions
1. **Is the platform a market participant or public infrastructure?** [Removing speculative intermediation in the commodity value chain and connecting production directly to consumption (the Fab.City model)](permitted-intermediation.md#removing-speculative-intermediation-in-the-commodity-value-chain-and-connecting-production-directly-to-consumption-the-fabcity-model) says the platform "manages the chain of production, storage and consumption"; [Market design without information asymmetry and the removal of property brokerage (Market Design without Information Asymmetry)](permitted-intermediation.md#market-design-without-information-asymmetry-and-the-removal-of-property-brokerage-market-design-without-information-asymmetry) says it is a "disinterested referee." One entity cannot hold both sentences unless the referee is a separate organ. Participant, infrastructure, or split the roles into two bodies? *(Why: the produce record's conflict-of-interest indictment of agencies transfers verbatim to an operator-referee platform, and the housing record's neutrality claim fails on an operator; every metric in both records depends on which entity the verdict names. Blocking: yes for any combined verdict — and it is the same ruling the payment-rails handoff opens as Q1.)*
2. **Is the 30-to-50-percent produce-price fall net of the platform's own take?** The produce record removes 4 to 6 brokerage layers and forecasts a 30-to-50-percent final price cut; the settlement and marketplace fees that would replace the brokers' margins are priced in another document ([Payment Rails and Service Charging](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md)), whose revenue lines are invoice fees and dispute charges. Net or gross? *(Why: if net, the platform's take is bounded at zero margin and the rails' revenue model breaks; if gross, the brokers are replaced rather than removed and the flaw text describes the wrong villain. Blocking: yes for the produce record's headline metric.)*
3. **Who mandates the referee?** The housing record vests neutrality in "software sovereignty" but names no institution, term, audit or appeal path; its dispute synergy points at the judiciary folder's online-ADR record (an idea of that document, linked, never assumed). What governance organ makes and enforces the referee's neutrality — and can it fire the algorithm? *(Why: "neutral" is a status the society must grant and police, not an adjective the platform may self-assign; the requirement is incomplete without the host organ. Blocking: yes for the housing record's implementation claim.)*
4. **Reviewer critique — a non-shared anchor.** The produce record's recorded [Cognitive anchor:] is European farmers' markets — not a shared Iranian memory of direct produce (the plain's own memory is the field-side "haviji" sale and the neighborhood greengrocer's credit ledger). The field stands unconfirmed; a replacement would derive from the record's mechanism (Atna vine to first-floor plate). *(Why: anchors are the set's coherence device; this one imports the foreign precedent the record's own flaw text — the corrupt traditional grounds — does not need. Blocking: no.)*
5. **Reviewer critique — an unmeasured metric.** "Reduction of the cost of access to shelter" (housing record, third metric) has no unit, no baseline and no instrument; unlike its siblings (zero-percent commission, 100-percent transparency) it cannot be audited. Does the owner want a numeric target (e.g., cost-to-income ratio of obtaining a unit) or is it a direction of travel? *(Why: unmeasurable metrics silently pass verdicts; this one is the requirement's social payoff and currently unverifiable. Blocking: yes for auditing the housing record's success; no for adopting its mechanism.)*
