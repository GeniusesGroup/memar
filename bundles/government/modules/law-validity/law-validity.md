# Law Form and Validity

## Abstract
This page holds one requirement that is really two debates welded together: what a legal text must contain, and what may bind a generation that never voted for it. The weld is visible only here: [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) legislates the form — a bill is void at the door unless its text travels with a stated purpose, defined vocabulary and an explicit sanction, and it must be codeable in the platform within 30 days or it returns for revision — while two sunset answers legislate the duration: [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) expires each citizen's vote at death or emigration yet shields hard infrastructural anchors behind a prepaid Deconstruction Cost, and [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-form-and-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) goes further — the law itself survives only by the new generation's platform re-affirmation. The disagreement is not about the sentiment "no law forever": it is about whether expiry attaches to votes or to laws, whether the four-facet form that makes a statute executable can also be the form that makes it un-repealable, and which of the two sunsets is the requirement's single answer.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The three answers were authored in different rooms — a constitution draft, a village-design dialogue, the legal documents of the Persian society — and apart they look like three separate refinements: a drafting standard here, an election-database rule there. But the records leak into each other in ways no single page can hide: one sunset record carries both debates at once (its vote-expiry clause belongs to the other record's doctrine, and its anchor clause exists to protect infrastructure from both), and its Fit text ends up arguing about fibre-optic excavation — a substrate answer to a question about time. Scattered, an objection to the 30-day codeability deadline reads as an objection to binding future generations, though the two are opposite worries; kept together, the real dispute is visible — whether law is a program the living run or an inheritance the dead leave — and the shared prerequisite (a citizenship-status-aware vote database) is visible too, declared three times in three places but built nowhere on this page.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
What structure should a legal text have, and for how long may it bind future generations? The question conjoins two demands without deciding how they touch: every answer must state what a law contains and who is bound when its authors are gone. Sharpened, each record takes a position on three axes the question leaves open: form (is a statute a text, or a 4-part machine-readable instrument?); duration (does the law die with its voters' status, does the vote die while the law lives, or does the law live only through re-approval?); and substrate (what remains standing when the vote that built it is annulled?). The question favors no position — it only makes the three records answerable against one another, which their migration into a single requirement asserts was always intended.

### Approaches

#### The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)
**Mechanism:** A redefinition of what a law is: the legal text is merely a label, and it must necessarily travel with three further components — 1. the "purpose" (a root-level account of why the rule was drafted), 2. the "interpretation of terms" (a meticulous definition of every word, so that when extension is needed, only the interpretation is appended and the text of the law is never changed), and 3. an "explicit, definite enforcement guarantee". A legislative proposal that lacks an enforcement guarantee or a precise interpretation is inherently void and not eligible for review.
**Metrics:** 4 mandatory facets; a maximum 30-day deadline to implement and code the law in the sovereign platform after ratification; if coding proves impossible, the law is returned immediately for revision.
**Prerequisites:** Creation of an integrated legislative system; digital acceptance of laws on the society's platform.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Traditional lawmaking built on vague articles and rider-notes and generalities without an execution mechanism — an escape route that lets judges and officers impose their preference.
**Tags:** law engineering, philosophy of law, legislative transparency, enforcement guarantee, vocabulary interpretation
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and the Persian society
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The traditional lawmaking system is built on vague laws; imposing the transparent 4-facet framework on the existing paper-and-rent bureaucracy will meet fierce resistance from vested interests.
**Cognitive anchor:** Legal transparency in "modern Swiss constitutions" — a law with no ambiguity, whose purpose is clear and whose enforcement guarantee is arithmetic.
**Narrative hook:** "A bill without a purpose, a vocabulary and a penalty is not a bad law — it is not a law; it bounces at the door, and 30 days later the code proves whether it was ever real."

#### Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)
**Mechanism:** Each citizen's vote on laws stands valid only during that citizen's life or presence in the society; with death or emigration it is annulled, so the decisions of the past are not imposed on the new generation. At the same time, to prevent physical instability, "soft behavioural laws" are separated from "hard infrastructural anchors" (civil projects such as PRT lines), and the repeal of any hard law is conditioned on estimating and pre-funding a "restoration-to-natural-state cost" (Deconstruction Cost) fund.
**Metrics:** Instant expiry of votes upon change of citizenship status in the software; a mandatory civil-recovery financial appendix on 100% of infrastructural approvals.
**Prerequisites:** Implementation of a vote-lifetime variable in the sovereign platform's database; economic estimation models for demolition and ecological recovery.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The sanctification of rigid laws from past decades and the imposition of inefficient administrative and physical structures on new generations without a real right of revision.
**Tags:** philosophy of law, fluid democracy, infrastructural anchors, vote time-bounding, future generations' rights
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and constitutional
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Fibre-optic deployment in the existing fabric is possible, but because of repeated excavation and municipal bureaucracy it faces severe friction.
**Cognitive anchor:** The high-speed, uninterrupted service of "gigabit internet in the advanced Scandinavian countries" — hundred-metre access to information without delay.
**Narrative hook:** "The dead stop legislating the moment they die or depart — and the towers they raised stay standing, because their demolition was paid for before their first stone."
**Syn (synergies):** [Clearing the concrete slab of services: ceiling-void routing and lightweight pumice/foam-concrete floor fill for parallel phase execution](../buildings/placement-and-routing-of-service-flows.md#clearing-the-concrete-slab-of-services-ceiling-void-routing-and-lightweight-pumicefoam-concrete-floor-fill-for-parallel-phase-execution) — 4-facet law engineering; [Level-accurate slab pouring with a power trowel (5 mm tolerance): no corrective sloping screed, direct epoxy/parquet finish](../buildings/placement-and-routing-of-service-flows.md#level-accurate-slab-pouring-with-a-power-trowel-5-mm-tolerance-no-corrective-sloping-screed-direct-epoxyparquet-finish) — direct democracy; legacy-ref (unresolved) — algorithmic governance

#### Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)
**Mechanism:** Full exit from the rule of the dead over the living in the legal system of the village. The effect of every vote is conditioned directly on continuing "active citizenship": when a person's death or definitive departure is recorded, the effect of their vote is annulled from the law-validation database. Generations and new citizens stabilize past laws only through platform re-affirmation, if they choose — no law ever imposes its will on the society forever.
**Metrics:** Time of vote-weight annulment after a recorded death or departure: instant; ratification of prior approvals by the incoming generation as it reaches the age of civic maturity; complete dynamism of the law pyramid.
**Prerequisites:** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) (software direct democracy); [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) (the self-sovereign digital identity system).
**Conventional-model flaw:** The imposition of laws and decisions made centuries ago by the past on today's generations; the rigidity of traditional constitutions and the denial of the young generation's freedom to recreate their living environment.
**Tags:** direct democracy, generational expiry, sovereignty of the living, citizenship continuity, legal platform
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and the voter database
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The sunset presupposes a vote-weight database the society starts clean; in the existing fabric, statutes have hardened into titles, sentences and debts that survive their voters whatever any annulment registry says, and voiding un-reaffirmed law retroactively dissolves the certainty on which the old orders rest — the same legal crisis the judiciary records warn against for an unbacked repeal; the generational reset is workable only where the ledger of votes has not yet been written.
**Cognitive anchor:** Nowruz — every spring the old year's account closes and the living set the table anew; a Persian audience already trusts an order that renews itself once a year without a revolution.
**Narrative hook:** "No law outlives the voters who made it — when they go, every statute comes back to the living for a fresh yes."
**Syn (synergies):** [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — 4-facet law engineering; [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) — separating physical infrastructural anchors from soft laws

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) welds legal validity to engineering capacity, and the weld runs straight into the sunsets' exception clause.** A law must become code within 30 days of ratification or be suspended and returned — validity is runtime-ability. Yet [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) exists precisely to shelter the uncodeable: hard infrastructural anchors — PRT lines, civil projects — which are neither repealed in a month nor restored without the funded Deconstruction Cost. Either, then, the anchors are not "laws" in the four-facet sense (the 4-facet contract governs only the soft layer, and the split [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) names is the form debate's own hidden exemption list), or [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) cannot bind the physical layer it is later supposed to protect from transience. The composition that survives either reading is a two-tier form regime no record spells out: 4-facet statutes for behavioural law, costed anchor contracts for the built inheritance — and adjudication owes the tier boundary before either record can be enforced.

**The two sunsets are one mechanism written twice, and they disagree on what dies.** Both annul a vote's effect at death or emigration; both presuppose the same citizenship-status-aware vote database (one as a "vote-lifetime variable", the other as the law-validation registry). But [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) stops there: the law stands on the remaining voters, and what is specially protected is the physical anchor, repeals of which must be prepaid. [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-form-and-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) moves the expiry from votes to laws: past statutes are stabilized only by the new generation's re-affirmation, down to each person ratifying them at the age of civic maturity. Under the first, a valid law can be maintained by citizens who never voted for it — their continuing assent is the default. Under the second, every inherited law is provisional — silence is not assent, and the pyramid never stops churning. The second reading also quietly unmakes the first's shelter: if laws lapse until re-affirmed, do the anchors they approved lapse with them, or is a bridge the only inheritance that outlives its ratifiers?

**One decision with two answers: does temporal validity attach to votes or to laws — and is this page holding one sunset or two?** The vote-lapse answer treats [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-form-and-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) as an extension of [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws): the requirement's rule is instant annulment of a departed voter's weight; the generational re-affirmation clause becomes a discretionary deepening that may be adopted or struck, and law stays law while the living maintain it — the stable reading for a society that also wants [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction)'s 30-day codeability to mean anything, since code that must be rewritten every generation is never finished. The law-lapse answer treats [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) as the half-measure: its flaw text already indicts inherited structures binding the young "without a real right of revision", which is a claim about laws, not about votes' arithmetic — and under the second answer the vote-lifetime database is merely the instrument of generational sovereignty, whose verdict is that every inherited statute (anchors excepted, if the Deconstruction Cost says so) returns to the ballot. The evidence that tips direction is buried in [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-form-and-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty)'s metrics, where ratification "by the incoming generation at civic maturity" appears: an audit trail of re-affirmations either exists as a legal requirement or the two records collapse into one — and only the owner's ruling on which object expires decides whether this document ships one sunset or a doctrine of two.
