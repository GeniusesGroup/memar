# Law Form and Validity

## Abstract
This page holds one requirement that is really two debates welded together: what a legal text must contain, and what may bind a generation that never voted for it. The weld is visible only here: [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) legislates the form — a bill is void at the door unless its text travels with a stated purpose, defined vocabulary and an explicit sanction — while two sunset answers legislate the duration: [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) expires each citizen's vote at death or emigration yet shields hard infrastructural anchors behind a prepaid Deconstruction Cost, and [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-form-and-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) goes further — the law itself survives only by the new generation's platform re-affirmation. The disagreement is not about the sentiment "no law forever": information originates in the vote (death or emigration lapses that citizen's weight) and also attaches to the law. The two sunset records are one doctrine with two attachment points, not rival answers. The 30-day code-or-return clock recorded in the four-facet metrics is not current owner doctrine. A record may enter voting only when it is complete, including its executable code; an empty code field keeps it in draft. Once in voting, it may remain there as long as the community keeps it there. "Constitution" is a label that categorises a law, not a higher tree that later laws must uniquely obey; the useful constraint is that a new law must not contradict an existing one. Civic maturity is an event — accepting the laws, joining — not a calendar age. Silence is not assent: inherited law needs an affirmative act.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The three answers were authored in different rooms — a constitution draft, a village-design dialogue, the legal documents of the Persian society — and apart they look like three separate refinements: a drafting standard here, an election-database rule there. But the records leak into each other in ways no single page can hide: one sunset record carries both debates at once (its vote-expiry clause belongs to the other record's doctrine, and its anchor clause exists to protect infrastructure from both). Scattered, an objection to the 30-day codeability deadline reads as an objection to binding future generations, though the two are opposite worries; kept together, the real dispute is visible — whether law is a program the living run or an inheritance the dead leave — and the shared prerequisite (a citizenship-status-aware vote database) is visible too, declared three times in three places but built nowhere on this page.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
What structure should a legal text have, and for how long may it bind future generations? The question conjoins two demands without deciding how they touch: every answer must state what a law contains and who is bound when its authors are gone. Sharpened, each record takes a position on three axes the question leaves open: form (is a statute a text, or a 4-part machine-readable instrument?); duration (does the law die with its voters' status, does the vote die while the law lives, or does the law live only through re-approval?); and substrate (what remains standing when the vote that built it is annulled?). The question favors no position — it only makes the three records answerable against one another, which their migration into a single requirement asserts was always intended.

### Approaches

#### The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)
**Mechanism:** A redefinition of what a law is: the legal text is merely a label, and it must necessarily travel with three further components — 1. the "purpose" (a root-level account of why the rule was drafted), 2. the "interpretation of terms" (a meticulous definition of every word, so that when extension is needed, only the interpretation is appended and the text of the law is never changed), and 3. an "explicit, definite enforcement guarantee". A legislative proposal that lacks an enforcement guarantee or a precise interpretation is inherently void and not eligible for review.
**Metrics:** 4 mandatory facets; a maximum 30-day deadline to implement and code the law in the sovereign platform after ratification; if coding proves impossible, the law is returned immediately for revision.
**Prerequisites:** Creation of an integrated legislative system; digital acceptance of laws on the society's platform.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Traditional lawmaking built on vague articles and rider-notes and generalities without an execution mechanism — an escape route that lets judges and officers impose their preference.
**Tags:** law engineering, philosophy of law, legislative transparency, enforcement guarantee, vocabulary interpretation
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and the Persian society
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The traditional lawmaking system is built on vague laws; imposing the transparent 4-facet framework on the existing paper-and-rent bureaucracy will meet fierce resistance from vested interests.
**Cognitive anchor:** Legal transparency in "modern Swiss constitutions" — a law with no ambiguity, whose purpose is clear and whose enforcement guarantee is arithmetic.
**Narrative hook:** "A bill without a purpose, a vocabulary and a penalty is not a bad law — it is not a law; it bounces at the door, and 30 days later the code proves whether it was ever real."
**Syn (synergies):** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — direct democracy; [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) — the platform executive; [3-stage platform adjudication with no physical judiciary, judged by a bench of 50 senior judges](judiciary-and-dispute.md#3-stage-platform-adjudication-with-no-physical-judiciary-judged-by-a-bench-of-50-senior-judges) — platform adjudication

#### Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)
**Mechanism:** Each citizen's vote on laws stands valid only during that citizen's life or presence in the society; with death or emigration it is annulled, so the decisions of the past are not imposed on the new generation. At the same time, to prevent physical instability, "soft behavioural laws" are separated from "hard infrastructural anchors" (civil projects such as PRT lines), and the repeal of any hard law is conditioned on estimating and pre-funding a "restoration-to-natural-state cost" (Deconstruction Cost) fund.
**Metrics:** Instant expiry of votes upon change of citizenship status in the software; a mandatory civil-recovery financial appendix on 100% of infrastructural approvals.
**Prerequisites:** Implementation of a vote-lifetime variable in the sovereign platform's database; economic estimation models for demolition and ecological recovery.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The sanctification of rigid laws from past decades and the imposition of inefficient administrative and physical structures on new generations without a real right of revision.
**Tags:** philosophy of law, fluid democracy, infrastructural anchors, vote time-bounding, future generations' rights
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and constitutional
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Vote-weight can be bound to living citizenship wherever a status-aware registry exists; the existing statute book need not be voided for the vote-lapse half to run. The Deconstruction Cost appendix can attach to new infrastructural approvals without first rewriting inherited titles. What the existing fabric resists is the registry itself — death and emigration are not currently events that touch the validity of a vote — and the funded-repeal discipline, which no conventional capital-project law requires before the first stone.
**Cognitive anchor:** An inherited title deed — the paper still binds after the signatory is gone, which is the rigidity the vote-lapse half undoes, while the building the deed describes stays until someone has paid to take it down.
**Narrative hook:** "The dead stop legislating the moment they die or depart — and the towers they raised stay standing, because their demolition was paid for before their first stone."
**Syn (synergies):** [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — 4-facet law engineering; [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — direct democracy; [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) — algorithmic governance
**Source:** Constitution.docx | National Constitution.odt

#### Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)
**Mechanism:** Full exit from the rule of the dead over the living in the legal system of the village. The effect of every vote is conditioned directly on continuing "active citizenship": when a person's death or definitive departure is recorded, the effect of their vote is annulled from the law-validation database. Generations and new citizens stabilize past laws only through platform re-affirmation, if they choose — no law ever imposes its will on the society forever.
**Metrics:** Time of vote-weight annulment after a recorded death or departure: instant; ratification of prior approvals by the incoming generation as it reaches the age of civic maturity; complete dynamism of the law pyramid.
**Prerequisites:** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) (software direct democracy); [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) (the self-sovereign digital identity system).
**Conventional-model flaw:** The imposition of laws and decisions made centuries ago by the past on today's generations; the rigidity of traditional constitutions and the denial of the young generation's freedom to recreate their living environment.
**Tags:** direct democracy, generational expiry, sovereignty of the living, citizenship continuity, legal platform
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and the voter database
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The sunset presupposes a vote-weight database the society starts clean; in the existing fabric, statutes have hardened into titles, sentences and debts that survive their voters whatever any annulment registry says, and voiding un-reaffirmed law retroactively dissolves the certainty on which the old orders rest — the same legal crisis the judiciary records warn against for an unbacked repeal; the generational reset is workable only where the ledger of votes has not yet been written.
**Cognitive anchor:** Nowruz — every spring the old year's account closes and the living set the table anew; a Persian audience already trusts an order that renews itself once a year without a revolution.
**Narrative hook:** "No law outlives the voters who made it — when they go, every statute comes back to the living for a fresh yes."
**Syn (synergies):** [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — 4-facet law engineering; [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) — separating physical infrastructural anchors from soft laws

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) still requires purpose, vocabulary and sanction; executable code is a gate into voting, not a 30-day after-clock.** The four-facet metrics still *propose* a 30-day code-or-return deadline. That clock is not current owner doctrine. A record cannot leave draft for voting while its executable code field is empty. Once voting has begun, it may last as long as the community keeps it there. [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) still shelters hard infrastructural anchors behind Deconstruction Cost; that shelter is about repeal of the built inheritance, not about a coding deadline. "Constitution" here is a category label on a law, not a superior tree; new law must not contradict existing law.

**The two sunsets are one mechanism written twice, and they disagree on what dies.** Both annul a vote's effect at death or emigration; both presuppose the same citizenship-status-aware vote database (one as a "vote-lifetime variable", the other as the law-validation registry). But [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) stops there: the physical anchor is specially protected, repeals of which must be prepaid. Continuing-assent-by-silence is not current doctrine. [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-form-and-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) moves the expiry from votes to laws: past statutes are stabilized only by the new generation's re-affirmation. Civic maturity is the event of accepting the laws or joining, not a birthday. Silence is not assent: a living person is not bound by remaining quiet. The Deconstruction Cost shelter for hard anchors still stands; it is about the built inheritance, not about quiet consent to statutes.

**One doctrine, two attachment points: information originates in the vote and also attaches to the law.** The either/or — votes versus laws, one sunset versus two — is closed. [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-form-and-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) supplies the vote-lapse half and the Deconstruction Cost shelter for hard anchors. [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-form-and-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) supplies the law-attachment half. They are not rival answers a citizen must choose between. Civic maturity is an event (accepting the laws / joining), not a calendar age. Silence is not assent.
