Blocking: yes for the combined verdict; no for the citizenship record tried alone.)*
2. **Which comes first, membership or identity — and what breaks the prerequisite loop?** Each record lists the other among its prerequisites (the citizenship framework needs the identity-verification platform; the identity system needs the citizenship framework, cited twice as migrated, plus the platform encryption). Is the society bootstrapped from a political act (accepting laws, verified how?) or a cryptographic act (an attestation issued by whom?) *(Why: the loop is doctrinal, not just technical — it is the same question as Q1 in miniature, and no deployment plan can satisfy both readings at once. Blocking: yes for both records' implementability; no for the requirement itself.)*
3. **Who decides "persons possessed of intelligence," and does the definition include the synthetic mind?** The citizenship metric — equal citizenship for all persons possessed of intelligence — reads on [Synthetic mind as neutral advisor and civic-education facilitator in legislation (Synthetic Mind as Neutral Civic Advisor)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#synthetic-mind-as-neutral-advisor-and-civic-education-facilitator-in-legislation-synthetic-mind-as-neutral-civic-advisor)'s insistence that synthetic agents hold no vote: if a processing agent qualifies under the citizenship clause, the two governance pages contradict each other; if not, the clause needs the criterion it does not state. *(Why: it is the most radical line in either record and the only place in the set where personhood is defined by property rather than procedure. Blocking: yes for the citizenship record's verdict; yes for the advisor record's consistency check.)*
4. **Is the attested expertise a ranking once the neighbors consume it?** The identity system attests expertise; [Advisory signalling, following expert opinions, and transparent recording of vote reasoning (Opt-in Public Staking & Expert Following)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#advisory-signalling-following-expert-opinions-and-transparent-recording-of-vote-reasoning-opt-in-public-staking--expert-following) ranks commentators by prediction accuracy, and [Skill-diversity and civic-resilience balancing in Dunbar matching (Dunbar Skill-Diversity Entropy Matching)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#skill-diversity-and-civic-resilience-balancing-in-dunbar-matching-dunbar-skill-diversity-entropy-matching) allocates moves by skill quota. Attestation that feeds ranking is, functionally, the file the identity record forbids. *(Why: three documents share one registry and disagree about what may be read from it; the answer belongs to this page because this page owns the promise. Blocking: yes for the combined identity verdict; no for the two consuming records' internal logic.)*
5. **Keep the owner's later no-ranking position, or restore the source's demotion incentive?** The recovered audit shows [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) records the opposite of what the National Constitution prescribes: «هر شخص حقیقی یا حقوقی دارای چند رتبه شهروندی همانند رتبه اجتماعی و مالی دارد که توسط میزان فعالیت و شاخص های دیگر مشخص می شود» — every natural and legal person holds several citizenship ranks (social and financial) determined by activity and other indicators — with the guarantee «عدم شرکت اعضا در رای گیری قوانین جامعه باعث کاهش رتبه اجتماعی و مالی فرد می شود», abstention from law-votes demotes the member's social and financial rank. Nothing has been flipped: the identity record stands as migrated. Answer one keeps the no-ranking doctrine (the body unchanged) and treats the constitution's tier clause as superseded by the owner's later Yalda-side position; answer two restores the demotion incentive, which would amend the identity record and rewrite the counting rules on the deliberation page (the resident tally and monthly count that the deliberation page’s voting record now carries were recovered from the same source). *(Why it matters: demotion for abstention is the strongest turnout engine in the source set — direct democracy's 50-percent-of-residents quanta depend on residents actually being residents — and it is also a graded, activity-derived file on every citizen, the exact mechanism the identity record's zero-file metric exists to forbid; the page cannot have both without scoping the score. Blocking: yes for the membership page verdict — it decides the identity record's fate and, through the vote quanta, the deliberation page's.)*
