# Membership and Identity

## Abstract
Two records answer one question — how to define membership and identity so they stay safe from ranking and surveillance — and they divide the protection differently: [Smart citizenship, maximal liberty, and community autonomy over service protocols](membership-and-identity.md#smart-citizenship-maximal-liberty-and-community-autonomy-over-service-protocols) draws the line against the center — every sentient being that accepts the laws holds permanent citizenship, macro law may never compel a lifestyle, and no organization may force its services on anyone — while placing the power to choose one's company in every building community, which may set its own entry and service rules. [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) draws the line around the person — a self-sovereign identity on zero-knowledge proofs attests voting eligibility, expertise and citizenship rights and nobody's eye may track behavior, belief or dress. Together they define the society's security apparatus negatively and completely; apart, they collide: entry rules are rankings, expertise attestations are credentials, and a community's right to exclude is precisely the discipline the identity record was built to make impossible — with each record listing the other among its prerequisites, and no owner ruling yet on which side of the person the no-ranking wall stands.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The two records arrive from the same constitution family — one from its liberty clauses, one from its platform clauses — and each reads, alone, as an unimpeachable rights statement: freedom from compelled lifestyle; freedom from behavioral scoring. Met in one place they stop being slogans: the freedom of the first is the power of every community to refuse whom it will, and the freedom of the second is a cryptographic guarantee that the same person's file is nowhere. A critic of community gatekeeping must then be heard as attacking membership itself, and a critic of attestation-only identity must be heard as attacking the anti-surveillance clause — unless the page shows both claims as positions on one question: who is allowed to know enough about you to say no to you. That is a decision, not a definition, and it is the one neither record states.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How are membership and identity defined so they stay safe from ranking and surveillance? The question picks neither the defender nor the boundary point; every answer to it must fix three things the records leave open: entry (who counts as a citizen, and who decides who joins them); attributes (what an identity may prove — eligibility, expertise, rights — and what it must never prove); and reach (whether the prohibition on ranking binds only the center or every institution, including the smallest community, since a ranking enforced nowhere is surveillance renamed as choice). The question itself favors neither placement of the wall; it only refuses to let "freedom" be declared twice on opposite sides of the same fence.

### Approaches

#### Smart citizenship, maximal liberty, and community autonomy over service protocols
**Mechanism:** Every sentient being is recognized, upon acceptance of the laws, as a permanent citizen. Individual freedoms are held at the highest level at the scale of macro law, and the society's law has no right to compel a lifestyle (such as general compulsory vaccination or a particular dress code). Instead, every building community, within its own geography, may set the rules for admission or for providing services — provided that no organization has the right to compel an individual to use its services.
**Metrics:** Equal citizenship for all persons possessed of intelligence; a 100% prohibition of service monopolies at the macro level; no deprivation of citizenship on account of lifestyle.
**Prerequisites:** The society's intelligent identity-verification platform; digital registration of residency and citizenship-membership contracts.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Uniform state compulsions that suppress cultural difference and, instead of accepting social plurality, manufacture violent conflicts.
**Tags:** individual liberty, smart citizenship, community autonomy, citizenship rights, prohibition of monopoly
**Scale:** Macro — the Persian society
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | Granting community autonomy in a traditional authoritarian settlement meets institutional resistance and requires a legal transition period.
**Cognitive anchor:** Freedom of action in "self-governing quarters of the world's free cities" — where each building chooses a way of life fitted to the wisdom of its residents.
**Narrative hook:** "Accept the laws and you belong forever: no rule tells you how to live, and no provider tells you that you must buy."
**Syn (synergies):** [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](../economy/land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) — the ownership of communities; [Co-living architecture with 25 square meters of private and 20 square meters of communal service space per resident](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#co-living-architecture-with-25-square-meters-of-private-and-20-square-meters-of-communal-service-space-per-resident) — co-living on shared taste; the ecological diversity of lifestyles
**Source:** National Constitution.odt

#### A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)
**Mechanism:** An identity system built on the self-sovereign identity (SSI) protocol with advanced zero-knowledge-proof (ZKP) cryptography. Unlike systems of social control and state disciplinary ranking, the structure attests only voting eligibility, expertise and citizenship rights — never granting anyone permission to track personal, ideological or private-lifestyle behavior.
**Metrics:** 100% supervision and ownership of identity data by the person; zero percent possibility of file-building or misuse of citizens' records.
**Prerequisites:** [Smart citizenship, maximal liberty, and community autonomy over service protocols](membership-and-identity.md#smart-citizenship-maximal-liberty-and-community-autonomy-over-service-protocols) / [Smart citizenship, maximal liberty, and community autonomy over service protocols](membership-and-identity.md#smart-citizenship-maximal-liberty-and-community-autonomy-over-service-protocols); the society platform's encryption infrastructure.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Centralized state police-surveillance and social-credit systems that abolish privacy and, by grading behaviors, suppress individual freedoms.
**Tags:** self-sovereign identity, privacy, SSI, civil liberties, zero-knowledge-proof cryptography
**Scale:** Macro — society and platform
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | An SSI stack can be layered on today's credential issuers — a person can hold a self-owned wallet without anyone's leave — but the no-ranking promise binds only where verifiers honor the zero-knowledge discipline: in the existing fabric, the same wallet meets gatekeepers who demand full records, and selective disclosure dies at the first service that refuses a partial proof; the system is therefore enforceable only within the platform that writes its own enrollment rules.
**Cognitive anchor:** The family shenasnameh kept under the household's own lock — the paper proved who you were, and no outside eye could read it without the house's leave.
**Narrative hook:** "Prove you may vote, and prove you are the surgeon — without telling anyone where you slept, what you believed, or what you wore."
**Syn (synergies):** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — secure direct voting; [Level-accurate slab pouring with a power trowel (5 mm tolerance): no corrective sloping screed, direct epoxy/parquet finish](../buildings/placement-and-routing-of-service-flows.md#level-accurate-slab-pouring-with-a-power-trowel-5-mm-tolerance-no-corrective-sloping-screed-direct-epoxyparquet-finish) (preserved as migrated); [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](../economy/payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) — the secure wallet; legacy-ref (unresolved)

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The wall against ranking is built on the person, but one record puts it at the center and the other leaves it open at the edge.** [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) forbids tracking behavior, belief or dress — a prohibition addressed to whoever holds the identity system. [Smart citizenship, maximal liberty, and community autonomy over service protocols](membership-and-identity.md#smart-citizenship-maximal-liberty-and-community-autonomy-over-service-protocols) then grants every building community the right to set the rules for admission — and admission rules are rankings applied to persons by institutions smaller than the state, which is exactly the grain at which a lifestyle becomes knowable. The composition these two make, read as one design, is a distribution of surveillance risk: the center is blind (attestation-only identity), the edges are free to choose (entry protocols), and the citizen's protection is exit — the mobility that governance/resident-matching-and-mobility.md carries. The collision is what no record prices: cumulative exclusion. A community cannot compel you to use its services, but a thousand communities can each refuse you, and the right that survives at every single gate can vanish at all of them together. Nothing in either text caps the aggregate, and nothing says whether a credential — expertise attested by [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity)'s own mechanism — becomes a ranking once communities are free to weigh it.

**Each record lists the other as its foundation, so the page has a dependency loop where the ledger has a sequencing problem.** [Smart citizenship...](membership-and-identity.md#smart-citizenship-maximal-liberty-and-community-autonomy-over-service-protocols) needs "the society's intelligent identity-verification platform" to know who has accepted the laws; [A self-sovereign citizen identity...](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) lists the citizenship framework (twice, as its migrated prerequisite line preserves) plus the platform's encryption as what makes attestation lawful. Neither can be deployed before the other exists, and the loop is not merely technical: it is the doctrinal question of whether membership (the political act of accepting laws) or identity (the cryptographic act of proving who accepts) is the primitive the society bootstraps from. The records also disagree in silence about the person's data once attestation happens: the citizenship record's digital residency contracts and the identity record's zero-file promise share one registry, and the migration left the two records' synergy lines pointing at wallets and slabs without ever saying which registry is the citizen's and which is the community's.

**One decision with two answers: does the prohibition on ranking bind only the center, or every institution that can read a name at a door?** The center-only answer reads [Smart citizenship...](membership-and-identity.md#smart-citizenship-maximal-liberty-and-community-autonomy-over-service-protocols) as the governing frame: the state may not compel lifestyles or score citizens; communities choose their residents the way any household chooses its guests, and the anti-compulsion clause plus the mobility instruments make exclusion survivable rather than fatal; on this reading [A self-sovereign citizen identity...](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) must attest and never publish scores, and community-level ranking is simply pluralism priced honestly. The universal answer reads the identity record's flaw text as principle, not address: behavioral grading is the evil, wherever the grader sits, and a thousand private registers of who you are is one disciplinary system wearing distributed faces; the "zero percent file-building" metric carries no issuer qualifier to license the edge exception; on this reading the community right to admit shrinks to rules of conduct inside a service contract, never to categories of person. Under the first answer the two records are independent and the loop is engineering; under the second, [Smart citizenship...](membership-and-identity.md#smart-citizenship-maximal-liberty-and-community-autonomy-over-service-protocols)'s entry clause is the amendment that must be written before [A self-sovereign citizen identity...](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) can be deployed anywhere a citizen might knock.
