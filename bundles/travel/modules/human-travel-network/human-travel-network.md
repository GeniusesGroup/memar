# Human Travel Network

## Abstract
Five records answer one question — by what mix of modes and network shall people move within and between the society — and together they build a single ladder from the dwelling suite to the nation: cable-free magnetic cabins that turn horizontally out of their shafts into the aerial network ([Cable-free multi-directional magnetic elevators (3D Cable-less Maglev Shuttles)](human-travel-network.md#cable-free-multi-directional-magnetic-elevators-3d-cable-less-maglev-shuttles)), suspended personal rapid transit at roughly 9 meters above ground with the car street deleted ([Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop)), tubed inter-cellular maglev corridors at 200 to 600 km/h replacing highway widening ([Ultra-fast tubed maglev corridors between cells (Inter-Cellular Vacuum Maglev Grid)](human-travel-network.md#ultra-fast-tubed-maglev-corridors-between-cells-inter-cellular-vacuum-maglev-grid)), and a five-second glass shaft lifting a boat passenger from deck level to a first-floor capsule platform at +6 meters ([Multimodal boat-to-PRT transfer shafts clear of the ground floor (Vertical Waterway-to-Skytran Multimodal Shafts)](human-travel-network.md#multimodal-boat-to-prt-transfer-shafts-clear-of-the-ground-floor-vertical-waterway-to-skytran-multimodal-shafts)). The ladder is the claim; its one loose rung is altitude. Three records board at the first floor (+6 meters, at the piloti and station level the plan gives the station tier); the aerial record runs its lines at 9 meters. One of those numbers is the network's boarding band, and the ledger has not yet chosen which. A fifth record stands under the whole ladder rather than on it: a network that signals the identity of every vehicle, even a bicycle, and uses that legibility to carry the movement of disabled and blind residents through every part of the settlement ([An identity-signaled network that manages every vehicle and assists the movement of disabled and blind residents (Assisted Accessibility Movement)](human-travel-network.md#an-identity-signaled-network-that-manages-every-vehicle-and-assists-the-movement-of-disabled-and-blind-residents-assisted-accessibility-movement)).

## Introduction

### Motivation
A reader of the aerial record alone learns that the network boards at 9 meters and connects "directly to the service floors"; a reader of the waterway record alone learns the Skytran boarding platform is at +6 meters and the cross-network record alone hands cabins to the aerial system "after reaching the first floor." Filed apart, the settlement is planned at three altitudes that no single audit will notice disagree — and every transfer-time and capacity number in the set is computed against a boarding height. The records also quote across the ledger: the elevator synergy line carries an unmapped label, "جامعه سیال," whose owner record has not yet been identified, and the aerial, grid and shaft records presuppose, respectively, the entry-gate parking hubs of the boundary requirement, the mechanized logistics depot of the goods requirement, and the canal-and-piloti substrate of the plane-allocation requirement. Keeping the four answers on one page is what makes the ladder's one seam — and its three borrowed premises — visible in a single reading.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
By what mix of modes and network shall people move within and between the society? The recorded answers differ on three axes: which range of trip each mode serves (inside the block, inside the settlement, between cells, across the country), which layer carries it (in-shaft vertical, +6-meter aerial, 9-meter guideway, ground waterway), and whether the traveler ever touches a street at all. The requirement itself — movement without the conventional car-dominated street — is not any of the answers; it survives whichever ladder is built.

### Approaches

#### Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)
**Mechanism:** Complete elimination of conventional car streets and lanes inside the living fabric of the society. The movement of people and goods is carried by aerial personal rapid transit systems (PRT) on magnetic levitation or self-guided automatic capsules (such as SkyTran or hyperloop pads), built on light concrete guideways suspended at a height of about 9 meters above ground. These lines connect directly to the service floors of the buildings, bringing three-dimensional, frictionless transport to pass.
**Metrics:** PRT guideway height above ground: about 9 meters; share of conventional asphalt lanes in the settlement: zero percent; delay-free, traffic-free service capacity to 50 thousand residents in 100 hectares.
**Prerequisites:** A stable solar-powered electrical supply network; multi-purpose stations connected into the building bodies; an algorithmic automatic-guidance system.
**Conventional-model flaw:** 25 to 30 percent of the total area of modern conventional settlements wasted on car lanes, billions of hours lost in traffic, pollution from tire friction and exhaust-heat emission.
**Tags:** aerial transport, PRT, SkyTran, hyperloop, elimination of the conventional car, sharing economy
**Scale:** Inter-building connective level / macro — settlement
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional instrument] | In the new settlement the continuous artery works; in the traditional fabric, bringing these lines into the alleys violates the sight-privacy of windows and devalues adjacent property. In the existing fabric the idea is implementable only as a transitional instrument, confined to the right-of-way of the wide highways and the arterial ring roads.
**Cognitive anchor:** The calm and silence of "the Tochal or Namak Abroud cable car," minus the stop-and-sway and the wind: a fast, silent flight at 100 kilometers an hour in a personal capsule, above the locked traffic.
**Narrative hook:** You loved the cable car's glide — we took away the swaying stop and the wind, and pointed it straight at your door at a hundred an hour.
**Syn (synergies):** [Centralized long-dormancy parking hubs at the entry gates with compact 3-car-deep stacking](transport-boundary-interface.md#centralized-long-dormancy-parking-hubs-at-the-entry-gates-with-compact-3-car-deep-stacking) — entry-gate parking; [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](../form-and-land/ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — no destruction of the soil beneath; [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — freeing 25% of the ground from asphalt and returning it to nature
**Source:** فیلم نامه.docx | Yalda Town.docx | New Text Document.txt

#### Cable-free multi-directional magnetic elevators (3D Cable-less Maglev Shuttles)
**Mechanism:** Conventional cable elevators are the bottleneck of movement between the 20 building levels (10 floors up and 10 floors down), and require exchanging cabins at the first-floor level to board the PRT. With a magnetic-levitation elevator system (such as ThyssenKrupp MULTI) several cabins move in one shaft, and a cabin, after reaching the first floor, can enter the connection bridge horizontally and join the PRT network directly — a three-dimensional circulation from suite to destination.
**Metrics:** 50 percent increase in the passenger-carrying capacity of the shafts; reduction of the elevator core's footprint in the plan by up to 30 percent; resident wait time for a cabin: under 15 seconds.
**Prerequisites:** A rigid central-core structure; inductive power feeding of the maglev rails; software coordination of cabin navigation with the PRT system.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Time wasted in lobbies, repeated line changes, and the occupation of valuable floor plate by the multiple parallel hoistways of conventional traction cables.
**Tags:** multi-directional elevator, maglev technology, vertical-horizontal traffic continuity, MULTI, elimination of the elevator bottleneck
**Scale:** Micro — building up to the inter-building level
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The system needs the building the ladder already assumes — a rigid core whose shafts are loops sized for traveling rails and induction supply, opening laterally onto a bridge at the first floor. The existing tower has rope-sized hoistways, a machine house and counterweight pit; converting them means rebuilding the core, i.e., gutting the building around a shaft that cannot be widened, so the device is a frame-erection choice, not a lift modernization.
**Cognitive anchor:** The first childhood ride on "the airport moving walkway" — standing still, and being carried sideways; the novelty that made the whole terminal feel like a machine that served you.
**Narrative hook:** The airport taught us to be carried sideways without moving — now the cabin learns the trick, and turns it into the elevator shaft.
**Syn (synergies):** [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — fast aerial transport; [Vertical zoning of 10 basement levels (Deep Subsurface Clustering) and 35-meter penetration](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#vertical-zoning-of-10-basement-levels-deep-subsurface-clustering-and-35-meter-penetration) — fast access from basement levels 10 to floor +10; legacy-ref (unresolved) — جامعه سیال
**Source:** supplementary idea of the systems architect — 3-dimensional integration of traffic

#### Ultra-fast tubed maglev corridors between cells (Inter-Cellular Vacuum Maglev Grid)
**Mechanism:** Replacing the expensive mega-projects of widening multi-lane asphalt highways with a network of high-speed maglev/hyperloop tubes and rails between the 100-hectare biological cells. Communication between cells, and their connection to the parent cities beyond, is made exclusively by magnetically suspended cars on narrow elevated rail paths. At their entry level these lines connect directly to the depot-distribution system and to the PRT lines; frictionless and with no damage to the soil of the plain, they move 50 thousand passengers between cells in less than 10 minutes.
**Metrics:** Corridor speed: 200 to 600 kilometers per hour; energy per passenger-kilometer: 80 percent below road cars; land occupied by the path: 90 percent less than a 6-lane freeway.
**Prerequisites:** [Cellular territorial planning and a self-sufficient biological archipelago instead of megapolitan concentration (Cellular Bioregional Planning)](../form-and-land/settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#cellular-territorial-planning-and-a-self-sufficient-biological-archipelago-instead-of-megapolitan-concentration-cellular-bioregional-planning) (territorial cellular planning), [The centralized photovoltaic mega-plant and the clean energy-pump hub (Clean Energy Station)](../energy/large-generation-site.md#the-centralized-photovoltaic-mega-plant-and-the-clean-energy-pump-hub-clean-energy-station) (the macro solar power grid), integration of the fleet's automatic-guidance software protocols.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Heavy investment in asphalt road infrastructure, deadly highway accidents, wasted fossil fuel, and the wide highways slicing natural ecosystems to pieces.
**Tags:** high-speed rail transport, maglev, hyperloop, elimination of asphalt freeways, national PRT corridor
**Scale:** Macro — national and territorial transport network
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | A narrow elevated corridor is an acquisition bargain struck on open plain between cells; imposed on the existing fabric, the same line becomes a viaduct above crowded streets — spending its 90 percent land saving on expropriated frontage and casting the walls and shadows its aerial sibling's Fit text already warns of. Its entry-level handoffs to the depot grid and the PRT also presuppose the cell-edge topology the replacing fabric will have; the replaced one has none to give.
**Cognitive anchor:** The window of "the overnight train from Tehran to Mashhad" — fields and villages sliding past at dusk while the family slept and woke in the same moving room.
**Narrative hook:** The night train already gave you a moving room between two cities; this gives you the next cell in ten minutes.
**Syn (synergies):** [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — the intra-settlement PRT system; [Mechanized warehousing and logistics depot at the settlement's perimeter edge (Smart Automated Logistics Hub)](last-mile-of-goods.md#mechanized-warehousing-and-logistics-depot-at-the-settlements-perimeter-edge-smart-automated-logistics-hub) — the entry mechanized depot hubs; [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](../form-and-land/ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — protecting the soil bed from asphalt
**Source:** supplementary idea of the systems architect — geopolitics of sustainable transport

#### Multimodal boat-to-PRT transfer shafts clear of the ground floor (Vertical Waterway-to-Skytran Multimodal Shafts)
**Mechanism:** With waterways passing at the foot of the buildings and the PRT station set at a height of 6 meters, passengers arriving by light boat at the building edge must not cross ground-floor pedestrian traffic to reach the fast aerial network. A cylindrical glass shaft is installed at the waterway margin which, with continuously circulating open cabins, carries a person within 5 seconds directly from the boat's deck level (level zero) up to the Skytran boarding platform on the first floor (+6 meters).
**Metrics:** Transfer time between the water and the aerial rail network: under 10 seconds; vertical transfer capacity: up to 1200 passengers per hour per shaft; electricity consumed, from complete counterweight balancing: under 0.2 kWh per person.
**Prerequisites:** [Block datacenter: distributed compute and data storage on one floor of every block's ten basements, in opposition to the centralized megadatacenter](../information-systems/data-storage-and-processing.md#block-datacenter-distributed-compute-and-data-storage-on-one-floor-of-every-blocks-ten-basements-in-opposition-to-the-centralized-megadatacenter) (the boat-navigation channels between the 100 blocks), [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) (the aerial PRT network), [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) (the first floor as station level).
**Conventional-model flaw:** Conventional transport hubs designed around hundreds of meters of exhausting horizontal walking, waiting escalators, and complex, time-consuming line changes.
**Tags:** vertical multimodality, boat navigation, PRT capsule, paternoster lift, two-layer settlement
**Scale:** Micro — building (central shaft between level zero and the +6-meter level)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The device stands where the boat meets the building — it needs the water at the foot, the open 6-meter ground-floor band its station level borrows, and the aerial network overhead, all at once; a settlement that has none of these offers it nothing to bind. In the existing fabric its parts arrive piecemeal only by wholesale rebuild: canals dug to block edges and pilotis cut under standing towers is the demolition the ladder exists to retire.
**Cognitive anchor:** The jetties of "the Anzali lagoon boats and the Karun ferries of Ahvaz" — stepping off the deck into the next ride within seconds, with no terminal to walk.
**Narrative hook:** The boatmen never made you walk a terminal — five seconds from deck to capsule is the same courtesy, kept.
**Syn (synergies):** [Safe pedestrian deck on the roofs of the PRT guideways (Elevated Guideway Pedestrian Deck)](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#safe-pedestrian-deck-on-the-roofs-of-the-prt-guideways-elevated-guideway-pedestrian-deck) — the pedestrian deck atop the PRT lines; [Centralized long-dormancy parking hubs at the entry gates with compact 3-car-deep stacking](transport-boundary-interface.md#centralized-long-dormancy-parking-hubs-at-the-entry-gates-with-compact-3-car-deep-stacking) — the elimination of every vehicle at the ground floor
**Source:** supplementary idea of the systems architect — vertical logistics and integrated multi-level transport

#### An identity-signaled network that manages every vehicle and assists the movement of disabled and blind residents (Assisted Accessibility Movement)
**Mechanism:** Make the network legible to the body that cannot read the street. Every vehicle in the settlement — «هر وسیله حتی دوچرخه» — even a bicycle «می بایستی دارای سیگنال هویتی باشد», must carry an identity signal, and the network controls passage «به صورت هوشمند و شبیه سازی شهر», smartly and against a simulation of the settlement, not against a stop sign. On top of that signaling runs the assist layer: technologies such as RFID and Bluetooth process the settlement's information to raise the standard of living and the movement of disabled and blind people «در تمامی قسمت های شهر» — in all parts of the settlement, so that the person who cannot see the crossing, or cannot step the curb, is seen and routed by the system instead of negotiating a world built for the unimpaired.
**Metrics:** Vehicles carrying an identity signal: 100 percent — «even a bicycle»; assist coverage: all parts of the settlement for disabled and blind residents; conflict management: by simulation-driven control, not by static signage.
**Prerequisites:** [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](../governance/membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) (the identity the assist layer reads — the assist must read presence without scoring persons); the settlement simulation of the observability ledger; a signal standard for human-powered vehicles.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Streets whose only accessibility is a curb ramp at the crosswalk — information for drivers, nothing for the blind, nothing for the wheelchair — and vehicle networks that see the unmarked bicycle as an obstacle rather than a registered road user.
**Tags:** accessibility, RFID assistance, identity signaling, blind navigation, network simulation
**Scale:** Micro — street, and mid — settlement
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The assist layer is a sensor-and-phone stack, the cheapest of this page's answers to trial over the existing fabric — beacons, accessible routing, and vehicle identity for cyclists are all installable street by street — and its force-fit risk is privacy, not engineering: a network that must know even every bicycle is one identifier away from the disciplinary tracking the identity page forbids, and the assist's promise collapses if the signal that guides the blind resident also accumulates against them.
**Cognitive anchor:** The white cane's logic made civic: the cane tells the step, not the street — this answer gives the step a network, so the crossing announces itself and the route answers before the question is asked aloud.
**Narrative hook:** "In the settlement every bicycle signs in, so that a resident who never sees the street is never unseen by it."
**Syn (synergies):** [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — the network whose capsules an assisted route books; [Digital twin and building lifecycle monitoring (6D/7D BIM Lifecycle)](../information-systems/observability-of-flows.md#digital-twin-and-building-lifecycle-monitoring-6d7d-bim-lifecycle) — the simulation passage control reads; [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](../governance/membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) — the identity the assist layer must read without ranking

### How the approaches collide and compose
As a ladder the four network modes compose cleanly and largely by their own lines: [Ultra-fast tubed maglev corridors between cells (Inter-Cellular Vacuum Maglev Grid)](human-travel-network.md#ultra-fast-tubed-maglev-corridors-between-cells-inter-cellular-vacuum-maglev-grid) carries the between-cell and national trip and hands off at its entry level to the depot and the aerial network; [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) carries the within-settlement trip and connects to the buildings' service floors; [Cable-free multi-directional magnetic elevators (3D Cable-less Maglev Shuttles)](human-travel-network.md#cable-free-multi-directional-magnetic-elevators-3d-cable-less-maglev-shuttles) carries the vertical trip across the 20 building levels and turns horizontally into the aerial system; [Multimodal boat-to-PRT transfer shafts clear of the ground floor (Vertical Waterway-to-Skytran Multimodal Shafts)](human-travel-network.md#multimodal-boat-to-prt-transfer-shafts-clear-of-the-ground-floor-vertical-waterway-to-skytran-multimodal-shafts) carries the water passenger up to that same aerial system without ever meeting the foot traffic below. They do not compete for a layer: ground water, +6-meter shafts and bridges, ~9-meter guideways, elevated narrow corridors across the plain. The ledger's composition, however, is asserted on borrowed premises, not owned ones — the ladder's landings lean on records elsewhere: the parking hubs at the gates (the boundary requirement), the mechanized depot (the goods requirement), the cellular grain, the piloti band and the basement zoning (the plane-allocation requirement), and one synergy line still unmapped to any record.

The sharpest collision is the one the ladder cannot ride around: the boarding altitude, one decision with two answers. Is the network's boarding band the first floor at +6 meters — the station level the piloti record guarantees, which both the shaft record (+6 meters, deck-to-platform in 5 seconds) and the elevator record (first-floor horizontal exit into the bridge) already design to — with the 9-meter guideway dipping into station towers where it meets them? Or is the band the ~9-meter guideway itself, "connecting directly to the service floors," with the shaft's platform and the bridge's cabin exit to be re-drawn upward at that height? The +6 answer argues from weight of records and from the wider ledger: three of the four board low, and the sound ledger's decoupling device is already titled as sitting at "the PRT first-floor interface," so a second settlement requirement has independently budgeted the handover at the first floor. The 9-meter answer argues from its record's own design logic: a guideway clear of the pedestrian deck at its crown and of window sight-lines needs altitude, and the service floors the line joins are generic — a shaft that rises to any height the corridor chooses costs only the last seconds of the transfer budget (under 10 seconds is stated at 6 meters; 9 meters is untested there). The evidence that would settle it is a single station section and one headway-and-wait model at each altitude; until it exists, every capacity number in the set (1200 passengers per hour per shaft; 15-second cabin wait; delay-free service to 50 thousand residents in 100 hectares) is computed against an undecided height, and the shaft record's +6 and the aerial record's 9 remain the network's one missing handshake.

Two further seams bound the ladder's ends. At the settlement's edge, [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop)'s Fit confines the aerial network in the existing fabric to wide highways and ring roads as a transitional instrument, and the corridor record's greenfield fit does the same — so the ladder is whole only in new construction, and in the existing fabric the nation-side trip keeps using the conventional systems that the boundary requirement is built to meet; the four answers do not, between them, delete the street from a settlement that already stands. At the ladder's inside end, the elevator record's synergy line still carries the unmapped legacy label "جامعه سیال" where a fourth mode-dependency should be — a cross-reference the ledger has not identified, recorded as unresolved rather than answered here.
