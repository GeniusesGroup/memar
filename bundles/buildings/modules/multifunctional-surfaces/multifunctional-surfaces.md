# Multifunctional Building Surfaces

## Abstract
A roof that only covers, and a facade that only encloses, are the two most expensive failures a sun-belt settlement can afford. This document holds one question — what duties beyond shelter (energy, shade, water, biomass) should the roof and the facade carry? — and four recorded answers that are not interchangeable: rotatable photovoltaic shades over 500 hectares of closed cultivation that convert surplus noon radiation into megawatts while protecting the crop ([Dynamic shading combined with photovoltaics in closed agriculture (Agrivoltaics)](multifunctional-surfaces.md#dynamic-shading-combined-with-photovoltaics-in-closed-agriculture-agrivoltaics)); a double-skin glass wall filled with circulating microalgae that shades, cools, sequesters the occupants' carbon and harvests protein biomass ([Algae photobioreactor double-skin facade at the level of the glass ground floor (Algae Facade PBR)](multifunctional-surfaces.md#algae-photobioreactor-double-skin-facade-at-the-level-of-the-glass-ground-floor-algae-facade-pbr)); a blue-green biosolar roof whose stored rainwater and plant transpiration cool the panels above them by evaporation and raise their yield ([Water-storing biosolar roof with evaporative photovoltaic cooling by plant cover (Blue-Green Biosolar Roofs)](multifunctional-surfaces.md#water-storing-biosolar-roof-with-evaporative-photovoltaic-cooling-by-plant-cover-blue-green-biosolar-roofs)); and solar-tracking canopies of flexible panels and creeping plants over the suspended walkways and the aerial deck ([Solar-biophilic dynamic canopies for suspended corridors and the aerial deck](multifunctional-surfaces.md#solar-biophilic-dynamic-canopies-for-suspended-corridors-and-the-aerial-deck)). Every one is a dynamic shading machine pointed at a different surface — farm roof, facade, building roof, aerial deck — which means the four records are one mechanism budgeted four ways, and the ledger contains no tie-breaker for the scarce things they all consume: the photovoltaic area, the tracking actuators, and the water. The algae record also presumes a glass envelope to inhabit — a wall — while the ground-floor answer it lists as its own synergy forbids enclosing walls at the same six-meter plane, a contradiction the four records leave for the owner to rule on.

## Introduction

### Motivation
These four records were drafted from two different tables — the greenhouse proposal for agrivoltaics, the system-architect's supplemental ideas for the other three — and each reads, alone, like a clever device: rotating panels, a green wall, a spongy roof, a shaded bridge. Placed together they are the surface budget of one settlement: the same noon radiation of a Shiraz summer, above 1,000 watts per square meter, is claimed by four systems that want to intercept it, the same bifacial panels and dual-axis trackers appear in all four bill-of-materials, and the same millimeters of precipitation are promised to transpiration by the roof, to the algae loop by the facade, and back to the aquifer by the drainage records. A reader who approves one device per page silently approves four competing claims on one roof inventory and one water ledger; the dispute only exists when the pages are merged, and the merger is this document.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
What duties beyond shelter — energy, shade, water, biomass — should the roof and the facade carry? Every answer below treats a horizontal or vertical surface as a machine that must earn rent from at least two of those four duties simultaneously, because in this climate a single-purpose surface wastes the one resource the settlement is built around: the sun that is also the threat. The records differ in what they intercept for and where they stand: above the crop, above the visitor, inside the wall, on top of the block. The question favors none of them; what it demands of an adjudicator is a surface-by-surface priority rule — which duty wins when the photovoltaic yield, the shade, the water and the biomass disagree — and an accounting of the water and panel inventory that all four spend in common.

### Approaches

#### Dynamic shading combined with photovoltaics in closed agriculture (Agrivoltaics)
**Mechanism:** Greenhouse roofs in hot and dry climates such as Shiraz face a permanent problem of excessive radiation intensity (above 1,000 watts per square meter at summer noon) and leaf scorch on the plants. Instead of conventional plastic net coverings, bifacial photovoltaic panels or intelligently rotatable solar shades are used above the 500 hectares of Atna farm. In the peak-radiation hours these shades convert the surplus light into electricity and keep the plant at its optimal light lux; in cloudy hours or at morning and evening they rotate their angle and conduct natural light to the cultivation bed.
**Metrics:** Farm surface scale: 500 hectares of fully controlled cultivation; a 50-to-70-percent reduction in evapotranspiration (ET); simultaneous production of megawatts of solar electricity without occupying additional land.
**Prerequisites:** Lightweight large-span truss structures resistant to storms; stepper motors; sun-seeking algorithms driven by the photosynthetic needs of the plant.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Leaving 500 hectares of greenhouse roof under the scorching radiation, and the dizzying electricity consumption of evaporative cell pads and cooling fans.
**Tags:** agrivoltaics, dynamic shading, evaporation reduction, radiation management, light-lux regulation
**Scale:** Macro — farm site
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | In the peri-urban gardens and existing greenhouses it is fully implementable. Its engineering risk is unprincipled panel angling that fails to deliver the photosynthetic light spectrum to the trees — clumsily executed, it destroys the gardener's crop and turns him against the technology.
**Cognitive anchor:** The cool shade-canopy of "the traditional grape trellises of Shiraz and Qazvin" — a double-duty roof that protects the grape clusters from sunburn while, here, also producing clean electricity for the light.
**Narrative hook:** "Shiraz trained its vines on overhead trellises to spare the fruit the sun — we only hung power on the same trellis."
**Syn (synergies):** [Developing the 500-hectare Atna Farm industrial agriculture complex on closed hydroponic/aquaponic loops](../food/food-production-and-assurance.md#developing-the-500-hectare-atna-farm-industrial-agriculture-complex-on-closed-hydroponicaquaponic-loops) — Atna's hydroponic farm; [The centralized photovoltaic mega-plant and the clean energy-pump hub (Clean Energy Station)](../energy/large-generation-site.md#the-centralized-photovoltaic-mega-plant-and-the-clean-energy-pump-hub-clean-energy-station) — the solar plant of the green settlement; recovery of the exergy of light
**Source:** GreenHouse-Proposal.docx

#### Algae photobioreactor double-skin facade at the level of the glass ground floor (Algae Facade PBR)
**Mechanism:** The six-meter glass envelope of the ground floor in the summers of Shiraz is exposed to heavy radiation load. By embedding liquid loops of microalgae culture (such as spirulina or chlorella) in a double-skin glass facade, the algae grow by absorbing the photons and act as a dynamic shade and a biological cooler; at the same time they absorb the CO2 breathed by the humans of the ground-floor hall and produce valuable biomass for the aquaculture feed of Atna farm and for biogas generation.
**Metrics:** Carbon sequestration of up to 8 grams per square meter of facade per day; absorption of 60 percent of solar radiant heat in summer; continuous production of protein biomass inside the food cycle.
**Prerequisites:** A gentle pumping system for the culture liquid; sensors monitoring the pH and the light density of the algae in the platform software.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Mechanical louvers that foul with dirt, darkening reflective glass, or the throwing of heat into the ground-floor halls.
**Tags:** photobioreactor, spirulina microalgae, biological shading, carbon sequestration, protein biomass
**Scale:** Micro — building (ground-floor envelope)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The culture loop is not a fixture on a facade but the facade itself: a pumped, monitored liquid cavity designed into a double-skin envelope. Retrofitted onto existing walls it adds a wet second skin that blinds the shopfronts behind it, runs water against masonry never detailed for it, and turns shading into maintenance scaffolding; and in the new settlement it presumes a sealed, occupied, glazed hall — while the very ground-floor answer it lists as its synergy may forbid enclosing walls at that same six-meter plane, leaving the algae, on the open-forrest reading, with no wall to live on at all.
**Cognitive anchor:** The Iranian home has always kept living green in plain sight — the grape trellis over the entrance, the rose and geranium pots lining a Tehran window-sill, the fish-tank that was many a childhood sitting room's greenest corner. The algae facade is that instinct industrialized: the living glass of the aquarium brought to architectural scale, so the wall itself shades the hall while the family's carbon feeds the culture, and the culture feeds the fish at Atna.
**Narrative hook:** "A glass that breathes your CO2, drinks the sun, and feeds Atna's fish — three jobs on one wall."
**Syn (synergies):** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — the six-meter transparent ground floor; [Biological stabilization of sewage-plant sludge at mesophilic and thermophilic temperature by recovered heat](../water-and-matter/fate-of-residues-and-nutrients.md#biological-stabilization-of-sewage-plant-sludge-at-mesophilic-and-thermophilic-temperature-by-recovered-heat) — feeding the biodigester; [Developing the 500-hectare Atna Farm industrial agriculture complex on closed hydroponic/aquaponic loops](../food/food-production-and-assurance.md#developing-the-500-hectare-atna-farm-industrial-agriculture-complex-on-closed-hydroponicaquaponic-loops) — feed for Atna's aquaculture
**Source:** System-architect supplemental idea (bioreactive ecosystem architecture)

#### Water-storing biosolar roof with evaporative photovoltaic cooling by plant cover (Blue-Green Biosolar Roofs)
**Mechanism:** A triple integration of a rainwater storage layer, a low-water native plant bed, and bifacial photovoltaic panels above the roofs of the 100 blocks. Rainwater is stored in the lower layer and reaches the roots through the wicking phenomenon; the plants' evapotranspiration keeps the air temperature around the panels low — and since a silicon panel loses efficiency as it heats, this evaporative cooling lifts solar electricity production by 8 to 12 percent at the peak heat of the Shiraz summer.
**Metrics:** An 8-to-12-percent increase in the production capacity of the PV panels; cell temperature kept below 40 degrees Celsius against the 65 degrees of bitumen-covered roofs; 100-percent retention of the roof's rainwater runoff.
**Prerequisites:** Hydraulic root-resistant insulation of the roof; use of native drought-hardy sedum species for the climate of the Shiraz plain.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Solar panels installed over the hot asphalt or bitumen waterproofing of roofs, where the overheating cells cause a severe efficiency drop and premature burning of the inverters.
**Tags:** green-blue roof, photovoltaics, panel efficiency, flood retention, biosolar roof
**Scale:** Micro — building (roof level)
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The roof garden and panels are an overlay any flat slab can in principle receive — subject to its dead-load margin for the water-storing layer and a root barrier the old waterproofing never included; force the system onto an existing roof without both and the stored water does what stored water does to an uninspected slab: it arrives in the top-floor ceiling, and the 8-to-12-percent production claim is inherited by a leak-repair ledger instead.
**Cognitive anchor:** Before the bitumen sheet, the Iranian rooftop was infrastructure with a surface: the grandmother's pots of jasmine and geranium, the grape vine trained on a lath, the water jar that sweated cool — everyone remembers that the old roofs kept the stairwell rooms degrees cooler than the sealed ones beside them. The biosolar roof restores that coolness on engineering terms and then bills the sun for it: the same transpiration that cooled the jasmine is what lifts the panel's yield.
**Narrative hook:** "A roof that keeps its own rain keeps its own cool — and the panels earn 8 to 12 percent more for it."
**Syn (synergies):** [The centralized photovoltaic mega-plant and the clean energy-pump hub (Clean Energy Station)](../energy/large-generation-site.md#the-centralized-photovoltaic-mega-plant-and-the-clean-energy-pump-hub-clean-energy-station) — the photovoltaic plant; [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](../energy/energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) — balancing the energy carriers; tempering the microclimate of the roof
**Source:** System-architect supplemental idea (hydraulic-photonic-vegetative convergence)

#### Solar-biophilic dynamic canopies for suspended corridors and the aerial deck
**Mechanism:** Smart movable canopies fitted with flexible photovoltaic panels and creeping plants are installed above the suspended connecting bridges between the blocks; tracking the sun's angle across the day, these canopies control direct radiation, simultaneously produce clean electricity, and turn the suspended walking route into a cool and pleasant corridor.
**Metrics:** A drop of up to 10 degrees Celsius in the felt temperature of the suspended pedestrian route in summer; supply of 20 percent of the lighting energy of the aerial connecting corridors.
**Prerequisites:** Lightweight aluminum tensile structures; automatic sun-tracking sensors.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Bare metal or concrete corridors and bridges under the scorching sun, which become too hot to use in summer.
**Tags:** dynamic canopy, biophilia, radiation control, microclimate regulation, aerial deck
**Scale:** Mid — neighborhood and aerial deck
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The canopy is carried by a suspended deck that exists nowhere in the existing fabric; bolted onto ordinary pedestrian bridges it adds the wind-sail of tracking panels and the saturated weight of creeping plants to structures never sized for either, and in a settlement without the aerial band there is nothing to shade overhead — the shade there belongs to a street tree, and the record's own substrate is a decision still open in the planes ledger.
**Cognitive anchor:** The pleasant deep shade of the old vine trellises — the courtyard canopy in which Shiraz households took their summer evenings, cool because leaves and lath stood between them and the noon.
**Narrative hook:** "Walking under the burning summer sun is torment — the suspended smart canopies turn your walk into a cool corridor that pays for its own light."
**Syn (synergies):** [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — the PRT network; [Graywater heat recovery ahead of MBR entry](../energy/directed-energy-transfer.md#graywater-heat-recovery-ahead-of-mbr-entry) — the two-layer settlement; [Water-storing biosolar roof with evaporative photovoltaic cooling by plant cover (Blue-Green Biosolar Roofs)](multifunctional-surfaces.md#water-storing-biosolar-roof-with-evaporative-photovoltaic-cooling-by-plant-cover-blue-green-biosolar-roofs) (roofs and reflective surfaces)

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The algae facade rents a wall that another record may demolish — one question, two answers, at the same six-meter plane.** [Algae photobioreactor double-skin facade at the level of the glass ground floor (Algae Facade PBR)](multifunctional-surfaces.md#algae-photobioreactor-double-skin-facade-at-the-level-of-the-glass-ground-floor-algae-facade-pbr) does not shade a hall; it is a liquid-filled double-skin glass envelope, and its metrics are those of a conditioned, occupied interior — 60 percent of radiant heat absorbed and the occupants' breath CO2 sequestered, up to 8 grams of carbon per square meter of facade per day. Its own synergy line credits [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) as its substrate — yet that record mandates zero enclosure by load-bearing or dividing walls at that very plane, declaring the space under the building open ground, identical to the surrounding 80 percent of forest. One question, two answers: is the ground floor a glass hall (which the algae need, and which the transparent-pilotis reading of the same six meters forbids) or an unenclosed forest floor (which the algae cannot envelope)? The owner ruling is inescapable before either record is adjudicable at this plane: the pilotis record itself permits glazed, unenclosed volumes — the contradiction lies in what the algae measure, the respiration and comfort of an occupied interior, which an open forest corridor does not have to give. Whichever way it goes, the facade record's carbon and biomass figures stand or fall with the envelope verdict, not with their own biology.

**Four records, one machine: every answer is a dynamic shade, and the settlement buys the inventory once.** The rotatable shades over Atna, the self-shading algae skin, the evapotranspirative roof and the tracking canopy over the deck are the same device family — intercept the noon's surplus photons, convert them to the useful output of the surface they shade (crop, interior, electricity budget, walking comfort), and let the hours of weak sun through. Read as one mechanism with four substrates, they compose beautifully: farm roof, facade, building roof and aerial deck tile the settlement's sun-facing area with no single surface left single-purpose. Read as four rival claims they collide on three budgets no record reconciles. Photovoltaic area: bifacial panels are promised to megawatts over 500 hectares, to the deck's 20-percent lighting supply, and to the roof's 8-to-12-percent uplift — the surface with the best yield per square meter is asserted by all three and computed by none. Water: the roof stores rain for transpiration (100-percent runoff retention), the facade circulates a liquid culture (topped up from where, no record says), and the farm shades to cut evapotranspiration by 50 to 70 percent — the same millimeters of a Shiraz winter claimed as cooling, as medium, and as saved. Structure: the canopy hangs from a suspended deck whose own height and load budget are unsettled in the planes ledger, and the deck's designers sized it for pedestrians and bicycles, not for wind-sails of tracking panels and saturated creepers. The owner decision is a surface budget: which duty holds priority on which plane when the panels, the shade and the water disagree.

**As a timeline the four compose from the existing fabric inward, and the sharpest open number is the deck's own load.** [Dynamic shading combined with photovoltaics in closed agriculture (Agrivoltaics)](multifunctional-surfaces.md#dynamic-shading-combined-with-photovoltaics-in-closed-agriculture-agrivoltaics) is the only answer [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] by its own critique — peri-urban gardens and existing greenhouses can be retrofitted today — and its Fit field also names the risk that sequences the others: clumsy angling destroys the gardener's crop and "turns him against the technology," which is the settlement-wide lesson any facade or canopy retrofit will repeat if actuators are tuned by the electricity meter rather than the organism shaded. [Water-storing biosolar roof with evaporative photovoltaic cooling by plant cover (Blue-Green Biosolar Roofs)](multifunctional-surfaces.md#water-storing-biosolar-roof-with-evaporative-photovoltaic-cooling-by-plant-cover-blue-green-biosolar-roofs) is retrofit-admissible but gated on slab margins and root barriers, so it arrives with renewal rather than with the first tender; the algae facade waits on the envelope verdict from the first collision above; and [Solar-biophilic dynamic canopies for suspended corridors and the aerial deck](multifunctional-surfaces.md#solar-biophilic-dynamic-canopies-for-suspended-corridors-and-the-aerial-deck) waits last, for a deck that does not yet exist and a load budget nobody has drawn. Sequenced that way, the farm answer pays for the research that the other three inherit — the same stepper motors, the same sun-seeking algorithms — while the settlement's roofs, facades and decks join the surface budget one layer at a time; the first number the owner owes the sequence is whether a shaded aerial deck adds more watts or more newtons to the structure beneath it.
