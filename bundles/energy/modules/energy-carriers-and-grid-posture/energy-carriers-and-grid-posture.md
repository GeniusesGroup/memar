# Admissible Energy Carriers

## Abstract
A carrier enters a society's distribution network by passing two independent tests, and the tests are not fuel loyalties: does the carrier's quality match the service it is bought for, and can the joule be moved off the minute it arrives? This document records one rule emerging from its two answers — electricity and time-shifted heat and cold in; methane piped to the wall out — because burning a flame above 1,900 degrees to make 45-degree domestic water is the clearest case of admissibility refused on quality, and because a building that balances its four carriers (electricity, water, air, ground) across a 24-hour cycle is the clearest case admitted on timing. Stability, on this account, is not a supply problem: it is a schedule-and-quality problem solved by separating the carriers and refusing the one whose exergy is wasted at the last meter. The remaining dispute — whether admissibility is tested at the building's own boundary or at the flame, and how far each answer may lean on the other's infrastructure — is decided by the owner, and the verdict will be logged in this document's changelog.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Kept apart, the two records of this page read as unrelated policies — one teaches a building to live on its own generation, the other tears the gas pipe out of every wall — and an objection to either is filed as an objection to the need itself. Read together they are one mechanism of substitution and its precondition: the ban on retail gas delivers an all-electric load whose coldest-night and hottest-noon peaks are exactly the shape that time-shifting into water and soil stores was defined to erase, and the self-sufficient complex, in turn, is the only place the ban's promised replacement services (heat pumps at COP above 3.5, clean electricity) can be paid for without importing the dependence the ban was meant to end. Neither record names the other as a prerequisite, yet each one's own Fit critique — forced blackouts without stores, frozen residents without a replacement network — is a veto over the other's timetable. Scattering the two answers across separate files hides the only question that actually needs an arbiter: which boundary the admissibility test is written against.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
Which energy carriers may enter the distribution network, and which are refused?

Sharpened, the shared problem is not a list but a test with two variables the two records pull apart. [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) treats a carrier as admissible if the consumer can move it off the instant it is demanded — the test is temporal, applied at the building's boundary meters, and its arithmetic is the 24-hour balance of four fluids (electricity, water, air, soil). [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction) treats a carrier as admissible if its quality survives the service it is bought for — the test is thermodynamic, applied at the flame, and its arithmetic is the ratio of 1,900 degrees to 45. A current that fails the timing test passes the quality test; a gas flame that passes the comfort test fails the quality test; nothing in either record says which failure is fatal. Whoever adjudicates this requirement is therefore choosing the site of the test — the boundary, the flame, or both in sequence — and the choice rewrites the other record's metrics before a single pipe is laid.

### Approaches

#### Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)
**Mechanism:** Redefine what energy stabilization means: a system is stable if it produces, within its own borders, everything it consumes — or, by shifting the time-phase of consumption (Time-Shifting), severs its momentary dependence on the external network. The in- and out-flows of a building are sorted into 4 primary energy carriers: 1. electricity (electromechanical work), 2. water (the heat-carrying fluid of high specific heat), 3. air (the ventilation and passive-cooling fluid), 4. soil (the bulky reservoir of seasonal heat absorption and rejection). By storing heat and cold in water and soil, the peak load is eliminated and the energy balance closes over a 24-hour cycle. The shifting is executed by the software, not the occupant: appliance and process shifts (cooking, laundry and the like) are scheduled from the analyzed hours of generation and consumption data, and the unit of the energy relationship is the person, not the dwelling — «طراحی بسته های انرژی فرد محور بجای خانه محوری», per-person energy packages that tempt the individual to move consumption to optimal hours, with the laundry hour chosen «بر اساس انتخاب هوشمند نرم افزاری به جای انتخاب زمان توسط کاربر» — a smart software selection in place of the user's own timing.
**Metrics:** Specific heat capacity of water: cp = 4.184 kJ/kg·K; 100% of the day's cooling peak load shifted onto the water and soil stores; an energy self-sufficiency coefficient above 90% in a residential complex; the scheduling agent: software acting on generation data, not a user at a dial; the billed unit of the energy package: the person, not the household.
**Prerequisites:** Real-time temperature and flow sensors in the 4-carrier circuits; subsurface storage volume.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Buildings leaning on instantaneous consumption of network-supplied carriers, dumping heat to the atmosphere without scruple — no temporal storage, no mass-and-energy balance.
**Tags:** building thermodynamics, energy carriers, load time-shifting, sharing economy, exergy
**Scale:** Micro — building / mid — neighborhood
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | Separating carriers and shifting consumption times in a traditional settlement without smart meters and thermal stores comes to nothing but forced blackouts and industry shutdowns. In the existing fabric, the idea is conditional on installing multi-tariff meters and the economic incentives that ride with them.
**Cognitive anchor:** The ancient engineering of the "mud-brick yakhdāns of the Yazd and Kerman deserts" — banking the free cold of chelleh's winter nights to drink cool, sweet water in the scorching noon of Tir.
**Narrative hook:** "Yazd stored a whole winter in mud so July could drink cold — a meter and a basement of water is the same bargain on a 24-hour clock."
**Syn (synergies):** [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction) — no gas flame left to waste exergy; [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) — 5GDHC thermal loop; [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes) — seasonal UTES storage; [Abolishing the domestic micro-kitchens and establishing a shared centralized restaurant on the first floor](../food/meals-and-shared-cooking.md#abolishing-the-domestic-micro-kitchens-and-establishing-a-shared-centralized-restaurant-on-the-first-floor) — removing the household cooking load

#### Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)
**Mechanism:** Burning methane — theoretical adiabatic flame temperature above 1,900 degrees Celsius — merely to raise cold water to the 45 degrees a household asks of it, or to cook food, is the most violent form of energy-quality (exergy) destruction. In this model, gas pipework into the residential, commercial and service fabric of the society is stopped completely. The gas thereby freed, at macro scale, is routed to high-efficiency combined-cycle power plants (efficiency 55% and above) outside the plain, or exported; in exchange, clean electricity is supplied, and cooling and heating are done by high-efficiency heat pumps (COP > 3.5).
**Metrics:** Warm-season gas consumption of Shiraz: 800 to 950 million cubic meters (daily average 5 to 6 million cubic meters); export revenue from the summer-freed gas: 300 to 350 million dollars; replacement firm power capacity in combined-cycle plants: 5.5 billion kilowatt-hours; the electricity imbalance of Fars's industries brought to zero.
**Prerequisites:** Replacing boiler-room (motorhouse) service systems with heat-recovery chillers and modular heat pumps.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Thousands of kilometers of hazardous gas pipe forced into the middle of the society, fossil fuel burned directly in dwellings at a meager 60% efficiency, and the heat island deepened.
**Tags:** exergy, eliminating municipal gas, electrifying the society, heat pump, Shiraz energy balance
**Scale:** Macro — plain and settlement
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Cutting the gas in existing traditional dwellings, before a replacement of cheap electricity and a central heat-pump network exists, freezes residents in winter, breeds social anger, and puts the grid itself at risk of fire under electric-heater load. The idea requires passive insulation of the buildings first.
**Cognitive anchor:** The safety and cleanliness of "modern Norwegian houses and Japanese skyscrapers" — a clean, silent home without yellow pipes and hazardous gas valves, without the nightmare of carbon-monoxide poisoning.
**Narrative hook:** "A flame of nineteen hundred degrees was never worth carrying indoors for forty-five-degree hands — Norway's kitchens stopped paying that fine; so do we."
**Syn (synergies):** [Abolishing the domestic micro-kitchens and establishing a shared centralized restaurant on the first floor](../food/meals-and-shared-cooking.md#abolishing-the-domestic-micro-kitchens-and-establishing-a-shared-centralized-restaurant-on-the-first-floor) — removing the household kitchens; [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) — 5GDHC thermal network; [Biological stabilization of sewage-plant sludge at mesophilic and thermophilic temperature by recovered heat](../water-and-matter/fate-of-residues-and-nutrients.md#biological-stabilization-of-sewage-plant-sludge-at-mesophilic-and-thermophilic-temperature-by-recovered-heat) — recovered heat for the plant's sludge; the retirement of explosion and gas-leak hazards

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The ban creates the load that only the time-shift is recorded to survive — and neither record admits the debt.** [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction) converts every heating, cooling and cooking service to electricity: the new winter demand is maximal on the coldest nights, exactly when photovoltaic surplus is absent and the 24-hour balance [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) promises is bought with stored heat and cold — a daily-cycle instrument, while [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting)'s own synergy lines concede the seasonal reach belongs to the aquifer record ([Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes)), a document outside this pair. [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) in turn has no market: its multi-tariff meters and economic incentives are worth writing only in a network where the alternative — the cheap flame in the wall — has been declared inadmissible, which is [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction)'s act and no other. The composition is real and the records' own Fit fields state it twice over without cross-referencing: [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting)'s force-fit failure is "forced blackouts and industry shutdowns" (time-shifting demanded of a fabric with nothing to shift into), [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction)'s is "frozen citizens" (a ban demanded of a fabric with nothing to shift onto). An adjudication that approves one Fit and ignores the other's veto is the collision this document exists to prevent.

**Admissibility at the boundary or admissibility at the flame: one question, two answers.** [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) writes the stability rule against the building's own border — produce within it, or move your draw off the network's worst minute — while [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction)'s central move is to relocate generation *outside* the plain (combined-cycle at 55%+, or export) and route the electrons in. Take the boundary test first, and [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction)'s replacement flow is a dependence the ban was meant to end: a complex answering an all-electric peak with imported grid power holds no more of its own stability than the boiler it removed, and the >90% self-sufficiency coefficient becomes the number that decides whether the ban is lawful under the very rule the same page records. Take the quality test first, and the boundary question is secondary bookkeeping: methane at 1,900 degrees for 45-degree service destroys exergy no matter where the current that replaces it was born, and [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting)'s self-sufficiency is then a prudent hedge, not an admissibility criterion. The evidence each answer must carry: for the boundary reading, [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting)'s own "or" — generation inside borders *or* time-shifted consumption — already licenses import as long as the schedule is moved, which quietly exempts [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction)'s grid; for the flame reading, the plain arithmetic of the mechanism texts (60% in-dwelling boilers versus 55%+ combined cycle times COP > 3.5 heat pumps) shows the ban raising exergy efficiency on its own, with no reference to borders at all. Whichever the owner rules, the other record's metrics must be restated: the >90% coefficient as a duty (boundary reading) or as an aspiration (flame reading); "complete stop of gas pipework" as an absolute (flame reading) or as conditional on replacement capacity per block (boundary reading).

**As a pair they are one admission test; the missing third variable is the price that ties them.** [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting)'s timing test needs tariffs, [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction)'s quality test needs "cheap electricity" — both conditions sit in their Fit and prerequisite lines, neither is recorded here, and by the direction-of-dependency doctrine this network page states the rule and names no consumers or instruments that enforce it. That is the clean way to see what the two answers share: they refuse the same default — a carrier delivered continuously, priced flat, and burned where it arrives — but only [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction) says so out loud, while [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) assumes the tariff it never argues. Sequenced, the reading is: declare the flame inadmissible (quality), meter the four carriers (timing made visible), price the hours (timing made worth doing) — the last step owned by no record in this document, and the two records' retrofit vetoes both waiting on it.
