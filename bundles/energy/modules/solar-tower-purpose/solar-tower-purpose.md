# Purpose of the Tall Thermal Column

## Abstract
This document stakes one fact and one question. The fact: a tall column with a sun-heated collector at its foot is a machine that converts the temperature difference between ground and sky into moving air, and moving air is a currency that can be spent several ways. The question — the requirement, stated without favouring an answer — is what service the suction column should give: power, farm ventilation, recreation, or a combination? Four recorded answers spend the same shaft: the stack as power plant and lung of closed farms ([Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures)), as night shift, via the water pond that holds the day's heat ([Storage water pond under the solar updraft stack (Solar Thermal Pond) for nighttime power production](solar-tower-purpose.md#storage-water-pond-under-the-solar-updraft-stack-solar-thermal-pond-for-nighttime-power-production)), as playground, by harvesting the exhaust itself for human flight ([Vertical wind tunnel and human-flight simulator in the central shaft's exhaust duct (Exhaust Updraft Wind-Tunnel Skydiving)](solar-tower-purpose.md#vertical-wind-tunnel-and-human-flight-simulator-in-the-central-shafts-exhaust-duct-exhaust-updraft-wind-tunnel-skydiving)), and as gravity chiller, running the whole column backwards at the peak of heat ([Reversed falling evaporative cooling in the solar updraft stack (Solar Downdraft Evaporative Cooling Mode)](solar-tower-purpose.md#reversed-falling-evaporative-cooling-in-the-solar-updraft-stack-solar-downdraft-evaporative-cooling-mode)). "Or a combination" is not a fifth option the answer space tolerates passively: exergy spent is exergy gone, the turbine and the skydiver want one current, and the downdraft mode inverts the very direction the other three assume — so the answers here are time-slices and valving claims on a single shaft, and the combination the requirement invites must be written as a schedule that none of the four records yet contains. Adjudication of these answers will be logged in this document's changelog.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The four answers were drafted out of three different proposals — a greenhouse plan, a complex plan, and a feasibility document with page citations — and scattered, each reads as a separate technology; gathered, they are four claims on one structure at one hour of the day. The turbine at the stack's throat and the skydiver in the same duct want the same kinetic energy at the same time; the downdraft mode reverses the flow the power record's turbines stand in; the night pond exists precisely because the farm's lung and the settlement's meter keep running after the sun's gift stops. Kept apart, an objection to indoor skydiving looks like an objection to solar power, and the settlement loses the only place where the real decision — which service holds precedence at which hour, over what measured quantity of air — can be made once for all four.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
What service should the air-suction column give: power, farm ventilation, recreation, or a combination?

Sharpened, the shared question has three edges no single record closes. (1) **One shaft, many contracts.** Every answer is a claim on the same moving air, and the air is metered once: whatever the turbine extracts does not reach the throat as lift, whatever the downdraft mode reverses un-serves every updraft answer, and the pond's night gradient is the same stack effect the farm's ventilation spends. A "combination" verdict is therefore a priority-and-schedule document the records collectively do not write. (2) **Two structures, one name.** The residential reading of the column is the Yalda tower's central shaft — 20 meters in diameter, a cross-section "about 500 square meters," rising through 100 dwelling floors — while the cooling mode's prerequisite names "a 400-meter chimney" as the same record's device, and the farm reading is an open-site concrete tower of unstated height; whether this document is about one tower type or two is a reference decision, not a detail. (3) **Water and sky.** The downdraft's cooling is evaporation bought with reclaimed water sprayed at the crown, and the collector's suction is a dividend of the dry air the same water moistens; the records that spend water (the spray) and the records that need the dry plateau (every updraft answer) lean on one climate from opposite ends. The settlement's unit is a society at any scale; the column is its single tallest promise, so its service schedule is a constitution question about an asset.

### Approaches

#### Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures
**Mechanism:** Combine the covered greenhouse with the stack's tall shaft to create an ascending airflow from the density-buoyancy gradient (the stack effect). The air trapped beneath the broad glass/polycarbonate collector on the ground is heated by the sun's radiation and, as its density falls, races upward at speed toward the mouth of the tall stack; axial wind turbines set in the stack's lower throat convert this kinetic energy into electricity. The mechanism runs both at the Atna agricultural fields and in the mid-core of the high-rise towers.
**Metrics:** In Yalda's residential tower: a central solar-stack shaft 20 meters in diameter with a cross-section of about 500 square meters at the height of 100 residential floors; at Atna Farm: a broad collector system tied to a concrete tower for steady electricity production and continuous ventilation.
**Prerequisites:** Seismic design of the central hollow shaft, resistant to wind load; thermal insulation of the stack's inner wall.
**Conventional-model flaw:** A 100 percent dependence of the towers' ventilation on power-hungry electromechanical chillers, and the emission of chlorofluorocarbon refrigerant gases (CFC/HFC).
**Tags:** solar chimney, thermal updraft, clean power around the clock, passive ventilation, renewable energy
**Scale:** Building micro-scale / region macro-scale
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Embedding a colossal shaft in an existing building would impose dangerous seismic loads, draw the polluted street air indoors, and spread the smell of the conventional settlement's sewers through the houses. The element must be integrated, from the first drawing, with the concrete core of the new settlement's supersstructures.
**Cognitive anchor:** The wondrous cool ventilation of "the windcatcher of Dolatabad Garden in Yazd" — a storm-current of fresh, agreeable air moving through the rooms on the sun's free suction alone, without a single rial ever paid to the cooler's meter.
**Narrative hook:** "Dolatabad's windcatcher cooled its room with nothing but sunlight — this is that same free suction, made large enough to spin a generator."
**Syn (synergies):** [Storage water pond under the solar updraft stack (Solar Thermal Pond) for nighttime power production](solar-tower-purpose.md#storage-water-pond-under-the-solar-updraft-stack-solar-thermal-pond-for-nighttime-power-production) — the water pond beneath the stack; [Dynamic shading combined with photovoltaics in closed agriculture (Agrivoltaics)](../buildings/multifunctional-surfaces.md#dynamic-shading-combined-with-photovoltaics-in-closed-agriculture-agrivoltaics) — Agrivoltaics shading; [Developing the 500-hectare Atna Farm industrial agriculture complex on closed hydroponic/aquaponic loops](../food/food-production-and-assurance.md#developing-the-500-hectare-atna-farm-industrial-agriculture-complex-on-closed-hydroponicaquaponic-loops) — Atna Farm; passive cooling of the tower in summer
**Source:** GreenHouse-Proposal.docx | Complex-Building-Proposal.docx | Yalda-Life-Complex-Proposal.docx

#### Storage water pond under the solar updraft stack (Solar Thermal Pond) for nighttime power production
**Mechanism:** One of solar energy's greatest limits is the halt of production at night. In this mechanism, a broad water reservoir of calculated depth and dark absorbing surface is built beneath the transparent collector roof and around the suction mouth. Water, by its exceptional heat capacity (4184 J/kg.K), takes in the sun's radiation through the day and warms without heavy evaporation; at nightfall, as the atmosphere above the stack cools, the water gives this heat back slowly to the adjacent air and keeps alive the temperature gradient that drives the ascending suction and the turning of the turbines through the whole night. At the farm, the same reservoir is doubled in office: it is the settlement's water store and the stack's foundation at once — a fully sealed tank built «به صورت سه بخش», in three sections, so that each section can be controlled, finally purified, and repaired and maintained «بدون توقف عملیات ذخیره آب», without interrupting the storage operation. Its geometry is drawn from the stack itself: total height 15 meters — 10 below grade, 5 above the finished floor at the greenhouse's highest point; the inner compartment 15 meters in diameter, equal to the stack's inner diameter, the middle ring 21 meters, equal to the stack's outer diameter, and the outer ring 30 meters, for a holding capacity of 10,000 cubic meters; and being at the top of the collector's slope, the tank gives the irrigation network its pressure with gravity rather than pumping.
**Metrics:** Stable nighttime temperature slope: a difference of at least 10 to 15 degrees Celsius between the inlet air and the air above the stack; continuous power production across the 24 hours of the day; farm reservoir: 10,000 cubic meters in three sections (diameters 15, 21 and 30 meters; height 15 meters, 10 below grade), any one section maintainable while the store keeps working.
**Prerequisites:** Hydraulic sealing of the soil bed with geomembrane; anti-evaporation transparent covers on the water to hold down the loss of the water's mass.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The use of expensive lithium chemical batteries, with short life and heavy environmental pollution, to store solar power.
**Tags:** thermal capacitor, heat capacity of water, continuity of nighttime production, solar chimney, exergy balance
**Scale:** Site macro-scale and power-plant infrastructure
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Building salt solar ponds inside the dense existing fabric carries the risk of subsidence in neighboring structures and of hot brine leaking into traditional foundations. The system needs a geotechnically safe bed at 10-meter depths in the new settlement.
**Cognitive anchor:** The delightful warmth of "Sarein's natural hot springs on cold winter nights" — a vast thermal capacitor that locks the sunny day's heat inside water, so that at nightfall the wheel of power production and air suction does not stop.
**Narrative hook:** "Sarein's springs prove water keeps a day's heat until midnight — the pond only asks the same water to turn a turbine instead of warming a bather."
**Syn (synergies):** [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures) — the stack's shaft; [The multi-purpose artificial lake (Iran-map geometry) as thermal sink and hydraulic buffer](../water-and-matter/water-bodies-as-common-infrastructure.md#the-multi-purpose-artificial-lake-iran-map-geometry-as-thermal-sink-and-hydraulic-buffer) — the artificial lake; steadying the hamlet's independent grid through the night
**Source:** GreenHouse-Proposal.docx

#### Vertical wind tunnel and human-flight simulator in the central shaft's exhaust duct (Exhaust Updraft Wind-Tunnel Skydiving)
**Mechanism:** Put to use the high-speed ascending airstream in the 20-meter central stack — born of thermal buoyancy and of the convection of the basement levels up to roof height — for indoor free-flight sport (Indoor Skydiving). Residents, in their leisure hours, ride the kinetic energy of the tower's natural exhaust to hover in the air and play, without consuming any fuel-burning motors.
**Metrics:** Central exhaust shaft diameter: 20 meters; employment of the natural convection flow speed at a cross-section of about 500 square meters in each floor; steady revenue from new leisure.
**Prerequisites:** Carbon-fibre safety nets; aerodynamic control of the dampers; backup systems for stabilizing the wind speed.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Releasing the buildings' heat and suction into the atmosphere without harvesting the kinetic and mechanical exergy on the exhaust path.
**Tags:** central stack, vertical wind tunnel, human flight, passive wind energy, leisure amusements
**Scale:** Building micro-scale (the tower's central shaft)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The chamber is a room inside a shaft that exists only in the new settlement — its structural case is [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures)'s own greenfield Fit, inherited whole; and even on new ground the sport asks more of the shaft than the power record ever had to give: a human-safe, steady, regulation-held lift band, a rescue floor, and a valve regime that lets a dwelling hover in the same current the turbines meter — the prerequisites' speed-stabilization backups are the first honest sign that natural suction alone does not hand a body to the air without engineering.
**Cognitive anchor:** The moment at the top of an arc on the iron swings of the old neighborhood park — the half-second of weightlessness every Iranian child chased with kicked legs, and the belief that with a strong enough blast of wind a person could stay up there, waiting, without coming down.
**Narrative hook:** "Every kid on a swing chased that floating moment at the top of the arc — the tower's own breath now holds you there, and no one has to push."
**Syn (synergies):** [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures) — the thermal stack; [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures) (the convection of warm air to the roof); [Interactive amusement park and multi-generational thrill park (Multi-Generational Theme Park)](../living-and-culture/recreation-and-culture-venues.md#interactive-amusement-park-and-multi-generational-thrill-park-multi-generational-theme-park) — the thrill park
**Source:** Yalda justification plan (ص 5 و 28) (Yalda feasibility plan, pp. 5 and 28)

#### Reversed falling evaporative cooling in the solar updraft stack (Solar Downdraft Evaporative Cooling Mode)
**Mechanism:** Reverse the airstream of the 400-meter stack at the peak of summer's heat and dryness by spraying mist of reclaimed water at the tower's highest point. The hot air at the stack's crown is cooled and becomes heavy and falls; this cool, moist air drives the hot air of the greenhouse precinct or the hamlet outward through lateral valves, and without any electric fan it produces cooling on a wide scale.
**Metrics:** Fall height: 400 meters; temperature drop: 10 to 15 degrees Celsius; an immense gravity-driven flow of cool air, resting on gravity and cooling inertia.
**Prerequisites:** [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures) (the 400-meter stack); a high-altitude mist-spraying pump driven by photovoltaic power.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Seeing industrial stacks only one-way — as vents for smoke and upward heat — while an inverted density difference could build the largest gravity chiller in the world.
**Tags:** reverse solar chimney, downdraft cooling, spray at the crown, inverted suction, climatic ventilation
**Scale:** Complex macro-scale and farms (a 400-meter tower)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The mode is a valve, but the valve hangs on a 400-meter shaft no existing building owns, so the retrofit question is settled by its prerequisite record before this one is asked; and even on new ground the physics keeps its own gate — the falling air is cool only insofar as mist evaporates into it, and evaporation is the thirst of dry air, so the 10-to-15-degree drop is a claim about the plateau summer, not about a humid coast, where the same spray would fall as clammy weight; the photovoltaic pump lifts the settlement's reclaimed water to the crown at the exact season the farms' own closed loops ask that water to stay at their roots.
**Cognitive anchor:** The cold face-blast that meets you when the door of the village *yakhchāl* — the old ice-house — is opened at midday in high summer: a coldness kept above and behind, released downward toward whoever is thirsty for it, the memory that cold, like water, can be stored and then let fall.
**Narrative hook:** "The ice-house kept winter under the ground and handed it out by armful — the inverted chimney keeps space's cold above the village and pours it over everything."
**Syn (synergies):** [Developing the 500-hectare Atna Farm industrial agriculture complex on closed hydroponic/aquaponic loops](../food/food-production-and-assurance.md#developing-the-500-hectare-atna-farm-industrial-agriculture-complex-on-closed-hydroponicaquaponic-loops) — Atna Farm; [The multi-purpose artificial lake (Iran-map geometry) as thermal sink and hydraulic buffer](../water-and-matter/water-bodies-as-common-infrastructure.md#the-multi-purpose-artificial-lake-iran-map-geometry-as-thermal-sink-and-hydraulic-buffer) — the artificial lake as water source
**Source:** Atna Farm justification plan (p. 12) (Atna Farm feasibility plan, p. 12)

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[Vertical wind tunnel and human-flight simulator in the central shaft's exhaust duct (Exhaust Updraft Wind-Tunnel Skydiving) ([Vertical wind tunnel and human-flight simulator in the central shaft's exhaust duct (Exhaust Updraft Wind-Tunnel Skydiving)](solar-tower-purpose.md#vertical-wind-tunnel-and-human-flight-simulator-in-the-central-shafts-exhaust-duct-exhaust-updraft-wind-tunnel-skydiving)) and [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures) are creditors of one current, and the exergy is spent once.** The turbines sit in the stack's lower throat to take the kinetic energy out; the skydivers ride the same 20-meter duct to stay inside the kinetic energy. A turbine upstream of a hover chamber slows the air the chamber must not slow; a chamber upstream robs the turbine of the head it was sized on; "steady revenue from new leisure" and "steady electricity production" are therefore rival revenue lines on one asset unless the shaft is valved or time-sliced. One question, two answers. **Answer one (time-share):** the column runs as power plant except in reserved flight hours, when the dampers bypass the throat — the prerequisites' "aerodynamic control of the dampers" and "backup systems for stabilizing the wind speed" already admit that the current must be governed for bodies, and the schedule is then a straightforward bookkeeping of kilowatt-hours versus tickets. **Answer two (one column, one job):** the sport and the power are separate towers, and this document's "the same shaft" reading is the artifact — which would multiply the greenfield cost of both and force the record to say which Atna or Yalda structure it actually assumes. The records lean toward time-share (one shaft, two claims, named controls) but neither answer is stated, and the difference in capital cost between them is the height of a supersstructure.

**[Reversed falling evaporative cooling in the solar updraft stack (Solar Downdraft Evaporative Cooling Mode)](solar-tower-purpose.md#reversed-falling-evaporative-cooling-in-the-solar-updraft-stack-solar-downdraft-evaporative-cooling-mode) is a mode of the machine, not a fifth machine — and its mode-switch is the sharpest seam in the document.** The record's own prerequisites say so: it presupposes [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures) as "the 400-meter stack," and its flaw text indicts seeing "industrial stacks only one-way." But a downdraft is not a different service of the column; it is the column's whole direction reversed, which means the updraft family — the turbines, the pond's night gradient, the farm's ventilation and the skydivers' lift — are all out of contract for exactly the hours the spray runs, at the peak of the heat, which is the best hour of every updraft answer except cooling. And the machinery of the reversal is unspoken: axial turbines stand in the throat; a reversed current through them is braking, spinning-up or damage, and no record in this set lists a bypass or freewheel clutch among the mode's prerequisites. The composition the four answers hint at is real but precise: the column is a bidirectional heat engine whose services are scheduled by hour, season and humidity, with water as the mode's fuel; the collision is that no schedule, no valve state and no interlock has been written — "combination" is not an option a verdict can simply select, it is a document this one does not yet contain.

**[Storage water pond under the solar updraft stack (Solar Thermal Pond) for nighttime power production](solar-tower-purpose.md#storage-water-pond-under-the-solar-updraft-stack-solar-thermal-pond-for-nighttime-power-production) is the composition partner every other answer silently needs, and it opens a water question the record's own framing hides.** The pond is what makes the column a 24-hour claim instead of a daytime one: the farm's "continuous ventilation," the settlement's night grid and the leisure hours after dinner all stand on the gradient the water holds until the night slope of 10 to 15 degrees runs out. That is the harmonious half; the seam lies in the metaphor the Fit line carries — the record speaks of "salt solar ponds" in its retrofit warning while its mechanism describes a dark-surfaced fresh reservoir around the suction mouth — and salt-pond stratification and a thermal pond are different machines with different water risks (hot brine leaking into foundations is a liability of the former, not obviously of the latter). Meanwhile the water the pond guards with "anti-evaporation transparent covers" is the same scarce mass the crown spray of [Reversed falling evaporative cooling in the solar updraft stack (Solar Downdraft Evaporative Cooling Mode)](solar-tower-purpose.md#reversed-falling-evaporative-cooling-in-the-solar-updraft-stack-solar-downdraft-evaporative-cooling-mode) throws into the driest air of the year: the storage answer seals its water, the cooling answer spends it, and no record states which reservoir the settlement draws on first in the season when both claims peak. Either the pond's covers and the spray's demand are adjudicated as one water ledger with stated priorities, or the settlement holds that the column's thermal store and its cooling makeup are fed from separate streams — a claim about infrastructure this document does not carry.

**The reference of "the column" itself is unresolved, and it decides what kind of document this is: one tower type or two structures.** [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures) fixes the residential reading — a central shaft 20 meters across, "about 500 square meters" of section, rising through 100 dwelling floors — while [Reversed falling evaporative cooling in the solar updraft stack (Solar Downdraft Evaporative Cooling Mode)](solar-tower-purpose.md#reversed-falling-evaporative-cooling-in-the-solar-updraft-stack-solar-downdraft-evaporative-cooling-mode)'s prerequisites and scale name "a 400-meter stack," a figure that fits the Yalda core (100 floors at four meters a floor) but is never attributed to it, and [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures)'s own farm reading is a concrete tower whose height no metric gives. The consequence is not cosmetic: the downdraft's "fall height: 400 meters" is its claim to the 10-to-15-degree drop, so if the mode runs on the residential core rather than a dedicated farm tower, a settlement's dwelling column is being asked to reverse its own airflow — and the skydivers' cross-section, the same "about 500 square meters," belongs to a duct that geometry says is nearer 314 square meters at 20 meters diameter, so one of the three numbers (diameter, area, or the per-floor phrasing) is governing and the records do not say which. One question, two answers: either the document's subject is a single family of columns whose heights and sections are tabulated once, in this document, from which every record's figures inherit — or it is two or three distinct structures sharing a name, and each record should carry its tower's identity the way [Vertical wind tunnel and human-flight simulator in the central shaft's exhaust duct (Exhaust Updraft Wind-Tunnel Skydiving)](solar-tower-purpose.md#vertical-wind-tunnel-and-human-flight-simulator-in-the-central-shafts-exhaust-duct-exhaust-updraft-wind-tunnel-skydiving) already carries its page numbers. The arithmetic of diameters and areas is kept out of the body — these texts are the owners' — but the handoff files the mismatch as a question that blocks any combined verdict.
