## Open Questions
1. **Who verifies the declared bands, and where do violations go?** The Explanation makes every system document declare its band toward the network (cooking 50 to 150, MBR 50 to 60, proofing 35 to 40, digester 55) and forbids the network from naming consumers — but no record names the registry that holds the declarations, the metering that checks them, or the destination of a breach. A self-declared band fails silently: the cascade's letter cannot see a reactor fed below its promise. *(Why: the cascade is the document's own heart and is unenforceable without this institution; every metric in the six approaches — 100% absorption, complete elimination of heat injection, −5 °C nights — is unverifiable until it exists. Blocking: yes for the core rule; no for the need itself.)*
2. **What does the cascade owe to uphill transfer?** The rule says every hotter stream may discharge only into a cooler consumer — a law about falling heat — but cooling is heat pumped upward, and [Integrated exergy balancing across cold stores, datacenters and heat pumps](directed-energy-transfer.md#integrated-exergy-balancing-across-cold-stores-datacenters-and-heat-pumps)'s hybrid heat pumps (and, in effect, [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks)'s recovered condensers, since the 40-to-60 bus is hotter than any stream they draw from) are exactly this counter-direction. Is work-purchased lift (a) exempt from the cascade, (b) a new stream born hotter and therefore re-bound by it, or (c) allowed only against metered work so the exergy ledger can net it? *(Why: the document currently contains the rule and its standing exception without any clause reconciling them; the collision section formulates (c) as a candidate but the second pass broke that candidate on two tests — see Question 9. Blocking: yes for [Integrated exergy balancing across cold stores, datacenters and heat pumps](directed-energy-transfer.md#integrated-exergy-balancing-across-cold-stores-datacenters-and-heat-pumps)'s verdict and for the rule's completeness.)*
3. **Pairing or pricing — which governs the summer surplus?** [Balancing the thermal capacity of the settlement's heat sinks (Thermal Sinks Capacity Matching); pairing condensers with a thermophilic digester](directed-energy-transfer.md#balancing-the-thermal-capacity-of-the-settlements-heat-sinks-thermal-sinks-capacity-matching-pairing-condensers-with-a-thermophilic-digester) assigns surplus condenser heat by declared capacity (the thermophilic hub drinks first because the 3-to-4 kWh tank physically cannot); [Local peer-to-peer thermal-energy market between 500-person blocks](directed-energy-transfer.md#local-peer-to-peer-thermal-energy-market-between-500-person-blocks) assigns it by token price between blocks. If a block may sell hub-bound surplus to a nearer bidder, [Balancing the thermal capacity of the settlement's heat sinks (Thermal Sinks Capacity Matching); pairing condensers with a thermophilic digester](directed-energy-transfer.md#balancing-the-thermal-capacity-of-the-settlements-heat-sinks-thermal-sinks-capacity-matching-pairing-condensers-with-a-thermophilic-digester)'s 100-percent-absorption metric becomes contingent on trades its authors do not control; if hubs reserve declared capacity off-market, [Local peer-to-peer thermal-energy market between 500-person blocks](directed-energy-transfer.md#local-peer-to-peer-thermal-energy-market-between-500-person-blocks) is licensed to trade only the residual — a different record than the one written. *(Why: rival governance of one allocation; neither text mentions the other's arbiter. Blocking: yes for any combined verdict on [Balancing the thermal capacity of the settlement's heat sinks (Thermal Sinks Capacity Matching); pairing condensers with a thermophilic digester](directed-energy-transfer.md#balancing-the-thermal-capacity-of-the-settlements-heat-sinks-thermal-sinks-capacity-matching-pairing-condensers-with-a-thermophilic-digester) + [Local peer-to-peer thermal-energy market between 500-person blocks](directed-energy-transfer.md#local-peer-to-peer-thermal-energy-market-between-500-person-blocks).)*
4. **Band-fit of the bakery on the bus.** [Preheating bakeries and processing industries with heat-pump waste heat](directed-energy-transfer.md#preheating-bakeries-and-processing-industries-with-heat-pump-waste-heat)'s proofing chambers demand a stable 35 to 40 degrees; [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks)'s bus runs 40 to 60. Blocking: no; the rule stands, but its metric does not.)*
7. **Is the cascade a prohibition that pre-decides the six, or one answer among them? (owner call — second pass, from stress-testing the cooling exception)** The collision analysis's second test found that the rule's letter — "no pumping happens without a named destination" — is not thermodynamically neutral toward the incumbent technology it never mentions: a bare air-cooled split unit is pumping with no named destination, so taken strictly the cascade *forbids* stand-alone cooling and makes the recovered-condenser bus the only lawful machine. That collapses the page's stated shape: four engineering answers, a price answer, and an upstream saver cannot all be rival "proposals for the same allocation" if the shared rule has already excluded every allocation that leaves heat in the alley. One question, two answers. **Answer one (prohibition reading):** the cascade is a strict rule, the bus is its mandatory compliance path, and the other five records are governance layers *inside* a network every chiller must already join — which forecloses any regime of point-source air rejection, seasonal-only storage, or radiative sinks that skips the bus, and the abstract's claim that the six "do not disagree about the thermodynamics" was understated because disagreement about entry was resolved before adjudication opened. **Answer two (preference reading):** "no pumping without a named destination" is a default ordering, not a bar — heat may be rejected after the cascade's steps are exhausted, so the six genuinely compete and the registry (Q1) prices rather than prohibits rejections. Evidence for the prohibition reading: the bus record's own metric is *complete elimination* of anthropogenic heat injection, not reduction; the rule's sentence is absolute. Evidence for the preference reading: none of the six records says stand-alone cooling is unlawful, and the page's entire adjudicative purpose assumes the answers are live; reading it as a bar makes the adjudication a formality. *(Why: it decides what this document *is* — a rule with six implementations, or six rival rules — and therefore what any changelog verdict would even settle; Q1's registry and Q2's rider are both downstream of it. Blocking: yes — for the document's structure and for every verdict; the need itself is not blocked.)*
8. **Where does the registry of declared bands get filed? (owner call — one question, two answers)** The second-pass stress test in the collision section concluded the registry is a requirement in its own right — its axes (operator, metering interval, breach destination) belong to verification, not to thermal allocation, and its own audit would serve water or matter as readily as heat — while the one-way dependency doctrine survives it, because the register only stores what system documents declare and names no consumers itself. That leaves the filing question live. Answer one — **its own requirement record**: verification is a design subject with rivals of its own (whoever operates the register will face the same engineering-versus-price split this page adjudicates), and the extensions already name `information-systems/observability-of-flows.md` as its candidate home; the doctrine's dependency direction says consumer docs declare needs to networks, so a cross-network institution cannot live inside one network's document without inverting that relation for everyone else's declarations. Answer two — **a clause of this document's rule**: a rule whose enforcement lives elsewhere is etiquette by the page's own test, and one changelog that records both the cascade's verdict and its verifier's design keeps the pair from drifting apart as requirements. Evidence for one: the registry serves every declared-band document, not just heat's; evidence for two: this document already holds the only worked statement of what the registry must carry (band, capacity, metered inlet, breach destination). *(Why: Q1's institution needs a home before any of the six records can be judged against an enforceable rule; the filing location also tells the other declaration-reading documents where to look for it. Blocking: no for the six verdicts; yes for closing Q1 and the core rule.)*
9. **Critique of the document itself.** (a) The Explanation stakes the cascade as "the heart of this document," yet the requirement sentence it cites is neutral — if the cascade is a premise, the six approaches compete only on governance and the abstract's earlier neutrality claim was wrong; if it is an answer, rival regimes (storage-first, radiative-only sinks, no bus at all) have no block here and the document silently chose a winner before adjudication. (b) [Integrated exergy balancing across cold stores, datacenters and heat pumps](directed-energy-transfer.md#integrated-exergy-balancing-across-cold-stores-datacenters-and-heat-pumps) and [Local peer-to-peer thermal-energy market between 500-person blocks](directed-energy-transfer.md#local-peer-to-peer-thermal-energy-market-between-500-person-blocks) carry no Source line and [Graywater heat recovery ahead of MBR entry](directed-energy-transfer.md#graywater-heat-recovery-ahead-of-mbr-entry)'s source is a 1405-06-22 review extract flagged as hypothetical; the reviewer-authored Fit/anchor/hook fields for [Balancing the thermal capacity of the settlement's heat sinks (Thermal Sinks Capacity Matching); pairing condensers with a thermophilic digester](directed-energy-transfer.md#balancing-the-thermal-capacity-of-the-settlements-heat-sinks-thermal-sinks-capacity-matching-pairing-condensers-with-a-thermophilic-digester), [Integrated exergy balancing across cold stores, datacenters and heat pumps](directed-energy-transfer.md#integrated-exergy-balancing-across-cold-stores-datacenters-and-heat-pumps) and [Local peer-to-peer thermal-energy market between 500-person blocks](directed-energy-transfer.md#local-peer-to-peer-thermal-energy-market-between-500-person-blocks) are proposals with no record backing and must be read as drafts until approved. *(Blocking: no — but Q8a decides what an adjudication in the changelog would even be about, and second pass found Q7 is the same fork with its edges sharpened rather than a new worry.)*
