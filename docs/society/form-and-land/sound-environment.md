# The Sound Environment

## Abstract
The decision this page holds is the order of operations: which sound problem is treated at its source ([Elastomeric Decoupling & Tuned Mass Dampers at the PRT First-Floor Interface](sound-environment.md#elastomeric-decoupling--tuned-mass-dampers-at-the-prt-first-floor-interface)), which at the boundary ([Green passive walls: acoustic design of the envelope and the elimination of noise pollution](sound-environment.md#green-passive-walls-acoustic-design-of-the-envelope-and-the-elimination-of-noise-pollution), [Earth Berms: peripheral hills of geomorphic spoil from basement excavation](sound-environment.md#earth-berms-peripheral-hills-of-geomorphic-spoil-from-basement-excavation)), which in the room itself ([Acoustic Micro-Baffle Geometry for the 500-Person Hall in Glass](sound-environment.md#acoustic-micro-baffle-geometry-for-the-500-person-hall-in-glass)), which by deliberate masking ([Active Bioacoustic Scape: wildlife monitoring and masking with flowing water](sound-environment.md#active-bioacoustic-scape-wildlife-monitoring-and-masking-with-flowing-water), [Hydraulic Pink-Noise Sound Masking for speech privacy in the transparent ground floor](sound-environment.md#hydraulic-pink-noise-sound-masking-for-speech-privacy-in-the-transparent-ground-floor)) — and who sets the listening norm of each space. Six answers are recorded, and they are not interchangeable: a settlement that reaches for pink-noise curtains ([Hydraulic Pink-Noise Sound Masking for speech privacy in the transparent ground floor](sound-environment.md#hydraulic-pink-noise-sound-masking-for-speech-privacy-in-the-transparent-ground-floor)) before damping the PRT interface ([Elastomeric Decoupling & Tuned Mass Dampers at the PRT First-Floor Interface](sound-environment.md#elastomeric-decoupling--tuned-mass-dampers-at-the-prt-first-floor-interface)) will be paying to hide a machine it could have quieted once. If the ordering fails, the sleepers in the bedrooms above the first-floor junction lose their 30-decibel nights, the dwellers of the glass halls lose private conversation, and the animals that share the 100-hectare ground lose the low-stress soundscape their coexistence promises.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The chain that carries a sound from capsule to ear to animal is one system even though its six treatments were recorded on six days; read in isolation, each record looks like an amenity, when its real meaning is a position in the hierarchy — suppress before you shield, shield before you mask, and monitor all three. Only together do they answer the second half of the requirement, the one none of the six records answers alone: who sets the auditory norm of each space.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How is unwanted sound suppressed, absorbed, or converted — and who sets the auditory norm of each space? That is the requirement the six records were filed against, and it stays fixed while their answers compete below it.

### Approaches

#### Green passive walls: acoustic design of the envelope and the elimination of noise pollution
**Mechanism:** A system of densely pre-grown vegetative cover and sound-absorbing acoustic panels is applied to the blocks' outer walls and party walls, so that noise pollution from wind or from building services is absorbed completely and absolute silence is established inside.
**Metrics:** reduction of environmental noise inside the units by up to 40 decibels; increased psychological comfort of the residents.
**Prerequisites:** dense vegetative root panels; acoustic wave computation on the facade.
**Conventional-model flaw:** stone and composite facades that reflect sound and multiply the ambient noise of the city severalfold.
**Tags:** acoustic design, green walls, sound elimination, biophilic sound absorption, auditory calm
**Scale:** building micro
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The panel is a cladding layer and can be bolted to a loud existing facade where the noise complaint already exists — but force a living wall onto envelopes built without a waterproofing reserve or irrigation risers, and the vegetation dies in season one or rots the insulation it was hung on, leaving the acoustic panels bare and the building trading noise damage for water damage.
**Cognitive anchor:** sound-absorbing walls
**Narrative hook:** Our society never honks or clamors; the living green walls swallow every sound so you can rest in complete silence.
**Syn (synergies):** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate); legacy-ref (unresolved); [Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)](../water-and-matter/treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#constructed-treatment-wetlands-living-machine)

#### Earth Berms: peripheral hills of geomorphic spoil from basement excavation
**Mechanism:** Hauling the hundreds of thousands of cubic meters of soil from the excavation of the 10 basement levels out to the deserts beyond the settlement is forbidden; the enormous volume is deposited and formed around the 50- or 100-hectare settlement and turned into a green, forested chain of miniature hills — a structure that acts as an acoustic dam against the highways, a natural windbreak, and an ecological retaining wall.
**Metrics:** 100 percent on-site recycling of the excavation soil; a 30- to 40-decibel cut in the noise of the neighboring highways; the mind-bending hauling and dump-truck budget reduced to zero.
**Prerequisites:** geotechnical stabilization of the hill slopes by deep-rooted tree planting and gravity-fed drip irrigation.
**Conventional-model flaw:** paying heavy dump-truck rents to carry the spoil beyond the settlement, then spending billions of tomans of public budget on concrete retaining walls.
**Tags:** soil recycling, geomorphic spoil, acoustic dam, natural boundary, ecological windbreak
**Scale:** metropolitan and the settlement's peripheral edge
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Siting large industrial vertical farms in the heart of a traditional city with its saturated alleys and high land rates lacks any economic justification and leaves the investor bankrupt.
**Cognitive anchor:** the advanced production system of the "Dutch high-tech greenhouses"; clean organic products that grow without polluted soil or pesticides.
**Narrative hook:** The ground dug from under the houses does not leave — it stands back up at the edges and becomes the wall that keeps the highway quiet out there.
**Syn (synergies):** [Vertical zoning of 10 basement levels (Deep Subsurface Clustering) and 35-meter penetration](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#vertical-zoning-of-10-basement-levels-deep-subsurface-clustering-and-35-meter-penetration) — digging the 10 basement levels; [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) — the forest biosphere; replacing garrison-like concrete walls with a green hill

#### Active Bioacoustic Scape: wildlife monitoring and masking with flowing water
**Mechanism:** The presence of 50 thousand humans alongside freely living tame animals requires managing their mutual sound stress. Bioacoustic monitoring through ambient microphones tracks the animals' stress frequencies and the precinct's noise level; to neutralize nuisance sounds, the geometry of the falling water of the channels is directed as natural white noise (sound masking), alongside active noise cancellation systems in the ceilings of the piloti bases, so that a stable forest quiet endures.
**Metrics:** a 10- to 15-decibel reduction of the background sound level on the promenades; noise kept under 45 decibels in the blocks' surroundings.
**Prerequisites:** an IoT network of sound sensors; hydraulically controlled nozzles for the gentle fall of the channels' water.
**Conventional-model flaw:** the noise of motorcycle traffic, car horns, and loud chillers that has made the life of birds and animals impossible in today's cities.
**Tags:** ecological acoustics, urban noise canceling, water sound, animal health, psychological calm
**Scale:** neighborhood middle and ground-floor level
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The sensor web can be bolted to any street, but the treatment half of the mechanism is architectural: without the channel network to fall water through, "natural white noise" degenerates into speakers playing recorded streams — exactly the dizzy electronic audio this record's own conventional-model flaw condemns — and the active-cancellation ceiling in an unprepared piloti spends its energy budget cancelling braking noise ([Elastomeric Decoupling & Tuned Mass Dampers at the PRT First-Floor Interface](sound-environment.md#elastomeric-decoupling--tuned-mass-dampers-at-the-prt-first-floor-interface)'s target) that should already be dead at the bearing.
**Cognitive anchor:** The courtyard ḥowz of the old family house at dusk — the small fountain whose constant water voice let the family speak on the porch while the street's comings and goings fell, unheard, behind it; the memory is that the house had a sound that made other sounds disappear.
**Narrative hook:** The village courtyard had one running fountain; we simply let the ground floor keep the same trick at full scale.
**Syn (synergies):** [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) — safe habitat for the tame animals; [Block datacenter: distributed compute and data storage on one floor of every block's ten basements, in opposition to the centralized megadatacenter](../information-systems/data-storage-and-processing.md#block-datacenter-distributed-compute-and-data-storage-on-one-floor-of-every-blocks-ten-basements-in-opposition-to-the-centralized-megadatacenter) — navigable waterway channels; [Space ergonomics and housing psychology for native digital generations (Gen Z/Alpha Ergonomics)](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#space-ergonomics-and-housing-psychology-for-native-digital-generations-gen-zalpha-ergonomics) — ergonomics of psychological calm

#### Elastomeric Decoupling & Tuned Mass Dampers at the PRT First-Floor Interface
**Mechanism:** Because PRT capsules run continuously at 6 meters' height and the stations connect directly into the first floor of 100 residential buildings, the capsules' braking and acceleration send dynamic vibration and harmonic resonance frequencies into the concrete frame; to stop these oscillations and impact noises reaching the residential floors above, the stations are seated on lead-rubber elastomeric bearings, and tuned mass dampers (TMD) are embedded in the first-floor columns, damping the vibration at its origin.
**Metrics:** suppression of 95% of the dynamic oscillations below 30 hertz; the noise level caused by passing fleet in the residential floors held under 30 decibels; increased fatigue life of the stations' steel and concrete connections.
**Prerequisites:** [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) (the PRT aerial network), [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) (the station's 6-meter piloti), decoupling ducts at the elastic expansion joints.
**Conventional-model flaw:** anchoring rail lines rigidly into the buildings, which delivers resonance and low-frequency vibration straight into the bedrooms and strips the residents of their nervous rest.
**Tags:** structural services, vibration control, structural acoustics, PRT system, TMD mass damper, residential comfort
**Scale:** building micro (the structural level of the first floor at 6-plus meters)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Seating a station on lead-rubber bearings is a foundation decision that must precede the frame; force the mechanism onto an existing guideway or transit structure and decoupling means unseating a live line — jacking spans out of service to insert what could have been cast once — and tuned mass dampers in occupied columns become a shutdown project instead of a spec line.
**Cognitive anchor:** The village mosque's thick carpet — Friday's hundred arrivals walked in and the felt beneath absorbed every footfall before it reached the rows at prayer; the oldest Iranian lesson in stopping impact noise at its source, under one's own feet.
**Narrative hook:** The mosque carpet swallowed a hundred footsteps so no one at prayer heard them; the PRT station gets the same courtesy in rubber and lead.
**Syn (synergies):** [Space ergonomics and housing psychology for native digital generations (Gen Z/Alpha Ergonomics)](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#space-ergonomics-and-housing-psychology-for-native-digital-generations-gen-zalpha-ergonomics) — psychological-calm ergonomics for the digital generation; [Safe pedestrian deck on the roofs of the PRT guideways (Elevated Guideway Pedestrian Deck)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#safe-pedestrian-deck-on-the-roofs-of-the-prt-guideways-elevated-guideway-pedestrian-deck) — the suspended pedestrian walk on the PRT line roof
**Source:** System-architect supplemental idea (system architect's supplementary idea — structural synergy | acoustics and aerial transport)

#### Acoustic Micro-Baffle Geometry for the 500-Person Hall in Glass
**Mechanism:** A 6-meter ground-floor space with full-glass walls and hundreds of people present normally suffers violent reflection of sound and the escalating "cocktail-party effect" — voices rising against each other to be heard; to solve this while keeping the glass clear, the ground-floor ceiling is fitted with angled suspended baffles of porous engineered timber that break and absorb sound in the human-speech band (500 to 2000 hertz).
**Metrics:** reverberation time (RT60) cut from 2.4 seconds to under 0.6 seconds; speech transmission index (STI) raised above 0.75; the continuity of the 360-degree glass sightline preserved.
**Prerequisites:** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) (the 6-meter glass ground floor), [Hybrid structure: impervious reinforced concrete below grade, mass engineered timber above (Hybrid Concrete-Mass Timber)](../buildings/structural-material.md#hybrid-structure-impervious-reinforced-concrete-below-grade-mass-engineered-timber-above-hybrid-concrete-mass-timber) (use of mass-timber structures and elements).
**Conventional-model flaw:** tall lobbies and dining halls surfaced in hard stone, concrete, and glass — punishing noise that makes quiet conversation impossible.
**Tags:** environmental acoustics, reverberation control, wooden baffles, glass facade, calm social interaction
**Scale:** building micro (ground-floor dining and living space)
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Baffles hang from whatever ceiling exists, so a loud existing hall is a natural client — but the 0.6-second promise was computed against the volume of a 6-meter glass floor; force the same target into a low-ceilinged existing room and the required depth steals headroom, the baffles become a canopy you can touch, and the cocktail-party effect returns around their edges.
**Cognitive anchor:** The vaulted brick serai ceilings of the historic bazaars — hundreds of voices under one roof, each stall's conversation intact, the dome's geometry breaking the shout before it reached the next carpet; the memory of a crowd that never became a wall of noise.
**Narrative hook:** The old bazaar held hundreds of voices under its vaults and never reached a roar; the glass hall gets its vault in suspended timber.
**Syn (synergies):** [Nudge-Driven Social Tea Hubs in the ground-floor commons](space-for-differing-minds.md#nudge-driven-social-tea-hubs-in-the-ground-floor-commons) — the tea house and ground-floor interaction plaza; [Neuro-Inclusive Spatial Zoning of the shared halls](space-for-differing-minds.md#neuro-inclusive-spatial-zoning-of-the-shared-halls) — neurotypical sensory zoning
**Source:** System-architect supplemental idea (system architect's supplementary idea — acoustics of sound and the architecture of collective spaces)

#### Hydraulic Pink-Noise Sound Masking for speech privacy in the transparent ground floor
**Mechanism:** The presence of 500 people in the glass ground-floor space and at shared tables requires guaranteeing conversational privacy without partitions or walls; a narrow ribbon of the channels' clear water is guided along the inner edge of the glass wall and falls in a sheet over porous basalt stones, producing a natural pink noise (a 3-decibel drop per octave) whose spectrum renders the words of neighboring tables unintelligible beyond 3 meters and preserves the quiet domain of conversation.
**Metrics:** speech privacy radius under 3 meters; speech transmission index (STI) reduced below 0.3 at farther distances; the water curtain's sound level: 40 to 45 decibels, matched to ear ergonomics.
**Prerequisites:** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) (the ground floor's glass walls and interaction tables), [Block datacenter: distributed compute and data storage on one floor of every block's ten basements, in opposition to the centralized megadatacenter](../information-systems/data-storage-and-processing.md#block-datacenter-distributed-compute-and-data-storage-on-one-floor-of-every-blocks-ten-basements-in-opposition-to-the-centralized-megadatacenter) (the clear-water channel network).
**Conventional-model flaw:** ugly insulated plastic partitions, or dizzying electronic music piped through halls, which exhaust the brain and make people shout to hear one another.
**Tags:** perceptual acoustics, pink noise, water-built sound cover, speech privacy, glass ground floor
**Scale:** building micro (ground-floor dining space)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The curtain's physics is the channel's architecture: water that has already fallen for the settlement's own use can mask for free. Force it onto existing fabric without that water and it becomes a recirculating plumbing fixture — pump hum, mineral crust, frozen sheets in winter, legionella liability — and the maintenance bill silently turns the last resort of privacy into a permanent fountain nobody asked for.
**Cognitive anchor:** The Ābshār pavilion of Chehel Sotoon in Isfahan — the garden hall whose cascading water face made the veranda the most private room in the city; every Iranian who has stood behind that fall and heard the garden roar while the conversation stayed between two people carries this mechanism in memory.
**Narrative hook:** Isfahan's garden pavilion hid its conversations behind falling water; the 500-person tea hall does the same with the settlement's own channels.
**Syn (synergies):** [Acoustic Micro-Baffle Geometry for the 500-Person Hall in Glass](sound-environment.md#acoustic-micro-baffle-geometry-for-the-500-person-hall-in-glass) — the wooden ceiling baffles; [Nudge-Driven Social Tea Hubs in the ground-floor commons](space-for-differing-minds.md#nudge-driven-social-tea-hubs-in-the-ground-floor-commons) — the tea house and ground-floor commons

### How the approaches collide and compose
The six records answer at four different sites of the same problem — source, path, envelope, and receiver — and the chain's internal logic is a hierarchy, not a menu: [Elastomeric Decoupling & Tuned Mass Dampers at the PRT First-Floor Interface](sound-environment.md#elastomeric-decoupling--tuned-mass-dampers-at-the-prt-first-floor-interface) is the only pure source treatment, killing the braking energy at the bearing before it becomes a sound in the structure at all; [Earth Berms: peripheral hills of geomorphic spoil from basement excavation](sound-environment.md#earth-berms-peripheral-hills-of-geomorphic-spoil-from-basement-excavation) and [Green passive walls: acoustic design of the envelope and the elimination of noise pollution](sound-environment.md#green-passive-walls-acoustic-design-of-the-envelope-and-the-elimination-of-noise-pollution) answer at the path and the boundary, one at the settlement's edge, one at the unit's wall; [Acoustic Micro-Baffle Geometry for the 500-Person Hall in Glass](sound-environment.md#acoustic-micro-baffle-geometry-for-the-500-person-hall-in-glass) answers inside the room the other three feed quietly; [Active Bioacoustic Scape: wildlife monitoring and masking with flowing water](sound-environment.md#active-bioacoustic-scape-wildlife-monitoring-and-masking-with-flowing-water) watches the whole ground plane and [Hydraulic Pink-Noise Sound Masking for speech privacy in the transparent ground floor](sound-environment.md#hydraulic-pink-noise-sound-masking-for-speech-privacy-in-the-transparent-ground-floor) answers only at the ear. Read in this order they compose into a single system whose cost falls as you move upstream — a decibel stopped at the bearing is free, one masked by the water curtain rents the channels forever — and [Active Bioacoustic Scape: wildlife monitoring and masking with flowing water](sound-environment.md#active-bioacoustic-scape-wildlife-monitoring-and-masking-with-flowing-water)'s sensors are the instrument that tells the design which layer is failing, making the hierarchy enforceable rather than aspirational.

The live tension sits in the trio the glass itself creates: [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) promises the 6-meter transparent ground floor; transparency gives hundreds of people a shared volume with no partitions, the cocktail-party effect that [Acoustic Micro-Baffle Geometry for the 500-Person Hall in Glass](sound-environment.md#acoustic-micro-baffle-geometry-for-the-500-person-hall-in-glass) was written to absorb, and once the room is acoustically clean, every nearby conversation becomes audible — the privacy loss [Hydraulic Pink-Noise Sound Masking for speech privacy in the transparent ground floor](sound-environment.md#hydraulic-pink-noise-sound-masking-for-speech-privacy-in-the-transparent-ground-floor) was written to mask. In other words, [Acoustic Micro-Baffle Geometry for the 500-Person Hall in Glass](sound-environment.md#acoustic-micro-baffle-geometry-for-the-500-person-hall-in-glass)'s success (STI above 0.75) is [Hydraulic Pink-Noise Sound Masking for speech privacy in the transparent ground floor](sound-environment.md#hydraulic-pink-noise-sound-masking-for-speech-privacy-in-the-transparent-ground-floor)'s problem (a speech privacy radius under 3 meters), and both apply to the same hall. The composition therefore has a strict rule the records imply but none states: masking is a last resort, engaged only at the edges of seating groups after [Elastomeric Decoupling & Tuned Mass Dampers at the PRT First-Floor Interface](sound-environment.md#elastomeric-decoupling--tuned-mass-dampers-at-the-prt-first-floor-interface), [Green passive walls: acoustic design of the envelope and the elimination of noise pollution](sound-environment.md#green-passive-walls-acoustic-design-of-the-envelope-and-the-elimination-of-noise-pollution), [Earth Berms: peripheral hills of geomorphic spoil from basement excavation](sound-environment.md#earth-berms-peripheral-hills-of-geomorphic-spoil-from-basement-excavation), and [Acoustic Micro-Baffle Geometry for the 500-Person Hall in Glass](sound-environment.md#acoustic-micro-baffle-geometry-for-the-500-person-hall-in-glass) have spent their budgets — otherwise the settlement masks its way out of problems it could have engineered shut, and [Hydraulic Pink-Noise Sound Masking for speech privacy in the transparent ground floor](sound-environment.md#hydraulic-pink-noise-sound-masking-for-speech-privacy-in-the-transparent-ground-floor)'s own conventional-model flaw (dizzy electronic audio) returns as its own mechanism. The boundary is visible in two fields that must be read together: [Active Bioacoustic Scape: wildlife monitoring and masking with flowing water](sound-environment.md#active-bioacoustic-scape-wildlife-monitoring-and-masking-with-flowing-water)'s "noise kept under 45 decibels" and [Hydraulic Pink-Noise Sound Masking for speech privacy in the transparent ground floor](sound-environment.md#hydraulic-pink-noise-sound-masking-for-speech-privacy-in-the-transparent-ground-floor)'s "40 to 45 decibels" — and the contradiction is not of degree but of scope: it turns on what the 45-decibel ceiling counts. Read as a total ambient budget — every source, engineered masking included — the curtain may run only where the zone already sits at five decibels or less, and the two records are rivals for the same air. Read as a nuisance budget — designed masking falls outside the ceiling the sensors police — the curtain is legal at full level, but then the monitoring half of [Active Bioacoustic Scape: wildlife monitoring and masking with flowing water](sound-environment.md#active-bioacoustic-scape-wildlife-monitoring-and-masking-with-flowing-water) reads blind precisely where the loudest designed sound is, and the instrument meant to make the hierarchy enforceable cannot hear what the hierarchy exists to police. Which budget the ceiling carries is the owner's call; it stands open in this document's handoff and its verdict will be recorded in its changelog.
