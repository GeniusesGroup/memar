# Stewardship of the Ground Cover

## Abstract
One continuous hundred hectares of open ground — 80 percent of the village's outdoors plus the plane beneath every building's ground floor — is claimed by two incompatible stewards. [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) would plant the whole cover as a dense artificial forest with safe habitat for domestic animals and birds; [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering) would surrender the same soil to natural selection and keep human judgment only for the entry list of animals. Planting or succession: the answer decides who absorbs the loss if the ground cover degrades — under [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) the loss lands on children raised without daily contact with living plants and tame animals, and under [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering) it lands on the residents and native fauna exposed the moment the ecological filter loosens. This page stages the two as rivals rather than blending them.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The two records cite the same hectare, so neither can be endorsed alone. Approve the forest without succession's minimum-intervention rule and you have committed the gardener's labor that [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering)'s metrics forbid; approve succession without the tame habitat [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) promises and the village has licensed a biome no parent will let a child walk into. Kept apart, a reader meets each position as an objection to the need itself — the barren ornamental lawn both answers reject — when in fact the disagreement, over who decides what may grow, is visible only where the two pages touch.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
Should the living ground cover of the ground plane be planted and gardened, or left to succeed of itself? That is the requirement both records answer. What they share is a refusal — the single-crop, ornamental, mown lawn — and a boundary condition, [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule)'s maximum 20 percent occupancy with [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate)'s free 6-meter piloti carrying the cover under the buildings. What they do not share is the theory of stewardship that fills the refusal, and that is the dispute adjudicated here.

### Approaches

#### The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals
**Mechanism:** The entire open ground span of the settlement — the 80 percent of the site that is open plus the area under the buildings' ground floors — is converted into a dense artificial forest with safe habitat for many kinds of tame animals and birds. The union of settlement and nature does not mean ornamental single-crop parks of mown grass: it means a rich, semi-natural, living biome in which children and residents grow up in direct daily contact with animals and plants.
**Metrics:** continuous vegetation cover across the village's 100 hectares at forest tree density; the artificial separation of human territory from tame-animal territory is removed.
**Prerequisites:** automatic systems for animal cleaning and drinking troughs; separation of biological zones; systems for monitoring animal behavior and health.
**Conventional-model flaw:** the sterile concrete-city paradigm and the total alienation of modern humans from nature, which treat animals as nuisance or pollutant and have expelled wildlife and vegetation from wherever people live.
**Tags:** urban ecology, biodiversity, artificial forest, coexistence with animals, ecological landscape
**Scale:** inter-building and plain-wide (macro)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Releasing tame animals into the existing fabric of asphalt, among its cars and sewer rats, ends in bloody collisions, slaughtered animals, an invasion of alien species, and fierce sanitary resistance. The system needs a living-soil substrate, the complete removal of asphalt, and ecological filtering around the perimeter.
**Cognitive anchor:** the serene courtyards of northern village houses, or "Isfahan's Birds Garden" — a place where geese and birds walk freely beside children and contact with nature is part of how a child is raised.
**Narrative hook:** "The geese that walked beside children in the old courtyards of the north — here the whole 100-hectare ground floor is that courtyard, and the walk to school never leaves it."
**Syn (synergies):** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — free 6-meter ground floor; [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — undisturbed soil; [Automated cybernetic security with 20-meter electric-shock projectors and guard robots](../security-and-resilience/securing-sensitive-spaces.md#automated-cybernetic-security-with-20-meter-electric-shock-projectors-and-guard-robots) — cybernetic security sensitive to harm against animals; decentralized water treatment

#### Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering
**Mechanism:** The formation of the ecosystem over the 80 hectares of open ground plane is surrendered to natural selection — no rigid gardening, no ornamental selection — while a strict ecological filter runs in parallel: animals with a wild disposition and invasive urban species that endanger people, such as stray dogs and released cats, are not admitted. Birds, tame animals, native plants, and microorganisms are left free to strike the human-nature biological balance themselves.
**Metrics:** biologically open territory of 100 continuous hectares; gardening and genetic amendment of soil at the absolute minimum; 100 percent monitoring and suppression of invasive species that threaten biosecurity.
**Prerequisites:** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) (maximum 20 percent occupancy), [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) (free 6-meter glass piloti), legacy-ref (unresolved) (retaining earth berm and moat).
**Conventional-model flaw:** artificial meddling with nature — water-thirsty lawns and decorative park-building — carried out while aggressive dogs are blindly released, destroying native wildlife and making the sidewalks unsafe.
**Tags:** natural evolution, ground-cover ecology, natural selection, ecological filter, safe biodiversity
**Scale:** site-macro (100 hectares of ground-plane territory)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Succession cannot be scheduled and cannot be inherited mid-term. Forced onto the existing fabric, [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering) adopts decades of compacted subgrade, established stray populations, and neighbors who experience the first year's weeds as neglect rather than biome; a filter that must suppress 100 percent of invasive species starts from a deficit it did not create, and the landscape license quietly becomes the abandoned lot next to the entrance.
**Cognitive anchor:** The Hyrcanian forests of Gilan and Mazandaran — the oldest and most-loved woodland in Iran, whose canopy closed without a planter's hand; the shared memory of an abundance that kept its balance without a gardener. [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering)'s mechanism is the admission that the walk, not the maintenance plan, is what made the forest.
**Narrative hook:** "Iran's favorite forest was never planted — the ground cover needs one rule instead of a gardener: nothing dangerous walks in."
**Syn (synergies):** [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) — continuous ground-cover vegetation; [Electrostatic precipitation of dust and an invisible mist curtain at the 6-meter openings (Electrostatic Dust Scavenging)](ground-surface-treatment.md#electrostatic-precipitation-of-dust-and-an-invisible-mist-curtain-at-the-6-meter-openings-electrostatic-dust-scavenging) — fine-mist air-tempering curtain; [Bird-Safe Glass & Bio-Sonar: ultraviolet-reflective glazing for the ground floor](wildlife-safe-surfaces.md#bird-safe-glass--bio-sonar-ultraviolet-reflective-glazing-for-the-ground-floor) — bird-safe glass

### How the approaches collide and compose
This is the sharpest rival pair in the form-and-land set and the collision must not be softened: the same 80 percent of the same 100 hectares is claimed twice, under opposite theories of what tending means. [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) is gardening — humans choose the cover (planted forest at tree density), humans engineer its services (automatic cleaning, water troughs, zoned habitats), and the intended outcome, children raised among tame animals, is held in place by design. [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering) is succession — humans choose nothing about the cover (its metrics cap intervention at the absolute minimum) and exercise judgment only at the gate of the fauna list. Their syn lines present them as complements — [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering) names [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals) as its first synergy — yet the mechanism fields say rival, and the document records both readings honestly.

Where composition is real: both answers reject the ornamental monoculture lawn, and [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering)'s filter is exactly the safety guarantee [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals)'s promise of "safe habitat" needs — a tame-animal settlement without the filter becomes the stray-dog hazard both records condemn. Where composition fails: on the same square meter, [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering)'s minimum-intervention metric is violated the moment [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals)'s planting is executed, and [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals)'s forest-density metric presumes the weeding and replacement [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering) forbids. There is a third collision the staging above has not named, and it is material rather than philosophical: water. [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering)'s own conventional-model flaw condemns "water-thirsty lawns and decorative park-building," yet [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals)'s headline metric — continuous vegetation cover at forest tree density across a hundred hectares — presumes an irrigation regime of exactly the kind its rival indicts, and no more water-thirsty act exists in either record than the establishment years of a planted forest the minimum-intervention cap forbids. The gardening-versus-succession dispute is therefore also an undeclared water ledger that every one of the readings below must settle to be buildable at all: planted and irrigated, and succession's cap is breached from year one; self-succeeding on whatever rain the site receives, and the forest-density metric quietly abandons the canopy the children's habitat was promised under. Two honest reconciliations exist: a temporal one, in which [The settlement's ground plane as a continuous artificial forest with habitat for tame animals](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#the-settlements-ground-plane-as-a-continuous-artificial-forest-with-habitat-for-tame-animals)'s "semi-natural" forest is the establishment phase that [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering) inherits once the canopy closes — though [Curated Evolutionary Ground Biosphere with ecological wildlife filtering](stewardship-of-ground-cover.md#curated-evolutionary-ground-biosphere-with-ecological-wildlife-filtering)'s ban on ornamental selection then retroactively invalidates the founding planting; and a spatial one, zoning hectares into tamed and wild domains — though neither record claims such a boundary, and inventing it here would exceed the answers as filed. The adjudication belongs to this document's changelog; the choice among the four readings is opened, unanswered, in the handoff — and the water ledger stands behind that choice, since each reading implies a different irrigation promise the records themselves never price.
