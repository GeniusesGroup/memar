# Service Placement Scale

## Abstract
The recorded answers fix a different floor under the same viability question — what aggregate of people a given service must be sized for. [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly) mandates every building to carry its own basic services, [Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services](service-placement-scale.md#synergistic-clustering-of-4-adjacent-complexes-for-an-economic-level-of-specialized-services) refuses to duplicate the capital-heavy ones and pools them across a cluster of four complexes (8 to 10 thousand people), and [Hyper-local distributed employment and co-working spaces adjacent to dwelling (Hyper-Local Co-Working)](service-placement-scale.md#hyper-local-distributed-employment-and-co-working-spaces-adjacent-to-dwelling-hyper-local-co-working) moves work itself next to the dwelling, into the service floors the first two answer provides. Get the allocation wrong downward and residents of a small cluster pay service charges for gyms and halls that close within months of opening — [Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services](service-placement-scale.md#synergistic-clustering-of-4-adjacent-complexes-for-an-economic-level-of-specialized-services)'s own flaw record; get it wrong upward and [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly)'s mandate lands on tiny buildings where, by its own risk note, the halls become abandoned storerooms and hubs of neighbor dispute while the pool, theater and clinic that genuinely need 8 thousand people are never built.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The three answers are one ledger: every service type has a population below which it cannot cover its fixed costs, and the records locate that line at three different heights — the building, the four-complex cluster, the residence floor. Only on one page can the reader see that [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly)'s per-building obligation and [Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services](service-placement-scale.md#synergistic-clustering-of-4-adjacent-complexes-for-an-economic-level-of-specialized-services)'s cluster pooling are the same rule applied two levels apart, and that [Hyper-local distributed employment and co-working spaces adjacent to dwelling (Hyper-Local Co-Working)](service-placement-scale.md#hyper-local-distributed-employment-and-co-working-spaces-adjacent-to-dwelling-hyper-local-co-working) quietly spends [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly)'s mandated floors as its offices. Scattered, each metric looks reasonable alone and the double-booking of the same service square meters goes unnoticed.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
At which grain — dwelling unit, building, cluster, district — should everyday services be aggregated?

### Approaches

#### Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)
**Mechanism:** No building is accepted as a single-function residential dormitory. All buildings are obliged, by rule, to design, construct, and continuously operate basic welfare and social services in shared form inside their own service levels — at minimum equipped sports and gym halls, gathering, celebration and party halls, and spaces of collective interaction — so that the residents' basic leisure and wellbeing needs are met within a few meters of where they live.
**Metrics:** Allocation of at least 20 square meters of covered service space per resident; sports and assembly facilities available within under 2 minutes, on foot or by elevator.
**Prerequisites:** Modular design of flexible spaces with standard acoustics and acoustic privacy separation from the residential sleeping zones.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Outsourcing the duty of public services to distant quarters of the settlement, forcing the citizen to drive long distances by car for one hour of sport or gathering.
**Tags:** building basic services, health and wellbeing, event space, functional richness, access justice
**Scale:** Building micro / neighborhood middle
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Easily conformed in large urban building complexes (above 100 units). But imposed on small traditional apartments, the per-capita share of the service charge is high and the owners are few: the halls immediately turn into abandoned storerooms and nuclei of neighborly dispute.
**Cognitive anchor:** The ceremony and comfort of "international 5-star luxury hotels" — the equipped sports club, pool and assembly hall at only a few steps' distance from the dwelling door.
**Narrative hook:** Check in to your own home and get the five-star treatment — gym and ballroom two minutes from your door, the pool one short bridge away.
**Syn (synergies):** [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) — large-scale grain; [Abolishing the domestic micro-kitchens and establishing a shared centralized restaurant on the first floor](../food/meals-and-shared-cooking.md#abolishing-the-domestic-micro-kitchens-and-establishing-a-shared-centralized-restaurant-on-the-first-floor) — removal of private kitchens; upgrading social capital and cutting demand for vehicular trips

#### Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services
**Mechanism:** Formation of local 4-complex clusters for the optimal distribution of heavy services during the transition stage of development: basic services (sport, dining hall, public health) stand independently in every tower, while capital-intensive services (a standard swimming pool, a kindergarten, an amphitheater hall, a comprehensive clinic) are distributed among 4 neighboring towers so they can run at an optimal scale factor.
**Metrics:** Population covered per cluster: 8 to 10 thousand people; travel time between cluster members by PRT or pedestrian bridges: under 3 minutes; 100% utilization efficiency of the shared facilities.
**Prerequisites:** Suspended aerial physical connection or a pedestrian passage without any car way; shared software for operating the cluster's services.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Building expensive, incomplete facilities in each standalone structure that are shut down after a few months because capacity is never filled.
**Tags:** neighborhood network, transition era, complex clustering, economic scale, spatial synergy
**Scale:** Neighborhood middle (4-complex cluster)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Four-complex clustering in the tangled fabric of the traditional settlement is impossible: ownership is fragmented, physical continuity is absent, and the managers fall out over costs.
**Cognitive anchor:** The powerful alliance of "neighborhood development cooperative companies" — pooling resources to get the best services at the lowest cost.
**Narrative hook:** Four towers, one pool, one theater: neighbors who sign a single contract afford what none of them could alone.
**Syn (synergies):** [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — PRT rapid transport; [Optimal scaling of the spatial grain (minimum 5-hectare domain and a 2,000-to-50-thousand-person biological module)](settlement-grain-and-scaling.md#optimal-scaling-of-the-spatial-grain-minimum-5-hectare-domain-and-a-2000-to-50-thousand-person-biological-module) — complex grain; [Dynamic partitioning of spaces and inter-complex service sharing in the transition era](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#dynamic-partitioning-of-spaces-and-inter-complex-service-sharing-in-the-transition-era) — flexibility of uses
**Source:** Complex-Building-Proposal.docx | Yalda-Life-Complex-Proposal.docx

#### Hyper-local distributed employment and co-working spaces adjacent to dwelling (Hyper-Local Co-Working)
**Mechanism:** Co-working infrastructure is embedded in the service floors of every building complex, in step with the falling working hours of modern humans and the growth of the digital economy: ergonomic work stations, dedicated gigabit internet, acoustic conference rooms, and digital fabrication workshops (FabLab). The resident reaches their office in under 2 minutes by elevator, and street traffic is removed.
**Metrics:** An 80 percent cut in the distance of daily work trips; 3 to 5 square meters of co-working space per worker; 2-minute access from suite to desk.
**Prerequisites:** [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly) (service floors in buildings), [100% recovery of megadatacenter thermal exergy (the Middle East datacenter)](../information-systems/data-storage-and-processing.md#100-recovery-of-megadatacenter-thermal-exergy-the-middle-east-datacenter) (direct connection to the datacenter).
**Conventional-model flaw:** The absolute separation of residential from office zones in comprehensive plans, which imposes hours of wasted time and burned gasoline to reach the workplace.
**Tags:** endogenous employment, co-working, travel reduction, remote work, work-life balance
**Scale:** Building micro (service floors)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Opening wind corridors in existing dense metropolises, whose flows are blocked by unregulated towers, would require demolition of the fabric on a wide scale — which is not doable.
**Cognitive anchor:** The pleasant cool of the mountain breeze in "the garden-lanes of Shemiran or the Qasr al-Dasht orchards" — a live current of cool air that makes breathing possible in summer.
**Narrative hook:** Two minutes by elevator from bed to desk — and the street below keeps its garden-lane quiet.
**Syn (synergies):** [Co-living architecture with 25 square meters of private and 20 square meters of communal service space per resident](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#co-living-architecture-with-25-square-meters-of-private-and-20-square-meters-of-communal-service-space-per-resident) — co-living; [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — easing the traffic load of the PRT lines; [Abolishing the domestic micro-kitchens and establishing a shared centralized restaurant on the first floor](../food/meals-and-shared-cooking.md#abolishing-the-domestic-micro-kitchens-and-establishing-a-shared-centralized-restaurant-on-the-first-floor) — centralized lunch restaurant

### How the approaches collide and compose
The three records read like rival placements, but they are three cuts of one viability line — what population each service type must serve to carry its fixed cost — and the interesting part is where the cuts disagree. Composed as a tier rule, [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly) and [Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services](service-placement-scale.md#synergistic-clustering-of-4-adjacent-complexes-for-an-economic-level-of-specialized-services) fit without contradiction: [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly) mandates the floor every building carries (the small-fixed-cost, high-frequency services — a gym is viable inside one large complex), while [Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services](service-placement-scale.md#synergistic-clustering-of-4-adjacent-complexes-for-an-economic-level-of-specialized-services) caps duplication above that floor (the standard pool, theater and clinic become viable only at 8 to 10 thousand people). [Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services](service-placement-scale.md#synergistic-clustering-of-4-adjacent-complexes-for-an-economic-level-of-specialized-services) already performs the split itself — basic services in every tower, heavy services distributed — so the pair is one graduated rule, not a dispute. But the tiers compose only where the line between them is drawn per service and priced, and the records draw it by examples that overlap at the margin: the cluster keeps "public health" in every tower yet moves the "comprehensive clinic" to four, and a gym — which the per-building mandate plants in every building — can cost as much fit-out as a small pool at tower scale. The tier line is a table to be priced (fixed cost against required population), not a fact both records can point at; until it is priced, "one graduated rule" is a reading of two example-lists, and both records' own flaw texts describe the cost of guessing the line wrong from either side — incomplete facilities that close, or halls no one fills. The same pricing logic disciplines the cluster's "100% utilization efficiency": a fixed-schedule facility against variable demand can never mean hundred-percent time-on — no pool fills at 3 p.m. — and is coherent only as peak capacity matched to required population, which is precisely the quantity the tier table must fix; read as uptime, the metric fails by arithmetic whatever the tier answer is.

The disputes live elsewhere, and two of them need a decision rather than commentary. The first is the metering unit of the mandate: [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly) demands at least 20 square meters of covered service per resident per building, yet [Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services](service-placement-scale.md#synergistic-clustering-of-4-adjacent-complexes-for-an-economic-level-of-specialized-services) spreads its capital-heavy services across the 4 towers so that no single complex carries the full set — and a building measured alone against the 20 will build precisely the "expensive, incomplete facility" both records execrate. The 20 must be metered per building for the basic tier and per cluster for the specialized tier, and no record yet says so. The second is governance: [Synergistic clustering of 4 adjacent complexes for an economic level of specialized services](service-placement-scale.md#synergistic-clustering-of-4-adjacent-complexes-for-an-economic-level-of-specialized-services) pools without chartering — it says nothing of who votes, who pays when utilization falls short, or what stops the richest tower from taking its pool and leaving — while its own risk line already forecasts managers falling out over costs in any fragmented-ownership setting. A four-complex cluster is a company; the page treats it as a geometry. [Hyper-local distributed employment and co-working spaces adjacent to dwelling (Hyper-Local Co-Working)](service-placement-scale.md#hyper-local-distributed-employment-and-co-working-spaces-adjacent-to-dwelling-hyper-local-co-working) then enters as the live dispute over that same slab: its prerequisite is [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly)'s service floors, but whether its 3 to 5 square meters per worker are cut from the mandated 20 per resident or added on top is a ruling the records do not supply, and the two answers diverge. Inside the 20: every resident who takes the elevator to a desk shrinks the gym by their allocation, and [Hyper-local distributed employment and co-working spaces adjacent to dwelling (Hyper-Local Co-Working)](service-placement-scale.md#hyper-local-distributed-employment-and-co-working-spaces-adjacent-to-dwelling-hyper-local-co-working)'s own success — the 80-percent trip cut — cannibalizes [Every building mandated to provide basic services (sports hall, gatherings and shared assembly)](service-placement-scale.md#every-building-mandated-to-provide-basic-services-sports-hall-gatherings-and-shared-assembly)'s leisure floor in direct proportion to uptake, work outvoting leisure for the same slab in exactly the white-collar complex the mechanism imagines. On top of the 20: the floor budget floats with employment, a complex that works from home buys itself more hall rather than less — and nothing ever forces the "abandoned storerooms" the mandate's Fit warns about back into use, because idle leisure meters and brand-new office meters can sit side by side on separate ledgers. The evidence that argues the first reading is what retrofits actually do — amenity-to-office conversions in existing residential towers show the cannibalization is the live failure mode; the evidence that argues the second is the mandate's own scope text, "basic welfare and social services," of which a workplace is not one. Its 80-percent trip-cut claim also presumes the jobs themselves exist within the 8-to-10-thousand cluster; specialized employment aggregates at larger scales (the settlement-grain question), so work-near-home is viable exactly to the depth of the local job pool — another number the three answers do not yet share.
