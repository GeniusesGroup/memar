# Passive Heat Rejection

## Abstract
This document stakes one bargain: unwanted heat must leave through passive paths before any machine is called to push it. Its requirement — "By which passive paths should unwanted heat be rejected before the machines?" — gathers four recorded answers that contract with three different sinks: the evaporation of reclaimed water at the scale of the grounds and facades ([Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds](passive-cooling-paths.md#local-ecological-cooling-through-surface-evaporation-of-reclaimed-water-on-facades-and-grounds)), the same evaporation folded into a down-draught windcatcher inside the building's shafts ([Inverted modular solar windcatchers with a ceramic evaporative downwell in the facade (Passive Evaporative Downdraft Wind-Tower)](passive-cooling-paths.md#inverted-modular-solar-windcatchers-with-a-ceramic-evaporative-downwell-in-the-facade-passive-evaporative-downdraft-wind-tower)), the direct release of heat to outer space through the atmospheric window ([Passive infrared radiative cooling to the depths of space around the clock (Sub-Ambient Radiative Cooling)](passive-cooling-paths.md#passive-infrared-radiative-cooling-to-the-depths-of-space-around-the-clock-sub-ambient-radiative-cooling)), and the delivery of the building's own mass to the cold of the night ([Passive night-flush ventilation cooling (Night-Flush) and neutralization of the radiation trap in climates with diurnal swings above 15 degrees](passive-cooling-paths.md#passive-night-flush-ventilation-cooling-night-flush-and-neutralization-of-the-radiation-trap-in-climates-with-diurnal-swings-above-15-degrees)). All four agree on shrinking the chiller and, downstream, the heat a thermal network would otherwise have to carry; they collide on the settlement's scarcest resources — the two evaporative answers spend the water the two sky answers conserve, and the surface claims compete for one building envelope. Adjudication of these answers will be logged in this document's changelog.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The four answers here look like four technologies at four scales — ground, façade, roof, wall mass — and each books its saving in the same currency: twenty to thirty percent off a cooling load that exists only once, with the night flush zeroing machine electricity between 23 and 7 in the morning. Read singly, they invite arithmetic no physical system will honour — an evaporative pair drawing on one effluent stream, two surface claims sealing a double count — and an objection to spending scarce water on cooling gets misfiled as an objection to passive cooling itself. Read together, the real shape of the requirement appears: the four paths are ranked inversely by the same climate variable (dryness), they share prerequisites with documents outside this one, and the savings ledger needs a single owner. No single record contains that ledger.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
By which passive paths should unwanted heat be rejected before the machines?

Sharpened, the shared question is not whether the four paths work — each is a recognised physics — but which sink the society contracts with, at whose resource cost, and with what rule for summing the savings. Three edges every answer leaves open. (1) **Resources.** The paths draw on different scarce things: treated effluent (the 75 thousand litres a day of the micro-MBR record is named as a feedstock for cooling), roof and canopy area, façade shafts, street paving, and structural mass; the settlement owns all of them once, and no record prices its claim. (2) **Arithmetic.** Two records book "20 to 30 percent" reductions of overlapping quantities, and one books a 100-percent reduction of a slice of the day; the document states no rule for whether such savings are marginal, absolute, or capped in combination. (3) **Climate.** Dry air, a clear night sky, and a diurnal swing above 15 degrees are premises one record states outright and the others imply; the paths are strongest precisely where water is scarcest, so a verdict that adopts all four without a climate ranking would spend two budgets — water and surface — that the same settlement cannot pay twice. And one edge faces outward: heat that leaves passively never reaches the cascade of [Directed Energy Transfer](directed-energy-transfer.md); this document is the load-side declaration that network document's sink arithmetic reads, and per the one-way dependency rule the declaration travels from here to the network, never the reverse.

### Approaches

#### Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds
**Mechanism:** Use the 75 thousand litres per day of treated water leaving the MBR reactor to feed multilayered vegetative covers on the grounds and evaporative green walls around the ground floor. The latent heat of evaporation swallows the heat of the air, raising a canopy of cool, stable air around the bases of the towers which is drawn inward through the corridors.
**Metrics:** A 4- to 6-degree reduction of the microclimate temperature around the building at the peak of summer heat; a 20 to 30 percent cut in the complex's chiller cooling load.
**Prerequisites:** [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](../water-and-matter/treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale) (continuous, uninterrupted supply of treated effluent); [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) (a 6-meter free ground-floor height to carry the cool breeze through).
**Conventional-model flaw:** Stripping the grounds of their vegetation on the excuse of water shortage and firing condenser heat directly into the desertified streets of the conventional settlement.
**Tags:** passive cooling, evapotranspiration, microclimate, water cycle, cooling-load reduction
**Scale:** Building micro-scale and the inter-building plane
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | Spraying reclaimed water in the existing fabric without automatic hygienic filtering ends in mosquito breeding and the spread of disease. The mechanism requires automatic monitoring of water quality.
**Cognitive anchor:** The pleasant, soul-lifting coolness of "the hawz pools and pāshūyeh basins of Shiraz's traditional courtyard houses" — the gentle circulation of water that makes hot summer air agreeable to sit in.
**Narrative hook:** "The courtyard pool cooled the house before any machine existed — we only hand the pool the water the building has already used."
**Syn (synergies):** [Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](../form-and-land/airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture) — wind corridors; [Thermal-suction solar updraft stack (Solar Updraft Tower) in closed farms and supersstructures](solar-tower-purpose.md#thermal-suction-solar-updraft-stack-solar-updraft-tower-in-closed-farms-and-supersstructures) — solar-stack suction; the removal of sidewalk heat traps

#### Inverted modular solar windcatchers with a ceramic evaporative downwell in the facade (Passive Evaporative Downdraft Wind-Tower)
**Mechanism:** A re-creation of the principles of the ancient windcatchers inside the integrated shafts of building facades. Hot, dry air above the tower is drawn down and passed through porous ceramic packings wetted with treated MBR effluent; as the latent heat of evaporation pulls the dry-bulb temperature down, the air — now dense and heavy — descends toward the living floors without any electromechanical fan, delivering a breeze at 22 degrees on the residential levels.
**Metrics:** A 12- to 16-degree drop of inlet-air temperature at the height of midsummer; a cooling output equivalent to 15 to 25 kilowatts per shaft with no compressor electricity.
**Prerequisites:** Antifungal porous nanoceramic packings; capillary drip piping that carries treated water to the head of the shaft.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Forgetting the engineering patterns of the desert and imposing power-hungry gas coolers that fling their heat back into the open air.
**Tags:** modern windcatcher, passive cooling, evaporative well, Iranian architectural heritage, fan-free ventilation
**Scale:** Building micro-scale (vertical shafts in the facade)
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | A wetted ceramic packing inside a building shaft is plumbing and microbiology, not ornament: in the existing fabric it can be hung on a facade only where a treated-water riser reaches the parapet and the down-draught can core through the floors — otherwise the shaft is a decorative duct; and packing wetted with any water whose quality is not continuously assured becomes the infection risk this document's own evaporative ground record names. The antifungal packing of the prerequisites is therefore a duty enforced by monitoring, not a material property. In greenfield, capillary lines cast into the facade at build time make the device the windcatcher's honest descendant.
**Cognitive anchor:** The down-draught *bādgīr* towers of old Yazd — structures that walked fresh air into the rooms at noon without ever consuming a coin of electricity, their breath cooled by damp surfaces the household kept wet as a matter of course.
**Narrative hook:** "The old windcatchers of Yazd blew through these rooms for free — this one only remembers to wet its breath, and hands the floors twenty-two degrees."
**Syn (synergies):** [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](../water-and-matter/treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale) — MBR treated water; [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — injection of the cool breeze at ground-floor level; reduced chiller electricity
**Source:** supplementary idea of the system architect — fusion of vernacular architecture with fluid dynamics

#### Passive infrared radiative cooling to the depths of space around the clock (Sub-Ambient Radiative Cooling)
**Mechanism:** Exploit the "atmospheric transmission window" (wavelength 8 to 13 micrometres) under the very clear, dry sky of Shiraz. Roofs and suspended canopies are clad in optical metamaterial films with high infrared emittance so they send their heat directly to the cold of space; the technology keeps the surfaces 4 to 8 degrees cooler than the surrounding air even under the midday summer sun.
**Metrics:** Net passive cooling power: 70 to 100 watts per square metre under clear sky; a 20 to 30 percent cut in buildings' cooling load with no water or electricity consumed.
**Prerequisites:** Weather with low relative humidity (the climatic nature of Shiraz); polymer paints and films reflecting 96% of visible light.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Black bitumenized roofs that drink in the noon heat and turn the ceiling into a 70-degree stove.
**Tags:** radiative cooling, atmospheric window, cooling without electricity, optical metamaterial, photonic physics
**Scale:** Building micro-scale (roof and PRT-canopy cladding)
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The film is a surface, not a structure, so it can be laid on existing roofs and station canopies building by building; the force-fit failure is exact — emissivity only pays through a clear sky-view and a dry atmospheric window, and the existing fabric's blackened, neighbour-shaded flat roofs deliver both badly: the 96-percent visible reflectance of the prerequisites decays to ordinary soiled sheeting within a dust season unless washed, and wash water is precisely the resource the settlement conserves. In greenfield the film is a roof specification written once, and every canopy that shades the sun also serves the sky.
**Cognitive anchor:** The roof-night sleeps of the old plateau houses — Yazd and Kashan summers in which a family slept out under the open sky and the pre-dawn cold woke them with blankets thrown aside, long before the same day's walls had finished ticking with stored heat: the memory that the open night sky drinks heat fast enough to be called cold.
**Narrative hook:** "A desert night is colder than its own day, because the sky drinks heat — the roof now owns that straw, and it never closes."
**Syn (synergies):** [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) — carrier balancing and time-shifting; [Seasonally reversible in-wall HVAC (In-Wall Seasonal Reversible HVAC)](../buildings/placement-and-routing-of-service-flows.md#seasonally-reversible-in-wall-hvac-in-wall-seasonal-reversible-hvac) — dump load of the in-wall HVAC systems; [Fast suspended personal-transport network at 9 meters height (PRT / SkyTran / Hyperloop)](../movement/human-travel-network.md#fast-suspended-personal-transport-network-at-9-meters-height-prt--skytran--hyperloop) — the elevated PRT station canopies as radiative surface
**Source:** supplementary idea of the system architect — quantum photonic thermodynamics

#### Passive night-flush ventilation cooling (Night-Flush) and neutralization of the radiation trap in climates with diurnal swings above 15 degrees
**Mechanism:** Exploit the climatic feature of settlements like Shiraz, whose summer night temperatures stay below 25 degrees even at the height of the heat (18 to 22 degrees Celsius). By redesigning the building envelope and a system of smart control dampers, cool night air is blown through channels in the body of the building's thermal mass, pre-cooling the structure for the next day. At the same time, replacing asphalt surfaces with permeable paving neutralizes the nighttime infrared re-radiation of the materials, and the electricity demand for cooling at night falls to zero.
**Metrics:** Shiraz's average summer night temperature: 18 °C to 22 °C; a 100 percent cut in cooling-electricity consumption in the hours 23 to 7 in the morning; day-night temperature difference of the plain: more than 15 degrees Celsius; cooling reserve stored in the structural mass: 4 to 6 hours at the start of the day.
**Prerequisites:** Keeping the open draught corridors of the mountain wind in the settlement's layout; smart ventilation dampers in the envelope; heavy thermal insulation of the external facade.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Trapping heat in streets paved with asphalt and concrete, and closing the windows at night while low-COP compression gas coolers run continuously — returning the day's heat to the street after dark and burning out the settlement's transformers.
**Tags:** night flushing, Night-Flush, Shiraz microclimate, structural thermal inertia, passive cooling, zeroing the night's electricity
**Scale:** Building micro-scale / the plain-climate plane
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Night-flush is what the conventional settlement's old houses did by habit, so the physics is free; what the record itself makes expensive is the package: heavy external insulation and controllable dampers are wet-wall work for whole facades, and the prerequisite — holding the mountain-breeze corridors open in the settlement layout — is a planning act no single building can retrofit, because the dense existing fabric has already sealed its own wind channels and its asphalt re-radiates past midnight, so the "cool" night air that arrives is often warmer than the mass it is meant to flush. In greenfield the path is the cheap one: set the layout, size the mass, and let the dampers do the arithmetic.
**Cognitive anchor:** Shiraz's summer nights with the windows thrown open — the mountain breeze crossing the rooms from the *takht-dar* courtyards and cooling the stone by dawn, a household habit that needed no switch, no wire and no apology to the meter.
**Narrative hook:** "The night was always the cheap half of the day — eighteen to twenty-two degrees walking through the house while it sleeps; we only added the dampers that never forget."
**Syn (synergies):** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — the transparent 6-meter ground floor; [Computed free distance between buildings in the aerial view, for wind-draught flow and radiation capture](../form-and-land/airflow-through-fabric.md#computed-free-distance-between-buildings-in-the-aerial-view-for-wind-draught-flow-and-radiation-capture) — free building distance and wind corridors; [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) — time-shifting of energy; [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) — the 5GDHC thermal loop
**Source:** session dialogue — thermodynamic analysis of Shiraz's nights and the heat trap

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The two evaporative answers draw on one water budget, and the budget is stated only once.** [Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds](passive-cooling-paths.md#local-ecological-cooling-through-surface-evaporation-of-reclaimed-water-on-facades-and-grounds) is explicitly fed by "the 75 thousand litres per day of treated water from the MBR reactor," and [Inverted modular solar windcatchers with a ceramic evaporative downwell in the facade (Passive Evaporative Downdraft Wind-Tower)](passive-cooling-paths.md#inverted-modular-solar-windcatchers-with-a-ceramic-evaporative-downwell-in-the-facade-passive-evaporative-downdraft-wind-tower) lists the same stream as its wetting source. But evaporation is consumption: every litre that cools the green walls has left the ledger and cannot also wet the ceramic packing the same afternoon. One question, two answers. **Answer one (single-pass cascade):** order the two uses on the water's path — grounds and facades first, because the breeze they cool the tower base with is drawn up past the shafts anyway, and the packing takes the permeate's later passes — which turns the pair into one machine with one meter but makes [Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds](passive-cooling-paths.md#local-ecological-cooling-through-surface-evaporation-of-reclaimed-water-on-facades-and-grounds) the throttle on [Inverted modular solar windcatchers with a ceramic evaporative downwell in the facade (Passive Evaporative Downdraft Wind-Tower)](passive-cooling-paths.md#inverted-modular-solar-windcatchers-with-a-ceramic-evaporative-downwell-in-the-facade-passive-evaporative-downdraft-wind-tower)'s 15 to 25 kilowatts per shaft. **Answer two (two meters, one ration):** size each use against the daily total and publish both quotas, which preserves each record's independence but forces a verdict on which use is curtailed first when the effluent is short or hot — a question neither record asks. Either way the adjudicator should note what the pair implies for the water cycle: the permeate's destination after these two evaporations is the atmosphere, and the settlement's water document owns that account, not this one.

**The savings are booked twice on the same load, and the document owns no rule of summation.** [Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds](passive-cooling-paths.md#local-ecological-cooling-through-surface-evaporation-of-reclaimed-water-on-facades-and-grounds) claims "20 to 30 percent" off the complex's chiller cooling load, and [Passive infrared radiative cooling to the depths of space around the clock (Sub-Ambient Radiative Cooling)](passive-cooling-paths.md#passive-infrared-radiative-cooling-to-the-depths-of-space-around-the-clock-sub-ambient-radiative-cooling) claims "20 to 30 percent" off buildings' cooling load; between them, [Passive night-flush ventilation cooling (Night-Flush) and neutralization of the radiation trap in climates with diurnal swings above 15 degrees](passive-cooling-paths.md#passive-night-flush-ventilation-cooling-night-flush-and-neutralization-of-the-radiation-trap-in-climates-with-diurnal-swings-above-15-degrees) zeroes cooling electricity between 23 and 7 in the morning, a slice of the night the 24-hour radiative claim also occupies. The records are not lying — they measure against different denominators (complex vs. building; machine electricity vs. heat load) — but combined adoption has no arithmetic owner. One question, two answers. **Answer one (marginal reading):** each path's percentage applies to the load the paths before it left, so a portfolio claim is capped by construction and every record's number survives intact as a marginal figure. **Answer two (ledger reading):** the document itself fixes a single combined ceiling for passive contribution and each record is judged against its share — which is honest about physics but re-cuts numbers the owners wrote, and invites exactly the commission bargaining the project's rules docs indict. The first keeps every claim; the second keeps one total.

**As a ladder of one heat's route the four compose without spending twice.** The paths address consecutive stations on the way heat escapes: the grounds and green walls ([Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds](passive-cooling-paths.md#local-ecological-cooling-through-surface-evaporation-of-reclaimed-water-on-facades-and-grounds)) cool the air before it enters the tower base; the shafts ([Inverted modular solar windcatchers with a ceramic evaporative downwell in the facade (Passive Evaporative Downdraft Wind-Tower)](passive-cooling-paths.md#inverted-modular-solar-windcatchers-with-a-ceramic-evaporative-downwell-in-the-facade-passive-evaporative-downdraft-wind-tower)) deliver a 22-degree downdraught to the living floors without a fan; the films ([Passive infrared radiative cooling to the depths of space around the clock (Sub-Ambient Radiative Cooling)](passive-cooling-paths.md#passive-infrared-radiative-cooling-to-the-depths-of-space-around-the-clock-sub-ambient-radiative-cooling)) stop the roof from adding its noon debt to the structure; and the night flush ([Passive night-flush ventilation cooling (Night-Flush) and neutralization of the radiation trap in climates with diurnal swings above 15 degrees](passive-cooling-paths.md#passive-night-flush-ventilation-cooling-night-flush-and-neutralization-of-the-radiation-trap-in-climates-with-diurnal-swings-above-15-degrees)) banks 4 to 6 hours of coolth in the mass before the day starts — each rung's gain is the next rung's supply, and the water cost falls only where the two evaporative rungs stand. The same ladder exposes the surface conflict no record names: roof and canopy area is claimed by the films, facade shafts by the windcatchers, street surface by the paving swap, and ground by the green walls — one envelope, four tenants; as a portfolio the document should adjudicate the envelope, not the percentages. And the ladder's top feeds a document outside this one: every watt that leaves passively is a watt the thermal bus of [Directed Energy Transfer](directed-energy-transfer.md) never sees — this document's reductions are that network's sink arithmetic, which makes them declarations the network should read, not news it discovers.

**The sharpest unresolved collision is water against sky, and it closes on one decision with two answers.** The two zero-water paths presuppose exactly the conditions — dry air, a clear 8-to-13-micrometre window, a swing above 15 degrees — that make the settlement's water scarce enough to need the two evaporative paths' careful rationing: the four answers are ranked inversely by the same climate variable. Should the portfolio be gated per-climate — each path carrying its own admission clause (evaporation only where effluent outlasts demand; radiative and flush only where the sky and the swing hold) — or should one sink be canonical plain-wide so that blocks remain comparable and the 20-to-30-percent claims auditable against a single denominator? The first answer follows the records: all four are written with Shiraz in mind, and none of them claims to work elsewhere. The second answer follows the arithmetic: per-climate gates multiply exceptions, and an exception-per-climate is the rule [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) exists to dissolve. Either way, an adjudication that keeps all four must say which resource is the numeraire — litres, square metres of envelope, or degrees of saved machine-work — because at present no two records count the same coin.
