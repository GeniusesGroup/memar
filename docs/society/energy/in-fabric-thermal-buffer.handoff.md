2. **Does the envelope hold a fixed share of the buffer, or earn it competitively?** The page's question ("fabric or equipment?") is answered here twice for the fabric and nowhere for the equipment — the equipment answer lives in `thermal-sources-and-storage.md` (water and soil stores, ice sink). Fixed share: envelope buffers take the fast daily swing by right of being built anyway, and equipment is sized to the remainder. Competitive: the equipment answer is fully specified first, and the fabric layers earn what the tank cannot catch — which lets a wall that fails its cycle-life prerequisite fail quietly into a covered network. One answer strands equipment capacity, the other prices a safety margin into plaster. *(Why: it decides which document's numbers the settlement's storage sizing reads from, and the direction-of-dependency rule forbids this page from sizing the equipment side itself. Blocking: yes for a fabric-equipment combined storage plan; no for either record's standalone adoption.)*
