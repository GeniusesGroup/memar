# Authority for the Indoor Climate Signal

## Abstract
This document stakes one position: the wall thermostat is not the authority it is treated as, and the setpoint is a claim about a body, not about air. Its requirement — "Should the indoor climate setpoint be given by a fixed standard program, or by measured physiology?" — carries two recorded answers that agree the single-point air sensor is the wrong instrument and disagree about what replaces it: the occupant's own operative temperature, read from skin, perspiration and metabolism and served room by room without a direct-blow draft ([Bio-adaptive control of the indoor climate on operative temperature and body physiology (Bio-Adaptive Control)](climate-signal-authority.md#bio-adaptive-control-of-the-indoor-climate-on-operative-temperature-and-body-physiology-bio-adaptive-control)); and an environmental-metabolic data bus that fuses barometric pressure, humidity, thermal gradient and physiological signal into the felt temperature, meters it against a sub-0.5-degree tolerance, and carries the whole picture into the building's digital record ([Environmental-metabolic data bus for optimizing HVAC operative temperature (Environmental-Metabolic Data Bus for HVAC)](climate-signal-authority.md#environmental-metabolic-data-bus-for-optimizing-hvac-operative-temperature-environmental-metabolic-data-bus-for-hvac)). The first is a controller, the second is the infrastructure a controller presupposes — the bus names the control record as a prerequisite, the control record never names the bus, and the asymmetry is the document's shape: one answer can fail quietly without the other, and the other cannot act at all without the first. Adjudication of these answers will be logged in this document's changelog.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Split into their own records, the two answers read as an app feature and a wiring protocol — and an objection filed against wearable sensors ("my sweat is not the building's data") lands on the wrong half of a control loop, while an objection filed against protocol lock-in never reaches the body whose discomfort both answers exist to end. They must be weighed together precisely because the second names the first as a prerequisite: the pair is one institution with two documents' worth of silence between them — who holds authority when a room contains several bodies, and who owns the physiological pipe that both answers drink from.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
Should the indoor climate setpoint be given by a fixed standard program, or by measured physiology?

Sharpened, the shared question has three edges the two records leave open. (1) **Authority.** A standard program is an averaged body legislated in advance; measured physiology is the present body legislated in real time — but neither record says what the unit of authority is: the occupant (in whose skin, perspiration and metabolism the signal is written) or the room (which must serve several such units at once). (2) **Actuation.** Both answers speak through terminals — in-wall air handlers the records presuppose, old chillers the retrofit Fit indicts — so the setpoint question cannot be settled apart from whether the terminal can obey a dynamic command; a signal with no actuator is a dashboard. (3) **Data.** The operative-temperature signal is personal health data held on a community platform and logged into the building's BIM, yet neither record states who may read it, on what consent, or who is told when it fails — the physiology the document elevates to authority arrives with no custody rule attached. The two answers do not disagree about the thermophysiology they share; they disagree about where in the settlement that knowledge gets to live.

### Approaches

#### Bio-adaptive control of the indoor climate on operative temperature and body physiology (Bio-Adaptive Control)
**Mechanism:** Dismantle the blind wall thermostats that merely measure the temperature of one point of air. Using ambient thermal infrared sensors or the biological data of wearable gadgets, the ventilation system monitors the occupant's felt "Operative Temperature" on the basis of skin temperature, perspiration and metabolism; it then secures that person's thermal comfort, without any direct and irritating blast of air, by regulating the relative-humidity rate and the radiant temperature of the surfaces.
**Metrics:** A 20 percent energy saving through the prevention of over-cooling or over-heating; dynamic fit to the sleep, rest and athletic-activity scenarios.
**Prerequisites:** Thermopile-array sensors (Thermopile IR); biological comfort-estimation artificial-intelligence algorithms on the complex's platform.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Conventional nausea-inducing HVAC that, from a false reading of stagnant air temperature, makes the room too cold or too hot and breeds headaches and illness.
**Tags:** thermal comfort, operative temperature, biological IoT, metabolic monitoring, thermal sensors
**Scale:** Building micro-scale
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Installable in smart buildings; its risk is that old conventional chillers do not answer the artificial intelligence's dynamic commands.
**Cognitive anchor:** The engineered comfort of "spaceship control rooms" — a temperature set by the occupants' bodily state rather than by the air itself.
**Narrative hook:** "The corner thermostat reads the air; this one reads you — like a mission control that keeps its eye on the crew, not the cabin."
**Syn (synergies):** [Seasonally reversible in-wall HVAC (In-Wall Seasonal Reversible HVAC)](../buildings/placement-and-routing-of-service-flows.md#seasonally-reversible-in-wall-hvac-in-wall-seasonal-reversible-hvac) — the in-wall air handlers; [Daylighting architecture and the optimization of incidence angle](../buildings/daylight-to-interiors.md#daylighting-architecture-and-the-optimization-of-incidence-angle) — the occupant's digital health-and-sleep file

#### Environmental-metabolic data bus for optimizing HVAC operative temperature (Environmental-Metabolic Data Bus for HVAC)
**Mechanism:** Connect the in-wall air-handler equipment to a multivariable data bus carrying barometric-pressure sensors, relative humidity, thermal gradient and the occupant's physiological signals. Instead of measuring dry air temperature, the system calibrates the felt "Operative Temperature" (Felt/Operative Temperature) against the ambient air pressure and the individual's respiratory metabolism, and regulates the airflow gently, without a direct and irritating blast.
**Metrics:** Environmental sampling rate: real time; thermal-comfort error tolerance: below 0.5 degrees Celsius; recording and transmission of the pressure, humidity and temperature parameters to the building's BIM.
**Prerequisites:** [Cancelling retail packaging: food and dairy flow from cold store to meal in large reusable steel vessels](../food/meals-and-shared-cooking.md#cancelling-retail-packaging-food-and-dairy-flow-from-cold-store-to-meal-in-large-reusable-steel-vessels) (reversible in-wall air handling), [Bio-adaptive control of the indoor climate on operative temperature and body physiology (Bio-Adaptive Control)](climate-signal-authority.md#bio-adaptive-control-of-the-indoor-climate-on-operative-temperature-and-body-physiology-bio-adaptive-control), an open sensor-communication protocol.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Building and vehicle fittings that lean on cheap single-point thermostats which, without any grasp of air pressure or humidity, throw cold or hot wind straight at the body and derange biological comfort.
**Tags:** infrastructural data, fittings Internet of Things, atmospheric-pressure monitoring, operative temperature, Felt Temp algorithm
**Scale:** Building micro-scale and automation network
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The bus is a spine of sensors and open protocols running between terminals the existing fabric does not own — conventional split and ducted units neither publish their state nor accept dynamic command, which is exactly the refusal [Bio-adaptive control of the indoor climate on operative temperature and body physiology (Bio-Adaptive Control)](climate-signal-authority.md#bio-adaptive-control-of-the-indoor-climate-on-operative-temperature-and-body-physiology-bio-adaptive-control)'s own Fit indicts in old chillers; force-fitted, the bus becomes instrumentation without actuation, a real-time reading of a room it cannot move. In a greenfield block the bus is a wiring clause beside the in-wall units it feeds: one protocol, many controllers, no per-room rediscovery of the occupant's skin.
**Cognitive anchor:** The *shāhdān* of the old desert houses — the basement summer room whose qanat-breeze mouth the household opened or choked back by feel, reading the body's coolness rather than the air's, until the whole family sat comfortable in a room no thermometer had been consulted about.
**Narrative hook:** "The old shāhdān cooled people, not rooms — the bus only gives the house eyes for who is sitting in it."

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[Bio-adaptive control of the indoor climate on operative temperature and body physiology (Bio-Adaptive Control)](climate-signal-authority.md#bio-adaptive-control-of-the-indoor-climate-on-operative-temperature-and-body-physiology-bio-adaptive-control) and [Environmental-metabolic data bus for optimizing HVAC operative temperature (Environmental-Metabolic Data Bus for HVAC)](climate-signal-authority.md#environmental-metabolic-data-bus-for-optimizing-hvac-operative-temperature-environmental-metabolic-data-bus-for-hvac) are one control loop wearing two records' names, and their asymmetry is a decision, not an accident.** The bus lists the controller as a prerequisite while the controller never mentions the bus — so the adjudication order is forced: the controller's promise (comfort "without any direct and irritating blast," 20 percent saved by refusing over-conditioning) requires the very fusion instrument the bus is, because operative temperature is not a sensor reading but a computation over air, radiant surface, humidity and body at once; the controller's own mechanism text quietly rebuilds a private mini-bus inside each room (ambient infrared arrays plus wearable feeds plus AI estimation), which is what the bus exists to share. Read as composition, the pair is efficient and unremarkable: the bus measures the room's world, the controller adjudicates the body's claim, the in-wall terminals obey. Read as rivals, they disagree about economy: does each dwelling run its own sensing stack (the controller as written) or does the block run one bus and thin each dwelling to a client (the bus as written)? The records' prerequisites do not overlap except by accident — the controller's list stops at sensors and algorithms; the bus's list reaches into health files and the BIM record — so whichever way the owner rules, one of the two records is missing a line the other already wrote.

**The sub-0.5-degree tolerance and the 20-percent saving are reconcilable only because they hold different things constant — and no record says so.** [Environmental-metabolic data bus for optimizing HVAC operative temperature (Environmental-Metabolic Data Bus for HVAC)](climate-signal-authority.md#environmental-metabolic-data-bus-for-optimizing-hvac-operative-temperature-environmental-metabolic-data-bus-for-hvac) guarantees comfort error below 0.5 °C; [Bio-adaptive control of the indoor climate on operative temperature and body physiology (Bio-Adaptive Control)](climate-signal-authority.md#bio-adaptive-control-of-the-indoor-climate-on-operative-temperature-and-body-physiology-bio-adaptive-control) saves 20 percent precisely by letting the *air* drift where the *felt* temperature need not. The pair works if the tight tolerance is a property of operative (felt) temperature and the savings a property of air temperature: the room air may swing several degrees while the occupant never feels it, because humidity and radiant surfaces absorb the correction. The moment the tolerance is read against dry air — the conventional meaning a commission would give it — the two metrics are in open contradiction: a 0.5-degree air band forbids the drift that pays for the 20 percent. One question, two answers. **Answer one (felt band):** the tolerance belongs to computed operative temperature, the savings to metered energy; the record must then say which quantity the BIM log carries — the tolerance is meaningless without the fusion algorithm it tolerates around. **Answer two (air band):** the tolerance is physical air measurement at the terminal, which keeps the bus verifiable by ordinary instruments but strands most of the claimed saving. Neither reading is stated; the collision is decided by a single omitted word.

**The authority question has a body problem the records share and neither answers: rooms hold plural bodies, and physiology does not average.** Both answers elevate "the occupant" — singular — to authority, but [Bio-adaptive control of the indoor climate on operative temperature and body physiology (Bio-Adaptive Control)](climate-signal-authority.md#bio-adaptive-control-of-the-indoor-climate-on-operative-temperature-and-body-physiology-bio-adaptive-control) runs its scenarios across sleep, rest and athletic activity at once, which is a shared suite at dusk: one dweller asleep, one recovering from a run, one reading. One question, two answers. **Answer one (per-body service):** the radiant and humidity levers are local enough (panels, per-zone in-wall terminals) to serve overlapping comfort bands simultaneously, and the "room" is demoted to a bag of microclimates — the direction the records' own surfaces-not-air physics points, since the mechanism's leverage is the mean radiant temperature, which a chair can own. **Answer two (declared precedence):** one authority per room per span — the sleeper's band wins over the exerciser's, by a rule written into the platform — which keeps the control problem solvable but legislates the old averaging tyranny under a physiological uniform. The choice is genuinely the owner's: it is a rule about people, not about sensors.

**Data custody is the sharpest open seam, and it runs opposite to the dependency direction the project's doctrine fixes.** The operative-temperature signal is, item by item, health data — skin temperature, perspiration, respiratory metabolism — logged by [Environmental-metabolic data bus for optimizing HVAC operative temperature (Environmental-Metabolic Data Bus for HVAC)](climate-signal-authority.md#environmental-metabolic-data-bus-for-optimizing-hvac-operative-temperature-environmental-metabolic-data-bus-for-hvac) into the building's BIM and readable through the health-watch file its synergy line names; yet the only prerequisite resembling a governance rule is "an open sensor-communication protocol," which is an interoperability promise, not a consent one. Per the direction doctrine, consumer documents declare needs toward shared records: here the consumer is declared — the comfort computation needs the body's stream — but the body never files its declaration back. What the document's adjudication must at minimum assign: who may read the pipe, for how long the readings persist, and whether a dwelling may run on environment alone when an occupant refuses the wearable. Refusing the wearable without an environmental-only fallback returns that room to the fixed standard program — the very authority this document was written to dethrone — which makes the fallback not a privacy nicety but a consistency requirement of the page's own thesis.
