## Open Questions
1. **Is the 75,000 litres-per-day effluent one budget or two?** [Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds](passive-cooling-paths.md#local-ecological-cooling-through-surface-evaporation-of-reclaimed-water-on-facades-and-grounds) names the MBR plant's daily treated output as its feed, and [Inverted modular solar windcatchers with a ceramic evaporative downwell in the facade (Passive Evaporative Downdraft Wind-Tower)](passive-cooling-paths.md#inverted-modular-solar-windcatchers-with-a-ceramic-evaporative-downwell-in-the-facade-passive-evaporative-downdraft-wind-tower) wets its packing from the same stream — but evaporation consumes: a litre that cooled the green walls is gone. Either a single-pass cascade (grounds first, packing downstream — which throttles the shaft record's 15 to 25 kilowatts per shaft by the grounds record's spending) or two published quotas with a curtailment order nobody has written. *(Why: the water is the binding resource of the whole document; both records' metrics are conditional on it, and an unruled shared feedstock guarantees over-commitment. Blocking: yes for any combined verdict on the two evaporative records; no for the radiative or night-flush records.)*
2. **What rule of summation governs the 20-to-30-percent claims?** the grounds record books "20 to 30 percent" against the complex's chiller load, [Passive infrared radiative cooling to the depths of space around the clock (Sub-Ambient Radiative Cooling)](passive-cooling-paths.md#passive-infrared-radiative-cooling-to-the-depths-of-space-around-the-clock-sub-ambient-radiative-cooling) books "20 to 30 percent" against buildings' cooling load, and [Passive night-flush ventilation cooling (Night-Flush) and neutralization of the radiation trap in climates with diurnal swings above 15 degrees](passive-cooling-paths.md#passive-night-flush-ventilation-cooling-night-flush-and-neutralization-of-the-radiation-trap-in-climates-with-diurnal-swings-above-15-degrees) zeroes machine electricity between 23 and 7 — a window the film record's 24-hour claim also occupies. Marginal reading (each claim applies to what the earlier paths left) preserves every number; ledger reading (one combined passive ceiling, records judged to their share) preserves one truth but re-cuts owners' figures. *(Why: this document's savings are the input to [Directed Energy Transfer](directed-energy-transfer.md)'s sink arithmetic — double-booked cooling reductions silently inflate the 40-to-60-kWh-per-dwelling condenser surplus the thermal bus is sized against. Blocking: yes for any portfolio verdict and for the cross-document declaration.)*
3. **Which climate variables gate which path, and where is the gate written?** Every record is authored with the Shiraz-type plateau in mind (dry air, clear 8-to-13-micrometre window, swing above 15 degrees, nights at 18 to 22 degrees); the two evaporative paths work best where water is scarcest, the two sky paths fail on humid coasts regardless of money. Should each record carry its own climate admission clause, or should the document declare the settlement-climate to which the portfolio is calibrated? *(Why: a society at any scale is the unit — a path adopted as universal will be force-fit in Bandar-type humidity where the film record's prerequisites collapse; the reviewer-authored Fits for the shaft record, the film record and the night-flush record flag the decay mechanisms but cannot choose the gate. Blocking: no for the need; yes for verdicts that generalize beyond the plateau.)*
4. **Does passive reduction of chiller load get declared toward the thermal network?** The one-way dependency doctrine makes consumer documents declare their needs toward network documents; here the relation is inverted in kind — passive cooling reduces what the network must carry. If the grounds record/the film record/the night-flush record reductions are not declared, [Directed Energy Transfer](directed-energy-transfer.md)'s capacity matching reads stale chiller surpluses; if declared, they need a format (band? percentage? seasonal curve?). *(Why: the two documents' metrics are coupled through one physical flow that neither document owns. Blocking: no for each record individually; yes for the pair's joint verdicts.)*
5. **The reviewer's own disagreement (critique).** (a) the grounds record's Fit demands "automatic monitoring of water quality" as its own condition, though the water is already declared MBR permeate — the reviewer reads this as either redundant with the treatment record's guarantees or as evidence the Fit was written for recycled water of unproven quality; the owner should say which, because it decides whether the grounds record's prerequisite list is complete. (b) the night-flush record's mechanism attributes a "100 percent" cut in cooling electricity to the hours 23–7 only under the assumption that the structure stays cool past 10 in the morning (its own metric says 4 to 6 hours of reserve) — the claim and the reserve window are in tension for late summer afternoons, and the record resolves it by silence. *(Why: (a) decides whether a fifth prerequisite belongs in the grounds record; (b) is the difference between a 100-percent-night claim and a 4-to-6-hour claim. Blocking: no — but both should be settled before adjudication is logged.)*
