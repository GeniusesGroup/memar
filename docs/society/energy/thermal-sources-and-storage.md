# Thermal Sources and Storage

## Abstract
Any system that takes energy in the low-use, cheap window and returns it in the peak, expensive window is a battery; the chemical cell is only one instance, and on this platform the native instances dominate — the foundation under the building, the aquifer under the plain, the counterweight in the shaft, the ice kept in the depths, the hydrogen jarred from August for Bahman, the water raised to the roof at night. This document holds one question — at what abstraction may the long heat and cold pools (soil, aquifer, geothermal, shaft) be defined as network source and battery while staying serviceable for everything else they already do? — and records seven answers that differ on two axes at once. Where they store: the open ground, the structure's own mass, the basement, the height above it. What physics returns the joule: temperature, chemistry, or gravity. The seven agree on the working definition of the battery and disagree on everything that follows it — drilling versus excavating, device versus schedule, a sovereign floor versus a shared one — and the deepest disagreement is [Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation)'s claim that the greatest store already in hand is the municipal water network itself, needing nothing but its pumping hours moved. Adjudication of these answers will be logged in this document's changelog.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The six records of this page compete over the same joule and the same cubic volume — a seventh, recovered from the owner's own energy-competition proposal, joins the competition as compressed air, the named-but-unrecorded member of the non-chemical battery family. Three of them move into the negative floors — a counterweight shaft, a hydrogen hall, a minus-40 ice zone — and none reconciles the excavation they share; the ground the aquifer store charges with summer heat is the same ground whose water table a metric promises to keep stable, and the foundation whose mass is recorded here as a free heat exchanger is a prerequisite of the very basement the others occupy. Scattered, an objection to one record — saltwater upconing from injection wells, say — is filed against all ground storage, while the affected reader never sees that another answer pays for that risk with an excavation it had to dig anyway. And the word "battery," left undefined in front of the six, drifts back to the lithium cell every record was written to refuse; the terminology section below is therefore placed before the approaches, not after them, so each record is read against the owner's working definition rather than against the industry default.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
At what abstraction do the long heat and cold pools — soil, aquifer, geothermal, shaft — count as network source and storage, and remain serviceable?

Sharpened, the shared problem is that a ground is never only a battery. The alluvium is the plain's drinking reserve and the settlement's support material and its heat bank at once, and every answer must name the abstraction at which it touches the ground without breaking what the ground already serves. [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes) reads the aquifer as a thermal reservoir and lists its temperature stabilization as a metric — a duty stated without a limit. [Thermoactive deep load-bearing foundations (Energy Piles & Diaphragm Heat Exchangers)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#thermoactive-deep-load-bearing-foundations-energy-piles--diaphragm-heat-exchangers) reads the structure itself as the exchanger, serviceable by construction: the loop is poured inside the rebar that was already paying for the soil. Three records — the shaft, the hydrogen hall, the cryo-zone — read the basement as a machine hall, and none asks the floor-area budget of a level that houses all three. [Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation) cuts the abstraction question from below: it adds no storage vessel at all, only a schedule — "its pumping time must shift" — which makes the network itself the largest store in hand. Whoever adjudicates this requirement is choosing, for each substrate, which of its services the battery abstraction may crowd and which it may never touch.

### Terminology: what "battery" means here
**Battery** in these documents does not mean a chemical cell. Working definition: any system that takes energy in a low-use, cheap window and delivers it in a peak, expensive window is a battery. The chemical storage tank is only one instance of that; on this platform the native, non-chemical instances dominate: the thermal mass of a building, ice and cold water underground, compressed air ([Compressed-air energy storage: the fifth non-chemical battery — air compressed in off-peak windows and returned at peak, the concept of peak itself abolished (Compressed-Air Battery)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#compressed-air-energy-storage-the-fifth-non-chemical-battery--air-compressed-in-off-peak-windows-and-returned-at-peak-the-concept-of-peak-itself-abolished-compressed-air-battery)), and **an elevated water reservoir**. Wherever this word appears, this definition is meant.

### Approaches

#### Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)
**Mechanism:** The alluvial bed and sand layers of the plains hold a colossal geotechnical thermal capacity. In summer, the surplus of reject heat from the settlement's chillers that the consumption systems did not absorb is injected, through deep closed-loop wells, into the subsurface alluvial layers; the soil holds the heat confined. In winter, reversing the direction of the pumps extracts this stored heat as the preheating source for the chillers and heat pumps, lifting winter heating efficiency to its highest possible level.
**Metrics:** Injection depth of the closed-loop wells: 50 to 120 meters into the sand layer; seasonal heat-recovery efficiency: 65 to 80 percent; stabilization of the water-table temperature to counter thermal stress on the soil.
**Prerequisites:** Hydrogeological modelling of the plain's groundwater flow, to prevent the stored heat from being washed away; doublet wells (paired injection and extraction wells).
**Conventional-model flaw:** Rejecting heat to the open air in summer and burning gas greedily in winter, without using the ground's free thermal capacitor.
**Tags:** seasonal geothermal, ATES, UTES, aquifer storage, thermal stability, soil inertia
**Scale:** Macro — plain and hydrogeology
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Drilling deep heat-injection wells under the dense fabric of the existing settlement risks interfering with the sewer network and the metro, coning up salt water and undermining structures' foundations, and drives municipal management into crisis.
**Cognitive anchor:** The ancient engineering masterpiece of the "mud-brick yakhdāns of the Yazd desert" — storing winter's cool in the depths of the earth for free use in the chelleh of summer's heat.
**Narrative hook:** "The yakhdān buried a winter's cold to spend it in July — the aquifer is the same vault, except now the pumps read the meter."
**Syn (synergies):** [Absolute protection of the soil bed: aerial concrete pedestrian lines and non-mortar dry-laid stone paving](../form-and-land/ground-surface-treatment.md#absolute-protection-of-the-soil-bed-aerial-concrete-pedestrian-lines-and-non-mortar-dry-laid-stone-paving) — protecting the soil, keeping it permeable; [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) — time-shifting of exergy; [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) — the thermal bus; the winter gas imbalance driven to zero

#### Thermoactive deep load-bearing foundations (Energy Piles & Diaphragm Heat Exchangers)
**Mechanism:** When the ten basement levels are built, the retaining walls and deep piles penetrate the soil down to 40 meters. By laying a network of polyethylene (PEX) pipes inside the rebar cages of the piles and retaining walls before the concrete pour, the structural foundation concrete plays, simultaneously, the role of a ground-source heat exchanger. The system supplies the seasonal exchange of heat with the ground without any separate spending on geothermal well drilling.
**Metrics:** Heat-exchange output: 40 to 60 watts per meter of pile length; a full 100 percent reduction of geothermal drilling cost; heat-exchange contact area: tens of thousands of square meters of excavation-wall concrete.
**Prerequisites:** [Vertical zoning of 10 basement levels (Deep Subsurface Clustering) and 35-meter penetration](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#vertical-zoning-of-10-basement-levels-deep-subsurface-clustering-and-35-meter-penetration) (construction of the ten basement levels), [Self-healing crystalline waterproofing and layered drainage of the depths (Crystalline Waterproofing)](../buildings/deep-dryness.md#self-healing-crystalline-waterproofing-and-layered-drainage-of-the-depths-crystalline-waterproofing).
**Conventional-model flaw:** Drilling separate geothermal wells on urban land at ruinous per-meter costs, without using the enormous thermal mass of the shoring structure and the buildings' foundations.
**Tags:** structural geothermal, energy piles, heat-exchanging retaining walls, eliminating separate drilling, UTES
**Scale:** Micro — building (foundation and excavation)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The exchangers are placed in the rebar cage before the pour, so this record exists only where a new deep foundation is being cast — its own prerequisite is the ten-basement excavation. Retrofitting an existing shallow foundation would pay for the very wells this record boasts of eliminating, and piping a finished raft would pierce the waterproofing envelope that deep dryness demands; the "zero drilling cost" metric is earned at pour time or not at all.
**Cognitive anchor:** The load-bearing that was already doing the second job: in the old desert houses, the thick adobe bearing wall *was* the thermal plant — the same mass that carried the roof carried the day's heat off the room and gave it back at night, and the household bought no machine for the second service. The energy pile is that double-duty arithmetic cast in concrete: the forty meters that had to go into the ground for the structure now go into it for the heat pump too.
**Narrative hook:** "The foundation your building had to dig anyway is now the geothermal plant you never paid to drill."
**Syn (synergies):** [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes) — seasonal heat storage in the soil; [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) — injecting summer surplus heat into the foundation bed
**Source:** System-architect supplemental idea

#### Mechanical energy storage on gravity blocks in subterranean shafts (Deep-Shaft GES)
**Mechanism:** Beside the thermal storage pools, electricity is stored without short-lived lithium chemical batteries, through gravity shafts. In the hours of peak sunshine, synchronous motor-generators hoist ultra-heavy concrete blocks — compounded of compacted rubble and iron turnings — inside vertical shafts down to a depth of 35 meters; in the dark hours of night or a grid outage, releasing those weights injects firm, high-efficiency power into the internal network.
**Metrics:** Round-trip efficiency: 75 to 82 percent; operating lifespan: above 60 years with no capacity fade; switch-to-grid time: under 1 second.
**Prerequisites:** A load-bearing structural vertical shaft within the excavation depth; hydraulic winch systems fitted with regenerative charging generators.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Environmental destruction through lithium and cobalt extraction, 5-yearly battery replacement costs, and thermal-fire hazards in enclosed building spaces.
**Tags:** gravitational storage, potential energy, eliminating the chemical battery, concrete inertia, solar grid stabilization
**Scale:** Micro — building (negative floors)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The counterweight needs a hoist shaft of structural depth that only exists where the excavation was planned deep; dropped under an occupied block it would cross the very carrier loops it exists to back up, and a suspended mass can be neither inspected nor rewound while the residents above it sleep. Its under-1-second switching is bought only where the shaft is cast together with the foundations.
**Cognitive anchor:** The old *saʿat-khāneh* clock tower at the town's centre, whose hands were driven by a falling weight — wound on Friday, ticking all week for the whole bazaar, burning nothing, and heard by everyone who set their hour by it. The deep-shaft gravity store is that same falling weight made reversible: the noon sun winds the tower that the night draws down.
**Narrative hook:** "Noon's surplus sun becomes your block's midnight current — wound like the old clock tower, paid back by falling."
**Syn (synergies):** [The centralized photovoltaic mega-plant and the clean energy-pump hub (Clean Energy Station)](large-generation-site.md#the-centralized-photovoltaic-mega-plant-and-the-clean-energy-pump-hub-clean-energy-station) — the solar plant; [Storage water pond under the solar updraft stack (Solar Thermal Pond) for nighttime power production](solar-tower-purpose.md#storage-water-pond-under-the-solar-updraft-stack-solar-thermal-pond-for-nighttime-power-production) — nightly storage; the township immunized against blackouts
**Source:** System-architect supplemental idea

#### Reversible solid-oxide fuel cells and seasonal hydrogen storage (Deep Subsurface rSOC)
**Mechanism:** To resolve the seasonal imbalance — summer's photovoltaic surplus, winter's energy deficit — reversible solid-oxide fuel cells (rSOC) are installed on level minus 10. In summer, surplus photovoltaic power electrolyses water, and the hydrogen produced accumulates in high-pressure composite tanks in the basement. In winter the system reverses, and by consuming the hydrogen it delivers uninterrupted power and 65-degree hot water for the thermal loop.
**Metrics:** Round-trip exergy efficiency of the combined cycle (power plus heat): above 85%; seasonal storage without battery capacity fade; system lifespan above 25 years.
**Prerequisites:** Hydrogen storage tanks in the deep basement with sparkproof ventilation; continuous gas-concentration monitoring by sensors.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Power plants burning mazut in winter for want of gas, while the enormous summer solar potential is lost to the inability to store it seasonally.
**Tags:** fuel cell, rSOC, seasonal hydrogen, energy storage, gas imbalance
**Scale:** Macro — infrastructure and micro — building (level minus 10)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Compressed hydrogen on the lowest floor makes that floor an industrial installation — explosion-proof ventilation, perpetual gas watch — which no existing basement was engineered, ventilated or zoned to host; the retrofit force-fits a hazard radius beneath residents who never consented to it. The record's above-85% round-trip claim is earned only where level minus 10 is drawn around the tanks from the first sketch.
**Cognitive anchor:** The household's summer store — the *anbārsalāneh* in which a season's excess was sealed for the hard months: the summer fruit in glass, the rice and walnuts set aside for chelleh, everything that could not be spent in August kept whole for Bahman. The rSOC jar is the same arithmetic with an element in place of the fruit: August's current, otherwise thrown away, sealed chemically and opened in the cold.
**Narrative hook:** "August's unloved sunshine becomes December's hot water — the house that jars summer for winter has learned to jar the light."
**Syn (synergies):** [The centralized photovoltaic mega-plant and the clean energy-pump hub (Clean Energy Station)](large-generation-site.md#the-centralized-photovoltaic-mega-plant-and-the-clean-energy-pump-hub-clean-energy-station) — the solar plant; [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction) — removing the winter gas need; [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) — the 5GDHC thermal loop
**Source:** System-architect supplemental idea

#### Ice-crystal and skimo living zones in the depths as a winter exergy heat sink (Subterranean Cryo-Zone and Ice Sink)
**Mechanism:** A share of the basement depths is allocated to freezing environments offering the polar-climate experience (temperatures down to minus 40 degrees) or to mechanized ice-making units in winter. Through heat pumps, the heat extracted from this frozen zone is fed into the heating circuits of the buildings and the bakeries; the cooling the cryo-zone demands thus supplies the living spaces with free thermal load.
**Metrics:** Working temperature of the evaporator zone: down to −40°C; a raised heating coefficient of performance (COP) at the depth of winter's cold; consumption and leisure ice produced without net energy loss.
**Prerequisites:** Ultra-heavy cold insulation at depth; a two-stage or cascade refrigerant cycle.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Domestic gas burners making heat while power-hungry chillers run simultaneously in other parts of the same building, burning non-renewable resources directly on both sides.
**Tags:** exergy balance, cold sink, heat pump, polar lifestyle, tourism income, ice production
**Scale:** Micro — building (basement depths)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | A minus-40 envelope inside an existing basement is a refrigerator built into walls it does not own: cold bridging reaches neighbouring units, interstitial condensation attacks the deep dryness the zone itself depends on, and the cascade refrigerant cycle needs a machinery room no conventional basement reserves. The record pays only where the polar zone, its insulation and the dryness around it are excavated together.
**Cognitive anchor:** The Yazdi *qandāb* — the deep cellar pool to which the household retired at the noon of Tir, water cooled by depth and not by machine, the guest honoured with coldness a dig had supplied for generations. The cryo-zone is the *qandāb* put to work in reverse: the cold you excavate keeps paying — first as hospitality, then as the heat your radiators would otherwise burn.
**Narrative hook:** "The cold your guests pay to stand inside is the heat your boiler would otherwise burn — dig the qandāb and collect on both halves."
**Syn (synergies):** [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) — the 5GDHC thermal loop; [Preheating bakeries and processing industries with heat-pump waste heat](directed-energy-transfer.md#preheating-bakeries-and-processing-industries-with-heat-pump-waste-heat) — bakery preheating; [Vertical zoning of 10 basement levels (Deep Subsurface Clustering) and 35-meter penetration](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#vertical-zoning-of-10-basement-levels-deep-subsurface-clustering-and-35-meter-penetration) (the basements' zoning)
**Source:** Yalda justification plan (p. 28)

#### Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation
**Tags:** gravity battery, night-pumped water, elevated reservoir, irrigation co-use, rooftop and foothill tanks
**Scale:** Mid — block (rooftops and the irrigation network) and macro — plain (the foothill reservoirs)
**Mechanism:** Water reservoirs are built on the rooftops of buildings and on the foothills around the plain; in the grid's off-peak night hours, when the surplus of marginal photovoltaic power and low demand coincide, water is pumped into these reservoirs, and by day the irrigation of green space and every pressure-requiring use is served from that height, with no pumping. This is exactly the "battery" in the working definition ([Terminology: what "battery" means here](#terminology-what-battery-means-here)): cheap nighttime electricity converted into gravitational potential and released in the peak window — no chemistry, no attritional recycling, a multi-decade life, and a consumer that already exists (the irrigation network).
**Metrics:** 100% of the irrigation pumping load shifted from the peak window to the night window; reservoir volume matched to the block's morning and evening irrigation flow; reservoir height = the pressure the irrigation network needs, with no daytime consumer pump.
**Prerequisites:** Rooftop structural capacity and siting (per the multi-purpose-roof rule, [Multifunctional Surfaces](../buildings/multifunctional-surfaces.md)); transmission lines from the foothill reservoirs to the irrigation network; level control so the stores are not drained empty on windless, sunless nights.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The notion that "energy storage = a lithium battery," which brings cost, attrition and import dependence — while the municipal water network itself is the largest storage in hand, and only its pumping time must shift.
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | This is the one battery in this document that does not wait for a deep excavation: a rooftop tank is a plumbing item, not a geotechnical one, and the neighbourhood already knows the concrete roof reservoir that kept taps alive through blackouts. The force-fit failure is structural and hydraulic, not thermodynamic — a full reservoir is a dead load the old roof slab was never designed for, and the foothill tanks need gravity legs the legacy irrigation network does not have; where the roof cannot carry the water or the network cannot deliver the pressure, the record pays in reinforcement the "no chemistry" virtue promised to avoid.
**Cognitive anchor:** The concrete water tank on every Iranian rooftop — the quiet reservoir that kept the taps running and the pressure alive on the nights the pumps stood still, the household's first battery, charged with someone else's carelessness. Night pumping makes that remembered independence civic: the same height that once saved a building from a blackout now saves the plain's grid from its peak.
**Narrative hook:** "Your roof already stored water for the dark nights — now it stores the night's cheap power for the garden's thirsty noon."
**Source:** dictated by Omid

#### Compressed-air energy storage: the fifth non-chemical battery — air compressed in off-peak windows and returned at peak, the concept of peak itself abolished (Compressed-Air Battery)
**Mechanism:** Store energy as pressure, not chemistry: «طراحی باتری های انرژی غیر شیمیایی مانند انتقال آب به سطوح بالاتر، تولید هوا یا گاز فشرده، تولید یخ…» — the non-chemical battery family this document already names (raised water, ice) is completed with its compressed-air member. Air or gas is compressed in the off-peak windows — explicitly not limited to the night hours — and expanded back through a motor-generator in the peak-consumption window, so that every hour the settlement used to call "peak" is met from stored pressure and the concept of peak consumption is effectively deleted. The heat of compression is held on the system side — the record is written with the thermal accounting left open, because on this platform waste heat is never heat alone: the same loops that bind chillers to heat sinks (the thermal bus, the ATES field, the cryo-zone) are the places a CAES vessel wants to lend and borrow its temperature.
**Metrics:** Charge windows: all off-peak hours, day or night; discharge window: the demand peak; chemical cells in the store: zero; design end-state: the settlement's peak concept reduced to zero (the store plus scheduling absorb the whole of the differential).
**Prerequisites:** [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting) (the scheduler that knows which hours are off-peak); pressure vessels or dedicated subterranean volume in the machinery floors; a thermal-management path for compression heat and expansion cooling into the 5GDHC/ATES system; the deep shafts and basement program as the sitable volume.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The identification of storage with the lithium cell, whose degradation, replacement cycle and thermal-fire risk in enclosed building spaces are already the indictment in this page's gravity-shaft record — compressed air ages only as fast as steel.
**Tags:** compressed air, CAES, non-chemical battery, peak elimination, off-peak compression
**Scale:** Micro — building (machinery floors), and mid — block
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | A pressure vessel and a compressor are equipment, not geology: they bolt onto any electrical room, which makes CAES the most retrofit-admissible of the four device-answers — but the energy density that makes it worth installing demands volume (a vessel field or a cavern) that the existing fabric reserves for nothing, and an adiabatic store that dumps its compression heat into a conventional building's street-facing condensers has paid for pressure by losing the one service the heat could have sold.
**Cognitive anchor:** The town gas-holder of the old quarter — the telescoping drum that stored a week of gas at walking pressure so the pipes stayed fed at dinner time, gone from memory but never from the logic of a network with unequal hours.
**Narrative hook:** "Squeeze the cheap hours into air and open the valve when the settlement shouts: the peak was only ever a schedule that lacked a tank."
**Syn (synergies):** [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes) — the ground that absorbs and returns the compression heat; [Mechanical energy storage on gravity blocks in subterranean shafts (Deep-Shaft GES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#mechanical-energy-storage-on-gravity-blocks-in-subterranean-shafts-deep-shaft-ges) — the sibling non-chemical device on the same floor; [Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation) — the water member of the same family; [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) — the bus that buys compression heat as product

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The plain's ground is charged by one record and conserved by another, and the two abstractions of "soil" meet at the same water table.** [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes) stores a settlement's summer heat in the alluvium at 50 to 120 meters and lists, as one of its own metrics, "stabilization of the water-table temperature to counter thermal stress on the soil" — a duty stated with no limit, no direction and no owner. [Thermoactive deep load-bearing foundations (Energy Piles & Diaphragm Heat Exchangers)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#thermoactive-deep-load-bearing-foundations-energy-piles--diaphragm-heat-exchangers) exchanges through the same ground at 40 meters, from inside a concrete skin, and its prerequisites hand that ground to a crystalline-waterproofing regime whose entire subject is keeping water where the battery would move it. Meanwhile [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes)'s own Fit indicts deep wells in the existing fabric for "coning up salt water" — naming, in passing, the exact failure mode that a shared substrate produces when a thermal abstraction crowds a hydraulic one. One question, two answers. Answer one — **aquifer primacy**: the groundwater body is a reserve the battery may borrow against, within a licensed temperature band, and [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes)'s stabilization metric hardens into a cap; the foundation loops of [Thermoactive deep load-bearing foundations (Energy Piles & Diaphragm Heat Exchangers)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#thermoactive-deep-load-bearing-foundations-energy-piles--diaphragm-heat-exchangers), bounded by their concrete skin and their shallow reach, pass under such a cap untouched. Answer two — **structure primacy**: the free thermal capacitor this platform can trust is the one it already pours ([Thermoactive deep load-bearing foundations (Energy Piles & Diaphragm Heat Exchangers)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#thermoactive-deep-load-bearing-foundations-energy-piles--diaphragm-heat-exchangers)'s "zero drilling" is not a cost line but an abstraction claim — the ground may be touched only where the ground is already load-bearing), and [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes) survives only as a surplus-season instrument permitted beyond the founded districts. Either ruling requires a rule recorded nowhere in this document — a thermal-interaction budget for the shared substrate — and notice which record cannot wait for it: [Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes)'s 65-to-80-percent recovery efficiency is a property of heat the aquifer agrees to hold, so whichever answer the owner gives, that metric is only auditable after the budget exists.

**Three records rent the same negative floor, and the landlord is unfiled.** The counterweight hoists blocks through a 35-meter shaft ([Mechanical energy storage on gravity blocks in subterranean shafts (Deep-Shaft GES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#mechanical-energy-storage-on-gravity-blocks-in-subterranean-shafts-deep-shaft-ges)), the rSOC hall electrolyses and stores hydrogen on level minus 10 ([Reversible solid-oxide fuel cells and seasonal hydrogen storage (Deep Subsurface rSOC)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#reversible-solid-oxide-fuel-cells-and-seasonal-hydrogen-storage-deep-subsurface-rsoc)), and the cryo-zone carves a −40°C experience and ice plant into the same depths ([Ice-crystal and skimo living zones in the depths as a winter exergy heat sink (Subterranean Cryo-Zone and Ice Sink)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#ice-crystal-and-skimo-living-zones-in-the-depths-as-a-winter-exergy-heat-sink-subterranean-cryo-zone-and-ice-sink)). As a plan they compose beautifully — the cryo-zone's extracted heat is precisely the low-grade lift the rSOC loop and the bakeries receive, the shaft's regenerative winches are the hydrogen hall's firm sub-second reserve, and one excavation pays for three batteries — and as a permit they collide: the explosion-proof ventilation [Reversible solid-oxide fuel cells and seasonal hydrogen storage (Deep Subsurface rSOC)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#reversible-solid-oxide-fuel-cells-and-seasonal-hydrogen-storage-deep-subsurface-rsoc)'s tanks require is a zoning regime a hydraulic winch and a public polar-experience hall do not respect, and the ultra-heavy cold insulation [Ice-crystal and skimo living zones in the depths as a winter exergy heat sink (Subterranean Cryo-Zone and Ice Sink)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#ice-crystal-and-skimo-living-zones-in-the-depths-as-a-winter-exergy-heat-sink-subterranean-cryo-zone-and-ice-sink) needs is the enemy of the dryness every one of them sits on. One question, two answers. **Shared plant**: the negative floor is adjudicated as one machinery hall whose streams are matched — then no record may be approved alone, because each one's safety prerequisite (sparkproof air, dry envelope, clear hoist shaft) is a duty on the other two, and the excavation economics that justify all three collapse into a single design brief this document does not own. **Sovereign floors**: each battery keeps sealed floors of its own — then the document's repeated "the basement" is a fiction, the zero-extra-drilling premise [Thermoactive deep load-bearing foundations (Energy Piles & Diaphragm Heat Exchangers)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#thermoactive-deep-load-bearing-foundations-energy-piles--diaphragm-heat-exchangers) won for its siblings is re-spent three times, and the plain is planned for three deep excavations where one was promised. The evidence that would decide is not physics but inventory: how much negative floor the ten-basement program allots to machinery at all, which is a number this document borrows and never states.

**[Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation) is the sharpest collision in the document because it is the smallest claim.** [Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation) adds no vessel, no shaft, no chemistry: its flaw text says the municipal network is already the largest store in hand and "only its pumping time must shift." Against the other five records that is a claim about the abstraction itself — if a battery is a *schedule*, then building thermal mass (named in the Terminology section) and every pump cycle in the settlement are already storage the network owns, and the five device-answers become investments a schedule could have displaced. Against the schedule reading stands the physics the device-records correctly insist on: a rooftop water tank releases at garden pressure, not at grid power — its "battery" returns joules as flow, and only its charging *timing* is electrical arbitrage — so what [Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation) stores is the pumping, not the power, and its 100% metric is a load-shifting claim, not a capacity claim. The pair composes elsewhere, however, and the phase is the gift: [Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation) charges at night and releases by day (serving evening irrigation peaks), [Mechanical energy storage on gravity blocks in subterranean shafts (Deep-Shaft GES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#mechanical-energy-storage-on-gravity-blocks-in-subterranean-shafts-deep-shaft-ges) charges at noon's solar peak and releases after dark — the plain's two gravity batteries have disjoint charge and discharge windows, so holding both needs no doubling of reserve, and both refuse the lithium cell whose import dependence [Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation)'s flaw text names. The question the owner must answer is not which battery is real but what a battery owes to be counted: a power-returning vessel, or any load whose hours can move — and one answer makes this document's question ("at what abstraction do the pools count as battery?") answerable by schedule alone, the other keeps it answerable only by named, metered storage.

**As a ladder the six order themselves by substrate, and the ladder is the enforceable reading.** The water network is re-timed where it exists ([Elevated water reservoir — night-pumped gravity battery co-serving green-space irrigation](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#elevated-water-reservoir--night-pumped-gravity-battery-co-serving-green-space-irrigation), the only record Fit-marked for the existing fabric); the structure's own mass is piped at pour time ([Thermoactive deep load-bearing foundations (Energy Piles & Diaphragm Heat Exchangers)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#thermoactive-deep-load-bearing-foundations-energy-piles--diaphragm-heat-exchangers), costing zero drilling where the excavation is unavoidable); the deep shaft and the hydrogen jar take the negative floors new construction digs anyway ([Mechanical energy storage on gravity blocks in subterranean shafts (Deep-Shaft GES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#mechanical-energy-storage-on-gravity-blocks-in-subterranean-shafts-deep-shaft-ges), [Reversible solid-oxide fuel cells and seasonal hydrogen storage (Deep Subsurface rSOC)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#reversible-solid-oxide-fuel-cells-and-seasonal-hydrogen-storage-deep-subsurface-rsoc)); the aquifer remains — the one substrate with a limit written as a metric rather than a license ([Seasonal heat and cool storage in aquifers and plain sand layers (ATES / UTES)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#seasonal-heat-and-cool-storage-in-aquifers-and-plain-sand-layers-ates--utes)) — and the cryo-zone ([Ice-crystal and skimo living zones in the depths as a winter exergy heat sink (Subterranean Cryo-Zone and Ice Sink)](thermal-sources-and-storage.md#ice-crystal-and-skimo-living-zones-in-the-depths-as-a-winter-exergy-heat-sink-subterranean-cryo-zone-and-ice-sink)) hangs off the loop all of them feed. Read this way, the six are not alternatives to one battery but the same working definition applied floor by floor from the cheapest substrate upward; the adjudication that survives the set is one that approves them in that order and refuses any record whose substrate is already promised to another's abstraction.
