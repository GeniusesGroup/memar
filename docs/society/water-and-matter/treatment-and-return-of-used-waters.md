# Treatment and Return of Used Waters

## Abstract
Seven recorded answers settle where used water is separated, by what process it is made clean again, and to what use it is returned — and they do not agree on the node at which the settlement's water is declared finished: the basement of the 500-person building, where a membrane bioreactor treats the whole effluent in place ([Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale)); the riser itself, where grey separates from black at the fixture ([Dual-pipe plumbing: separating greywater from blackwater at the source (Dual-Pipe System)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#dual-pipe-plumbing-separating-greywater-from-blackwater-at-the-source-dual-pipe-system)); the neighborhood's river edge, where planted wetlands finish what machines started ([Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#constructed-treatment-wetlands-living-machine)); the laundry's tailpipe and the kitchen sink, where the poisons are arrested before they reach any reactor ([Enzymatic laundry treatment and electrostatic microfiber filtration of textile effluent (Microfiber Zero-Discharge)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#enzymatic-laundry-treatment-and-electrostatic-microfiber-filtration-of-textile-effluent-microfiber-zero-discharge), [Biocatalytic grease separation by enzymatic hydrolysis at the kitchen source (Biocatalytic Enzymatic Grease Traps)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#biocatalytic-grease-separation-by-enzymatic-hydrolysis-at-the-kitchen-source-biocatalytic-enzymatic-grease-traps)); the root zone at the foot of the pilotis ([Autonomous phytoremediation cells for greywater at the foot of the pilotis](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#autonomous-phytoremediation-cells-for-greywater-at-the-foot-of-the-pilotis)); and the plain's edge, where the desalination brine that no membrane can drink away is crystallized into cement feed and halophyte fodder ([Upcycling desalination brine into geopolymer cement and halophyte culture (ZLD Brine Upcycling and Halophytes)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#upcycling-desalination-brine-into-geopolymer-cement-and-halophyte-culture-zld-brine-upcycling-and-halophytes)). Five of the seven decentralize treatment to the building or the block; one regionalizes the hardest fraction; one asks the riverbank to work — and because the chain records partial credits (up to 70 percent at the pilotis cells, the reactor's "ultra-clear" outlet, the wetland's 100 percent chlorine removal) with no node ledger between them, which single record may claim the water is clean, and what the reactor may be excused from because the biology catches its failures, is an owner question these answers leave open.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The seven records are one conduit read from its far end backward: a liter leaves a fixture, and [Dual-pipe plumbing: separating greywater from blackwater at the source (Dual-Pipe System)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#dual-pipe-plumbing-separating-greywater-from-blackwater-at-the-source-dual-pipe-system)'s two risers decide its class before [Autonomous phytoremediation cells for greywater at the foot of the pilotis](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#autonomous-phytoremediation-cells-for-greywater-at-the-foot-of-the-pilotis)'s roots or [Biocatalytic grease separation by enzymatic hydrolysis at the kitchen source (Biocatalytic Enzymatic Grease Traps)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#biocatalytic-grease-separation-by-enzymatic-hydrolysis-at-the-kitchen-source-biocatalytic-enzymatic-grease-traps)'s enzymes touch it; the block's reactor ([Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale)) and the plant's brine ponds ([Upcycling desalination brine into geopolymer cement and halophyte culture (ZLD Brine Upcycling and Halophytes)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#upcycling-desalination-brine-into-geopolymer-cement-and-halophyte-culture-zld-brine-upcycling-and-halophytes)) then hand their outputs to irrigation, the wetlands ([Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#constructed-treatment-wetlands-living-machine)) and the soil. Kept scattered, each reads as a standalone water technology and invites a single tired quarrel — centralized against decentralized treatment — in which a reader who distrusts basement reactors rejects also the laundry trap that protects them, the dual pipe that preloads them, and the wetland that catches what they spare. The quarrel the set actually records is narrower and real: at which node the settlement stops being allowed to call its water "used," and whether the reactor's guarantee stands alone or quietly leans on the biology upstream and downstream that none of the records metered together.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
At what node should used waters be separated, and by what process made clean and returned? Three decisions are bundled in the question, and the answers split on all three. Where treatment happens: at the fixture's plumbing (source separation), in the building's basement (in-place reactors), at the neighborhood's ground plane (root zones, wetlands), or at the plain's industrial edge (brine ponds). Against what standard it happens: a flush-and-irrigation quality ([Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale)), a washing quality ([Dual-pipe plumbing: separating greywater from blackwater at the source (Dual-Pipe System)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#dual-pipe-plumbing-separating-greywater-from-blackwater-at-the-source-dual-pipe-system)), an ecological-river quality ([Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#constructed-treatment-wetlands-living-machine)), a membrane-food quality ([Enzymatic laundry treatment and electrostatic microfiber filtration of textile effluent (Microfiber Zero-Discharge)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#enzymatic-laundry-treatment-and-electrostatic-microfiber-filtration-of-textile-effluent-microfiber-zero-discharge), [Biocatalytic grease separation by enzymatic hydrolysis at the kitchen source (Biocatalytic Enzymatic Grease Traps)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#biocatalytic-grease-separation-by-enzymatic-hydrolysis-at-the-kitchen-source-biocatalytic-enzymatic-grease-traps)). And what "returned" means: returned to the same building's cisterns and garden, returned to the river as living water, or returned to the ground as fertilizer and carbon. Every conventional failure named in the seven flaw texts — mixed streams multiplied into expensive volume, gravity's long haul to the plain's bottom, raw grease, microfiber, brine, discharge to absorption wells — is the same mistake in a different uniform: deciding the question late, downstream, at the node where cleaning is most expensive.

### Approaches

#### Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale
**Mechanism:** Dismantle the gravity-conveyance system that carries sewage to the bottom of the plain. Place membrane-bioreactor (MBR) packages in the lower floors or the grounds of each complex for on-site treatment that is odorless and sheds no active sludge; the exceptionally clear outlet effluent is claimed immediately, at the same point, as the building's own non-drinking water: «آب مورد نیاز فلش تانک های سرویس های بهداشتی، شست و شوی فضاها، سیستم اطفا حریق، برج های خنک کننده…» — the cistern flushes, the washing of spaces, the fire-extinguishing system and the cooling towers, the record adds, «براحتی برای تمام طول سال», comfortably through the whole year.
**Metrics:** Effluent flow for the 500-person module: about 75 cubic meters (75 thousand liters per day); footprint taken by the MBR reactor: only 25 to 30 square meters (the area of 2 car parks); elimination of the 100-meter pumping head for returning water from across the plain; reclaimed duties named: cistern flush, space washing, fire-fighting supply and cooling towers, all year.
**Prerequisites:** source-segregated in-situ plumbing, physical pre-treatment, continuous aeration oxygen supply at high efficiency.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Gravity-conveying hundreds of thousands of cubic meters of sewage to the lowest point of the plain, then spending megawatts pumping it back uphill to a dried-up headwater.
**Tags:** decentralized hydraulics, water treatment, Micro-MBR, 500-person scale, removal of the plain's slope
**Scale:** Micro — building / the parking and basement level
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The system can be installed in the basements of large existing residential complexes, but municipal managers cannot impose reactors on old buildings with worn-out plumbing without automatic monitoring.
**Cognitive anchor:** The magical clarification of "the clean treatment plants of modern airports" — dark sewage turned to clear irrigation water within hours.
**Narrative hook:** "By evening, the sewage of your 500 neighbors is watering the courtyard — it never left the building, and the plain never had to carry it downhill to bring it back."
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — the 80% open ground; [Mandatory minimum open- and green-space quota (at least 25 square meters per permanent resident)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#mandatory-minimum-open--and-green-space-quota-at-least-25-square-meters-per-permanent-resident) — the 25 m² green-space quota; [Dual-pipe plumbing: separating greywater from blackwater at the source (Dual-Pipe System)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#dual-pipe-plumbing-separating-greywater-from-blackwater-at-the-source-dual-pipe-system) — greywater separation; [Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds](../energy/passive-cooling-paths.md#local-ecological-cooling-through-surface-evaporation-of-reclaimed-water-on-facades-and-grounds) — evaporative cooling

#### Dual-pipe plumbing: separating greywater from blackwater at the source (Dual-Pipe System)
**Mechanism:** Separate the flow from bath, shower, washbasin and laundry (greywater) from the toilet and kitchen sink (blackwater) through distinct risers at the vertical level of the structure. Because greywater carries a negligible organic load (low BOD5), it is treated by rapid sand filtration and inline UV without ever mixing with feces, and re-enters the circuit as washing and orchard-irrigation water.
**Metrics:** Greywater forms 60 to 70 percent of all household sewage; a 50 percent reduction of potable-water demand through direct recirculation into cistern flushes and grounds.
**Prerequisites:** distinct duct modeling in the building information model (BIM), separated collection tanks at the basement level.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Mixing the comparatively clean water of the shower with the toilet bowl's contents in one shared pipe, which multiplies the volume to be treated and makes it expensive.
**Tags:** mechanical installation, dual-pipe plumbing, greywater, blackwater, water resource management
**Scale:** Micro — building
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Running dual plumbing through the existing residential fabric requires destroying floors and walls at astronomical cost; in the existing fabric it is feasible only at full block renovation.
**Cognitive anchor:** The intelligent supply network of "modern Japanese homes" — separate paths for washing and irrigation that never waste a drop of precious drinking water.
**Narrative hook:** "Your shower never meets your toilet — so neither pays for the other's treatment: 60 percent of the sewage is clean water that took a wrong pipe."
**Syn (synergies):** [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale) — the MBR reactor; [Redefining the orchards' spatial justice (replacing the ban with the formula of 20% built in height and 80% living orchard)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#redefining-the-orchards-spatial-justice-replacing-the-ban-with-the-formula-of-20-built-in-height-and-80-living-orchard) — orchard irrigation; reducing the pollution load entering the bioreactors
**Source:** Yalda-Life-Complex-Proposal.docx | Complex-Building-Proposal.docx

#### Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)
**Mechanism:** Shape the banks of the surrounding river and the water channels as cascaded artificial wetlands planted with reed and rush; the secondary-treated effluent, before returning to the environmental cycle, passes through these living biological filters and removes the remaining organic load and salts completely.
**Metrics:** 100 percent removal of chlorine compounds and disinfectants; full clarity of the water for ecological uses.
**Prerequisites:** gentle hydraulic sloping, plant-species selection — especially native, resilient species.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Releasing effluent into dry, fetid concrete urban channels, or treating it with expensive chemicals.
**Tags:** constructed wetland, self-purification of water, living machine, phytoremediation, effluent treatment
**Scale:** Mid — neighborhood and the river's edge
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Where an open riverbank survives in the conventional settlement, planting its edge is the cheapest tertiary treatment on this page and asks nothing of anyone's basement. The force-fit begins at the record's own premise: "secondary-treated effluent" must exist upstream of the wetland; fed raw sewage or chlorine-disinfected effluent — what an un-retrofitted settlement would send — the living filter either dies or becomes the fetid channel the flaw text describes, only planted; and the gentle hydraulic slope its prerequisites demand cannot be conjured in a settlement whose drains were cut to a fall and culverted under asphalt.
**Cognitive anchor:** Attractive natural wetlands
**Narrative hook:** "The neighborhood's bathwater is filtered by a living wetland and keeps the river permanently clear."
**Syn (synergies):** [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale); [Local ecological cooling through surface evaporation of reclaimed water on facades and grounds](../energy/passive-cooling-paths.md#local-ecological-cooling-through-surface-evaporation-of-reclaimed-water-on-facades-and-grounds); legacy-ref (unresolved)

#### Enzymatic laundry treatment and electrostatic microfiber filtration of textile effluent (Microfiber Zero-Discharge)
**Mechanism:** Concentrating the laundry of 500 people in the central basement laundry is a great advantage — but washing-machine effluent is dense with plastic microfibers (polyester and nylon) that plug the pores of the MBR treatment membranes and contaminate the compost sludge. With inline electrostatic traps and centrifuges, 99 percent of the fibers are separated before the effluent enters the reactor; and synthetic detergents are replaced by enzymes that break down at the condenser water's 35-degree temperature.
**Metrics:** 99.2 percent removal of microplastics from the effluent; a 35 percent reduction in wear of the MBR reactor's membranes; biological sludge raised in quality to fully organic grade.
**Prerequisites:** a centralized industrial laundry with variable-spin machines, a dosing system for environmentally compatible enzymes.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Releasing hundreds of tons of microplastic a year from home washing machines into surface waters, and the inevitable entry of the particles into the human food chain.
**Tags:** central laundry, microplastic adsorption, enzymatic detergents, MBR-membrane protection, living cycle
**Scale:** Micro — building (level minus 4)
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The fiber trap and the enzyme dosing are inline mechanical-room equipment, attachable wherever a central laundry already exists — hospitals, hotels, existing towers with a laundry floor — and the harm they arrest (microfiber into surface water) is already happening in the conventional settlement. Its force-fit limit is the record's own premise: in the existing fabric most laundry is per-home, so a 99.2 percent figure measured at one tailpipe becomes a million un-serviced filters, and the "great advantage" of concentrating 500 people's washing — the maintenance attention the trap and the dosing need — is exactly what distributed retrofits lose; without the reactor downstream, the trapped fibers are only a bucket nobody empties.
**Cognitive anchor:** The courtyard clothesline of the old house — linen washed with soap and sun and dried in the wind, in a culture that held clean laundry as a dignity that must never cost the river its health.
**Narrative hook:** "Five hundred people's clothes, washed in one place: the plastic they would have shed is caught, weighed and retired there — it never reaches your membrane, your compost, or your plate."
**Syn (synergies):** [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale) — protecting the Micro-MBR's performance; [Biological recovery of nitrogen and phosphorus from MBR activated sludge for organic soil fertility](fate-of-residues-and-nutrients.md#biological-recovery-of-nitrogen-and-phosphorus-from-mbr-activated-sludge-for-organic-soil-fertility) — guaranteeing the health of the farms' compost fertilizer; [Vertical zoning of 10 basement levels (Deep Subsurface Clustering) and 35-meter penetration](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#vertical-zoning-of-10-basement-levels-deep-subsurface-clustering-and-35-meter-penetration) (the basement zone)
**Source:** supplementary idea of the system architect — clean textile metabolism

#### Upcycling desalination brine into geopolymer cement and halophyte culture (ZLD Brine Upcycling and Halophytes)
**Mechanism:** The concentrated brine leaving the solar desalination unit is not released. Its sodium and magnesium are separated by crystalline evaporation and consumed as the alkaline activator in producing "clinker-free geopolymer concrete"; the remaining mineral-rich liquid is pumped to ponds of halophyte (salt-loving) crops — salicornia, for instance — so that oilseed and salt-resistant fodder are produced without spending any fresh water.
**Metrics:** 100 percent attainment of the Zero Liquid Discharge standard; harvests of up to 15 tons of halophyte fodder per hectare; elimination of polluting Portland cement.
**Prerequisites:** [Central solar desalination and water-sweetening plant (Solar Industrial Desalination)](water-supply-and-security.md#central-solar-desalination-and-water-sweetening-plant-solar-industrial-desalination) (the industrial solar desalinator), solar salt-evaporation ponds lined with insulating geomembrane.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Discharging the desalinators' highly concentrated brines into aquifers and plains, salinating the soil and killing the vegetation cover.
**Tags:** zero liquid discharge, ZLD, reverse-osmosis brine, salicornia agriculture, geopolymer concrete
**Scale:** Macro — regional and infrastructure zone
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The answer's two products — activation salts for a geopolymer line and mineral liquor for fodder ponds — are the tail end of one specific machine: the centralized solar desalinator, sited beside evaporation basins, on cheap peripheral land, within haul distance of both the concrete plant and the halophyte fields. The existing fabric does produce brine — from scattered household and industrial reverse-osmosis units whose concentrate goes, today, exactly where the flaw text says — but no retrofit assembles a thousand small tailpipes into a crystallization yard, and a "zero discharge" policy enforced by trucking brine from doorstep RO units is the pipeline-and-lorry model the water ledger already indicts.
**Cognitive anchor:** The Qaleh Bala salt works of Firuzabad — a salt garden on the Fars plain worked continuously for more than two thousand years: the Iranian memory that the desert's salt is treasure, not waste, when a patient harvest takes it out of water and sun.
**Narrative hook:** "The desalinator's poison is two harvests deep: the brine gives concrete its hardener and the plain its fodder — and goes nowhere as liquid."
**Syn (synergies):** [Developing the 500-hectare Atna Farm industrial agriculture complex on closed hydroponic/aquaponic loops](../food/food-production-and-assurance.md#developing-the-500-hectare-atna-farm-industrial-agriculture-complex-on-closed-hydroponicaquaponic-loops) — the food-security chain of Atna farm; [Hybrid structure: impervious reinforced concrete below grade, mass engineered timber above (Hybrid Concrete-Mass Timber)](../buildings/structural-material.md#hybrid-structure-impervious-reinforced-concrete-below-grade-mass-engineered-timber-above-hybrid-concrete-mass-timber) — low-carbon building material
**Source:** supplementary idea of the system architect — salt metallurgy and brine ecology

#### Biocatalytic grease separation by enzymatic hydrolysis at the kitchen source (Biocatalytic Enzymatic Grease Traps)
**Mechanism:** Cooking the daily meals of 500 people pushes an enormous volume of oil, fat and lipids (FOG) into the sewage. Piped directly, it would coat the surface of the methanogenic bacteria in the anaerobic digester of level minus 10, poisoning methane production and blocking the pipes. At the kitchen sinks' outlets, biological traps equipped with lipolytic microorganisms and lipase-enzyme dosing convert the fats into volatile fatty acids and glycerol — making them a predigested, easily digestible feed for the biodigester's bacteria.
**Metrics:** Free and emulsified grease removal efficiency: above 98 percent; 100 percent prevention of blockage in the gravity sewage lines; a 15 percent rise in the biogas volume of the final reactor.
**Prerequisites:** [Hermetic gravity fall of shredded food waste to the anaerobic digester (Gravity Waste Slurry)](fate-of-residues-and-nutrients.md#hermetic-gravity-fall-of-shredded-food-waste-to-the-anaerobic-digester-gravity-waste-slurry) (the concentration of cooking on levels minus 3 and minus 4), a continuously dosed system of live bioactive enzymes.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Direct discharge of dense animal and vegetable fats into pits and pipes, forming fatbergs, blocking lines and breeding a fetid smell.
**Tags:** sewage treatment, bioenzyme, lipid hydrolysis, protecting the digester, high-efficiency biogas
**Scale:** Micro — building (level minus 4)
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | A lipase trap is a fixture-scale device and the fatberg is an existing-fabric catastrophe already in progress — under conventional streets, the record's flaw text is municipal fact, and any centralized kitchen, refectory or restaurant line can take the trap today. The force-fit is where the product is supposed to go: the mechanism's payoff — a 15 percent biogas gain — presumes the deep anaerobic digester at level minus 10 that the conventional settlement does not own, and without a digester the hydrolyzed fatty acids merely re-solidify in the next main, delivering the trap's maintenance bill and none of its energy credit.
**Cognitive anchor:** The old confectioner's and household's copper pot of oil and ghee — grease as a provision always too valuable to throw away: a cooking culture that collected what today's drain washes off.
**Narrative hook:** "Feeding 500 people guarantees one byproduct: fat. Here the fat is not the pipes' enemy but the digester's payroll — the same barrel that would clog a riser runs 15 percent more biogas through it."
**Syn (synergies):** legacy-ref (unresolved) — the anaerobic digester reactor of level minus 10; legacy-ref (unresolved) — the gravity fall of food waste
**Source:** supplementary idea of the system architect — biochemical engineering and the durability of deep plumbing

#### Autonomous phytoremediation cells for greywater at the foot of the pilotis
**Mechanism:** Build small natural ponds and planted phytoremediation packs at the level of the transparent ground floor's open, 6-meter pilotis; the light greywater from washbasins and showers passes through these treating garden beds before entering the main reactors, so suspended and soapy matter is taken up by special roots and pre-treated.
**Metrics:** preliminary removal of up to 70 percent of the soapy grey load at the ground-floor level, with no mechanical pumping.
**Prerequisites:** resilient native plant species (reed, iris), gravel and washed-sand layering.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Conveying all household effluent directly to absorption wells or long underground pipes without using nature's capacity for biological treatment.
**Tags:** local phytoremediation, greywater treatment, treating garden beds, ground-floor sustainability, water-purifying organisms
**Scale:** Ground-floor level of the blocks
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The cell's substrate is the record's own pilotis — the open 6-meter clear ground plane whose absence the flaw text mourns ("conveying all effluent to absorption wells or long underground pipes"): in the existing fabric the space under a building is enclosed parking or storage, the grey risers are shared, and the gravity fall from bathtub to root zone that buys the 70 percent without pumping simply does not exist; retrofitting it means opening every wet floor to re-cut a slope the old plumbing was poured flat of.
**Cognitive anchor:** Small, serene home wetlands
**Narrative hook:** "Your bath and basin water is not thrown away; at the foot of the tower, purifying plants make it clear and return it to the garden."
**Syn (synergies):** [Wall-free transparent ground floor on pilotis with at least 6 meters of clear height (continuous 100-hectare substrate)](../form-and-land/which-plane-carries-which-flow.md#wall-free-transparent-ground-floor-on-pilotis-with-at-least-6-meters-of-clear-height-continuous-100-hectare-substrate) — the transparent, open ground floor; [Dual-pipe plumbing: separating greywater from blackwater at the source (Dual-Pipe System)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#dual-pipe-plumbing-separating-greywater-from-blackwater-at-the-source-dual-pipe-system) — dual-pipe plumbing; legacy-ref (unresolved) — living treatment wetlands

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale)'s reactor promises, and [Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#constructed-treatment-wetlands-living-machine) and [Autonomous phytoremediation cells for greywater at the foot of the pilotis](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#autonomous-phytoremediation-cells-for-greywater-at-the-foot-of-the-pilotis) promise — but only one may be the guarantee.** The reactor's mechanism claims the whole effluent is treated in place to "exceptionally clear" water, reused immediately at the same point; [Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#constructed-treatment-wetlands-living-machine) claims full chlorine-compound removal and "full clarity for ecological uses" from a living filter; [Autonomous phytoremediation cells for greywater at the foot of the pilotis](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#autonomous-phytoremediation-cells-for-greywater-at-the-foot-of-the-pilotis) claims up to 70 percent of the soapy grey load from root beds before anything reaches a machine. As written these are a chain (pre-treatment, main treatment, tertiary polish — [Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#constructed-treatment-wetlands-living-machine)'s mechanism even says "secondary-treated effluent," and [Autonomous phytoremediation cells for greywater at the foot of the pilotis](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#autonomous-phytoremediation-cells-for-greywater-at-the-foot-of-the-pilotis)'s says "before entering the main reactors"), and chains compose. But an adjudication cannot adopt a chain: it must decide which link is the enforceable guarantee, because a promise that holds only with help is a different kind of requirement than one that stands alone. One question, two answers. The first: name [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale) the guarantee — the reactor's metrics (75 cubic meters per day, a 25-to-30-square-meter footprint) are auditable at the building and fail into the wetland, not into the aquifer; the biology is a rebate, not a defense. This answer argues from its Fit text's own condition — reactors cannot be imposed on buildings "without automatic monitoring," which presupposes metered responsibility at the node. The second: name the biology the guarantee — the settlement's water is clean when the river and the garden say so ([Constructed treatment wetlands (Living Machine)](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#constructed-treatment-wetlands-living-machine)'s flaw text makes clarity-to-nature the end, and [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale)'s Fit concedes the retrofit only works supervised), and the reactor becomes the insurance that lets wetlands size themselves for average days instead of storms.
