# Autonomy in Crisis

## Abstract
How far should every part of the society stay self-sufficient when the external networks fail? This page — a consumer document: it declares the settlement's autonomy needs to the energy, water and information networks, whose own rules stay where they belong, abstract — records two answers, one about matter and one about messages. The first closes the block's water, energy and waste into circuits within the blocks and the village and sets its dependence on the worn national networks to zero percent ([Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)). The second keeps the settlement's communications alive through mesh wireless, independent of the national internet and outside towers, at 100 percent availability under total outage and under 2 milliseconds of internal latency ([Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm)). Between their absolutes the page records a doctrine the owner has not yet chosen: [Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm) achieves crisis-survivable communication by refusing single points while remaining a network, and [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s text is ambiguous — "zero dependence" and "circuits within the blocks and the village" can each be read as the island or as the ring — so whether the settlement's autonomy posture is disconnection by choice or redundancy by design is settled on this page only by ruling which of its two records generalizes.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Both records on this page carry absolute numbers — zero percent, one hundred percent — and absolutes are exactly what a reader separated from the set misjudges: an "Off-Grid" clause read alone sounds like a settlement that has opted out of infrastructure (and a builder objects to half a contract: who manufactures the capacitors, who meters the closed loops?), while the mesh clause read alone sounds like a gadget with no load to carry (routers run on the batteries [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s storage premise holds, and the mesh's 100 percent availability is only as long as its nodes' solar batteries do). Kept together, the two answers reveal the actual question this requirement was framed to hold: not whether the society connects to national networks, but where its last line of defense is placed — in the severed cable or in the ring of many — and the passive-defense tag both records carry makes the placement a security decision, not an engineering preference.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How far should each part of the settlement remain self-sufficient when the external networks go down? The question spans three domains the records treat differently — the flows (energy, water, waste), the messages (data and telephony), and, implicitly, the services (food, care, command, whose outage behavior is recorded in other pages) — and it carries a hidden variable: the duration. "Stay self-sufficient" is meaningless without a "for how long": a block that can ride out an hour and a village that can ride out a season are different physical settlements, and neither record states its horizon. What the answers share, therefore, is the burden of choosing the point of autonomy (building, block, village) against the horizon (hours, days, seasons) — a ruling from which every downstream decision follows: how deep the batteries, how many the routers, how closed the water.

### Approaches

#### Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)
**Mechanism:** Design the complexes so that they hold no dependent consumption connections at all to the worn national networks (state water, gas, electricity); all energy, water and waste management are produced and recycled inside the blocks and the village, as fully closed cycles.
**Metrics:** Reliance coefficient on the external network: zero percent; stability through nationwide disconnection crises: 100 percent.
**Prerequisites:** capacitive energy-storage infrastructure, biodigesters, closed treatment systems.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The absolute dependence of conventional megacities on fragile energy-transfer lines that fail at the slightest imbalance.
**Tags:** island society, Off-Grid, infrastructure resilience, passive defense, self-sufficiency
**Scale:** Macro — the village and the blocks
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The metric is the wall: zero percent is a design datum for complexes not yet wired, while the existing fabric can only travel toward it — rooftop arrays, water recycling and storage cut the exposure in a retrofit, but pulling a national service connection in a conventional settlement leaves the neighbors carrying the loop the building abandoned, and the "closed cycles within the blocks and the village" the mechanism presumes (the district treatment of the water ledger, the storage of the energy ledger) are program the existing settlement does not own. The transitional sense is exact: each existing building's retrofit buys part of the zero; the whole of it belongs to the settlement that designs the cable out before it lays one.
**Cognitive anchor:** Homes independent of the state
**Narrative hook:** "If the electricity and gas go out across the whole country, our settlement will not go dark for a single moment — the closed cycles that were its plumbing all along simply carry on."
**Syn (synergies):** [Energy stabilization by carrier separation and time-shifting of consumption (Energy Time-Shifting)](../energy/energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#energy-stabilization-by-carrier-separation-and-time-shifting-of-consumption-energy-time-shifting); [Decentralized effluent treatment by membrane bioreactor (Micro-MBR) at the 500-person building scale](../water-and-matter/treatment-and-return-of-used-waters.md#decentralized-effluent-treatment-by-membrane-bioreactor-micro-mbr-at-the-500-person-building-scale); legacy-ref (unresolved)

#### Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)
**Mechanism:** Design the blocks' local communications and data networks on wireless mesh protocols, independent of the national internet or outside telecom towers, so that during nationwide power or network cut-offs the village's internal communication and emergency services remain entirely in force.
**Metrics:** Resilience of the communications network during total loss of national internet and electricity: 100 percent; internal-network latency: under 2 milliseconds.
**Prerequisites:** low-power local routers with backup solar batteries installed in each block.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The absolute dependence of urban communications on a few central telecom towers, and total loss of contact in crises.
**Tags:** island communications, passive defense, mesh network, telecom independence, crisis safety
**Scale:** Macro — infrastructural and communicational
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The mesh is this page's most retrofit-capable clause: low-power routers with solar backup attach to any existing roof, tower or balcony, and no re-plumbing of the existing settlement is asked for — the network is built of the buildings there already. Its force-fit boundary is in the mechanism's own scope: the 100 percent claim is for the settlement's internal communications and emergency services — an islanded mesh routes the village, not the country — so grafted onto the conventional fabric it delivers independence from towers while the fiber the mesh was designed not to need still carries everything the village cannot generate alone, and the metric's silence about outside contact is honest engineering, not yet a security doctrine.
**Cognitive anchor:** The reassurance of emergency gear on a mountaineering team
**Narrative hook:** "If the internet and the power of the entire country are cut, your line to your family and to your neighborhood's emergency services does not break even for a single second on the local mesh."
**Syn (synergies):** [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid) (the self-sufficient Off-Grid society); [The organizational cloud operating system of unikernel images and a modular marketplace (Unikernel OS Images)](../information-systems/platform-custody-and-records.md#the-organizational-cloud-operating-system-of-unikernel-images-and-a-modular-marketplace-unikernel-os-images) — the unikernel operating system; [Green passive walls: acoustic design of the envelope and the elimination of noise pollution](../form-and-land/sound-environment.md#green-passive-walls-acoustic-design-of-the-envelope-and-the-elimination-of-noise-pollution) — the crisis greenhouse

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The page's two records are one doctrine in two media — and read against each other, they say opposite things about what "island" means.** [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s zero percent, taken literally, is disconnection as design: dependence on external networks is a vulnerability whose cure is the absent connection, and its own "Tags: island society" line seems to confirm it. But [Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm), printed under the same "passive-defense island" heading in its own mechanism's phrase, survives the loss of every single point in its system by being nothing but connections — a mesh has no tower to cut, and its 100 percent under total outage is achieved by many paths, not by none. Set beside each other the two records define the two answers to one owner question: does the settlement's crisis posture disperse dependence (rings: many loops, redundant carriers, village-scale circuits that [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s "within the blocks and the village" wording supports), or abolish it (islands: a building as a sealed cell, which the same wording's "no dependent consumption connections at all" supports)? The first answer argues from the page's own engineering: the mesh's latency metric (under 2 milliseconds) is a loop's number, not a bunker's; [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s prerequisites — storage, biodigesters, closed treatment — are plant that serves circuits, and a block sealed off from its neighbors could not afford them at the village scale the record names. The second argues from the flaw texts: both records indict the same failure — "dependence on fragile transfer lines," "dependence on a few central towers" — and dependence on a ring is still dependence on lines someone can cut, the vulnerability passive defense exists to deny. The ruling matters because the two postures cast different foundations: rings need shared carriers through the fabric that flood, fire and shrapnel also pass through; islands need nothing from neighbors and survive everything from no one but themselves.

**The records compose along the one axis neither crosses: the mesh speaks after the blackout because [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid) keeps the mesh's routers powered; [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid) stays metered after the blackout because [Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm)'s network is how its capacitors are balanced and its emergency services are called.** The mesh's prerequisite — "low-power local routers with backup solar batteries in each block" — is a load on the storage premise [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid) names first among its own; [Complete infrastructural autonomy of buildings from the national networks (Off-Grid)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#complete-infrastructural-autonomy-of-buildings-from-the-national-networks-off-grid)'s stability claim ("100 percent through nationwide disconnection") is a claim about flows that no operator can hold without the telemetry and demand-shedding that live on communications — whose availability [Passive-defense island emergency communications protocol (Mesh Emergency Comm)](autonomy-in-crisis.md#passive-defense-island-emergency-communications-protocol-mesh-emergency-comm) then promises. The composition is sound but incomplete in one dimension neither record owns: neither names a horizon — hours, days, a season — and "100 percent" without a duration hides the one number the owner will have to supply, the autonomous days per domain, after which the island resupplies or the ring re-links. And the page records this as a consumer document should: it declares the autonomy need and the communication need to the energy, water and information ledgers (whose abstract rules — the carrier posture, the treatment node, the platform custody — are written there, not here), without dictating how those networks deliver what independence requires of them.
