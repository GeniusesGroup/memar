# Decision and Deliberation Process

## Abstract
Six recorded answers to a single question — who may propose, test, and take a collective decision — and they are not six of a kind. Two answer with authority: [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) seats every vote in every qualified citizen and forbids delegation under any title; [Monopoly of bill initiation by nonprofit parties barred from any economic activity](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#monopoly-of-bill-initiation-by-nonprofit-parties-barred-from-any-economic-activity) keeps the power to draft away from citizens, marketable only to parties that may earn nothing. Three answer with instruments that touch the decision before it is made: [Policy-simulation sandbox with agent-based computational modelling (Agent-Based Policy Sandbox)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#policy-simulation-sandbox-with-agent-based-computational-modelling-agent-based-policy-sandbox) runs every bill through 50 thousand software citizens and shows the 5-year consequences before the ballot; [Advisory signalling, following expert opinions, and transparent recording of vote reasoning (Opt-in Public Staking & Expert Following)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#advisory-signalling-following-expert-opinions-and-transparent-recording-of-vote-reasoning-opt-in-public-staking--expert-following) publishes who argued what and who was right the last time; [Synthetic mind as neutral advisor and civic-education facilitator in legislation (Synthetic Mind as Neutral Civic Advisor)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#synthetic-mind-as-neutral-advisor-and-civic-education-facilitator-in-legislation-synthetic-mind-as-neutral-civic-advisor) annotates bills with opposing scenarios, hidden consequences and possible alternatives while disclaiming any part in counting; and a recovered fourth instrument answers the amendment problem itself — a competitor does not edit a bill but submits a rival version, and the citizens' votes on both texts reveal which one wins ([Competing bill variants: every modifier submits a new version on a proposal graph, and the vote between rival texts reveals the winner (Bill Variants on a Proposal Graph)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#competing-bill-variants-every-modifier-submits-a-new-version-on-a-proposal-graph-and-the-vote-between-rival-texts-reveals-the-winner-bill-variants-on-a-proposal-graph)). The documents' own guardrails police the ballot — no citizen may delegate; the machine may never weight a vote — but nothing in any of these six records draws the line the set presupposes: which aids only inform, and which, by naming alternatives or framing consequences, quietly draft.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Kept in their original sources, three of these answers read as software features and two as constitutional clauses, and the reader never meets the process they are five parts of. The records themselves insist on one process: the sandbox lists the megacenter and the 4-facet law as prerequisites; the advisor and the staking mechanism cite each other and both cite direct democracy as their stage; the party monopoly names the 50-senior-judge review the judiciary page runs. Assembled, the dependence graph is coherent — and it exposes the one seam none of the five closes: the abolition of representatives is total ("under no title"), yet the influence over what citizens decide has simply moved into instruments whose only stated limit is that they not vote. Without this page, an objection to the synthetic mind's scenario list is logged as an objection to technology in government, while the constitution — which is what the objection really reaches — goes unamended.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
Which actors, and which tools (simulator, synthetic advisor), may propose or test a collective decision? The question supplies two verbs — propose and test — and the answers distribute them without agreeing on the distribution: initiation (only parties, per one record; nothing forbids the synthetic mind's "possible alternatives" from initiating by other means), decision (only citizens, never delegated), and testing (the sandbox before the vote, staking and the advisor around it, all shaping what a voter knows). Sharpened, every answer must say what its instrument may produce, not only what it may not do: records that bound the ballot but not the agenda have answered half the question, and the page keeps the unanswered half visible.

### Approaches

#### Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives
**Mechanism:** Abolish parliament and the institution of representation. Every qualified natural person votes directly on the enactment of laws, and no citizen, under any title — attorneyship, representation or delegation — may transfer their vote to another. With the intermediaries removed, citizens are directly engaged in their civic fate and the structure of behind-the-scenes lobbying dissolves. The counting rules the same sources attach to this answer: majority is absolute headcount, not turnout — the affirmative votes must exceed 50 percent of all resident citizens; a member proposal reaches the public ballot only after at least 10 percent of residents have declared their preliminary support; the tally is taken monthly, and only the votes of resident citizens count at the counting time — a resident being a natural person the society's software recognizes as actually active, so that inactivity, death, migration or loss of standing extinguishes the effect of a person's vote without any cancellation procedure.
**Metrics:** 100% direct citizen participation; enactment threshold: affirmative votes above 50 percent of all resident citizens; minimum preliminary support to reach the ballot: 10 percent of residents; counting cadence: monthly, restricted to residents in good standing; no more 4-year cycles of decision torpor; electoral and parliamentary costs reduced to zero.
**Prerequisites:** Unforgeable digital identity (public-key cryptography / digital signature); free universal access to the platform.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Traditional parliamentary democracy, in which the citizen is dispossessed once every 4 years and representatives, lobbied by wealth, pass laws against the collective interest.
**Tags:** direct democracy, collective wisdom, inalienable suffrage, abolition of the traditional parliament, digital sovereignty
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Imposing absolute direct democracy on a traditional metropolis built on lobbying ends in decision-making collapse and chaos; the system requires a native digital substrate.
**Cognitive anchor:** The participation and transparency of "the direct-democracy assemblies of the Swiss cantons" — where each person rules directly over their own fate.
**Narrative hook:** "Every citizen a parliament of one — and there is no one to whom you may sell the seat, not even someone who offers to be wise on your behalf."
**Syn (synergies):** [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — 4-facet law; [Monopoly of bill initiation by nonprofit parties barred from any economic activity](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#monopoly-of-bill-initiation-by-nonprofit-parties-barred-from-any-economic-activity) — initiating parties; [Abolishing the domestic micro-kitchens and establishing a shared centralized restaurant on the first floor](../food/meals-and-shared-cooking.md#abolishing-the-domestic-micro-kitchens-and-establishing-a-shared-centralized-restaurant-on-the-first-floor) — practising interaction in shared spaces
**Source:** Constitution.docx | National Constitution.odt | فیلم نامه.docx

#### Monopoly of bill initiation by nonprofit parties barred from any economic activity
**Mechanism:** The right to propose the creation or amendment of a law is reserved exclusively to formal parties, on condition that the party, as a legal person, holds no economic, for-profit or speculative activity whatsoever. This strict separation keeps guild and commercial interests out of the bill-drafting process and concentrates the parties' motive solely on advancing civic welfare.
**Metrics:** Permissible commercial activity of parties: 0%; constitutionality-review threshold: the request of at least 3 senior judges and the opinion of at least 50 senior judges.
**Prerequisites:** Fully transparent financial and accounting books on the sovereign platform, for monitoring the parties' absence of economic activity.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Economic cartels and investment firms entering political parties and writing monopoly laws for rent-seekers and land brokers.
**Tags:** specialized parties, conflict-of-interest removal, nonprofit parties, bill initiation, speculation control
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | In the existing fabric, parties are joined to economic cartels; imposing this rule without a transparent monitoring substrate ends in formal circumvention of the law.
**Cognitive anchor:** The intellectual independence of "international nonprofit watchdog organizations" — institutions in which money plays no role in setting direction.
**Narrative hook:** "Anyone may vote; only parties that can earn nothing may propose. The signature at the gate of the statute book is audited down to the last unit of currency."
**Syn (synergies):** [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — legislative methodology; [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — citizens' direct vote on bills
**Source:** Constitution.docx

#### Policy-simulation sandbox with agent-based computational modelling (Agent-Based Policy Sandbox)
**Mechanism:** Before any general vote on a legislative proposal, the text is fed into an agent-based simulation (ABM) model in the datacenter. The system simulates the behavior of 50 thousand software agents — built on real citizens' energy-consumption data, movement patterns and habitat preferences — and shows the 5-year consequences of the enactment, its economic side effects and possible imbalances, visually to the citizens, before the final vote.
**Metrics:** Simulation of the 50 thousand-person system in under 15 minutes; prediction of emergent social behavior and PRT capsule traffic with above 85% accuracy.
**Prerequisites:** [100% recovery of megadatacenter thermal exergy (the Middle East datacenter)](../information-systems/data-storage-and-processing.md#100-recovery-of-megadatacenter-thermal-exergy-the-middle-east-datacenter) (the datacenter's cloud processing capacity); [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) (4-facet law engineering); de-identified behavioral data.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Emotional, unripe lawmaking in traditional institutions, without examination of the secondary and chain effects on the economy and the infrastructure.
**Tags:** agent-based modelling, urban simulation, anticipatory democracy, policy artificial intelligence, ABM
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign, and the settlement's datacenter
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The sandbox is fed by the settlement's own instrumented flows — the energy, movement and habitat datasets the record lists among its prerequisites — and in the existing fabric that behavioral data sits in other firms' silos, so a simulator trained on proxies predicts the consequences of a society that is not the one voting: the record's "unripe lawmaking" flaw reappears as the sandbox's own risk. Its 50-thousand-agent run also presumes the centralized compute named in its prerequisites, and the architecture verdict between the megacenter and the block datahall is still open one document away — an open prerequisite of this record too.
**Cognitive anchor:** The game Persia gave the world: the chessboard of shatranj — the player who sees the consequences before the move, and pays nothing for seeing.
**Narrative hook:** "Every bill lives 50 thousand simulated lives before it is allowed to touch one real one — and the voters watch which lives survive."
**Syn (synergies):** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — informed direct democracy; [A private municipality as overseer, regulator and non-provider (Private Municipal Regulator)](government-organs.md#a-private-municipality-as-overseer-regulator-and-non-provider-private-municipal-regulator) — the private municipality regulating on empirical evidence
**Source:** the system architect's supplementary idea (governance cybernetics and simulation of complexity)

#### Advisory signalling, following expert opinions, and transparent recording of vote reasoning (Opt-in Public Staking & Expert Following)
**Mechanism:** The principle of pure direct democracy is kept, with no transfer of the vote to attorneys; at the same time, a mechanism is created inside the governance software for following the views of trusted experts. Citizens and specialists may publish their vote and their reasoning text, publicly and optionally, in the face of new laws, so that others, examining the arguments, decide in a knowing way.
**Metrics:** Informed participation above 70% in enactments; access in under 1 minute to the digest of both sides' expert reasoning on a bill; credibility ranking of opinion sources built on the accuracy of their predictions.
**Prerequisites:** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) (software direct democracy); [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) (self-sovereign digital identity, SSI).
**Conventional-model flaw:** Corrupt, unaccountable representative democracy, or blind populist referenda without transparent public expert argument.
**Tags:** direct democracy, collective wisdom, vote transparency, expert signalling, civic social network
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and the society's platform
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The reasoning ledger is plain software — it can be published and followed over whatever voting substrate exists, which makes it the cheapest of the five records to trial in the existing fabric; the force-fit sits in the credibility metric: ranking commentators by prediction accuracy means nothing until predictions are recorded and scored by someone, and transplanted onto conventional platforms the mechanism reverts to the follower-count popularity contest its flaw text indicts — the above-70% informed-participation figure is honest only once the accuracy ledger precedes it.
**Cognitive anchor:** The old village assembly in which the farmer whose weather-judgement had been right in past years was heard out — an authority borrowed from nothing but being right, and re-paid publicly every season.
**Narrative hook:** "You delegate nothing — you simply see, in under 1 minute, who argued what, and who was right the last time they argued it."
**Syn (synergies):** [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — 4-facet law engineering; [Policy-simulation sandbox with agent-based computational modelling (Agent-Based Policy Sandbox)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#policy-simulation-sandbox-with-agent-based-computational-modelling-agent-based-policy-sandbox) — agent-based simulation of laws; [Synthetic mind as neutral advisor and civic-education facilitator in legislation (Synthetic Mind as Neutral Civic Advisor)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#synthetic-mind-as-neutral-advisor-and-civic-education-facilitator-in-legislation-synthetic-mind-as-neutral-civic-advisor) — the synthetic-mind assistant
**Source:** the future-village design dialogue | the new-legislation documents

#### Synthetic mind as neutral advisor and civic-education facilitator in legislation (Synthetic Mind as Neutral Civic Advisor)
**Mechanism:** Deploy "synthetic mind" processing agents strictly in the position of neutral advisor and assistant in citizens' education, without any right to vote or to make sovereign decisions. When bills or proposals arise in the village's social network, the system sets out, neutrally, the supporting scenarios, the hidden consequences, the ecological conflicts and the possible alternatives, so that the final decision rests entirely on human judgment.
**Metrics:** Turnaround for annotating and analyzing a bill: instant; balanced coverage of at least 3 distinct views on every topic; no intervention in the counting or weighting of votes (Bias: 0%).
**Prerequisites:** An independent open-source software platform; [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) (digital direct democracy).
**Conventional-model flaw:** Ceding sovereignty to autocratic artificial-intelligence technocracies, or leaving voters helpless against waves of fake news and cognitive traps.
**Tags:** synthetic mind, collective wisdom, information literacy, critical thinking, legislative facilitation
**Scale:** Macro — software and cloud processing
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | The advisor is text, and referenda everywhere drown in text — an open-source facilitator annotating any ballot with at least 3 balanced views could deploy over the existing fabric from day one; the force-fit risk is the one its mechanism disowns: "Bias: 0%" is a claim about the role, not a measured property, and a model trained on the existing fabric's data carries the very biases it vows to neutralize — its neutrality becomes auditable only through the public-accuracy ledger kept by the neighboring record, which is a different owner's prerequisite.
**Cognitive anchor:** The coffeehouse naqqal — the storyteller who voiced every hero's side so the audience could judge for itself; the room chose, the teller never did.
**Narrative hook:** "It holds no vote, no stake and no side — only the opposing scenarios, the hidden consequences and the alternatives, laid on the table before you decide."
**Syn (synergies):** [Advisory signalling, following expert opinions, and transparent recording of vote reasoning (Opt-in Public Staking & Expert Following)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#advisory-signalling-following-expert-opinions-and-transparent-recording-of-vote-reasoning-opt-in-public-staking--expert-following) — expert signalling by the trusted; [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — the 4-facet structure of laws; [Policy-simulation sandbox with agent-based computational modelling (Agent-Based Policy Sandbox)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#policy-simulation-sandbox-with-agent-based-computational-modelling-agent-based-policy-sandbox) — ABM simulation
**Source:** the future-village design dialogue | philosophy of mind and intelligence

#### Competing bill variants: every modifier submits a new version on a proposal graph, and the vote between rival texts reveals the winner (Bill Variants on a Proposal Graph)
**Mechanism:** There is no amendment procedure — there is competition between versions. Whoever wishes to change an existing proposal does not edit it and does not negotiate a redline: they submit, in the same model, a new proposal of their own, and a graph of relationships forms among the different proposals. The graph is presented to users in the simplest possible form — rival texts side by side, distinguishable by labels — so each citizen votes for the law they actually have in mind; if one proposal says 20 percent and someone argues that is too little, they create a rival proposal saying 50 percent, and the votes cast on the two texts reveal which one wins. The bill itself keeps the three declared fields every version must carry — purpose, interpretation, enforcement guarantee — so rival versions are comparable clause by clause, not by slogan.
**Metrics:** Amendments filed against a live proposal: zero — every modification is a new version node; comparison fields per node: 3 (target, interpretation, enforcement guarantee); ballot of a contested bill: all rival versions counted simultaneously, with the majority rule of the record this answer counts on (above 50 percent of resident citizens).
**Prerequisites:** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) (the vote that compares versions without delegation); [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) (the field structure that makes versions comparable); a simple version-graph interface, labelled side by side.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The committee amendment pipeline — a bill frozen at draft and then negotiated in rooms the voters never see, where the number of competing texts on the ballot is decided by whoever controls the agenda rather than by whoever holds the disagreement.
**Tags:** version graph, competitive legislation, no redline amendment, bill variants, agenda openness
**Scale:** Macro — legislative process
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The mechanism is software and survives any franchise it is bolted to, but its numbers depend on the record whose quanta it shares — with a mere plurality rule the graph still works and the "winner" means something weaker; in the existing fabric the version graph degrades to a primary election between clone bills unless the 3-field discipline of the 4-facet law structure is enforced at submission.
**Cognitive anchor:** The bazaar's competing offers — nobody begs a merchant to lower the price; a second stall opens across the alley at the price it believes, and the shoppers' feet name the winner.
**Narrative hook:** "Do not amend the law — out-propose it: a graph of rival texts, three honest fields each, and the ballots count which disagreement the society prefers."
**Syn (synergies):** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — the vote that resolves version contests; [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — the per-version field discipline; [Synthetic mind as neutral advisor and civic-education facilitator in legislation (Synthetic Mind as Neutral Civic Advisor)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#synthetic-mind-as-neutral-advisor-and-civic-education-facilitator-in-legislation-synthetic-mind-as-neutral-civic-advisor) — annotation across competing versions; [Policy-simulation sandbox with agent-based computational modelling (Agent-Based Policy Sandbox)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#policy-simulation-sandbox-with-agent-based-computational-modelling-agent-based-policy-sandbox) — each version run before its voters

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The five authority-and-instrument records divide on a question none of them asks: which aids merely inform, and which, by naming alternatives or fixing consequences, draft.** The instruments are governed by their ballots and not at all by their agenda. [Synthetic mind as neutral advisor and civic-education facilitator in legislation (Synthetic Mind as Neutral Civic Advisor)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#synthetic-mind-as-neutral-advisor-and-civic-education-facilitator-in-legislation-synthetic-mind-as-neutral-civic-advisor) promises "no intervention in the counting or weighting of votes" — and its own mechanism text lists "the possible alternatives" among what it sets out before the citizen: enumerating the live options is a drafting act, and [Monopoly of bill initiation by nonprofit parties barred from any economic activity](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#monopoly-of-bill-initiation-by-nonprofit-parties-barred-from-any-economic-activity) reserves exactly that act to audited parties. [Policy-simulation sandbox with agent-based computational modelling (Agent-Based Policy Sandbox)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#policy-simulation-sandbox-with-agent-based-computational-modelling-agent-based-policy-sandbox) is subtler: it never writes a line of law, but the 5-year consequence-visualizations it runs before the vote fix what "informed" means — an influence no voter can delegate away or audit at the ballot, arriving from a bench of software agents that no 50-judge quorum reviews. [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) abolishes the intermediary "under no title", and the instruments are a title: the records police the vote because they trust no deputy; they leave the scenario list unpoliced because they do not recognize it as one.

**The two authority answers already contain the compromise — and each other's flaw.** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) gives every citizen a veto over representation itself, yet keeps no mechanism for drafting at all: bills appear, citizens vote, and the record's dissolution of "behind-the-scenes lobbying" assumes someone lawful has filled the initiation space — a vacancy [Monopoly of bill initiation by nonprofit parties barred from any economic activity](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#monopoly-of-bill-initiation-by-nonprofit-parties-barred-from-any-economic-activity) answers with a drafting guild whose 0% commercial bar polices money, not influence. Assembled, the pair is an elected assembly reborn as a two-chamber process — initiate in parties, decide in citizens, review in the 3-judge-and-50-judges quorum the judiciary page carries — and as separate absolutes they are rivals: a party monopoly is the delegation of authorship that direct democracy's "under no title" clause, read strictly, forbids.

**One decision with two answers: must the inform/draft line be drawn — and where?** The no-new-line answer argues from the records' ordering: the advisor annotates "when a bill or proposal is raised" and the sandbox runs "before the vote on any proposal" — both presuppose initiated text, and initiation is already monopolized to audited parties by the second answer's mechanism; on this reading the instruments are downstream of the drafting gate by construction, and the agenda is set where the constitution set it. The line-drawing answer argues from the same texts: "possible alternatives" is production, not annotation, and a projected consequence with above 85% claimed accuracy is an argument the sandbox cannot disclaim — machine output is currently outside both the 0%-commercial bar (no currency, no interest to audit) and the inalienability clause (no vote transferred, no title claimed), which is precisely why it can do what the abolished parliament could: choose what the citizens may choose between. Adjudicating the line means extending [Monopoly of bill initiation by nonprofit parties barred from any economic activity](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#monopoly-of-bill-initiation-by-nonprofit-parties-barred-from-any-economic-activity) into a drafting monopoly covering synthetic outputs — instruments may annotate only what a party has initiated and generate no option not already on the table — while leaving [Policy-simulation sandbox with agent-based computational modelling (Agent-Based Policy Sandbox)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#policy-simulation-sandbox-with-agent-based-computational-modelling-agent-based-policy-sandbox)'s rehearsal and [Advisory signalling, following expert opinions, and transparent recording of vote reasoning (Opt-in Public Staking & Expert Following)](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#advisory-signalling-following-expert-opinions-and-transparent-recording-of-vote-reasoning-opt-in-public-staking--expert-following)'s ledger of public reasons untouched; the first answer needs nothing, because it claims the gate already holds. Whichever way the owner rules, the sandbox's prerequisite chain crosses this page's neighbor too: it names the megacenter whose central-versus-block verdict is open, so a ruling here may land on an architecture that no longer stands at the end of the dependency.
