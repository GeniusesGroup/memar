## Open Questions
1. **One staircase or three jurisdictions?** [3-stage platform adjudication with no physical judiciary, judged by a bench of 50 senior judges](judiciary-and-dispute.md#3-stage-platform-adjudication-with-no-physical-judiciary-judged-by-a-bench-of-50-senior-judges) claims all litigation; [Online arbitration and non-judicial dispute resolution in the marketplace (Online Dispute Resolution - ADR)](judiciary-and-dispute.md#online-arbitration-and-non-judicial-dispute-resolution-in-the-marketplace-online-dispute-resolution---adr) says it reduces the platform court's caseload; [Neighbourhood-level decentralized arbitration protocol based on collective wisdom](judiciary-and-dispute.md#neighbourhood-level-decentralized-arbitration-protocol-based-on-collective-wisdom) routes around courts entirely. Is the relation an ordered ladder (neighbourhood 48 hours, commerce 24 hours, the 50-judge constitutional quorum as the court of consequence), or three independent venues a claimant may choose among? *(Why: the platform court's own metric — paper summonses and multi-year trials at zero — is unreachable unless the fast venues take the mass first, but no mechanism text concedes a case to another venue; the choice decides whether adjudication needs a precedence rule at all. Blocking: yes for any combined verdict; no for the marketplace record standing alone.)*
2. **Which machine of force runs first when two verdicts overlap?** Algorithmic execution, automatic escrow release, and commitment-token compliance are all self-executing; a dispute that is both commercial and communal can receive two instantly enforceable awards, and escrow once released cannot be un-released by a later council verdict without breaking the marketplace record's automaticity. Is there an appeal and restitution line from token and escrow up to the platform court? *(Why: without it, the ladder reading (Q1) is unimplementable — a staircase needs handrails — and the rivals reading collapses on the mixed case. Blocking: yes for the ladder; yes for the rivals reading too, which needs the same boundary from the other direction.)*
3. **Which sentence of the platform record governs its scope — the Mechanism's total repeal or the Fit's caution?** The Mechanism abolishes the physical judiciary outright; the Fit says removing the traditional judiciary without new legal backing ends in a legal crisis, and tags the record Retrofit/Brownfield, mappable onto commercial courts first. Is the repeal an end-state deferred behind a transitional commercial-venue phase, or is the mechanism's absolutism wrong as written? *(Why: the record currently answers both ways, and its verdict must record which text is the rule and which is the risk note. Blocking: yes for the [3-stage platform adjudication with no physical judiciary, judged by a bench of 50 senior judges](judiciary-and-dispute.md#3-stage-platform-adjudication-with-no-physical-judiciary-judged-by-a-bench-of-50-senior-judges) verdict; no for the other two records.)*
4. **Is a fee-earning arbiter the conflict of interest this page indicts?** The marketplace record names its arbitration fee as a stable platform revenue channel, while its flaw text charges the traditional model with corruption bred by interested relationships. Should the revenue be restructured (fee escrowed to the panel, not the platform; or the platform barred from case outcome interest) before the record is adjudicable as clean? *(Why: the answer shapes whether the marketplace venue can sit on the ladder at all as a neutral rung. Blocking: no for the requirement; yes for the marketplace record's verdict.)*
