# Form of Government Organs

## Abstract
Three answers share one slogan — no offices, no permits, no signatures — and disagree about what survives its repeal. Two recovered records join them from the constitutions, one supplying what all three presuppose and none assigns, and one seating a single named human above the software: the constitutional sanctions suite — suspension of offending organs, universal non-enforcement of an offender's transactions, and a hard size cap on private security legal persons ([The constitutional sanctions suite: suspension of offending organs and non-enforcement of offending transactions (Sanctions Suite)](government-organs.md#the-constitutional-sanctions-suite-suspension-of-offending-organs-and-non-enforcement-of-offending-transactions-sanctions-suite)) — and the Leader chosen by a law that nominates the Leader, provisionally the founder, personally answerable for every hired team member ([The Leader selected by a self-proposing law: founder provisional, team hireable, personally accountable (Self-Proposing Leadership)](government-organs.md#the-leader-selected-by-a-self-proposing-law-founder-provisional-team-hireable-personally-accountable-self-proposing-leadership)). [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) keeps no body at all: 100% of physical administrative offices removed, every statute executable code within 30 days or suspended. [A private municipality as overseer, regulator and non-provider (Private Municipal Regulator)](government-organs.md#a-private-municipality-as-overseer-regulator-and-non-provider-private-municipal-regulator) keeps a staffed private legal person — an 80% smaller municipal corps whose job is monitoring ecological rules and paid by regulatory fees. [Transition from ministry-run provision to open-protocol regulation (API Governance & Open Protocol State)](government-organs.md#transition-from-ministry-run-provision-to-open-protocol-regulation-api-governance--open-protocol-state) keeps three open living-settlement protocols and nothing else: whoever proves conformance through the state's APIs is autonomous, with permits cut from 6 months to 0 seconds. The disagreement is not about deregulation — all three end the provider state — but about the residual organ, and it runs along the one function the records presuppose and none assigns: verification. A protocol state whose certificates must be checked either dissolves into the regulator's budget or borrows the judiciary's bench, and a regulator paid per regulation is funded by the same financial logic its own flaw text indicts in the density-selling municipality.

## Introduction

### Motivation
In their separate sources — the constitution drafts, the founding settlement proposals, the architect's supplementary notes — the three answers each read as the end of government-as-we-know-it, and objections to any one of them sound like defense of the bureaucracy. Read side by side they are three different states of the same organ: the executive as pure code, the municipality as private referee, the ministries as three API surfaces. And the shared language hides divergent arithmetic — elimination here, 90% there, 80% reduced staff plus a new revenue line — which only one page can expose, because the question that decides between them is not rhetorical: who verifies conformance, discretion and "health" when the data stops speaking? Kept scattered, a reader of the protocol record never learns that its zero-permit autonomy quietly needs the regulator record's monitored body, and a reader of the regulator record never sees that its fees are the very commission economy the platform record exists to abolish.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
Should government have a physical structure — and is its role provision or regulation? The question fixes neither answer; it asks only which functions remain state's and in what body they sit. Sharpened, the records must place themselves on three axes the question leaves open: the organ (software running on platforms, a private legal person, a protocol surface); the trigger of legitimacy (a vote ratified into code, a license earned by conformance, monitoring paid by fees); and the verifier — the unstated fourth term, since every "zero permits" claim in this set is also a claim about who checks the proofs that replace permits. The question favors no placement; it merely makes a combined verdict state one. The two recovered records place what the three slogans leave unowned: the sanction that runs when no clerk issues anything any more ([The constitutional sanctions suite: suspension of offending organs and non-enforcement of offending transactions (Sanctions Suite)](government-organs.md#the-constitutional-sanctions-suite-suspension-of-offending-organs-and-non-enforcement-of-offending-transactions-sanctions-suite)), and the one human process the software state cannot impersonate when it speaks to societies that have no processes ([The Leader selected by a self-proposing law: founder provisional, team hireable, personally accountable (Self-Proposing Leadership)](government-organs.md#the-leader-selected-by-a-self-proposing-law-founder-provisional-team-hireable-personally-accountable-self-proposing-leadership)).

### Approaches

#### An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software
**Mechanism:** Dissolve all state organizations, buildings and physical offices. Every process of the executive branch — enforcement of regulations, issuance of permits, monitoring of standards and verification of conditions — runs purely as software code on the comprehensive platform of the society. Laws must be turned into executable code within 1 month of ratification; where coding proves impossible, the law is suspended and returned.
**Metrics:** 100% removal of the government's physical administrative offices; a 30-day execution deadline for turning a law into code; a 90% cut in the society's administrative costs.
**Prerequisites:** Stable, failure-tolerant software architecture; distributed processing infrastructure with continuous availability.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Bloated paper bureaucracy, employees behind desks, golden signatures, bribery, people's time wasted in offices, and hand-drawn development plans.
**Tags:** platform state, elimination of physical bureaucracy, algorithmic governance, government streamlining, transparency
**Scale:** Macro — national, and the settlement plain
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Removing the physical offices in a metropolis with millions of bureaucratic employees ends in administrative chaos and service collapse; this government must take shape from zero in the new settlement.
**Cognitive anchor:** The unmatched speed and agility of "e-government services in Estonia" — administrative affairs completed with one click, without a single visit.
**Narrative hook:** "A law has one body — code: thirty days after ratification it runs, or it is dead and back with the drafter."
**Syn (synergies):** [The organizational cloud operating system of unikernel images and a modular marketplace (Unikernel OS Images)](../information-systems/platform-custody-and-records.md#the-organizational-cloud-operating-system-of-unikernel-images-and-a-modular-marketplace-unikernel-os-images) — the Unikernel organizational operating system; [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](../economy/payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) — the integrated financial interface; [A private municipality as overseer, regulator and non-provider (Private Municipal Regulator)](government-organs.md#a-private-municipality-as-overseer-regulator-and-non-provider-private-municipal-regulator) — the supervising municipality
**Source:** Constitution.docx | National Constitution.odt

#### A private municipality as overseer, regulator and non-provider (Private Municipal Regulator)
**Mechanism:** Convert the municipality from a bloated, expensive contractor into an independent private oversight and monitoring body. The municipality is not an institution for the physical delivery of services (water, electricity, asphalt, waste), because those services are provided by the large-scale complexes themselves; its task is solely regulation, monitoring compliance with the ecological rules (such as the [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) and the health of effluents), and preventing conflicts of interest on the plain.
**Metrics:** An 80% cut in the municipality's administrative staff; elimination of dependence on the unhealthy revenues of density-selling; a stable income created from regulation fees.
**Prerequisites:** Devolution of operation and urban development rights to accountable joint-stock construction consortiums.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The bloated traditional municipalities that trample the master plans in order to pay their staff's salaries, taking up density-selling and destroying the settlement.
**Tags:** private municipality, decentralized governance, regulation, divestment from operation, urban optimization
**Scale:** Macro — settlement
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | Converting a bloated traditional municipality into a private overseer meets the resistance of surplus staff and rent-eating managers; this model must be born in the new settlement.
**Cognitive anchor:** The efficacy of "private management companies of modern townships" — an institution that merely monitors standards instead of running a bureaucracy.
**Narrative hook:** "The municipality stops paving and starts keeping score — paid for the whistle, never again for the wall."
**Syn (synergies):** [Hard 20% cap on the buildable coverage of the developed domain (the golden 20/80 rule)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#hard-20-cap-on-the-buildable-coverage-of-the-developed-domain-the-golden-2080-rule) — the 20% coverage ceiling; [Redefining housing ownership as indirect shareholding to guarantee energy quality and building survival](../economy/land-housing-tenure.md#redefining-housing-ownership-as-indirect-shareholding-to-guarantee-energy-quality-and-building-survival) — the shareholding ownership of buildings; [Full repeal of blind urban taxes in favor of transparent direct payment for services (Fee-for-Service)](../economy/payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#full-repeal-of-blind-urban-taxes-in-favor-of-transparent-direct-payment-for-services-fee-for-service) — the elimination of blind settlement levies
**Source:** شهر سبز.docx | Complex-Building-Proposal.docx | 980203-farakhan-shora.docx

#### Transition from ministry-run provision to open-protocol regulation (API Governance & Open Protocol State)
**Mechanism:** Dissolve the wide, heavy provision structures of the ministries (Energy, Agriculture, Roads and Urban Development) and reduce the function of sovereignty to authoring and overseeing three "open living-settlement protocols": 1. the zero-water-leakage protocol with aquifer-balance monitoring; 2. the exergy protocol with ceilings on carbon and soil footprint; 3. the protocol for data-privacy preservation and communication standards. Any village or company that meets the standards — by sending provable data to these governmental APIs — holds full operational and legal autonomy, with no need to receive so much as one paper permit or discretionary signature.
**Metrics:** A 90% cut in the burden of administrative bureaucracy and government employees; development-permit issuance reduced from 6 months to 0 seconds (real-time algorithmic conformance via API); 100% elimination of golden signatures and bribery.
**Prerequisites:** [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) (a platform executive with no physical office); a national open database and cryptographic protocols for the verification of infrastructure data.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The bloated state administration with hundreds of thousands of employees behind desks, arbitrary and contradictory permit issuance, systemic corruption, and civil innovations paralyzed in the bureaucratic queues.
**Tags:** open governance, API governance, abolition of ministries, non-providing state, algorithmic transparency
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and national administration
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | A protocol surface can be opened over the existing ministries sector by sector — grids and water boards already carry the telemetry from which the three protocols would be read — but in the conventional order an API cannot stand in for a licence: administrative law recognizes signed permissions, and until the statute text is rewritten into the transparent form the law page records, a compliant operator holding its certificate can still be closed by an authority that demands paper. The protocol state is a shadow government until a legislature gives it force — which is the ratification-to-code clause of the first record doing the one job neither record admits.
**Cognitive anchor:** The qanat master who asked no one's leave — the water running through his fields was the certificate; proof lay in the land, not in an office.
**Narrative hook:** "Send the data, keep the autonomy — three protocols, zero signatures, and the permit queue becomes a query."
**Syn (synergies):** [A private municipality as overseer, regulator and non-provider (Private Municipal Regulator)](government-organs.md#a-private-municipality-as-overseer-regulator-and-non-provider-private-municipal-regulator) — the private regulator municipality; [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — 4-facet law engineering; [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) — the self-sovereign digital identity
**Source:** the system architect's supplementary idea (government cybernetics and sovereignty engineering)

#### The constitutional sanctions suite: suspension of offending organs and non-enforcement of offending transactions (Sanctions Suite)
**Mechanism:** Name the sanction once and apply it everywhere, instead of building an enforcement office per rule. The suite has three blades. (1) Suspension of organs: «تعلیق سازمان های ناقض حقوق اشخاص حقیقی» — an organization that violates the rights of natural persons, or that fails the security duty owed to persons inside its claimed territory, is suspended from providing services and selling goods. (2) Universal non-enforcement: the guarantee written against almost every offence in the constitutions is the same sentence — «تعلیق فرد خاطی می باشد و عدم تنفیذ این معاملات برای اعضای جامعه», suspension of the offending individual and the refusal to give effect to that offender's transactions toward society members; the same clause backs the currency rule, the goods-and-services-sold-only-by-legal-persons rule and the all-transactions-on-the-platform rule. For claims of direct ownership the sibling guarantee is «عدم قبول ادعای مالکیت و عدم ترتیب اثر دادن در سیستم حل اختلاف» — the dispute system simply refuses to register the claim. (3) The size cap on private security: security of each physical region is contracted to legal persons with defined purposes, with no direct role for government — and «شخصیت های حقوقی امنیتی قابلیت بزرگتر شدن از سطح مشخصی از اشخاص حقیقی و امکانات نمی باشند», a security legal person may not grow past a defined ceiling of natural persons and facilities.
**Metrics:** Distinct enforcement guarantees across the constitution drafts: one universal clause, reused; sanction for an organ offending natural-person rights: suspension from service provision and goods sale; sanction for off-platform or prohibited transactions: non-enforcement toward every society member; security legal persons: a hard ceiling on headcount and facilities they may never exceed.
**Prerequisites:** [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) (the platform on which suspension and refusal are states of an account, not a raid); [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](../economy/payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) (so a non-enforced transaction is one the rails visibly will not settle); a public registry of legal persons, their claimed territories and their security contracts.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Enforcement as a per-ministry police apparatus: fines nobody pays, licenses as the only effective lever (and licenses as the only effective bribe), and private security forces with no ceiling — the state's monopoly on coercion either imported into the settlement or outsourced beyond control.
**Tags:** enforcement guarantee, suspension, non-enforcement of transactions, private security ceiling, deterrence by ledger
**Scale:** Macro — constitutional
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The suite presumes the platform is the only place transactions and service provision can happen; in the existing fabric an offender transacts outside the rails and a suspended organ simply keeps selling fuel and shelter by hand — the ceiling on security companies likewise reads as law only where no other law already licenses private force.
**Cognitive anchor:** The account freeze that ends the quarrel: nobody comes to the shop with a writ; the ledger itself has already closed the register and turned off the door.
**Narrative hook:** "Break the rights of a person and the society does not send police — it stops believing your contracts, and your territory stops believing you."
**Syn (synergies):** [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) — the platform the sanctions run on; [Ex-post recognition enforcement: suspension or non-recognition of any organization that fails the coverage cap inside its own claimed territory (Ex-Post Recognition Enforcement)](../form-and-land/buildable-share-and-density-allocation.md#ex-post-recognition-enforcement-suspension-or-non-recognition-of-any-organization-that-fails-the-coverage-cap-inside-its-own-claimed-territory-ex-post-recognition-enforcement) — the land-rule instance of the same guarantee; [The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)](law-form-and-validity.md#the-four-facet-engineering-structure-of-a-law-text-purpose-interpretation-sanction) — the sanction facet, written once; [3-stage platform adjudication with no physical judiciary, judged by a bench of 50 senior judges](judiciary-and-dispute.md#3-stage-platform-adjudication-with-no-physical-judiciary-judged-by-a-bench-of-50-senior-judges) — the forum that registers a refused claim
**Source:** Constitution.docx | National_Constitution.odt

#### The Leader selected by a self-proposing law: founder provisional, team hireable, personally accountable (Self-Proposing Leadership)
**Mechanism:** Guide the society by law, not by office: the Leader is chosen through the ordinary legislative process, and the ballot is a self-proposing law — «فرد پیشنهاد شده صرفا توسط خودش قانونی را پیشنهاد می‌دهد که هدف اصلی آن انتخاب خودش به عنوان رهبر جامعه می‌باشد», the proposed person submits, alone, a law whose main purpose is their own selection as Leader, setting out in detail to the others why they should be chosen. The reason for the office is the world outside: other societies have no precise process-oriented channels, so matters such as transferring a land title whose ownership claim in other societies has a non-automatic structure must be done by verbal and written communication outside the society's legal processes — all processes requiring human interaction are run by the Leader and the Leader's chosen team. At the society's founding, before any public announcement of a Leader selection, the founder is the Leader; the incumbent continues in the work until a new Leader is named, except when the sitting Leader's citizenship is taken. The Leader may hire others into the team at their own choice, and «مسئولیت تمام اعضای تیم با رهبر جامعه می باشد» — responsibility for every team member rests on the Leader personally, which is why a candidate must arrive with a definite plan and a good strategic team, failing which the Leader may even lose citizenship in the society.
**Metrics:** Candidacies per selection: one self-proposing law each; the founder's mandate: until a Leader is chosen by law; hire authority: the Leader alone; grounds the Leader may lose: absence of plan and team — up to loss of the Leader's own citizenship.
**Prerequisites:** The voting machinery this page cannot supply (digital direct democracy and the inalienable vote, with the resident-citizen and minimum-interest quanta); [The constitutional sanctions suite: suspension of offending organs and non-enforcement of offending transactions (Sanctions Suite)](government-organs.md#the-constitutional-sanctions-suite-suspension-of-offending-organs-and-non-enforcement-of-offending-transactions-sanctions-suite) — suspension and citizenship loss as the credible end of the office; the standing treaty partners whose informal channels justify the office at all.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Leadership as inherited or seized office, and platform states with no address to write to: the president who answers to nobody because the party nominated, not the law proposed; or the leaderless protocol that cannot close a land transfer "whose ownership claim in other societies has a non-automatic structure."
**Tags:** executive leadership, constitutional selection, personal accountability, founder's term, external interface
**Scale:** Macro — constitutional
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | A self-proposing law is legible only where law, candidacy and vote counts are the same software; in the existing fabric the mechanism degrades into a personal campaign with legal costumes, and its accountability end — citizenship loss — presumes the self-sovereign identity registry.
**Cognitive anchor:** The founder who signs the first page of the company's articles as an offer: "I will run this until you choose — and I have put my own name on the job description."
**Narrative hook:** "The only way to become Leader is to propose, as law, that you should be — and to stake your citizenship on having a team that can keep the law."
**Syn (synergies):** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — the vote that confirms the self-proposing law; [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) — the division of labour the Leader is the remainder of; [The constitutional sanctions suite: suspension of offending organs and non-enforcement of offending transactions (Sanctions Suite)](government-organs.md#the-constitutional-sanctions-suite-suspension-of-offending-organs-and-non-enforcement-of-offending-transactions-sanctions-suite) — the loss the Leader actually risks
**Source:** National_Constitution.odt

### How the approaches collide and compose
**All three records end the provider state — and then disagree about whether the state has a body, an office or a budget left at all.** [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) measures its repeal absolutely: 100% of physical offices gone, a law suspended if the engineers cannot run it within 30 days — so the legislature's reach is exactly the length of the executive's compile chain. [Transition from ministry-run provision to open-protocol regulation (API Governance & Open Protocol State)](government-organs.md#transition-from-ministry-run-provision-to-open-protocol-regulation-api-governance--open-protocol-state) measures its own only relatively: a 90% cut, three protocol surfaces maintained by someone, cryptographic verification operated by someone. [A private municipality as overseer, regulator and non-provider (Private Municipal Regulator)](government-organs.md#a-private-municipality-as-overseer-regulator-and-non-provider-private-municipal-regulator) is the body the other two's arithmetic keeps: it retains staff (80% cut is a fifth still standing), takes fees, and assigns itself the unmeasurable verbs — monitoring the "health of effluents", preventing "conflicts of interest". The three are therefore not three brands of no-government but one distribution of the residue — code, organ, protocol — and the distribution is not stated; it has to be inferred from metrics that disagree about what "abolition" counts.

**The fee-funded regulator is the flaw of the bureaucracy it replaces, inverted rather than removed — and the protocol state inherits the problem with the sign flipped.** The regulator record's own flaw text condemns municipalities that violate plans to pay salaries; its mechanism then founds the successor institution on exactly the same financial tie: regulatory fees as stable income. A body paid per regulation has an appetite for regulation — the commission economy the platform record promises to dissolve, preserved in a private coat with a 20%-coverage cap and an effluent standard to sell. The protocol state appears to escape the trap by automating enforcement, but conformance is only as honest as its verification: the record's prerequisites already name cryptographic verification of infrastructure data and a national open database, and a verified certificate that nobody audits is the paper permit wearing telemetry. Verification has no organ in any of the three texts — which is the seam along which the set either composes or collapses.

**One decision with two answers: does the state keep a body with discretion, or is the verifier a function and not an organ?** The function answer absorbs the regulator into the protocol: the monitoring duties of [A private municipality as overseer, regulator and non-provider (Private Municipal Regulator)](government-organs.md#a-private-municipality-as-overseer-regulator-and-non-provider-private-municipal-regulator) become conformance checks running on [Transition from ministry-run provision to open-protocol regulation (API Governance & Open Protocol State)](government-organs.md#transition-from-ministry-run-provision-to-open-protocol-regulation-api-governance--open-protocol-state)'s three APIs; the 80% staff cut completes to the engineering team that maintains the protocol surfaces; fees die with the permits, and the residual dispute goes where every recorded judiciary already sends it — the platform courts. Supporting texts: the first record's 100% metric and the third's "not even one paper permit" absoluteness. The organ answer holds that some duties are not scalar: "conflict of interest" and the "health" of an effluent are judgments that telemetry can bound but not decide, and a named, fee-paid custodian is what keeps that judgment independent of the legislature whose laws it audits. Supporting texts: the second record's mechanism verbs, and the third record's own prerequisite ladder — proofs verified, conformance enforced, complaints heard — which ends, like every permission path in this set, before a human bench. Under the first answer this page keeps two records and demotes one; under the second, the API state is the regulator's instrument, not its successor — and the fee line becomes the thing the page must redesign rather than delete.
