# Resident Matching and Mobility

## Abstract
How are people bound into compatible groups, and how freely may they move? Three records answer, and they disagree about what an algorithm may say no to. [Fluid society and cognitive matching across moves among 100 blocks (Cognitive Matching)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#fluid-society-and-cognitive-matching-across-moves-among-100-blocks-cognitive-matching) separates legal ownership from the right of residence, so a citizen moves between the 100 building blocks with a single suitcase in under 2 hours, at zero logistics cost, while software recommends the most compatible neighbors on Dunbar cognitive capacity and temperament tests. [Skill-diversity and civic-resilience balancing in Dunbar matching (Dunbar Skill-Diversity Entropy Matching)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#skill-diversity-and-civic-resilience-balancing-in-dunbar-matching-dunbar-skill-diversity-entropy-matching) accepts the fluid premise and then bounds it: to prevent single-discipline ghettos that would wreck a building's crisis resilience, the same move algorithm applies a skill-entropy balance — at least 5 vital skills per 500-person module, a Shannon Diversity Index above 0.85 per block — while holding 90% resident satisfaction. The first answer's enemy is the apartment that imprisons a family; the second's is the tower that cannot survive its own success. A recovered third answer dissolves the boundary the first two share — between resident and guest: the hotel concept is abolished, one continuum of stay-lengths from a single day to several years replaces it, and a defined capacity of the complex's stays is reserved for short-term visitors so the trade balance with the outside world holds ([Abolition of the hotel concept: one continuum of stay-lengths from a day to years, with reserved short-guest capacity (One Continuum of Stays)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#abolition-of-the-hotel-concept-one-continuum-of-stay-lengths-from-a-day-to-years-with-reserved-short-guest-capacity-one-continuum-of-stays)). Their collision is exact and unrecorded anywhere: when a chosen cohort lacks a doctor, does the move stand — and is a quota that must be met by admissions anything but the entry-ranking the identity page forbids?

## Introduction

### Motivation
As drafted in the founding proposals, the first answer reads as a logistics feature — furnished modular units, suitcase moves, a matching app — and the second as a resilience footnote in the architect's supplementary notes. Placed apart, no one notices that both speak with one algorithm and that the algorithm has two masters: the first record wants it to maximize felt compatibility (the "social burnout" it names as the disease), the second wants it to enforce an ecological constraint no resident feels (the mechanic who must move in, the programmer who must share a tower with a nurse). Kept together, the page exposes what neither record admits alone: the matching function is the society's quietest legislator — it decides, one move request at a time, who may bind to whom — and the "voluntary" premise of fluid society is either a constraint the optimizer may break or it is the objective the optimizer must serve, with the 90% satisfaction figure standing in the records as the truce line nobody has explained.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
How are people bound into compatible groups, and how freely may they move? The question treats binding and mobility as one design problem — a group that can dissolve and re-form is bound differently from one that cannot leave — and it fixes neither whose preference governs nor what the system may refuse. Sharpened, each answer must state three things the question leaves open: the substrate of belonging (legal ownership versus residence right; the module size that makes compatibility meaningful), the objective of matching (felt similarity, resilience composition, or a weighted truce), and the veto (who may deny a move, and on what evidence about skills that [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) promises never to hold in the open). The question favors neither the pure market of moves nor the algorithmically managed mosaic; it only makes the refusal visible.

### Approaches

#### Fluid society and cognitive matching across moves among 100 blocks (Cognitive Matching)
**Mechanism:** Separate legal ownership from the physical right of residence. Because units are fully furnished and modular (plug-and-play), a citizen may register a transfer request across the 100 building blocks on the platform with nothing but a single suitcase of personal effects and no moving ordeal; software algorithms, built on Dunbar cognitive capacity (the 500-person module) and personal temperament tests, propose the most compatible like-minded neighbors and dissolve social burnout.
**Metrics:** Ceiling of social interaction per block: 500 persons (aligned with the Dunbar tier); moving-in and settling time in the new home: under 2 hours; relocation logistics cost: zero.
**Prerequisites:** Full standardization of furniture and the suites' digital infrastructure; a peer-to-peer transfer-request registration platform.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Families permanently imprisoned in one apartment by the terrifying cost of moving and by enduring years of conflict with incompatible neighbors.
**Tags:** fluid society, cognitive science, Dunbar capacity, matching, plug-and-play living
**Scale:** Mid — neighbourhood, and macro — village
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | Implementing property tokenization in the present opaque traditional market, without fintech laws, ends in legal crises and fraud.
**Cognitive anchor:** Confident, level-headed investing in "exchange-traded funds (ETFs)" — where every citizen shares in the profit of development.
**Narrative hook:** "One suitcase, two hours, a new neighbourhood chosen for the way you actually live — and the lease you could never leave is the one you just left."
**Syn (synergies):** legacy-ref (unresolved) — indirect shareholding ownership; legacy-ref (unresolved) — co-living; [20-square-meter kitchen-less dwelling suites with multi-state transformable furniture](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#20-square-meter-kitchen-less-dwelling-suites-with-multi-state-transformable-furniture) — compact suites
**Source:** Complex-Building-Proposal.docx | Yalda-Life-Complex-Proposal.docx

#### Skill-diversity and civic-resilience balancing in Dunbar matching (Dunbar Skill-Diversity Entropy Matching)
**Mechanism:** In a fluid society where voluntary moves among the 100 blocks follow personal inclination, there is a risk of single-discipline ghettos forming (for instance, programmers pooling into one tower) that destroys the building's resilience in crisis. Besides temperament similarity, the platform's move algorithm applies a skill-entropy balancing function so that a defined percentage of vital skills — doctor, mechanic, farmer, teacher, electrical specialist — is always present in every 500-person community, and the building-society reaches self-sufficient emergency response.
**Metrics:** Guaranteed presence of at least 5 vital skills in each 500-person module; a Shannon Diversity Index above 0.85 per block; 90% resident satisfaction preserved in matching.
**Prerequisites:** [Fluid society and cognitive matching across moves among 100 blocks (Cognitive Matching)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#fluid-society-and-cognitive-matching-across-moves-among-100-blocks-cognitive-matching); [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) (verification of expertise within the self-sovereign identity).
**Conventional-model flaw:** The class and regional segregation of cities into poor and rich neighbourhoods, or the isolation of specialists, leaving a complex's residents unable to solve the simplest everyday technical crises.
**Tags:** algorithmic sociology, Dunbar capacity, fluid society, skill balance, crisis resilience
**Scale:** Mid — neighbourhood (the 500-person block)
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The balancing function needs a complete, current census of skills per module and a power over move requests that only a platform-native registry can exercise; in the existing fabric no block boundary has a composition anyone can hold at 0.85, and attempting to enforce entropy ratios through conventional housing turns "matching" into an admissions bureau — the permit queue this set's own governance records exist to abolish, re-opened at the front door.
**Cognitive anchor:** The old village that carried its own mullah, barber and builder as a matter of course — everyone who would be needed was there, and nobody ever asked why.
**Narrative hook:** "Choose your neighbours — the algorithm keeps its promise that someone on the floors above can set your fracture, and the one below grows the food."
**Syn (synergies):** [Co-living architecture with 25 square meters of private and 20 square meters of communal service space per resident](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#co-living-architecture-with-25-square-meters-of-private-and-20-square-meters-of-communal-service-space-per-resident) — co-living; [A wellness centre with continuous preventive health monitoring at the complex's service level](../living-and-culture/continuous-health-watch.md#a-wellness-centre-with-continuous-preventive-health-monitoring-at-the-complexs-service-level) — local health care and emergency response
**Source:** the system architect's supplementary idea (computational sociology and civic resilience)

#### Abolition of the hotel concept: one continuum of stay-lengths from a day to years, with reserved short-guest capacity (One Continuum of Stays)
**Mechanism:** Erase the categorical line between "resident" and "tourist." In the settlement a person may stay for anything from one day to several years, so there is no meaningful difference between residents and travelers on the basis of how long they stay, and «مفهومی به نام هتل در این سبک زندگی وجود ندارد» — the concept of a hotel does not exist in this lifestyle; every guest can obtain an ideal stay in the complex itself. The abolition is bounded, not absolute: to keep the settlement's commercial balance with the outside world and its need to house specialists staying long stretches, a defined capacity of the residence stock must be reserved for short-stay arrivals (tourists), so the fluid continuum never quietly closes the door on the visitor who pays.
**Metrics:** Distinct building type "hotel": zero; permitted stay lengths: one day through several years, one continuous spectrum; reserved short-stay (tourist) share of residence capacity: a defined quota held at all times.
**Prerequisites:** [Fluid society and cognitive matching across moves among 100 blocks (Cognitive Matching)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#fluid-society-and-cognitive-matching-across-moves-among-100-blocks-cognitive-matching) (the furnished, plug-and-play units a one-day or ten-year guest occupies alike); [At least one capsule-residence floor per complex for tourists and shift-working service staff (Capsule-Residence Floor)](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#at-least-one-capsule-residence-floor-per-complex-for-tourists-and-shift-working-service-staff-capsule-residence-floor) — the cheapest cell for the short end of the continuum; the residency-registration platform that can meter a stay's length without changing its status.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The hotel as a separate industry and building type — dedicated floor stock, lobby, check-in ritual and seasonal vacancy — while the settlement's residents travel to it for one night and its specialists must be housed in a wholly different, longer-term system beside it.
**Tags:** abolition of the hotel, tourism continuum, stay-length neutrality, commercial balance, guest capacity quota
**Scale:** Mid — complex, and macro — settlement
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The continuum is a bookkeeping and cultural change before it is a physical one — furnished suites with metered stays already exist in any serviced-apartment market — but reserving tourist quota against the incentive to let every unit drift to the longest-paying tenure is a regulation the existing fabric has no neutral registrar for, and without that registration the "no hotel" claim silently becomes "no tourists."
**Cognitive anchor:** The guest room of an Iranian family's house — nobody there was a "hotel"; one relative stayed a night, a colleague a season, and the room and the welcome were simply the same room and welcome.
**Narrative hook:** "The only difference between you and a tourist is the date you both leave it — so the settlement stopped building two houses for one question."
**Syn (synergies):** [Fluid society and cognitive matching across moves among 100 blocks (Cognitive Matching)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#fluid-society-and-cognitive-matching-across-moves-among-100-blocks-cognitive-matching) — the furnished mobility the guest reuses; [At least one capsule-residence floor per complex for tourists and shift-working service staff (Capsule-Residence Floor)](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#at-least-one-capsule-residence-floor-per-complex-for-tourists-and-shift-working-service-staff-capsule-residence-floor) — the short end of the continuum; [Co-living architecture with 25 square meters of private and 20 square meters of communal service space per resident](../buildings/dwelling-provision-and-adaptability.md#co-living-architecture-with-25-square-meters-of-private-and-20-square-meters-of-communal-service-space-per-resident) — the shared services a one-day and a ten-year guest both draw on

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The two answers share one algorithm and two masters: the compatibility maximizer and the diversity constraint cannot both be the objective, and the 90% satisfaction figure is a truce line, not a solution.** [Fluid society and cognitive matching across moves among 100 blocks (Cognitive Matching)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#fluid-society-and-cognitive-matching-across-moves-among-100-blocks-cognitive-matching) names its disease socially — families imprisoned with neighbors they did not choose — and promises the matching function will end it by proposing whom it will end social burnout with. [Skill-diversity and civic-resilience balancing in Dunbar matching (Dunbar Skill-Diversity Entropy Matching)](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#skill-diversity-and-civic-resilience-balancing-in-dunbar-matching-dunbar-skill-diversity-entropy-matching) names a disease the first cannot see — the ghetto of the successful, the tower where everyone codes and no one rewires the riser — and binds the same algorithm to a Shannon Diversity Index above 0.85 per block. Between the two, the "voluntary" premise of fluidity is exactly the contested object: if the entropy function can reject a cohort that is otherwise compatible, moves are conditionally voluntary; if it cannot, the resilience guarantee is aspirational, because homophilous matching drifts toward single-discipline clustering as surely as the first record's flaw text says families drift into incompatible captivity. The "preserved at 90% satisfaction" clause is the record's own attempt to price the collision — a ceiling on how much constraint similarity may absorb — but no text says whether 90% is a floor under the quota or a cap on it.

**The quota runs on the attestations the identity page forbids ranking.** [Skill-diversity and civic-resilience balancing...](resident-matching-and-mobility.md#skill-diversity-and-civic-resilience-balancing-in-dunbar-matching-dunbar-skill-diversity-entropy-matching) lists [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) as the source of verified expertise — and consumes those verifications distributionally: the algorithm decides, block by block, where a doctor may move (to fill a shortage) and where a programmer may not (the entropy floor is a soft admissions screen). A skill attestation used to allocate residence is functionally a social ranking by profession, precisely the behavioral-scoring mechanism the identity record indicts, though it scores categories, not persons. The composition that respects both pages is thin and unwritten: attestation must remain a claim the citizen presents, the entropy check must run on aggregate shares (is there a doctor in this module?) rather than on named individuals' applications, and the matching result must be a proposal the citizen accepts — nothing on either page records which of these three disciplines exists.

**One decision with two answers: when a chosen cohort lacks a vital skill, does the move stand?** The quota-binds answer reads resilience as a commons good: the module has a right to a composition that survives a crisis, the at-least-5-vital-skills floor and the 0.85 index are hard because emergency response cannot be retrofitted after the flood, and the first record's zero-cost, 2-hour machinery makes admission — not exit — the scarce thing; on this reading the 90% satisfaction metric is a design constraint on how the quota is filled (route applicants, never refuse the cohort outright), and the fluid society is free because the algorithm is generous, not because it is powerless. The preference-binds answer reads the first record's flaw text as constitutional: forced composition recreates the imprisonment the page exists to abolish — a family chained to a block it did not choose for a doctor the platform needed — and a quota that must be met by admissions becomes exactly the community-level entry-ranking the identity record's zero-file clause is designed to make impossible; on this reading the entropy floor is a target that moves may fail, the 90% figure is the true constraint, and resilience belongs to the health and service records, not to the door. Adjudication evidence waits on neither side's philosophy but on one fact no record states: whether enough vital-skilled citizens would consent, unrewarded, to the moves the quota requires — and if they would not, the quota is a rationing bureau wearing a recommendation engine's face.
