#### What changed
The transfer audit found two lost tenure doctrines that never reached the sheet, recovered from 980203-farakhan-shora.docx and National_Constitution.odt. Added as new approaches: "Inalienable public land trust for garden lands: the municipality keeps undivided title forever, sells it in no circumstance, and dedicates every revenue to more land (Public Garden-Land Trust)" — id (new; lowest free owner id, claimed this session), carrying the farakhan letter's preservation-by-economics clause; and "Reversion of the deceased's financial assets to public ownership — the concept of inheritance does not exist (Public Reversion at Death)" — id (new), carrying the National Constitution's death-reversion clause and its lifetime-disposition alternative (generosity, or a registered attorney/smart contract triggered by system events). The Abstract's answer count and enumeration, and the shared-problem holder list, were extended to name both; no existing text was removed or softened. In both records the Mechanism, Metrics, Prerequisites, Conventional-model flaw and Tags carry the owner's claims (translated, with the key clauses quoted in the original); Fit & substrate, Cognitive anchor and Narrative hook are reviewer-authored and unconfirmed.

#### Deliberation
- Contradiction disclosed against source texts: within National_Constitution.odt itself, the death-reversion clause (no inheritance exists) coexists with the mandatory-investment section's clause "در صورت ارث رسیدن واحدهای سرمایه گذاری…" ("if investment units are inherited…"), which contemplates heirs receiving units. The recovery records the first as the owner's stated principle and notes the second as an unresolved tension in the source; no owner ruling has been invented — it is neither answered nor raised as a handoff question because the recovered clause does not depend on it (the reversion stands on the source's own declaration, the investment clause rides on top of it).
- Inalienable public title and [Repeal of the standalone plot…](land-housing-tenure.md#repeal-of-the-standalone-plot-title-deed-and-conversion-of-land-into-common-ecological-shares-reits-based-land-dematerialization)'s common ecological shares both make ground indivisible, but in opposite directions — the public entity that may never sell, versus the shareholders who may always trade; the collision section already questions one-machine-or-three, and [Inalienable public land trust for…](land-housing-tenure.md#inalienable-public-land-trust-for-garden-lands-the-municipality-keeps-undivided-title-forever-sells-it-in-no-circumstance-and-dedicates-every-revenue-to-more-land-public-garden-land-trust) now sits inside that question rather than beside it.

#### Considered and not done
- Mandatory investment of 30 percent of every resident's income into investment-accepting organizations, continuing until investment income reaches the legal minimum income, enforced by deprivation of social services for refusers (National_Constitution.odt: «تمام مقیمان، الزام دارند حداقل 30% از درآمد خود را در سازمان‌های سرمایه‌پذیر، سرمایه گذاری کنند»), proposed in the audit as a pension-replacement funding engine behind this page's treasury records. Not added: it presumes the constitution's single-currency, service-linkage platform as a precondition of citizenship — a funding doctrine whose sanction (service deprivation) collides with [Shelter security and targeted social responsibility endowment (Shelter Security & Impact Endowment)](land-housing-tenure.md#shelter-security-and-targeted-social-responsibility-endowment-shelter-security--impact-endowment)'s eviction-and-service bans and would silently rewrite who [Decentralized development and maintenance investment fund (Green REIT & Asset Trust)](land-housing-tenure.md#decentralized-development-and-maintenance-investment-fund-green-reit--asset-trust) is funded by (mandated residents, not operating surplus). The source's own pension critique (a monopoly fund's rent-seeking, "we do not need thousands of lines of law") is recovered as the diagnosis behind [Decentralized development and maintenance investment…](land-housing-tenure.md#decentralized-development-and-maintenance-investment-fund-green-reit--asset-trust)'s flaw text; the 30-percent obligation itself stays out of the body pending an owner ruling, and is recorded here so the rejection is not lost.
