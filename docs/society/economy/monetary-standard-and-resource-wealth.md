# Monetary Standard and Resource Wealth

## Abstract
A society that abolishes the rentier state still has to say what its money is and who owns the gas it no longer burns. This document holds one question — what should money be backed by, and how should resource income be spent or reserved? — and two records that answer it from opposite edges of the same ledger: [Exergy territorial wealth fund and currency independence from exported saved gas (Territorial Exergy Sovereign Wealth Fund)](monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#exergy-territorial-wealth-fund-and-currency-independence-from-exported-saved-gas-territorial-exergy-sovereign-wealth-fund) makes the gas each electrified cell saves its own exportable property, sending 300 to 350 million dollars a month of Shiraz's summer savings and over 1 billion dollars a year into a local fund that finances solar, hydroponic and digital infrastructure at zero dependence on the state capital budget; [Joule-based work-backed monetary standard on net exergy (Exergy-Backed Energy Currency)](monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#joule-based-work-backed-monetary-standard-on-net-exergy-exergy-backed-energy-currency) makes money itself impossible to counterfeit at any scale by minting it only against metered physical work — one unit of "work-capital" per kilowatt-hour of useful exergy, zero structural inflation, settlement under 0.01 percent. The collision is not between them but inside their composition: a hard-currency fund that earns abroad feeds an internal currency that, by its own definition, cannot be issued against foreign reserves — and neither record states where the dollar meets the joule. The 20/80 rule, the deflation engine and the fee-for-service rails these answers lean on are linked ideas of other documents, never premises.

## Introduction

### Motivation
The two records answer different halves of one constitutional-money problem, and the seam between the halves is the document's reason to exist. [Exergy territorial wealth fund and currency independence from exported saved gas (Territorial Exergy Sovereign Wealth Fund)](monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#exergy-territorial-wealth-fund-and-currency-independence-from-exported-saved-gas-territorial-exergy-sovereign-wealth-fund) solves where capital finance comes from once blind taxes and the hydrocarbon bureaucracy are repealed — but its 100-percent hard-currency inflow is exactly the kind of "fiat and credit" import the [Joule-based work-backed monetary standard on net exergy (Exergy-Backed Energy Currency)](monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#joule-based-work-backed-monetary-standard-on-net-exergy-exergy-backed-energy-currency) record exists to make impossible: "money creation without producing or storing the energy equivalent is impossible" says nothing about a billion dollars a year that is neither produced nor stored, only exported. Kept apart, each reads as a complete answer — a fund, a standard; together they show the requirement the set never names: an exchange regime between the village's joules and the world's dollars, which is where the sanctions and currency crises the second record promises immunity from actually bite.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
What should money be backed by, and how should resource income be spent or reserved? A currency that owes its value to the state that prints it transfers the state's corruption into every price; a resource income that belongs to no local treasury turns efficiency into a loss for the community that earned it. Each answer below fixes one edge — the external income, or the internal unit of account — and the question itself favors neither edge alone.

### Approaches

#### Exergy territorial wealth fund and currency independence from exported saved gas (Territorial Exergy Sovereign Wealth Fund)
**Mechanism:** Transfer the usufract and export right of the natural gas saved away from the Ministry of Oil to the self-sufficient cells and villages themselves. Every region or village that dismantles its home gas network and brings fossil-fuel consumption to zero with heat pumps and a 5GDHC network takes delivery of the freed methane quota in the export ring of the Energy Exchange, and exports it or sells it to the petrochemical plants. 100 percent of the foreign-currency proceeds flow into the "local territorial wealth fund" and are spent directly on capital finance for solar infrastructure, hydroponics and the digital platform.
**Metrics:** Export value of saved gas from Shiraz in the summer: 300 to 350 million dollars per month; direct foreign-currency income of the local fund for infrastructure return on investment: over 1 billion dollars per year; dependence rate on the government's capital budget: zero percent.
**Prerequisites:** [Banning retail gas distribution in the society and preventing exergy destruction (Exergy Destruction)](../energy/energy-carriers-and-grid-posture.md#banning-retail-gas-distribution-in-the-society-and-preventing-exergy-destruction-exergy-destruction) (the ban on urban gas distribution); [Fifth-generation district heating/cooling loop (5GDHC / Thermal Bus) for binding chillers to heat sinks](../energy/directed-energy-transfer.md#fifth-generation-district-heatingcooling-loop-5gdhc--thermal-bus-for-binding-chillers-to-heat-sinks) (the 5GDHC thermal loop); a legislative act granting the usufract to export the saved energy carriers, passed in parliament in favor of the project's executing body.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The 100-percent concentration of oil and gas revenues in the hands of the state bureaucracy, the rent-distribution of subsidies, the dumping of home gas at artificial prices, and the forced blackouts imposed on productive industry in summer and winter.
**Tags:** energy economics, tax independence, exported saved gas, local wealth fund, exergy balance
**Scale:** Macro — governance, and the national economy
**Fit & substrate:** [Dual — transitional] | The fund's revenue leg can be fitted to the existing fabric the moment any quarter electrifies and surrenders its gas main — the quota it earns is counted in molecules, not in new buildings; its savings leg cannot, because zero fossil consumption presupposes the 5GDHC loops and heat pumps of built cells, an idea of the energy records this document links and does not assume. Force-fit in the existing fabric with the loops unbuilt, the mechanism would export gas it never saved: the quota is defined against a counterfactual consumption nobody in this document meters, and the "zero-percent dependence" metric would read as a bookkeeping act — the same import dependency renamed at village scale, with the ministry still holding the export ring between itself and the sea.
**Cognitive anchor:** The old waqf economy — a bazaar's revenues returning to the same town's well, bath and school, so prosperity had a local address and a named purpose; the fund is the waqf deed rewritten for molecules instead of shops.
**Narrative hook:** "The village that stops burning gas in its homes still owns the gas it did not burn — it exports the unused molecules, and every dollar comes back as solar panels."
**Syn (synergies):** [The centralized photovoltaic mega-plant and the clean energy-pump hub (Clean Energy Station)](../energy/large-generation-site.md#the-centralized-photovoltaic-mega-plant-and-the-clean-energy-pump-hub-clean-energy-station) — the centralized solar plants; [Full repeal of blind urban taxes in favor of transparent direct payment for services (Fee-for-Service)](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#full-repeal-of-blind-urban-taxes-in-favor-of-transparent-direct-payment-for-services-fee-for-service) — the removal of conventional taxes and state costs; [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) — the platform wallet

#### Joule-based work-backed monetary standard on net exergy (Exergy-Backed Energy Currency)
**Mechanism:** Replace inflationary fiat and credit moneys with a unit of value measurement and settlement based on produced physical exergy — kilowatt-hours of solar electricity, or megajoules of useful heat delivered to the network. The monetary token "work-capital" is issued directly by smart contracts on the readings of exergy meters; creating money without producing or storing the physical-energy equivalent is impossible. The system, while uprooting structural inflation, immunizes the trade within and between biological cells against currency crises and sanctions.
**Metrics:** Monetary backing: 1 unit of work-capital = 1 kilowatt-hour of useful exergic work; structural inflation rate of the system: zero percent; real-time settlement fee of the network: under 0.01 percent.
**Prerequisites:** [Real-time exergy balance and monitoring at subsystem level (Real-Time Exergy Ledger)](../information-systems/observability-of-flows.md#real-time-exergy-balance-and-monitoring-at-subsystem-level-real-time-exergy-ledger) (the real-time exergy accounting system); [The organizational cloud operating system of unikernel images and a modular marketplace (Unikernel OS Images)](../information-systems/platform-custody-and-records.md#the-organizational-cloud-operating-system-of-unikernel-images-and-a-modular-marketplace-unikernel-os-images) (the unikernel security architecture of the platform); a wide industrial Internet-of-Things network and power-metering sensors.
**Conventional-model flaw:** Money printing without backing by the conventional banking system, debt creation, the continuous erosion of workers' purchasing power, and the separation of the financial economy from the physical and ecological realities of the planet.
**Tags:** energy money, joule standard, inflation control, interest-free money, currency stability, sustainable fintech
**Scale:** Macro — monetary, and the international economy
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The standard mints only against metered exergy, so every producing and consuming point must carry a certified meter before it carries a single unit of money — the instrumentation of built cells and new platforms, not of retrofitted buildings whose meters the fee records themselves declare corruptible. Force-fit on the existing fabric with partial metering, the joule standard does not abolish counterfeiting; it moves it from the printing press to the meter room, and the "under 0.01 percent settlement fee" and "zero structural inflation" become statistics of the metered minority while the unmetered majority keeps the fiat it is stuck with — two moneys in one market, an arbitrage exactly like the dual-exchange regimes this record's flaw indicts.
**Cognitive anchor:** The qanat villages' water share — ground that could not be printed; each shareholder's turn measured in the sun's shadow ("pāy-āb"), real, timed and unforgeable; the joule standard is payāb for energy.
**Narrative hook:** "Money like a qanat share: measured, real, and no one can print more water — the kilowatt-hour is a sun that cannot be debased."
**Syn (synergies):** [Integrated financial interface, digital wallet and real-time settlement without queues or checkout counters](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#integrated-financial-interface-digital-wallet-and-real-time-settlement-without-queues-or-checkout-counters) — the unified digital wallet; [Citizen time-banking and tokenization of cooperative micro-tasks (Decentralized Time-Banking)](reward-cooperation-thrift.md#citizen-time-banking-and-tokenization-of-cooperative-micro-tasks-decentralized-time-banking) — citizen time-banking; [Full repeal of blind urban taxes in favor of transparent direct payment for services (Fee-for-Service)](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#full-repeal-of-blind-urban-taxes-in-favor-of-transparent-direct-payment-for-services-fee-for-service) — the zero-tax regime with direct service pricing

### How the approaches collide and compose
**[Exergy territorial wealth fund and currency independence from exported saved gas (Territorial Exergy Sovereign Wealth Fund)](monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#exergy-territorial-wealth-fund-and-currency-independence-from-exported-saved-gas-territorial-exergy-sovereign-wealth-fund)'s dollars cannot enter the currency [Joule-based work-backed monetary standard on net exergy (Exergy-Backed Energy Currency)](monetary-standard-and-resource-wealth.md#joule-based-work-backed-monetary-standard-on-net-exergy-exergy-backed-energy-currency) defines — so either the fund is outside the money or the standard has an exception.** The fund's metric is unapologetically monetary in the old sense: 300 to 350 million dollars a month into a local treasury, over 1 billion a year, financing capital works. The standard's defining sentence is "creating money without producing or storing the physical-energy equivalent is impossible," and its stated immunity — "against currency crises and sanctions" — is immunity from exactly the dollar system the fund depends on. Read as one monetary constitution, three alternatives are open and the records choose none: the fund's revenue is converted to joules through the imported solar and hydroponic hardware it buys (dollars as a claim on future metered production — the standard survives, but the billion is only money to the degree the exergy ledger will attest the hardware arrives); the fund holds dollars as a reserve outside the money (a stateless foreign-currency sector, and every figure in the fund's metrics is then unbacked by the definition the standard itself enforces on everyone else); or the standard exempts resource income (the printing press, renamed "territorial wealth fund"). Adjudication consequence: the composition of the two records is decided by an exchange regime neither document contains — the one question the collision cannot dodge is whether the fund's spending authority in work-capital is capped by the exergy the purchased infrastructure actually delivers.

**The zero claims line up, but both stand on instruments the other documents own.** The fund promises zero-percent dependence on the state capital budget; the standard promises zero-percent structural inflation and settlement under 0.01 percent; both lean on [Full repeal of blind urban taxes in favor of transparent direct payment for services (Fee-for-Service)](payment-rails-and-service-charging.md#full-repeal-of-blind-urban-taxes-in-favor-of-transparent-direct-payment-for-services-fee-for-service) to remove the conventional revenue base, and the exergy ledger and unikernel platform are prerequisites owned by the information-systems records. The composition is a stack: taxes repealed (payment rails) ⇒ the fund becomes the public investor ⇒ the standard makes the fund's internal circulation unforgeable. Each layer is fine until its unadopted dependency fails upward: if the parliament never grants the export usufract (a single legislative point of failure the fund's own prerequisites name), the fund's income vanishes and the society is left with a joule standard whose only large-scale backing is rooftop sun; if the metering network's 0.01-percent settlement cannot be certified, the standard's inflation proof degrades into trust in the meter room — the same trust the fund's flaw text indicts in the ministry. The linked ideas stay ideas: nothing in this document makes the exergy ledger exist.

**The sharpest unresolved collision closes on one owner decision with two answers: does work-capital have one issuance rule or two?** The first answer — one rule, exergy only — holds the standard's sentence absolute and makes the fund an investment authority, not a monetary one: it buys metered capacity abroad and at home, and its dollars are spent, never minted; the fund's billion becomes, in money terms, exactly what the solar fields produce, and the immunity claim holds at the cost of a foreign-currency sector that cannot circulate internally. The second answer — two rules — lets resource income mint work-capital directly on a declared conversion (dollars-to-joules at import parity), which makes the fund immediately liquid in the internal currency and the infrastructure finance frictionless, at the price of importing the volatility the standard exists to exclude: a bad gas-export month is then a contraction of the village's own money supply, the opposite of the "zero structural inflation" promise. The evidence that would tip the balance is external — how much of the capital goods the fund buys (PV, hydroponics, platforms) are domestically metered producers whose joules could attest the conversion — until the owner rules, the two records of this document answer the same question in two monetary systems and no paragraph yet names the border between them.
