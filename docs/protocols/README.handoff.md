## Open Questions

### Are the `docs/protocols/` category folders the right layout?
- The category folders under `docs/protocols/` (`computer/`, `codec/`, `process/`, `memory/`, `net/`, and the rest) are provisional and certainly open to critique. The first-level `modules/` tree has the same open question — whether a subject like type, identity, or concurrency has one owning category — and the answer is the owner's. The `modules/` tree uses top-level `memory/` per [Memory → The memory/storage dichotomy rejected](memory/memory.md#the-memorystorage-dichotomy-rejected); `docs/protocols/memory/` matches that name.
