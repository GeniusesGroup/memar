## The category layout of this repository
The category folders of `docs/modules/` (`docs/modules/README.md#one-layout-for-both-trees`) are the layout `modules/` follows, and that layout was decided by transfer and by reading, never by an argument recorded anywhere. **The owner's position (2026-10-07): the categories themselves are to be critiqued, not only the folders inside them** — `runtime/` in particular looks as though it should break up into `computer/`, and that reading is itself held as provisional. Each question below was measured against the tree; nothing here is decided, and every entry is the owner's — except the one entry marked settled, which was decided by the owner's own ruling rather than by this list. Three further questions were counted in a port session's notes and never named; they are not recoverable, so they are absent rather than reconstructed.
- **`runtime/` against `computer/runtime/`.** `modules/computer/runtime/` holds `protocol/`, `scheduler/` and `thread/`; `modules/runtime/` holds only `flow/`, whose README states a question rather than a subject, and `docs/modules/runtime/` holds only immutable infrastructure (`docs/modules/runtime/immutable_infrastructure.md`). `modules/lib/lib.md` places "the runtime and its mechanisms" under `computer/runtime/`, while `modules/README.md` lists `runtime/` as a category of its own.
- **Where the concurrency primitives live.** `computer/runtime/` holds `thread/` and `scheduler/`, while immutable infrastructure (`docs/modules/runtime/immutable_infrastructure.md`) and concurrency (`docs/modules/computer/concurrency.md`) both reach into the area; no document states which of runtime, process, or computer owns a primitive.
- **The `computer/` against `hardware/` boundary.** `modules/hardware/` holds one folder, `cpu/` (`core.kh`, a capsule over a body-less assembly function; `core.s`, that assembly; `cache.txt`, a Go snippet for `cache_line_size`). Whether a CPU is hardware, a computer, or neither is stated nowhere.
- **`time/`'s mixed axes.** `modules/time/` holds calendar systems (`astronomy/`, `earth/`, `mars/`, `utc/`, `unix/`), epoch families (`epoch/`), clocks (`monotonic/`), spans (`duration/`), and timers (`timer/`, `timing-wheel/`) in one folder beside `protocol/`.
- **Identity's ownership.** `modules/identifier/` holds identity, yet the `UUID` name is declared by four modules at once — `computer/type/protocol`, `identifier/record/protocol`, `memory/record/protocol`, `process/request/protocol` (2026-10-01). `bundles/khayyam/rules/qualified-names/qualified-names.handoff.md` records the two-module form of the question; the four-module form is wider.
- **`datatype/` against `datatypes/` — settled 2026-10-08.** `modules/computer/datatype/` and `modules/computer/datatypes/` both existed, each with a `protocol/`, and one spelling was wrong. `datatype/` was it: it has been renamed `modules/computer/type/`, taking the concept's own name per the owner's ruling recorded in The Type's changelog (`docs/modules/computer/type.changelog.md#realization-folder-renamed-from-datatype-to-type`), and every `in` URI naming it was migrated with it. The plural folder keeps the plural, because its declarations — `Types_Map`, `Computer_Types`, `Observer_Types` — are about a collection of Types, not about a Type; under the owner's ruling of 2026-10-10 that no `DataType` trace stays in the corpus, it was renamed `modules/computer/types/`. The two folders no longer collide. What survives under that folder's own name is a separate question: `dt-types.kh` and `dt-types.observers.kh` carry a `dt-` prefix no sibling file carries, and one of the three declarations carries a `Computer_` prefix its two siblings do not.
- **One naming convention for the layer.** `sRPC` and `immutable_infrastructure` stand against kebab-case and snake_case neighbours. None is worth a decision alone; the question is whether one convention is chosen for the layer and applied.
