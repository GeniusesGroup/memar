# Task Rule — A step is judged by its cost over the whole work
Status: Draft

## The rule
Judge a step by what its development costs over the whole work, rework it prevents included, not by the tokens of its own chat alone.

## Why it exists
A cheap step that is done again costs more than a dear one done once.

## How a check would run
A choice defended only by the tokens of one chat breaks the rule. Moved on 2026-10-26 from the Memar skill (task 20261026-T120341Z-16244); this repository runs no automatic check of it today.
