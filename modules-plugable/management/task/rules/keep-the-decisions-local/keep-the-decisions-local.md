# Task Rule — What stays local when work is delegated
Status: Draft

## The rule
Keep local the target's structure — built from reports, not from a survey — the decisions over findings, and the merge. Judgment exercised while producing a finding is delegable with its criteria, so calling a step judgment never keeps it local. Do not delegate work whose intermediate context is the deliverable, or work that needs the task's decision between its steps; where such work exceeds the size test, split it at those decisions and delegate the parts between. A tangent the task will not otherwise need is not a step to delegate; it is handled per [Mid-task tangents](../../../../knowledge/documentation/handoff/handoff.practice.md#mid-task-tangents).

## Why it exists
A decision made in a context that lacks the task's reasons is made again, or wrongly.
