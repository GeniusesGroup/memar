# Task Rule — A call lists what the called agent reads and states what its model needs
Status: Draft

## The rule
- **An agent is called by a content: an ask addressed to a role that no running agent holds.** The participant who calls is one with authority over the Task ([delegate](../../../../../modules/rights/delegate/delegate.md)); it places the call, and the delegating agent starts a fresh agent for it, as Memar starts none ([the Task handoff](../../../../../modules/management/task/task.handoff.md#tentative-several-humans-and-several-agents-at-once-2026-10-24)).
- **The call lists the contents the callee may read, by number and reason, and the documents it must read,** and states the three facts [the model rule](../model-follows-need/model-follows-need.md) judges a level by: who checks the answer and how deeply, how open the judgment is, what an unnoticed error costs.
- **A critic is a reviewer who has not seen the work** ([A reviewer has not seen the work](../reviewer-is-a-fresh-sub-agent/reviewer-is-a-fresh-sub-agent.md)): it reads what the call lists and what it searches in the governing documents, and not the thinking text of the participant whose output it examines unless the call lists it. Where what each participant may see is not yet held by the system, the call is the only restriction, and a critique that must not see another critique is a Task of its own ([A new person is a participant unless the ask is a Task of its own](../participant-or-new-task/participant-or-new-task.md)).
- **The answer is a reply to the examined content, placed by the callee; a callee that cannot place it returns it as text and the caller places it, saying so.**

## Why it exists
A critic that is told what to read and what is at stake chooses its depth and cost knowingly, and one that is not told reads everything or nothing; and a critique that shares the maker's context shares its blind spots.

## How a check would run
Read the call: it holds the list with a reason for each number, the documents, and the three facts, or it breaks the rule. The statement is the owner's, recorded in [the Task handoff](../../../../../modules/management/task/task.handoff.md#critique-as-a-content-on-the-timeline-2026-10-30-tentative); this repository runs no automatic check of it today.
