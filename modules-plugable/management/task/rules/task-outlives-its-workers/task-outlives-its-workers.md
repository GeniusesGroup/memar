# Task Rule — A Task may live as long as the organization
Status: Draft

## The rule
A Task may be created when the organization is, and hundreds of Tasks may be made over its whole life to write and raise one subject; one titled the vision of the organization may have Tasks until the last day of the organization. Nothing in the model assumes a short life, one worker, or one session.

## Why it exists
A model tied to a short job breaks on the work an organization exists for.

## How a check would run
A field or status that expires a Task by age alone breaks the rule. The statement is the owner's, recorded in [the Task handoff](../../../../../modules/management/task/task.handoff.md#owners-answers-to-the-six-questions-2026-10-18); this repository runs no automatic check of it today.
