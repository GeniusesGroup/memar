# Task Rule — A Tangent Is Recorded at Once, Not Followed
A rule of this repository for a task that meets a request outside its own work. Moved on 2026-11-01 from the Handoff Practice.

## The rule
- **A tangent is a request that opens a development thread whose context the task will not otherwise need.** A clarifying question or a small edit on the task's topic is not one, and neither is bounded work the task needs integrated: that is delegation to a sub-agent, and it stays in the task.
- **When a tangent appears, it is recorded at once and not at the task's end.** The task proposes a separate task for it, once, and the person's choice to continue here is final; it records the tangent's state as a content of the task that will take it up, so that the fresh task starts from the record and not from scrollback.
- **It says in one line what was recorded and where,** because a silent record hides what was selected.
- **It loads no more of the tangent's context than recording requires.**

## Why it exists
A tangent followed in place spends the context of a task for work the task does not need, and a tangent left in scrollback is lost when the context is compacted.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may let a task follow its tangents, and then pays their context in every later turn of that task.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not say where the record is kept: [a record is a message on a task](../a-record-is-a-message-on-a-task/a-record-is-a-message-on-a-task.md), and the recording is made with the verbs `memar task ensure --help` and `memar conversation post --help` describe.
- This repository runs no check of it today.
