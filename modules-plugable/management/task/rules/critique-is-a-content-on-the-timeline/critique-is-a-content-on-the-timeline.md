# Task Rule — A critique of a Task's output is a content on its timeline
Status: Draft

## The rule
- **A critique of the output of a Task, or of a part of it, is a content on that Task's timeline, and is not a Task.** The critic's answer is a content that replies to the content it examined ([A reply names what to read](../a-reply-names-what-to-read/a-reply-names-what-to-read.md)), with one verdict for each part examined.
- **It is a Task only when the three questions of [A new person is a participant unless the ask is a Task of its own](../participant-or-new-task/participant-or-new-task.md) say so,** which is the case of a critique of material no Task of this organization made (the legacy archive, a published model of another organization) or of one that needs its own acceptor and its own check.
- **A critique changes no Status by being written.** What it finds becomes one of three things, told apart by what changes: the Task's request and check are unchanged and the check is not met, so it is another round of the same Task (a new plan content, and the Status returns to Active by a Request, as [Plan](../../../../knowledge/documentation/plan/plan.md#a-task-holds-rounds-of-plans) says); work is to be added, so it is a child Task through `part_of`; nothing is to change, so it is a closing reply and nothing more.
- **The kind `critique` of a Task stays for the critique that is a Task.** The act of critiquing, and the passages it reads, do not depend on whether the critique is a Task or a content.

## Why it exists
A Task made only to judge a part of another Task has no state, acceptor or check of its own, fills the graph with nothing it carries, and cuts the critique away from the timeline where its subject, and the answer to it, are.

## How a check would run
Find a Task whose Request is to judge a named output of one other Task and whose check is that the judgment was written: it breaks the rule unless it answers yes to one of the three questions. The statement is the owner's, recorded in [the Task handoff](../../../../../modules/management/task/task.handoff.md#critique-as-a-content-on-the-timeline-2026-10-30-tentative); this repository runs no automatic check of it today.
