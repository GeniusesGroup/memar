# Task Rule — A Priority Change States Its Reason
A rule of this repository for the priority a plan carries to order the queue. Moved on 2026-11-01 from the Handoff Practice.

## The rule
- **Anyone, a person or an agent, may change a task's priority, whatever its status.**
- **The one who changes it says in that plan why.**

## Why it exists
A priority is a judgment about the task, and the next reader of the plan weighs it only when its reason is there.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may order its queue by a number alone, and then cannot tell a considered priority from a guess.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not give the range or the default; `memar task priority --help` says them.
- This repository runs no check of it today.
