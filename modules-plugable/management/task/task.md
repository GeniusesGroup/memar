# Task — what the task asks of the domains that keep its texts
Status: Tentative

## Abstract
The kernel [Task](../../../modules/management/task/task.md) holds a task's intent, check, status and relations and nothing of any text. This document holds what the task needs from the domains that do keep texts: every kind of text that [Documentation](../../knowledge/documentation/documentation.md) governs today (plan, report, handoff, changelog, explanation, practice, research) is, here, a template for writing a text and nothing beyond that; its main use is a message on a task's timeline. It names no file, folder or path: where a text is kept is [text](../../../modules/knowledge/text/text.md)'s, how it is joined to a task and ordered is [content](../../../modules/knowledge/content/content.md)'s and [timeline](../../../modules/management/timeline/timeline.md)'s, and each of them is asked, not told. The shape is Tentative (the owner reviews it) and the rules it applies are the [Task rules](rules/README.md).

## Explanation

### What a documentation facet becomes
Each facet is a template of a text: the parts it asks for and what each part is for, with no document, companion, file name or location attached. The handoff is one example and not the whole: a plan, a report, a decision, a question, an assumption, a piece of anticipated work and a change record are each a text written to a facet's template. A text written to one, such as a record of what a task learned or changed, is a content on the task's timeline whose text follows a template and which has an edge to the thing it concerns. The companions are not written by agents: the handoff of a thing is the open records that concern it, found by the edge, and its changelog is the change records that concern it, found the same way. A handoff is therefore one template of a descriptive message on a task, not a document ([owner, 2026-10-30](../../../modules/management/task/task.handoff.md#critique-as-a-content-on-the-timeline-2026-10-30-tentative)).

### What the task asks of the other domains
Each question is raised in the handoff of the domain that owns the answer, and the task module assumes none.

| Asked of | The requirement |
| --- | --- |
| text | keeps the text, versions it, and finds a word in it; every facet is a text template ([documentation by text template or edge](rules/documentation-by-text-template-or-edge/documentation-by-text-template-or-edge.md)) |
| content | joins a record to the thing it concerns by an edge, and a reply to the content it answers; says what a label of a record kind is |
| timeline | gives every content a number that is assigned at placement, is never reused, and is the same wherever the content is read; says what reads the contents that concern one thing, across tasks |
| record and request | carries a change to a record as a change of state ([Request](../../../modules/computer/request/request.md)), so that an answered question is a reply and not an edit |

Until those domains answer, the rules of this module state only what a task must be able to do (a reply names numbers with reasons, an ask is addressed, a chain ends) and no way of storing it.

### A task has contents, and a verb adds one
A text of a task is a content of it, numbered in the order added; the current plan is the latest content of kind plan and the current report the latest of kind report. `memar task add-content --task-id ID --kind KIND [--content-file FILE] [--author A]` adds a text as the next content of the kind (the text on standard input when no file is given) and prints its number. It takes any text and any kind that is a label of the label module, knows no template and checks the text against none, which is the caller's; it never writes over a content, and a content of the kind added between its read and its write refuses it. A task in the old shape is refused untouched. The abstraction is [the content store](protocol/task-content-store.kh) and [the service](services/task-content-add/task-content-add.kh); the realization is `services/add_content.py` over `services/task_files.py`, and the rule is [a text of a task is added and never replaced](rules/a-text-of-a-task-is-added-never-replaced/a-text-of-a-task-is-added-never-replaced.md).

### A new task is compared where it is made and after it exists
Owner (2026-10-30): the task domain does not understand text, so whether a task repeats another is asked of text, content and timeline. The comparison is mechanical and reads everything both tasks hold, title, plan, messages and the documents named; a title-only comparison is refused, because what it leaves out would be paid for by the organization's workers at the price of an intelligence. It is made by a comparer widget, an aggregate in a pluggable module, over a base service that only ranks (ids and a likeness each, normalized, the task and its relatives excluded); the widget may show a task's status, asked of the service that owns it. The design is for millions of tasks: the text domain keeps a structure it updates on an event and does not rebuild per call. A human meets the widget in the GUI form, an intelligence in the process that makes a task, which first prints and holds nobody; a gate follows only after a test on the existing tasks. `ensure` itself is to be split into single-purpose services composed by widgets. On a likeness the widget only asks and relates and closes nothing. The structure the text domain keeps is its own and no file beside the documents. The rule is [a task is compared by everything it holds](rules/a-task-is-compared-by-everything-it-holds/a-task-is-compared-by-everything-it-holds.md).

### The making of a task is composed of single-purpose services, and widgets compose them
Owner (2026-10-30): `ensure` does many things in one function; it is split so that each service has one responsibility, and a widget, the aggregator, is what a maker meets ([a widget aggregates, a service does one thing](../../../modules/computer/ui/rules/widget-aggregates/widget-aggregates.md)). A concept is declared once, by the module whose subject it is, with its storage, retrieval, validation, display and refusals, and the task domain imports it ([a concept has one owner module](../../../modules/knowledge/modularity/rules/one-owner/one-owner.md)): the title is the title module's, the priority the task's, the template of a plan and of a report the plan's and the report's, the writing of a plan the timeline's, labels the label module's, the project, its location and its preferences the project module's, the conversation a task belongs to a new pluggable conversation module's (a widget over the timeline and content domains), and the log, the idle sweep, the hand-over brief and the new id their owners'. What stays in the task domain is its own: the relations and the status of a task, finding and opening one, the answer to which tasks hold a text, the gate and its replay, and the widgets that compose all of these.

The first abstraction written for this (a hundred definitions in this module) declared the owners' things here and is withdrawn; the critique that withdrew it, with the table of who owns each thing, is in `docs/ownership-critique.md` of task 20261030-T094103Z-28324. The abstraction is rewritten to the reduced set when the owners' abstractions exist (tasks 20261030-T163540Z-10624 to 20261030-T163545Z-10624), and the implement tasks wait on it.

- **The kernel calls no pluggable module.** The widgets and services are in this pluggable module and call the kernel's task and the text domain.
- **Everything is checked before anything is made,** by the intake widget; a refusal leaves no half-made task.
- **The comparer is a widget** of the text domain's ranker, the relatives, the status and the display; both makers use it: the form for a human as the title and text are typed, the process for an intelligence once before making, printing the hits. A Complete task among the hits is cited as done before.
- **The first build holds nobody.** The process composes the advisory gate; the holding gate is bound by a project's choice only after a replay over the existing tasks held nobody wrongly (a task is held wrongly when every hit of it is its parent, a child, a sibling made on purpose or a series).
- **Making is not file-based in its abstraction;** where a task is kept is the project's place.
- **Not designed here:** the event after a task is made for a maker that never met the widget, and the `same_ask` relation it would propose, whose trigger, author and remover the owner has not named.

### Migration
The old texts of every facet (handoffs and changelogs first, being the largest) are not converted by a script that moves their text. Each such text, alone or with the ones related to it, becomes one or more tasks that take the queue's statuses; the handoff's text becomes content on the timeline of that task, and what is open in it becomes asks that are open. A changelog record becomes a change record on the timeline of the task that made it, where its `Tasks` field names one, and on a task made for it where it names none. The migration is queued after the series of the memory-port programme (the task `20261023-T074642Z-5420` and what depends on it) has settled where tasks and texts are kept, so that no domain is rewritten against a file layout. Until then the texts of the facets that are records (handoff, changelog) are not extended: a result that would have gone into a handoff is written as a message with the template on the task that finds it, and the creation of that task is made quick and checked for a related one first (task `20261030-T073507Z-6324`, which is to be done before the handoffs are removed).

### The size of what is migrated
Counted on 2026-10-30 by `services/inventory.py` in the design task's folder: 205 task folders; 199 plans, in 23 key sets and 13 section sets; 198 reports, in 16 section sets; 221 changelog files holding 1294 entries, of which 1281 carry an hour and 13 none; 256 handoff files, whose entries carry no time and seldom a task, so the task each belongs to has to be made and cannot be found. These numbers size the migration tasks; they are not a layout.
