## Methodology
The owner's statement and examples were taken as the claim. Four situations outside those examples were then asked against it: agents who already share an interest, raw wishes that are all unsound, a contest scored by one winner, and a buyer and a seller. Governing was asked again only as a merge test. No second cognitive system was consulted. Findings are arguments from those cases. They are not a count of how often conflict appears in life.

## Findings

### The two examples fit
Who hosts each week: several sound answers, interests weighed, one arrangement the friends carry. The road: slope and length are one input; the later spurs are how the villages' interests join the work. In both, politics is the weighing. (Omid, stated; Grok, checked)

### Co-presence is not the question
Two parents who want the same treatment for a child are in medicine. Nothing in that question points their interests at different answers. The science is not triggered by two agents sharing a room. The owner's "because a conflict of interest exists" explains why the science is often needed wherever agents live together. It does not make every shared moment already a political question. The tested condition is the question whose interests diverge. (Grok)

### Unsound raw wishes still leave the science in place
Both villages can want a line that would destroy the spring they share. Weighing can reach a joint answer that is neither raw wish, and can still add an instrument so the landowners join. The better answer is not required to be one of the answers each interest first named. (Grok)

### A single-winner contest stays a contest
Chess is two agents and opposed wishes, and its point is that one of them wins. That point is the contest. The same people are in this science when they step outside the board to seek an answer they carry together. The definition does not swallow every competition. (Grok)

### A price composes with economics
A buyer and a seller can each live with several prices. Weighing those interests is this science. The account and the transaction that record the result stay economics types. The sciences meet. One does not replace the other. (Grok)

### Governing stays a different module
A friendship has this science and has no governing apparatus. No argument in these cases was strong enough to fold the science into that apparatus. (Grok)

## Conclusion
The owner's science stands, with one precision graduated into [politics.md](politics.md): politics is the weighing of a question on which interests already point at different sound answers, toward an answer the group can carry together. Mere co-presence is not the trigger. A single-winner contest and an aligned technical or medical question stay what they are. Governing stays apart. The owner has not yet reviewed this graduation. The concept page is ready to add to the project on that statement. A graph type and a final folder parent were outside this inquiry and remain on the handoff.
