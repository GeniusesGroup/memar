The page moved from a root `modules-plugable/politics/` to `modules-plugable/management/politics/`, the nearest existing category. The definition is the owner's: weighing conflicting interests among intelligent agents, with the weekly host and the road between two villages as the worked examples. Government is left as its own topic.

#### Deliberation
Owner: a plugable folder with no parent invites every later folder to skip categorization. When the exact home is unknown, choose the nearest parent. A later move remains possible.

Owner: political science is needed wherever more than one intelligent agent is present, because interests conflict and each agent's interest lies in a different answer. In a friendship, who hosts each week can be the person with the larger home, the better cook, or the richer host. Each answer can be sound. Politics weighs the interests and reaches a better answer. For a road, each village gains if the road passes beside it. A shorter line on a better slope can be the better route, and politics also answers the villages: a spur to each in a later phase, so both sets of landowners join.

Owner: bring another definition if politics is something else, and keep governance separate unless the argument is strong.

Grok: the friendship and the road are the definition. Governance is the apparatus that may later carry an instituted answer. I have no argument that merges the science into that apparatus. The earlier objections (undefined "correct", science versus skill, friendship as merely a contract) treated the examples as edge cases. The owner made them the center: several sound answers, interests weighed, a better group answer, including the side that brings cooperation.
