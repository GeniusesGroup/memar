# Priority Rule — A Priority Change States Its Reason
A rule of this module: when a thing's Priority_Rank on the Priority label edge changes, the reason is recorded where that thing's durable narrative lives (for a Task: a plan content). Moved on 2026-11-01 from the Handoff Practice; moved on 2026-11-07 from the Task rules folder to Priority's owner.

## The rule
- **Anyone, a person or an agent, may change a thing's Priority_Rank, whatever other labels it holds.**
- **The one who changes it records why** in that thing's durable narrative (not in this module's document).

## Why it exists
A Priority_Rank is a judgment; the next reader weighs it only when its reason is there.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may order peers by the number alone, and then cannot tell a considered rank from a guess.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not give the range or the default; `Priority_Rank` does.
- It does not own queue or cache ordering.
- This repository runs no check of the reason today.
