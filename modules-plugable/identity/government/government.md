# Government (identifier)

Holds `Field_GovernmentID` — a government-issued identifier on a user — not subsidy, tax, or a state-as-organization model.

This is an optional identity capability: User and Person remain identifiable without a government ID scheme. Kernel `modules/identity/` must not own a particular issuer's numbering. Related URI schemes (national ID, passport) stay under `modules-plugable/uri/`.
