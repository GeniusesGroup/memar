# Articles
A comment label for a law of an organization.

## Abstract
Articles, in the sense of an organization's articles of association, names a [law](../../../../modules/rights/law/law.md) the commenter treats as that organization's own instrument. The instrument is not a different type because the organization is not a government.

## Explanation

### Definition
The commenter means: this law is the one this organization uses as its articles.

A company, a club, or a society can each be that organization. The label does not say which. It does not move the instrument out of the law type, and it does not rank it above or below any other law.
