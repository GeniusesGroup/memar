# Claim Labels Handoff

Open work for the claim-label draft. The adjudication questions are on the [case handoff](../../../modules/rights/case/case.handoff.md). This file does not move them here.

## Topic & Purpose

A claim-type word, including complaint (shekvaieh), is a label. It is not a case and not an edge. This module is the registry of those words.

## Status

Active

## Decisions

- **Decided (2026-09-27).** A complaint is a tag or label that groups claim types, plus the text explanation a type already carries. It is not a node. He treats typed categories as a paper-era aid. The label stays because a label is already recognized. Each claim-type label gets a folder on the pattern of law labels. Complaint is the word registered in this draft.
- **Decided (2026-09-27, evening).** Civil versus criminal stays a label, not a different adjudication model. Execution is one compelled service whose input is not only a number, for either word. Those two words are not registered here. Which labels exist is pluggable. A folder is not required to keep one adjudication model.
- **Decided (2026-09-27).** A label does not link claims, does not weight a vote, and does not redraw kidnap, stolen, or illegal.

## Ambiguities Resolved

Critique v2's question on whether the pilot keeps separate entry types is answered by one adjudication model and a label.

## Open Questions

1. **Is there a numeric constraint on the civil and criminal split?** He left a subtlety open on 2026-09-27 and called for it to be critiqued. The evening recorded the subtlety as closed because it was not a numeric constraint. He has since said he is not certain that close was right, so it is open again. The only candidate any consulted source names is a **joint numeric remedy floor across per-issue amounts**: ICC Article 78(3) has the chamber pronounce an individual sentence per crime and then a joint sentence that cannot be less than the highest individual sentence, so the total is not the sum of the per-count terms. Critique v2's test for whether the candidate would force a **different edge** rather than a different label is the falsifier: if the constraint is only an aggregation rule over amounts already produced, it belongs to the vote-aggregation question on the [case handoff](../../../modules/rights/case/case.handoff.md), not to this label. Matters because the two words are unregistered on the strength of the close, and registering them is not required to keep one adjudication model. Blocks whether civil and criminal may ever need separate treatment, and blocks any statement that the split carries no numeric constraint.
