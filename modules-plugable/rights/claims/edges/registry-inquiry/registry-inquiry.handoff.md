# Registry Inquiry Handoff

Open work for the registry-inquiry draft. Votes, the ballot, and execution of a changed service stay on the [case handoff](../../../../../modules/rights/case/case.handoff.md).

## Topic & Purpose

A registry inquiry is a claim like any other. It asks a target organization for information. It is not a gate on other cases.

## Status

Active

## Decisions

- **Decided (2026-09-27).** A registry inquiry is its own claim. It is not a draft-gate that must exist before other cases. Later claims do not stay draft until it is accepted. The draft chain may change after the inquiry, or it may not exist yet, because the filer may not know the defendant.
- **Decided (2026-09-27).** The inquiry's defendant is the target organization. One defendant. Example: an account opened in someone else's name; the information must come from that organization into the forum. A second organization is a second claim. Memar UUIDs name the organizations. A blockchain is not required.
- **Decided (2026-09-27).** The claimant must prove the need. If that proof needs other claims, those claims are chained so the judge can see the inquiry is not a pretext to harvest information. He compares the inquiry to theft.
- **Decided (2026-09-27 afternoon).** When the claim is accepted, the inquiry returns an information package. The requester can file a claim against that package. The requester does not receive the underlying records. Inquiry data stays off the person. Confidentiality here is that package rule, not only a rule about judges. The package's content shape is a note for the content domain. It is not an end of this claim, and this handoff does not invent that shape.
- **Decided (2026-09-27).** Joint adjudication across two organizations is allowed as a direction. The joint procedure is not drawn (question 1).

## Open Questions

1. **Joint procedure.** He allowed joint adjudication across two organizations as a direction, and the draft says the joint procedure is not drawn. A second organization is a second claim on the petition chain. What, if anything, the procedure is beyond that chain is not stated. Matters because two forums can otherwise be drawn as two defendants on one case, which he did not allow. Blocks a joint-procedure edge. It does not block the inquiry as one case with one defendant.

2. **The identity-theft service list.** Model v1 asked for the ordered list of services in that example, which step is the registry inquiry, and which thing and service are the endpoints. He answered the defendant and rejected the draft-gate. He gave the account-opened-in-someone-else's-name example. He did not give the ordered list or the other endpoints. Matters for any end besides the target organization. Blocks those ends. It does not block the inquiry case.

## Assumptions

- **Strong.** The inquiry is optional in the same way as other claim kinds. An organization that does not inquire leaves it out.
- **Weak.** "None for a second defendant" in the draft is the draft's reading of his one-defendant rule. Question 2 is still his unanswered list.
