# Family
An optional contract draft for people who opt into a household together.

## Abstract
Family is a [contract](../../../modules/rights/contract/contract.md) the parties adopt. A bundle includes this module when that organization offers the draft. Another bundle leaves it out.

## Introduction

### Motivation
A household is an arrangement among the people who choose it. This module is that draft, on its own, so a piece of software can offer family without taking every other draft with it.

## Explanation

### Definition
Family is the contract among the people who opt into one household. The parties adopt the draft, edit it, or pass it by. The terms of the draft are added on this module when a founding writes them.
