# Tax
A comment label for a law the commenter reads as a levy.

## Abstract
Tax is one word a person may attach beside a comment. Another person may attach [extortion](../extortion/extortion.md) to a comment on the same law. Neither word is the law.

## Explanation

### Definition
The commenter means: they read the instrument as a levy owed under its human code.

The label does not set the rate, the payer, or the base. Those are the human code and the computer code. The label does not win against extortion. Both may be attached. A reader may filter which comments they see. The filter does not weight a signature.
