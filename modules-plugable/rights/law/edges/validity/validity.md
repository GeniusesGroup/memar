# Law Validity

## Abstract
This page answers how long a law may bind people who did not vote for it. Two sunset records are one doctrine with two attachment points: [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) expires each citizen's vote at death or emigration yet shields hard infrastructural anchors behind a prepaid Deconstruction Cost, and [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) attaches the same expiry to the law — past statutes survive only by the new generation's re-affirmation. Information originates in the vote and also attaches to the law. Civic maturity is an event — accepting the laws, joining — not a calendar age. Silence is not assent: inherited law needs an affirmative act. The *form* of a law is not this page's subject; that type lives at kernel [Law](../../../modules/rights/law/law.md).

## Introduction

### Motivation
A vote that outlives the citizen, and a statute that outlives every voter who made it, impose the dead on the living. The two records on this page attack that inheritance at different attachment points — the vote's weight, and the law's remaining force — and they share one substrate: a citizenship-status-aware vote registry. What a legal text must contain is a different failure, already answered by the kernel type; mixing the two makes an objection to a coding gate look like an objection to generational reset.

## Explanation

### The problem the answers share
For how long may a law bind future generations, and what remains standing when the vote that built it is annulled? Each record takes a position on duration (does the law die with its voters' status, does the vote die while the law lives, or does the law live only through re-approval?) and substrate (what remains standing when the vote is annulled?). Form — what a law node contains — is not asked here.

### Approaches

#### The four-facet engineering structure of a law (text, purpose, interpretation, sanction)
The form of a law is the kernel type at [Law](../../../modules/rights/law/law.md), not a statute of a government bundle. This heading is kept so inbound anchors do not break. Do not restate the type here.

#### Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)
**Mechanism:** Each citizen's vote on laws stands valid only during that citizen's life or presence in the society; with death or emigration it is annulled, so the decisions of the past are not imposed on the new generation. At the same time, to prevent physical instability, "soft behavioural laws" are separated from "hard infrastructural anchors" (civil projects such as PRT lines), and the repeal of any hard law is conditioned on estimating and pre-funding a "restoration-to-natural-state cost" (Deconstruction Cost) fund.
**Metrics:** Instant expiry of votes upon change of citizenship status in the software; a mandatory civil-recovery financial appendix on 100% of infrastructural approvals.
**Prerequisites:** Implementation of a vote-lifetime variable in the sovereign platform's database; economic estimation models for demolition and ecological recovery.
**Conventional-model flaw:** The sanctification of rigid laws from past decades and the imposition of inefficient administrative and physical structures on new generations without a real right of revision.
**Tags:** philosophy of law, fluid democracy, infrastructural anchors, vote time-bounding, future generations' rights
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and constitutional
**Fit & substrate:** [Adaptable to existing fabric (Retrofit/Brownfield)] | Vote-weight can be bound to living citizenship wherever a status-aware registry exists; the existing statute book need not be voided for the vote-lapse half to run. The Deconstruction Cost appendix can attach to new infrastructural approvals without first rewriting inherited titles. What the existing fabric resists is the registry itself — death and emigration are not currently events that touch the validity of a vote — and the funded-repeal discipline, which no conventional capital-project law requires before the first stone.
**Cognitive anchor:** An inherited title deed — the paper still binds after the signatory is gone, which is the rigidity the vote-lapse half undoes, while the building the deed describes stays until someone has paid to take it down.
**Narrative hook:** "The dead stop legislating the moment they die or depart — and the towers they raised stay standing, because their demolition was paid for before their first stone."
**Syn (synergies):** [Law](../../../modules/rights/law/law.md) — the law type; [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](../../../bundles/government/modules/decision-and-deliberation-process/decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) — direct democracy; [An executive branch with no physical structure, implementing government wholly as software](../../../bundles/government/modules/government-organs/government-organs.md#an-executive-branch-with-no-physical-structure-implementing-government-wholly-as-software) — the software executive

#### Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)
**Mechanism:** Full exit from the rule of the dead over the living in the legal system of the village. The effect of every vote is conditioned directly on continuing "active citizenship": when a person's death or definitive departure is recorded, the effect of their vote is annulled from the law-validation database. Generations and new citizens stabilize past laws only through platform re-affirmation, if they choose — no law ever imposes its will on the society forever.
**Metrics:** Time of vote-weight annulment after a recorded death or departure: instant; ratification of prior approvals by the incoming generation as it reaches the age of civic maturity; complete dynamism of the law pyramid.
**Prerequisites:** [Digital direct democracy and the inalienability of the vote from representatives](../../../bundles/government/modules/decision-and-deliberation-process/decision-and-deliberation-process.md#digital-direct-democracy-and-the-inalienability-of-the-vote-from-representatives) (software direct democracy); [A self-sovereign citizen identity with no disciplinary ranking (Sovereign Digital Identity)](../../../bundles/government/modules/membership-and-identity/membership-and-identity.md#a-self-sovereign-citizen-identity-with-no-disciplinary-ranking-sovereign-digital-identity) (the self-sovereign digital identity system).
**Conventional-model flaw:** The imposition of laws and decisions made centuries ago by the past on today's generations; the rigidity of traditional constitutions and the denial of the young generation's freedom to recreate their living environment.
**Tags:** direct democracy, generational expiry, sovereignty of the living, citizenship continuity, legal platform
**Scale:** Macro — sovereign and the voter database
**Fit & substrate:** [Greenfield only] | The sunset presupposes a vote-weight database the society starts clean; in the existing fabric, statutes have hardened into titles, sentences and debts that survive their voters whatever any annulment registry says, and voiding un-reaffirmed law retroactively dissolves the certainty on which the old orders rest — the same legal crisis the judiciary records warn against for an unbacked repeal; the generational reset is workable only where the ledger of votes has not yet been written.
**Cognitive anchor:** Nowruz — every spring the old year's account closes and the living set the table anew; a Persian audience already trusts an order that renews itself once a year without a revolution.
**Narrative hook:** "No law outlives the voters who made it — when they go, every statute comes back to the living for a fresh yes."
**Syn (synergies):** [Law](../../../modules/rights/law/law.md) — the law type; [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) — separating physical infrastructural anchors from soft laws
**Source:** the village-design dialogue | the legal documents of the Persian society

### How the approaches collide and compose
**The two sunsets are one mechanism written twice, and they disagree on what dies.** Both annul a vote's effect at death or emigration; both presuppose the same citizenship-status-aware vote database (one as a "vote-lifetime variable", the other as the law-validation registry). But [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) stops there: the physical anchor is specially protected, repeals of which must be prepaid. Continuing-assent-by-silence is not current doctrine. [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) moves the expiry from votes to laws: past statutes are stabilized only by the new generation's re-affirmation. Civic maturity is the event of accepting the laws or joining, not a birthday. Silence is not assent: a living person is not bound by remaining quiet. The Deconstruction Cost shelter for hard anchors still stands; it is about the built inheritance, not about quiet consent to statutes.

**One doctrine, two attachment points: information originates in the vote and also attaches to the law.** The either/or — votes versus laws, one sunset versus two — is closed. [Time-bounding of laws' validity and separating infrastructural anchors from soft laws (Time-Bounded Laws)](law-validity.md#time-bounding-of-laws-validity-and-separating-infrastructural-anchors-from-soft-laws-time-bounded-laws) supplies the vote-lapse half and the Deconstruction Cost shelter for hard anchors. [Forced annulment of votes' effect upon death or emigration and generational reset of laws (Civic Vote Sunset & Generational Sovereignty)](law-validity.md#forced-annulment-of-votes-effect-upon-death-or-emigration-and-generational-reset-of-laws-civic-vote-sunset--generational-sovereignty) supplies the law-attachment half. They are not rival answers a citizen must choose between. Civic maturity is an event (accepting the laws / joining), not a calendar age. Silence is not assent.

Completeness of a law node — goal, explanation, executive guarantee, computer code as a gate into voting, constitution as a label, non-contradiction — is kernel [Law](../../../modules/rights/law/law.md). This page does not legislate form.
