#### What changed
The module holds basic statutes: no retroactivity, contradictory execution, contract within law, and law supersedes contract.

#### Deliberation
Owner: the module should not be limited to the two prohibitions. How a law stands relative to a contract belongs here, in the way Iranian civil code article 10 states it. The module name is laws, and the statutes are a series, not one combined document and not one folder each under rights.
