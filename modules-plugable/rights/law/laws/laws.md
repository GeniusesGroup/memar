# Laws
Basic statutes an organization may adopt. Not the law type.

## Abstract
This module holds statutes written on the [law](../../../modules/rights/law/law.md) type. A bundle includes the ones that organization wants. The type stays what a law node is. These documents are instances.

## Introduction

### Motivation
Rules about retroactivity, about two contradictory laws executing together, about when a contract binds, and about whether a law may take the place of a contract, are themselves laws. They are not fields of the law type, and they are not one module each under rights.

## Explanation

### Definition
A document in this module is a statute the organization adopts, edits, or passes by. Four are written. Further basic statutes are added as their own documents here.

- [No retroactivity](no-retroactivity/no-retroactivity.md) — a law applies to acts after it is in force.
- [Contradictory execution](contradictory-execution/contradictory-execution.md) — two laws that contradict do not both execute.
- [Contract within law](contract-within-law/contract-within-law.md) — a contract binds the people who made it, unless it explicitly contradicts a law in force.
- [Law supersedes contract](law-supersedes-contract/law-supersedes-contract.md) — a law may take the place of a contract.

What a successful dispute does about a law is an optional edge in [claims](../claims/claims.md), not a clause of these statutes.
