# Contradictory execution
Two laws that contradict do not both execute.

## Abstract
This statute is an instance of the [law](../../../../modules/rights/law/law.md) type. It forbids the pair from both executing. It does not choose which claim a dispute brings.

## Explanation

### Definition
When two laws contradict, they do not both execute. Which one a dispute removes or replaces is an optional edge in [claims](../../claims/claims.md): [annul for conflict](../../claims/edges/annul-law-for-conflict/annul-law-for-conflict.md) or [annul for update](../../claims/edges/annul-law-for-update/annul-law-for-update.md).
