# Personal
A label for an account whose quantity belongs to a person, a business, a legal entity, or a bank account.

## Abstract
Personal is one word a person may attach to an account. Another person may attach [Real](../real/real.md) to a comment on a different account. Neither word is a second account type.

## Explanation

### Definition
The commenter means: the holder they have in mind is a business, a legal entity, or a bank account.

The label does not create the legal person. Identity of that person stays in the identity domains. The label does not record title. Title stays in ownership.
