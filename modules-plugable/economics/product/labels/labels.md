# Product-kind labels
Classification of a product node (commodity, intermediate, final) is a pluggable capability, not a second `product` domain. The folder is the `labels` part of `modules-plugable/economics/product`, the pluggable module that holds core `product`'s optional parts, and restates nothing of the type.

The `label-*` folders are self-classifications under `edges-loop/`. Storefront `products.html/js/css` live under `modules-plugable/economics/product/ui/page-products`.
