# Product — optional parts
The optional parts an organization attaches to the [product](../../../modules/economics/product/product.md) type, held as one pluggable module.

## Abstract
The product type is kernel; what an organization may choose otherwise about products sits here, in one module that is installed and removed as one unit. Its one part today is the classification of a product node by labels ([labels](labels/labels.md)). Which words an organization takes is configuration inside this module. This module restates nothing of the product type.
