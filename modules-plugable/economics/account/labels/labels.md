# Account labels
Registered meanings of words a person may attach to an account.

## Abstract
Real and Personal are [labels](../../../../modules/knowledge/label/label.md) on an account. Attaching a word uses that label domain's `(thing)-[:label]`; the account stays the [bookkeeping](../../../../modules/economics/account/edges/bookkeeping/bookkeeping.md) edge.

## Explanation

### Definition
An organization includes this module when it wants a registry of account-comment labels. Each document under this module is one registered word: what a reader may mean by it.

### Registered words
- [Real](real/real.md)
- [Personal](personal/personal.md)
