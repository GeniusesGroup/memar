# Real
A label for an account whose quantity is a thing in the world, or an intangible that still has no separate legal person behind it.

## Abstract
Real is one word a person may attach to an account. It names the kind of quantity the commenter has in mind. It does not split the account node.

## Explanation

### Definition
The commenter means: the quantity is physically tangible, or it is one of the intangibles that have no physical existence and still are not a legal person.

The label does not choose the unit. The unit stays `(thing)-[:count_unit_is]->(count_unit)`.
