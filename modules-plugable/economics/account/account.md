# Account — optional parts
The optional parts an organization attaches to the [account](../../../modules/economics/account/account.md) type, held as one pluggable module.

## Abstract
The account type is kernel; what an organization may choose otherwise about accounts sits here, in one module that is installed and removed as one unit. Its one part today is the words a person may attach to an account ([labels](labels/labels.md)). Which words an organization takes is configuration inside this module. This module restates nothing of the account type. It takes the shape the owner ruled for law on 2026-10-20 ([Modules Research 001 → Owner decisions needed](../../../.agents/tasks/20261019-T191746Z-12632/docs/modules.research.001.md#6-owner-decisions-needed), D4; [Law — optional parts](../../rights/law/law.md)).
