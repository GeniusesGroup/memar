# Sensitivity
A label a person may attach to any node to mark material a reader may not want to meet unwarned.

## Abstract
Sensitivity is one word beside a comment. It can be attached to any node. It is not a rank, and it is not [Toxic](../toxic/toxic.md).

## Explanation

### Definition
The commenter means: the node should be approachable with a warning, or grouped with other material of that kind.

The label does not hide the node by itself. A rule may read it and change what a view shows. The rule is not this module.
