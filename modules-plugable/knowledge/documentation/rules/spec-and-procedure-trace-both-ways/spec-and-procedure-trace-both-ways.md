# Documentation Rule — A Template Document and Its Rules Trace Both Ways
A rule of this repository, held for [Documentation](../../documentation.md), which owns the claim in [Relationship between this document and its facet specifications](../../documentation.md#relationship-between-this-document-and-its-facet-specifications). This rule states the check a reviewer applies to a template document and to the rules of its module.

## The rule
- **Every rule of a module traces back to a claim of the template document it serves,** and the rule cites that claim.
- **Every claim of the template document that requires an act appears in a rule,** or the document says why it needs none.
- **A mismatch in either direction is a defect in one of the two,** and the reviewer names which. The template document states structure and the rules state what is done to keep it; neither restates the other.
- **A new text of the procedural kind is a rule of the owning module,** not a companion file beside the document ([No Record Is Written Beside a Document](../no-record-beside-a-document/no-record-beside-a-document.md)).

## Why it exists
A document and the rules that carry it out are written at different times, and each is edited without the other open. A claim with no rule is never done, and a rule with no claim binds on nothing.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may let its rules and its documents differ. Then neither can be trusted alone, and the reader must open both to learn which one is current.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not require every claim to have a rule; a claim that asks nothing of the reader needs none.
- It does not say that a rule is a procedure; a rule states the check and the act it governs.
