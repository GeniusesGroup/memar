# Documentation Rule — A Document Names Only Its Own Domain
A rule of this repository, held for [Documentation](../../documentation.md), which owns the claim in [Content Rule: No Fabricated or Redundant Provenance](../../documentation.md#content-rule-no-fabricated-or-redundant-provenance); it is what [A Concept Has One Owner Module](../../../../../modules/knowledge/modularity/rules/one-owner/one-owner.md) requires of a module's prose. The owner's rulings of 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06 (task 20261101-T073218Z-4460, decisions 94 and 95).

## The rule
- **A module's document names the concepts of its own module and of the modules its module imports, and no other,** not even as an example: a Group document mentions no Task. Where a concept of another domain seems needed, the dependency is the module's to declare first, or the sentence belongs to the document of the module that composes both.
- **A rule is stated once, in the module that owns its subject.** Every other document links to it and neither restates, paraphrases nor summarizes it; a document that needs the rule's effect names it by its link.
- **A base document** names a module only as the concept, as [Citation Direction](../../documentation.md#citation-direction) says.

## Why it exists
A concept named by a document that does not own it is a second statement of that concept with no owner to keep it true, and a reader takes it as the domain's own; a rule restated in a second place drifts from its home at the next change. The owner's cases were a Group document that named Task, and a phrase of One Owner that restated where a priority is declared instead of linking the rule that says it.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may let its documents illustrate with other domains' concepts, at the cost that each such mention is kept true by hand.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not limit a widget's or a bundle's document, whose subject is the composition of the modules it names.
- Until `memar content check` reports a link from a document to a module its module does not import, a reviewer applies the rule.
