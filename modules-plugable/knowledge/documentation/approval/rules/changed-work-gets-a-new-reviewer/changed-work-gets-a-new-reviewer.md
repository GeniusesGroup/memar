# Approval Rule — Changed Work Gets a New Reviewer
A rule of this repository, held for [Approval](../../approval.md), which owns the claim in [Approval Requires a Reviewer Who Did Not Do the Work](../../approval.md#approval-requires-a-reviewer-who-did-not-do-the-work). This rule states who reviews work that was reviewed once and then changed.

## The rule
- **A reviewer that has seen the claim is not asked again after the work changed.** Where the work changed materially, the review is a new review by a party that has not seen the earlier version.
- **A change that is not material** (a reworded sentence, a repaired link) may be answered by the same reviewer, whose earlier approval stays as it is and is not edited.

## Why it exists
A reviewer that has seen the claim holds a model of it, and the model is the thing the separation exists to avoid: it finds what it expects, as the maker does.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may send changed work back to the reviewer that withheld the verdict, which is faster. It then has a second pass by a reader who already knows what the work was meant to be.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not define material; the task that asks judges it, and a doubt is settled toward a new reviewer.
- It does not say how a reviewer is started; [A reviewer has not seen the work](../../../../../management/task/rules/reviewer-is-a-fresh-sub-agent/reviewer-is-a-fresh-sub-agent.md) does.
