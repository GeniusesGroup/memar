# Approval Rule — Approved Means Checked, Not Correct
A rule of this repository, held for [Approval](../../approval.md), which owns the claim in [Approval Requires a Reviewer Who Did Not Do the Work](../../approval.md#approval-requires-a-reviewer-who-did-not-do-the-work). This rule states what a reader may take from an approval.

## The rule
- **An approval is read as "a second party checked this against this evidence", and as nothing more.** It is not read as "the work is correct", and it is not quoted as a certificate.
- **A document that cites an approval states what it examined,** not only that it exists.

## Why it exists
An audit by a party that did not do the work, over evidence it gathered and weighed itself, is the strongest scrutiny available inside one collaboration. [Terminology → On Independent Verification, Today](../../../../../../docs/terminology.md#on-independent-verification-today) states what that is worth against an outside institution.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may present an approval as a certification. It then claims an independence it does not have, to readers who cannot tell.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not lower the value of an approval inside the collaboration; it bounds the claim made outside it.
