# Handoff Rule — Inventory the Task Against the Kinds
A rule of this repository, held for [Handoff](../../handoff.md), which owns the claim in [Analysis, Not Transcription](../../handoff.md#analysis-not-transcription). This rule states how the writer chooses what to place.

## The rule
Before the task ends, the writer walks the kinds of [the handoff](../../handoff.md#the-kinds) one by one (`question`, `decision`, `assumption`, `anticipated-work`) and asks of each: did this task produce anything in this category? A kind in which it produced something gets a content for each thing, and a kind in which it produced nothing is passed over knowingly.

## Why it exists
The writer chooses what counts as important, and that choice is invisible to the reader unless it is made visible. A walk by kind makes an omission a decision taken kind by kind, and not a habit that leaves a task with no question looking like a task with none open.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may write a handoff from memory of what mattered. It then gets what the writer remembers, and nobody can tell what was left out.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not require a content of every kind; the walk is required, not the result.
- It does not say how many contents a kind gets; a task places as many as its discussion produced.
