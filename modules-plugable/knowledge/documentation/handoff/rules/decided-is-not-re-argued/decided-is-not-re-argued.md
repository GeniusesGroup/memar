# Handoff Rule — A Decided Point Is Not Re-Argued
A rule of this repository, held for [Handoff](../../handoff.md), which owns the claim in [Analysis, Not Transcription](../../handoff.md#analysis-not-transcription). This rule states how a receiving task treats a decision by its confidence.

## The rule
- **A decided point is not re-argued unless the owner asks or new evidence exists.**
- **A tentative point may be revisited;** that is what the marker means.
- **A revisit is a new content** that refers to the decision and states the new evidence or the owner's word.

## Why it exists
Re-arguing without new evidence repeats the cost the handoff was produced to avoid, and a confidence that does not bind tells the reader nothing.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may let any task reopen any decision. It then pays for each decision as many times as there are tasks.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not make a decided point final; new evidence and the owner's word reopen it.
