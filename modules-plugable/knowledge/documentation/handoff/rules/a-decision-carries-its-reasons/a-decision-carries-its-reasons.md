# Handoff Rule — A Decision Carries Its Reasons
A rule of this repository, held for [Handoff](../../handoff.md), which owns the claim in [Analysis, Not Transcription](../../handoff.md#analysis-not-transcription). This rule states what a `decision` content holds beyond the verdict.

## The rule
- **A decision names the options that existed, the criteria used, and what tipped the balance.**
- **Dissent and rejected alternatives travel with the conclusion,** as its options, where a conclusion had competing considerations.
- **A resolved ambiguity records how it was resolved,** not only the verdict.
- **A decision that was approved cites the approval** ([Approval](../../../approval/approval.md)), and does not repeat the status of the task.

## Why it exists
A decision without its reasons cannot be safely revised or defended by the task that receives it. Reasons that travel with it prevent re-litigating a settled point and distinguish "settled with reasoning" from "settled by fatigue".

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may record only the verdict. It then re-argues each decision whenever the reasons are needed, and cannot know whether they still hold.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not require the reasons to be long; a reason a session with no memory can follow is enough.
- It does not move a rejected alternative into the governing document ([A Rejected Position Leaves the Body](../../../rules/rejected-position-leaves-the-body/rejected-position-leaves-the-body.md)).
