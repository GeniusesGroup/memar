# Handoff Rule — A Graduation Proposal Names the Document and the Ambiguity
A rule of this repository, held for [Handoff](../../handoff.md), which owns the claim in [What a Handoff Is](../../handoff.md#what-a-handoff-is), where a concluded discussion graduates into the governing document. This rule states the proposal made when a decision has hardened.

## The rule
- **A decision that has hardened into settled design is proposed into the governing document,** and the document is not changed before the proposal is approved.
- **The proposal names the exact document and section** and gives replacement-ready text in English.
- **It states the ambiguity the change resolves.** An addition that answers no currently-unanswerable question is not an improvement.
- **It names its type:** ambiguity resolution, gap filling, decision recording, terminology alignment, or structural improvement.
- **Its text is self-contained, precise and defined,** with key terms defined or linked, and cross-referenced to related documents and terms.
- **Once the decision is durably recorded in the governing document and in a `changelog` content, the content that carried it is answered** by a content that refers to it, and the document carries the rule from then on.

## Why it exists
A handoff is for discussions that have not reached the governed documents. A hardened decision left in it is a second home for the claim, and a proposal that names no ambiguity adds volume and no understanding.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may edit the governing document as soon as a point is settled. It then changes a governed document without the approval that makes the change a claim of the document.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not say who approves; that is the owner's, or the owner's word relayed.
- It does not replace the document's own rules for a change ([Documentation](../../../documentation.md)).
