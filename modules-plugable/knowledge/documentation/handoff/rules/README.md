# Handoff Rules (`modules-plugable/knowledge/documentation/handoff/rules/`)
Rules for how the contents that carry a discussion's state are produced and consumed. The general Memar concept of Rule is defined in [`modules/process/rule/rule.md`](../../../../../modules/process/rule/rule.md); a Rule is held by the module that owns its subject ([Rule → Which Module a Rule Belongs To](../../../../../modules/process/rule/rule.md#which-module-a-rule-belongs-to)), and the subject here is [Handoff](../handoff.md).

| Rule | Subject | Tier | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [A Handoff Is Produced When Something Persists](./a-handoff-is-produced-when-something-persists/a-handoff-is-produced-when-something-persists.md) | When contents are placed | Governance | At the end of a task or conversation that decided, resolved or opened something |
| [Inventory the Task Against the Kinds](./inventory-the-task-against-the-kinds/inventory-the-task-against-the-kinds.md) | What the writer chooses | Governance | Walk question, decision, assumption and anticipated work, kind by kind |
| [Mark Confidence While It Is Fresh](./mark-confidence-while-fresh/mark-confidence-while-fresh.md) | Confidence and stability | Governance | Marked when placed, from the declared vocabulary |
| [A Decision Carries Its Reasons](./a-decision-carries-its-reasons/a-decision-carries-its-reasons.md) | What a decision holds | Governance | Options, criteria, what tipped the balance, and the dissent |
| [A Handoff Reads Alone](./handoff-reads-alone/handoff-reads-alone.md) | The writer's check | Governance | Re-read as a reader who was not there |
| [Read the Whole Handoff, Then State It](./read-the-whole-handoff-then-state-it/read-the-whole-handoff-then-state-it.md) | How a continuing task begins | Governance | Read all, state what it builds on, move the state by new contents |
| [A Decided Point Is Not Re-Argued](./decided-is-not-re-argued/decided-is-not-re-argued.md) | Treatment of a decision by confidence | Governance | Reopened by new evidence or the owner's word only |
| [A Graduation Proposal Names the Document and the Ambiguity](./graduation-proposal-names-document-and-ambiguity/graduation-proposal-names-document-and-ambiguity.md) | A hardened decision | Governance | Proposed into the governing document with replacement-ready text |

The Rules are governance-tier and are enforced by no check this repository runs today; an organization adopting this repository's Rules may keep, relax, or replace any of them without forking anything.
