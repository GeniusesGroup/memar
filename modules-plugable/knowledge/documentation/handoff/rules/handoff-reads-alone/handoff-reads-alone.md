# Handoff Rule — A Handoff Reads Alone
A rule of this repository, held for [Handoff](../../handoff.md), which owns the claim in [The kinds](../../handoff.md#the-kinds). This rule states the check the writer applies before the task ends.

## The rule
- **The writer re-reads the contents as the next task will,** a reader who was not there: could a task with no memory of the discussion pick up every thread from them alone? A content for which the answer is no is rewritten before the task ends.
- **A question states what is asked, why it matters, what each answer would change, and whether it blocks.**
- **An assumption states its stability and the consequence of its failure.**
- **Each content is specific.** "We discussed modeling approaches" fails; a decision with its option and its reason passes.

## Why it exists
The reader is a session, a returning person or a colleague with none of the discussion. A content they cannot follow costs the re-explanation the handoff exists to avoid.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may let the writer judge the contents clear. It then finds the gap when the next task asks what was meant, and the writer is no longer there.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not require the reader to avoid the cited documents; a content may point at them.
- It does not say that a clear content is a true one.
