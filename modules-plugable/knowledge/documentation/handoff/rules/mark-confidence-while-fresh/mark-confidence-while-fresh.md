# Handoff Rule — Mark Confidence While It Is Fresh
A rule of this repository, held for [Handoff](../../handoff.md), which owns the claim in [Analysis, Not Transcription](../../handoff.md#analysis-not-transcription). This rule states when and with what vocabulary a content is marked.

## The rule
- **Each decision gets its confidence, and each assumption its stability, when the content is placed,** before the certainty the discussion had has faded.
- **The levels are the controlled vocabulary the kinds declare** ([`primitive`](../../primitive/), the types carrying the labels `confidence` and `stability`); no level is invented, so the receiving session never guesses what the writer meant.
- **An assumption states what would change if it proved wrong.**

## Why it exists
The most common error of distilling is a tentative point hardening into a decided one. It happens in the minutes after the discussion, when the writer remembers a conclusion and not how firm it was.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may mark confidence at the end of a longer session. It then marks what it remembers, and a point that was tentative is recorded as decided.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not choose the level; the writer judges it from the discussion.
- It does not forbid revising a level later; a revision is a new content that refers to the earlier.
