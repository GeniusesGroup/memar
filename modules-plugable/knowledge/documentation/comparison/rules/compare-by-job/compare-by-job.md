# Comparison Rule — Compare by Job
A rule of [Documentation — Comparison](../../comparison.md).

## The rule
- **An essay separates projects by the job a reader would hire each to do, rather than treating implementation layers or delivery shapes as rival purposes.**

## Why it exists
Workflow packs, distribution platforms, domain knowledge, and development frameworks may meet in one stack while answering different questions.
