# Comparison Rule — Durable Claims
A rule of [Documentation — Comparison](../../comparison.md).

## The rule
- **An essay states claims that remain true as models and tools change, rather than framing a conclusion around a transient weakness of the current generation of agents.**

## Why it exists
A comparison is retained as task content, so its lessons must survive the technology incident that prompted the examination.
