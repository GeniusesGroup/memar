# Comparison Rule — Disclose Unverified Claims
A rule of [Documentation — Comparison](../../comparison.md).

## The rule
- **Under “Open for critique,” an essay names claims it could not verify from the inspected material, including its own project's claims cited in the essay.**
- **When every claim was verifiable, the essay records that clean result.**

## Why it exists
A reader can then distinguish a clean verification pass from an omitted verification pass.
