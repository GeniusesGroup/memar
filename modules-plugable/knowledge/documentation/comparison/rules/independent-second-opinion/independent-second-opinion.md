# Comparison Rule — Independent Second Opinion
A rule of [Documentation — Comparison](../../comparison.md).

## The rule
- **A party that did not draft an essay re-verifies each count, file-and-line citation, and restated normative sentence.**
- **The essay records that pass and its corrections, including a clean pass.**

## Why it exists
The pass checks the evidence without replacing the drafter, and its record lets a reader distinguish an independent check from none.
