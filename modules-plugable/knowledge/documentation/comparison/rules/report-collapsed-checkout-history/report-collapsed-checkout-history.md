# Comparison Rule — Report Collapsed Checkout History
A rule of [Documentation — Comparison](../../comparison.md).

## The rule
- **When an inspected checkout has collapsed history, an essay states that once in its source-status account and treats the missing history as a finding about the source.**

## Why it exists
The reader needs to know whether the inspected material can show why its state changed.
