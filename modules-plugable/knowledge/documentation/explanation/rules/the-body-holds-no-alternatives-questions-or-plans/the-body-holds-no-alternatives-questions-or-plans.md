# Explanation Rule — The Body Holds No Alternatives, Questions or Plans
A rule of this repository, held for [Explanation](../../explanation.md), which owns the claim in [Relevance discipline](../../explanation.md#relevance-discipline). This rule states where the matter a body must not hold is placed.

## The rule
- **A considered and rejected alternative** is written in the Options of a `decision` content ([Handoff → The kinds](../../../handoff/handoff.md#the-kinds)), not in the body ([A Rejected Position Leaves the Body](../../../rules/rejected-position-leaves-the-body/rejected-position-leaves-the-body.md)).
- **An open question** is a `question` content of a task.
- **Anticipated work** is an `anticipated-work` content.
- **A result** has no section: its home is set by its type ([Results routing](../../../documentation.md#results-routing)), and an observed outcome is not body content.
- **A claim that is genuinely tentative says so in its own statement;** the question and the work that resolves it live in the content.

## Why it exists
An open question may be answered or moot, and an anticipated extension is not current design. Loaded without the task, the body reads both as design, and a negated alternative as a position still held.

## Keep it or drop it
An organization may keep open questions in the body. It then keeps working state in a place read as the settled state.

## What this rule does not claim
- It does not remove the premise evidence of a claim; that stays in the body, cited inline at the claim it supports.
- It does not say how a content is placed; the store does.
