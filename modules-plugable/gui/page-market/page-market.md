# Market
[Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market) is a term used to describe concepts such as:
- [Market (economics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_(economics)), system in which parties engage in transactions according to supply and demand.
- [Marketplace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketplace), a physical marketplace or public market
- [Marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketing), the act of satisfying and retaining customers
